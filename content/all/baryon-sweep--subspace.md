# Baryon Sweep

## Summary

Maintenance procedure using baryon radiation to remove accumulated particles, lethal to organic life.

## Description

Baryon sweeps emit high-intensity baryon radiation that effectively removes accumulated baryon particles from ship systems and structures. While highly effective for maintenance, the radiation is immediately lethal to all organic life forms, requiring complete evacuation during the procedure.

The sweep progressively moves through the vessel at a controlled rate, allowing for systematic decontamination. Baryon buildup can interfere with ship systems over time, making periodic sweeps necessary despite the logistical challenges of evacuating the entire crew. Emergency protocols exist for rapid sweep cancellation if organic life is detected in the sweep zone.
