# Self-Healing Fuselage Materials

## Summary

Bio-mimetic composites that automatically repair micro-cracks and damage.

## Description

Advanced composite materials embedded with micro-capsules of healing agents (or vascular networks) that rupture and polymerize upon impact or fatigue cracking. This technology aims to extend airframe lifecycles, reduce maintenance costs, and prevent catastrophic failures by arresting structural damage at the microscopic level before it spreads.
