# Subspace Anomaly Detector

## Summary

Specialized sensors for identifying distortions and unusual phenomena in subspace fabric.

## Description

Subspace anomaly detectors monitor the underlying structure of space-time, identifying distortions, rifts, and unusual phenomena that exist in subspace layers. These sensors are critical for detecting wormholes, subspace tears, spatial anomalies, and other exotic phenomena that pose navigation hazards.

The detectors measure variations in subspace field strength, graviton fluctuations, and temporal distortions. They provide early warning of dangerous spatial phenomena and help chart safe routes through regions with unstable subspace conditions. The technology is essential for maintaining warp field stability and avoiding catastrophic encounters with subspace anomalies.
