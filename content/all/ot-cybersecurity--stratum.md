# OT Cybersecurity Defense

## Summary

Securing industrial control systems from digital threats.

## Description

As operational technology (OT) becomes more connected, specialized cybersecurity solutions are required to protect SCADA systems and industrial controllers. These technologies prevent unauthorized access that could lead to physical damage, safety incidents, or production stoppages.
