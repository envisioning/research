# Crisis Mobility Coordination Systems

## Summary

Real-time platforms managing evacuations, refugee flows, and emergency travel.

## Description

Interoperable systems coordinating airlines, governments, NGOs, and ground transport to rapidly move people during climate disasters, conflicts, or health emergencies, optimizing routes, capacity, and humanitarian aid distribution.
