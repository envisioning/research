# AI Romance Disclosure Standards

## Summary

Policies requiring clear disclosure when intimacy is mediated or simulated by AI.

## Description

Design and regulatory standards that ensure users know when they are engaging with bots, AI-augmented partners, or synthetic media. They aim to prevent deception, coercive design, and over-attachment to entities misrepresented as human.
