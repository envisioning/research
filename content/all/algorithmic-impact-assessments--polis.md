# Algorithmic Impact Assessments

## Summary

Mandatory transparency reports for high-risk AI in government.

## Description

Standardized frameworks (mandated by EU AI Act and similar laws) requiring agencies to document AI system risks, training data, bias mitigations, and human oversight before deployment in sensitive areas like benefits, policing, or immigration. They create audit trails for accountability and public scrutiny.
