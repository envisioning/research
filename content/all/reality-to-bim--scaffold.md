# Continuous As-Built Verification (Reality-to-BIM)

## Summary

Frequent scans and photos auto-compared to BIM to catch clashes, deviations, and missing installs early.

## Description

Reality capture becomes more valuable when automated: point clouds and images are registered to the model, deviations are quantified, and exceptions are routed to the right trade. This turns quality control into a continuous process that reduces punch lists and prevents expensive rework from compounding late in the schedule.
