# Green Hydrogen Distribution Networks

## Summary

Dedicated pipelines and storage hubs for renewable hydrogen transport.

## Description

Purpose-built or repurposed natural gas pipelines carry green hydrogen from electrolyzers at renewable sites to industrial clusters, ports, and power plants. Coupled with underground salt-cavern storage, these networks enable seasonal energy storage and decarbonization of sectors like steel, chemicals, and heavy transport.
