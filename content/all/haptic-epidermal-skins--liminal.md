# Haptic Epidermal Skins

## Summary

Ultra-thin, flexible interfaces that deliver touch sensations.

## Description

Wearable electronic skins containing arrays of micro-actuators. These interfaces can render textures, temperature, and pressure from virtual objects directly onto the user's skin, bridging the sensory gap between the digital and physical worlds.
