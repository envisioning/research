# Embodied Carbon Accounting (LCA + EPD Automation)

## Summary

Tools that compute embodied carbon from models and procurement data using EPDs and lifecycle assessment.

## Description

Embodied carbon is increasingly a permitting, financing, and procurement constraint. Software that connects BIM quantities to Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), LCA databases, and bid packages enables apples-to-apples comparisons of material options and supports reporting requirements for public and private owners.
