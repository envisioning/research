# AI-Driven Air Traffic Management

## Summary

Machine learning systems for dynamic airspace optimization and routing.

## Description

AI-driven Air Traffic Management (ATM) utilizes machine learning algorithms to predict traffic flows, optimize flight paths in real-time, and manage congestion. This technology is essential for integrating new airspace users like drones and air taxis into existing commercial traffic safely and efficiently.
