# Gamma Frequency Neuro-entrainment

## Summary

Software protocols modulating light at 40Hz to stimulate cognitive resilience and memory.

## Description

Emerging research suggests that imperceptible 40Hz flicker (gamma entrainment) can reduce amyloid plaque load and improve cognition in Alzheimer's patients. This software layer manages the precise timing and safety of therapeutic flicker, integrating medical protocols into standard lighting environments.
