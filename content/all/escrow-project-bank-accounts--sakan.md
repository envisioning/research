# Escrow & Project Bank Accounts for Off-Plan Delivery

## Summary

Controlled cashflow mechanisms that release buyer funds by verified construction milestones, strengthening trust and reducing delivery risk in off-plan markets.

## Description

Escrow and project bank account mechanisms ring-fence buyer payments and tie fund releases to construction progress verified by third parties. This improves investor confidence, reduces fraud risk, and can lower financing costs by demonstrating stronger governance over delivery.

In GCC, off-plan sales are central to market liquidity—especially in Dubai—so escrow plumbing is a major 'trust infrastructure' signal. For Saudi giga-projects entering global markets, credible escrow frameworks and milestone verification will increasingly influence foreign demand and institutional participation.

The signal represents the maturation of off-plan markets from relationship-based trust to regulated, auditable cashflow controls. Key challenges include standardizing verification processes, aligning developer financing structures with escrow requirements, and ensuring transparent reporting without slowing project execution.
