# OLED Lighting Panels

## Summary

Large-area diffuse light sources enabling ultra-thin, low-glare architectural illumination.

## Description

Organic LED (OLED) lighting produces uniform, low-luminance surfaces that can reduce glare compared to point sources. Emerging form factors (flexible/transparent panels) support luminous ceilings, wall-integrated lighting, and decorative luminaires where visual comfort and spatial ambience matter as much as efficiency.
