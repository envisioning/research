# Cross-Sector Hybrids (NGO + DAO + Public Body)

## Summary

Cross-sector hybrids (NGO + DAO + public body), as new organizational forms blend traditional categories.

## Description

New organizational forms are emerging that blend NGO structures, decentralized autonomous organizations (DAOs), public bodies, and other models in ways that don't fit traditional categories. These hybrids experiment with governance, ownership, decision-making, and accountability models that combine elements from different sectors. This signal reflects attempts to create organizations better suited to complex challenges that require cross-sector coordination and innovation in organizational design.
