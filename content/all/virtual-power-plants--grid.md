# Virtual Power Plants (VPP)

## Summary

Aggregated networks of distributed energy resources acting as one.

## Description

VPPs coordinate thousands of home batteries, EVs, and smart thermostats to provide peak capacity and grid services, reducing the need for carbon-intensive peaker plants.
