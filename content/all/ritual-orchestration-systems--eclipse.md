# Ritual Orchestration Systems

## Summary

Engines that generate culturally-sensitive, personalized end-of-life rituals.

## Description

AI-assisted planning tools that synthesize cultural norms, spiritual preferences, accessibility needs, and legal constraints to propose multi-stage rituals—from bedside vigils to memorial events—coordinating media, participants, and locations.
