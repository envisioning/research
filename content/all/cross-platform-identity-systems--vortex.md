# Cross-Platform Identity Systems

## Summary

Unified digital identities and avatars across services.

## Description

Interoperable protocols that let users carry their identity, avatars, purchases, and social graphs across streaming platforms, games, and virtual worlds, reducing lock-in and enabling seamless experiences.
