# AI-Assisted Foresight & Portfolio Sensing

## Summary

AI used for grant triage, pattern detection, bias auditing, and continuous learning systems in philanthropic intelligence.

## Description

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used to support philanthropic decision-making, from initial grant triage to pattern detection across portfolios. AI-assisted foresight tools help foundations scan the horizon for emerging issues, while portfolio sensing systems provide continuous learning about what works. However, this signal also raises questions about bias, transparency, and whether AI systems reinforce existing power structures or enable more equitable decision-making.
