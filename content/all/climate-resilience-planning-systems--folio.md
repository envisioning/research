# Climate-Resilience Planning Systems

## Summary

Predictive modeling for disaster preparedness and migration.

## Description

Decision-support tools that model climate risks—flooding, fire, heat—against collection locations and recommend mitigation strategies, evacuation protocols, and digitization priorities. They help libraries anticipate and adapt to escalating environmental threats.
