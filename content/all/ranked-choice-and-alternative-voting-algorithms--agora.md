# Ranked Choice & Alternative Voting Algorithms

## Summary

IRV, approval, STAR, and other non-plurality methods.

## Description

Tested implementations of instant-runoff voting (IRV), approval voting, STAR voting, and Condorcet methods with secure tallying, clear audit trails, and voter education interfaces. Expands democratic choice beyond first-past-the-post while maintaining verifiability.
