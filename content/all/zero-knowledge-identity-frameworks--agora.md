# Zero-Knowledge Identity Frameworks

## Summary

Proving eligibility without revealing personal data.

## Description

Cryptographic protocols enabling citizens to prove specific attributes (e.g., 'over 18', 'resident of district X') to access services or vote, without sharing their name, address, or other private information with the government or third parties.
