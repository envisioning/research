# Advanced Cold-Chain Thermal Storage

## Summary

Phase-change materials for resilient refrigeration.

## Description

High-capacity phase-change materials integrated into cold rooms, trucks, and containers buffer temperature fluctuations, protecting perishables during grid outages and long-haul transport.
