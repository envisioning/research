# Equitable Death Tech Access

## Summary

Frameworks ensuring death tech reaches underserved populations.

## Description

Policy and technology initiatives addressing the 'death tech divide'—subsidized digital legacy tools for low-income families, multilingual interfaces, offline-first designs for low-connectivity regions, and partnerships with community health workers to democratize access to end-of-life innovations.
