# Long-Term Stewardship Models

## Summary

Development models where developers or community organizations maintain long-term responsibility for social value, beyond initial construction.

## Description

Long-term stewardship models create ongoing responsibility for maintaining and delivering social value from developments, beyond initial construction. These models can include community land trusts, developer-operated community facilities, or resident-managed amenities.

In Benelux, stewardship models can ensure developments continue generating social value over time, addressing concerns about short-term extractive development. Examples include Dutch housing associations managing community facilities, Belgian cooperative management models, and Luxembourg long-term community partnerships.

The signal represents a shift from one-time development to ongoing community partnership. Key challenges include ensuring long-term viability, maintaining community engagement over time, and funding ongoing stewardship activities.
