# Biometric Identification Systems

## Summary

Large-scale iris, facial, and gait recognition across theaters.

## Description

Integrated biometric suites combine multi-modal sensors with watchlist databases to authenticate personnel at bases, checkpoints, and coalition networks, supporting trusted access at scale.
