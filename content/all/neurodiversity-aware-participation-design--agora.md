# Neurodiversity-Aware Participation Design

## Summary

Inclusive interfaces for non-neurotypical civic engagement.

## Description

Deliberation and voting systems designed for autistic, ADHD, dyslexic, and other neurodivergent users. Features include sensory-friendly interfaces, asynchronous participation, structured inputs, and explicit social cues to reduce cognitive load.
