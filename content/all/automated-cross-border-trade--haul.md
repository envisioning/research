# Automated Cross-Border Trade Compliance

## Summary

Systems that automate customs, trade compliance, and documentation for global shipments.

## Description

These applications codify complex tariff schedules, trade agreements, and local regulations into rules engines and AI models. They automate document generation, classification, and screening for sanctions and restricted parties, reducing delays and compliance risks in global logistics flows.
