# Virtual Nature Therapy

## Summary

Immersive VR environments for psychological restoration.

## Description

High-fidelity Virtual Reality applications that simulate natural environments to provide rapid psychological restoration. Used in workplaces and clinical settings, these experiences are proven to lower heart rate and cortisol levels, offering a digital respite for mental recovery.
