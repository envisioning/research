# Chronic Disease Virtual Care Programs

## Summary

Longitudinal telehealth and remote monitoring models for chronic conditions like heart failure and diabetes.

## Description

Chronic care programs combine home monitoring devices, asynchronous messaging, and scheduled virtual visits with multidisciplinary teams. Algorithms flag early signs of deterioration—such as weight gain in heart failure or glucose trends in diabetes—enabling proactive medication titration and lifestyle interventions that reduce avoidable hospitalizations and ED visits.
