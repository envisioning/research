# Participatory GIS & Civic Mapping

## Summary

Resident-driven mapping of assets, risks, and inequities.

## Description

Tools for community mapping (housing, transit, environmental hazards, public safety) that combine official datasets with lived experience. Supports more legitimate planning by making contested realities explicit and enabling evidence-backed advocacy.
