# Citizen Deliberation Platforms

## Summary

Sortition-based digital assemblies for long-horizon decision-making.

## Description

Scalable platforms that convene randomly selected, demographically representative citizens to deliberate on issues like geoengineering, AI deployment, and nuclear policy. Supported by expert briefings and deliberative tools, they add a democratic layer to decisions with civilizational stakes.
