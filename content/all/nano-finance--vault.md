# Nano-Finance & Micro-Insurance

## Summary

Ultra-small-ticket financial products for emerging markets.

## Description

Mobile-first platforms delivering micro-loans ($1–$100), nano-insurance (pay-per-day, event-based), and fractional savings products targeting unbanked and underbanked populations in Africa, South Asia, and Latin America.
