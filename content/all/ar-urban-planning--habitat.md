# AR Urban Planning

## Summary

Augmented reality tools for in-situ visualization of future infrastructure.

## Description

Mobile and wearable AR applications that allow citizens and stakeholders to visualize proposed developments overlaid on the physical world at 1:1 scale. This technology democratizes the planning process, facilitating public engagement and enabling immediate feedback on visual impact and spatial integration of new projects.
