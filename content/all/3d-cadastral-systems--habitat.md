# 3D Cadastral Systems

## Summary

Three-dimensional land and property registries for dense, mixed-use environments.

## Description

Digital cadastre platforms that represent ownership, rights, and restrictions in 3D rather than flat parcels. Essential for high-rise, underground, and mixed-use developments, these systems clarify air rights, easements, and subsurface infrastructure, reducing legal friction and enabling more intricate spatial planning.
