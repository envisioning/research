# Privacy-Preserving Analytics

## Summary

Usage insights with strong mathematical privacy guarantees.

## Description

Analytics pipelines that apply techniques like differential privacy and secure aggregation to circulation and clickstream data. They help libraries understand patterns of use and inform service design without exposing individual reading histories or sensitive behavior.
