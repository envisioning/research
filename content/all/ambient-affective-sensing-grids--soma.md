# Ambient Affective Sensing Grids

## Summary

Distributed sensor meshes that infer group mood and social dynamics in spaces.

## Description

Infrastructure that combines microphones, RF sensing, wearables, and environmental sensors to estimate crowd affect, engagement, and tension in workplaces, campuses, galleries, or public spaces while abstracting away explicit identities.
