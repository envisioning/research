# Tamper-Evident Voting Kiosks

## Summary

Secure, transparent hardware for verifiable in-person voting.

## Description

Next-generation voting terminals combining open-source hardware designs with physical paper trails and transparent casing. These kiosks allow voters to verify their ballot physically while ensuring digital tallies are auditable and tamper-proof.
