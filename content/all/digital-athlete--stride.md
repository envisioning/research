# AI Digital Athlete Twins

## Summary

Virtual simulations of athletes for injury prediction and workload management.

## Description

Advanced machine learning models that create a 'digital twin' of an athlete by synthesizing millions of data points from training and competition. These simulations predict injury risks under various scenarios and optimize rehabilitation protocols by modeling physiological responses to specific workloads.
