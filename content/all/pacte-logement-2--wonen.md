# Pacte Logement 2.0

## Summary

Luxembourg's national housing pact incentivizing municipalities to increase housing supply through financial rewards and planning flexibility.

## Description

Pacte Logement 2.0 is Luxembourg's policy framework incentivizing municipalities to increase housing production. Municipalities that meet housing targets receive additional state funding and planning flexibility; those that don't face reduced support. It aims to overcome local resistance to development.

The pact includes requirements for affordable housing quotas, density bonuses, and streamlined permits for compliant projects. It represents a national framework to address local opposition to development.

The signal represents financial incentives to align local and national housing goals. Key challenges include ensuring targets are ambitious enough, preventing municipalities from meeting targets with luxury housing only, and maintaining quality alongside quantity.
