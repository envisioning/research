# Space Tether Systems

## Summary

Momentum-exchange cables that fling payloads without expending fuel.

## Description

Rotating tethers in orbit catch and release spacecraft, transferring momentum to change their trajectories. This enables propellant-free boosts from LEO to higher orbits or interplanetary trajectories. Electrodynamic tethers can also generate power or provide drag for orbital maneuvering.
