# Quadratic Voting Protocols

## Summary

Algorithms allowing expression of preference intensity.

## Description

Voting systems where participants receive a budget of credits to 'buy' votes for issues, with the cost increasing quadratically (1 vote = 1 credit, 2 votes = 4 credits). This captures not just what the majority wants, but how strongly they care about it, protecting minority interests.
