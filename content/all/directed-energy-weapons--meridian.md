# Directed Energy Weapons

## Summary

Laser and microwave systems for defense.

## Description

High-power laser and microwave systems capable of disabling drones, missiles, and electronic systems at speed-of-light engagement, fundamentally changing air defense economics and reducing reliance on kinetic interceptors.
