# Distributed Ledger Verification

## Summary

Blockchain for treaties and supply chains.

## Description

Tamper-evident distributed ledgers that create verifiable records for arms control treaties, trade agreements, carbon credits, and supply chain provenance, reducing verification costs and increasing trust in international commitments.
