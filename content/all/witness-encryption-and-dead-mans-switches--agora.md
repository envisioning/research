# Witness Encryption & Dead Man's Switches

## Summary

Cryptographic insurance for high-risk accountability.

## Description

Encryption schemes where data decrypts automatically if a specific condition is met (e.g., 'if I don't check in for 7 days' or 'if a specific blockchain block hash appears'). Used by whistleblowers and activists to ensure evidence is released if they are silenced.
