# Prediction Markets for Policy (Futarchy)

## Summary

Harnessing market incentives for forecasting policy outcomes.

## Description

Information markets where participants bet on the likelihood of future events (e.g., 'Will GDP rise if Policy X passes?'). These markets aggregate distributed knowledge to predict the consequences of policy decisions more accurately than expert committees, offering a 'vote on values, bet on beliefs' governance model.
