# Telescoped Ammunition

## Summary

Cartridges with projectiles nested inside propellant for compact weapon systems

## Description

Telescoped ammunition nests the projectile within the propellant, reducing cartridge length. Research and development. Benefits: more compact magazines, reduced weapon size. Applications: small arms, Lightweight Small Arms Technologies.
