# Ladder voor Duurzame Verstedelijking (Ladder for Sustainable Urbanization)

## Summary

Dutch sequential test requiring proof of housing need and prioritization of existing urban areas before greenfield development is permitted.

## Description

The Ladder voor Duurzame Verstedelijking (Ladder for Sustainable Urbanization) is a Dutch planning instrument requiring developers to demonstrate: (1) a regional need for the proposed development, and (2) that the need cannot be met within existing urban areas before greenfield sites can be developed.

This 'brownfield first' principle aims to prevent sprawl and protect green space, but can delay projects that fail to demonstrate sufficient need or urban alternatives. Courts have rejected developments based on ladder arguments.

The signal represents structured prioritization of infill over expansion. Key challenges include defining 'need' in housing shortage contexts, balancing brownfield priority with development speed, and ensuring the ladder doesn't become a tool for blocking rather than guiding development.
