# Internal Talent Marketplaces

## Summary

Gig-style platforms matching employees to projects across the enterprise.

## Description

Applications that list internal projects, missions, and micro-gigs, letting employees bid their time and skills rather than waiting for top-down assignments. They enable dynamic reallocation of talent, support career exploration, and surface underutilized capabilities across regions and business units.
