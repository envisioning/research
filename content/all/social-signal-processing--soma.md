# Social Signal Processing

## Summary

Computational analysis of non-verbal cues in group interactions.

## Description

Systems that extract and interpret social signals—turn-taking patterns, proxemics, gaze coordination, vocal synchrony, and gesture mirroring—to understand power dynamics, rapport, influence, and cohesion in teams and communities.
