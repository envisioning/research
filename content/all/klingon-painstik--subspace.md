# Painstik

## Summary

Electrified rod used in rites of passage symbolizing endurance and warrior will.

## Description

Ceremonial weapon delivering controlled electrical shocks, used in the Age of Ascension and other Klingon rituals. Tests a warrior's ability to endure pain without showing weakness, proving their readiness for adult responsibilities.
