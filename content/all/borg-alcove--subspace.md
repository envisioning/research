# Borg Regeneration Alcove

## Summary

Recharging and maintenance station for Borg drones providing power and software updates.

## Description

Borg alcoves are specialized docking stations where Borg drones regenerate, recharge, and receive software and hardware updates from the Collective. These alcoves provide both physical support and neural connection to the larger Borg network, serving as the closest equivalent to sleep for assimilated beings.

During regeneration cycles, the alcove replenishes the drone's power reserves, repairs damaged cybernetic components, and updates nanoprobes and software. The neural connection allows the drone to remain linked to the Collective consciousness while in a low-power state, processing shared information and receiving new directives. Drones must regenerate periodically or face declining efficiency and eventual system failure. Severed drones who regain individuality often experience profound psychological adjustment to sleeping without the constant presence of the Collective.
