# Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)

## Summary

Factory-built nuclear reactors with simplified designs and enhanced safety.

## Description

SMRs are compact nuclear fission systems (50–300 MW) manufactured in factories and shipped to sites for assembly. Their passive safety features, shorter construction times, and modularity make them suitable for replacing retiring coal plants, powering data centers, or providing off-grid baseload in remote regions.
