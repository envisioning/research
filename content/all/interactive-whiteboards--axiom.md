# Interactive Whiteboards

## Summary

Touch-enabled boards integrating video, annotation, and remote learners.

## Description

Interactive whiteboards are large-format touch-sensitive displays that combine the functionality of traditional whiteboards with digital capabilities, enabling multi-user touch interaction, real-time annotation, and seamless integration with video conferencing, cloud storage, and educational software platforms. These systems use infrared, capacitive, or optical touch detection to support multiple simultaneous users, allowing collaborative note-taking, diagramming, and problem-solving. Advanced models integrate cameras and microphones for video conferencing, enabling remote learners to participate fully in classroom activities, while cloud connectivity allows content to be saved, shared, and accessed from any device, making these boards essential tools for blended and hybrid learning environments.

This innovation addresses the need for collaborative, interactive learning tools that work seamlessly across in-person and remote settings, where traditional whiteboards and projectors are limited in their interactivity and connectivity. By providing digital, connected, multi-user interfaces, interactive whiteboards enable more engaging, collaborative learning experiences. Companies like SMART Technologies, Promethean, and various display manufacturers have developed these systems, with interactive whiteboards becoming standard equipment in modern classrooms and corporate training facilities.

The technology is particularly significant for hybrid and blended learning, where tools that seamlessly connect in-person and remote participants are essential. As display technology improves and costs decrease, interactive whiteboards could become standard in all educational and training settings. However, ensuring teacher training, maintaining content quality, and maximizing the interactive capabilities remain important considerations. The technology represents a mature and widely-adopted educational technology, with clear benefits for collaborative learning and hybrid instruction.
