# Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)

## Summary

Hardware for theoretically unbreakable encryption.

## Description

Physical devices that use quantum mechanics to distribute encryption keys, ensuring secure diplomatic and military communications that are immune to decryption by future quantum computers.
