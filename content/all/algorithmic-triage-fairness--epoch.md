# Algorithmic Triage Fairness

## Summary

Ensuring AI-driven longevity resource allocation does not encode historical biases.

## Description

Standards and auditing tools for AI systems used in healthcare to allocate scarce longevity treatments. This ensures that decisions about who receives life-extending therapies are based on medical need and potential benefit rather than socioeconomic status, race, or other discriminatory factors.
