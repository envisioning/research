# Digital Product Passports (DPP)

## Summary

Standardized digital records tracking product origin and composition.

## Description

Driven by regulations like the EU Battery Regulation, Digital Product Passports provide a scannable digital twin for materials. They host verified data on chemical composition, carbon footprint, recycled content, and human rights due diligence, enabling circular economy loops and proving compliance for market access.
