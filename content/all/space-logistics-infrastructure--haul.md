# Space Logistics Infrastructure

## Summary

Orbital depots, lunar bases, and cislunar transport systems for off-world supply chains.

## Description

As space commercialization accelerates, dedicated logistics infrastructure is emerging for satellite servicing, lunar mining operations, and Mars missions. This includes orbital refueling stations, autonomous cargo spacecraft, and AI-driven mission planning systems that operate without real-time Earth communication.
