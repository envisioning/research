# Wireless Power Transfer Infrastructure

## Summary

Inductive, resonant, and RF systems eliminating cables for robotics and IoT.

## Description

Wireless power transfer embeds charging coils in roads for dynamic EV charging, workspace surfaces for robot swarms, or urban environments for sensor networks. By removing charging downtime and physical connectors, it enables truly autonomous mobile systems and reduces infrastructure maintenance.
