# Natural Language to BIM (Multimodal Generative AI)

## Summary

Converting text/voice descriptions and sketches into 3D models and parametric families.

## Description

Generative models that interpret natural language specifications, hand-drawn sketches, and reference images to produce BIM geometry and parametric objects. This lowers the barrier for conceptual design iteration and enables non-modelers (clients, field supervisors) to contribute ideas, though validation and compliance checks remain essential.
