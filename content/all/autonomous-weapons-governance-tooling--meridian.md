# Autonomous Weapons Governance Tooling

## Summary

Technical enforcement of emerging norms.

## Description

Verification, logging, and constraint-enforcement systems embedded in autonomous weapons platforms that provide auditability, compliance checks, and remote disablement aligned with international humanitarian law and arms-control regimes.
