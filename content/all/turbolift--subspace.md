# Turbolift

## Summary

High-speed transportation system moving personnel vertically and horizontally throughout starships and stations.

## Description

Turbolifts are the primary internal transportation system on Starfleet vessels and space stations, providing rapid movement between decks and sections. Unlike simple elevators, turbolifts can travel both vertically and horizontally through complex shaft networks that span entire vessels.

The system uses electromagnetic propulsion and inertial dampening to achieve high speeds while maintaining passenger comfort. Computer-controlled routing optimizes traffic flow, with multiple turbolifts sharing shaft space through sophisticated scheduling. Voice-activated controls allow users to specify destinations naturally, and the system includes safety features like emergency stops and backup power. On large starships, the turbolift network comprises hundreds of cars and thousands of meters of shaft space, making it essential infrastructure for crew mobility.
