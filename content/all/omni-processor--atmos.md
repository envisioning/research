# Omni Processor

## Summary

Waste-to-energy system converting sewage to electricity and potable water.

## Description

Omni Processor (Gates Foundation / Janicki Bioenergy) burns sewage solids to generate steam for electricity and purifies water for drinking. Research and development; some prototypes deployed. Applications: sanitation and energy in resource-limited settings.
