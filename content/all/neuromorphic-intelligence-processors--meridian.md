# Neuromorphic Intelligence Processors

## Summary

Brain-inspired chips for real-time analysis.

## Description

Hardware that mimics neural architectures to process massive unstructured intelligence data—video, audio, signals—with orders of magnitude better energy efficiency than traditional architectures, enabling edge deployment in contested environments.
