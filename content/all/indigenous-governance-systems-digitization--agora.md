# Indigenous Governance Systems Digitization

## Summary

Technology that respects and enables customary decision-making.

## Description

Platforms co-designed with Indigenous communities to support consensus, elder consultation, oral traditions, and non-Western decision structures. Prioritizes cultural protocol, data sovereignty, and self-determination over imported democratic templates.
