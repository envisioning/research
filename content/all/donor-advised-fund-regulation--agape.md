# Donor-Advised Fund Regulation & Scrutiny

## Summary

Growing regulatory scrutiny of donor-advised funds, questioning tax benefits without mandatory payout requirements.

## Description

Donor-advised funds (DAFs) have become one of the fastest-growing vehicles for charitable giving, but face increasing scrutiny over their structure—donors receive immediate tax benefits while funds can sit indefinitely without being distributed. This signal captures regulatory proposals requiring minimum payouts, increased transparency, and questions about whether DAFs serve the public interest or primarily benefit wealthy donors seeking tax advantages. The debate reflects broader tensions about accountability, timing, and who benefits from the tax treatment of charitable giving.
