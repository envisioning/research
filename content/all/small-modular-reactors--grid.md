# Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)

## Summary

Compact nuclear fission reactors designed for modular manufacturing.

## Description

SMRs offer a lower-carbon baseload power source that is safer and more adaptable than traditional nuclear plants, suitable for repowering retiring coal sites and providing consistent energy.
