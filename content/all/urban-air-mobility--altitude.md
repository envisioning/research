# Urban Air Mobility (UAM)

## Summary

On-demand electric air taxi networks for intra-city transportation.

## Description

Urban Air Mobility envisions a safe and efficient air transportation system where everything from small package delivery drones to passenger-carrying air taxis operate above populated areas. Key enablers include quiet eVTOL aircraft, vertiport infrastructure, and automated traffic management.
