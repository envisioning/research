# Relationship Transition & Ending Tools

## Summary

Platforms for graceful dissolution, divorce mediation, and closure.

## Description

Applications that support the often-neglected work of ending relationships well—offering mediation, asset division, co-parenting transitions, social network navigation, and emotional processing. They reframe breakups as life transitions deserving of structure and care.
