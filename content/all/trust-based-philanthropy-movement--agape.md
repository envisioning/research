# Trust-Based Philanthropy Movement

## Summary

Growing movement toward unrestricted, multi-year funding with reduced reporting burdens and greater grantee autonomy.

## Description

The trust-based philanthropy movement advocates for fundamentally different funder-grantee relationships: unrestricted funding, multi-year commitments, simplified applications and reporting, and genuine partnership rather than transactional exchange. This signal captures how trust-based approaches are spreading from early adopters to mainstream philanthropy, driven by evidence that trust improves outcomes and grantee demands for less burdensome relationships. The movement challenges deeply embedded practices and power dynamics in the philanthropic sector.
