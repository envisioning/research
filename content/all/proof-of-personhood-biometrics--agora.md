# Privacy-Preserving Proof of Personhood

## Summary

Hardware-based sybil resistance without identity disclosure.

## Description

Specialized hardware (like iris scanners) that verifies a user is a unique human without storing their image or linking to real-world identity documents. It generates a unique zero-knowledge hash to prevent bots and multiple voting in global digital systems.
