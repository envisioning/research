# Adaptive Focus Pods

## Summary

Smart micro-environments that reconfigure for different modes of work.

## Description

Sound-isolating work pods with programmable lighting, acoustics, and environmental controls that automatically adjust to focus work, deep collaboration, or recovery. Integrated sensors infer the occupant’s activity and cognitive load, then dynamically tune the space or suggest breaks.
