# Impact-Sensing Helmets & Mouthguards

## Summary

Embedded accelerometers tracking head impacts and concussion risk.

## Description

Instrumented helmets and mouthguards with high-frequency accelerometers and gyroscopes that record linear and rotational head impacts. Data streams into sideline dashboards to flag concussion risks, inform tackling technique changes, and support long-term brain health research.
