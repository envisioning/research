# Large-Scale 3D Printing

## Summary

Additive manufacturing of full-scale building components or entire structures.

## Description

The use of massive 3D printers to extrude concrete or other composites layer-by-layer to build walls, housing modules, or bridges. This drastically reduces material waste and construction time.
