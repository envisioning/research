# Epigenetic Wearables

## Summary

Noninvasive devices that track methylation drift, inflammation signatures, and mitochondrial output in real time.

## Description

Next-generation wearable sensors capable of detecting circulating biomarkers and physiological correlates of epigenetic aging (e.g., methylation drift) through skin interstitial fluid or sweat. These devices provide continuous, real-time feedback on biological aging rates, allowing individuals to immediately assess the impact of lifestyle and therapeutic interventions.
