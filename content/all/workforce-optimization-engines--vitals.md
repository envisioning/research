# AI Workforce Optimization Engines

## Summary

Predictive scheduling and workload-balancing tools for clinical and non-clinical staff.

## Description

These systems forecast patient volumes and acuity, then generate optimized staffing plans that match the right skill mix to expected demand across units. They factor in staff preferences, labor regulations, and fatigue risk to build equitable schedules, helping reduce burnout, overtime costs, and unsafe staffing levels while maintaining high-quality care.
