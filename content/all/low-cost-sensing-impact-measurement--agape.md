# Low-Cost Sensing Devices for Impact Measurement

## Summary

IoT sensors and devices tracking program outcomes in the field, enabling real-time monitoring of social interventions.

## Description

Low-cost sensing devices—from air quality monitors to water sensors to activity trackers—enable real-time monitoring of program outcomes in the field. These devices promise to provide objective, continuous data about intervention effectiveness without relying on self-reporting or periodic evaluations. However, they also raise questions about privacy, whether sensor data captures meaningful outcomes, how to interpret sensor readings in context, and whether monitoring creates surveillance risks. The devices represent a shift toward continuous, objective measurement while highlighting tensions between monitoring and autonomy.
