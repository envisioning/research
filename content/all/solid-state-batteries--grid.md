# Solid-State Batteries

## Summary

Battery technology using solid electrolytes for higher safety and energy density.

## Description

Solid-state batteries replace liquid electrolytes with solid materials, significantly reducing fire risk and enabling higher energy density, making them ideal for safe, compact grid storage installations in urban areas.
