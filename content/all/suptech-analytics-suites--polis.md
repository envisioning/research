# SupTech Analytics Suites

## Summary

Advanced analytics tools used by supervisors to oversee regulated sectors.

## Description

Supervisory technology (SupTech) systems that ingest high-frequency data from financial institutions, utilities, or platforms to detect anomalies, systemic risks, and non-compliance. They augment human supervisors with dashboards, alerts, and investigative workflows.
