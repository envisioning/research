# mmWave Small Cells

## Summary

Compact base stations operating in millimeter-wave bands for dense hotspots.

## Description

Low-power radio nodes deployed on street furniture, buildings, and indoors that operate in high-frequency bands (24–100 GHz). They provide extreme capacity over short ranges, ideal for stadiums, transport hubs, and dense city centers, but require careful planning due to line-of-sight constraints.
