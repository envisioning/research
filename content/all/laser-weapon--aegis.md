# Laser Weapon

## Summary

## Description

Laser weapons use high-power lasers for tracking and destruction of rockets, bombs, drones, and other targets. Research, development, trials. Applications: missile defense, drone defense, counter-UAS. Challenges: power, beam quality, atmospheric effects, cost.
