# Climate Adaptation & Resilience Scenario Modeling

## Summary

Simulating flood, heat, wildfire, and storm risk to inform siting, design, and operations.

## Description

Combining climate projections, hydrology, and structural models to evaluate long-term risk and adaptation strategies (elevated foundations, passive cooling, fire-resistant materials). Increasingly required for insurance, permitting, and financing in vulnerable geographies including coastal cities, floodplains, and wildfire zones globally.
