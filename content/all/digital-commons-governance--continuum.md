# Digital Commons Governance

## Summary

Stewarding shared digital infrastructure as public goods.

## Description

Institutional models (cooperatives, public trusts, multi-stakeholder bodies) governing essential digital resources—DNS, protocols, datasets, AI models—as commons rather than private property. They ensure equitable access, prevent enclosure, and align digital infrastructure with long-term civilizational resilience rather than short-term extraction.
