# Cross-Border eID Schemes

## Summary

Interoperable electronic identity systems spanning countries and regions.

## Description

Cross-Border eID Schemes connect national and regional electronic identity systems so that citizens and organizations can authenticate and sign across jurisdictions. They rely on shared assurance frameworks, trust registries, and mutual recognition of credentials, enabling seamless access to public services, finance, and cross-border commerce.
