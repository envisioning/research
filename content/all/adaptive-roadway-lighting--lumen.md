# Adaptive Roadway & Tunnel Lighting

## Summary

Dynamic illumination that responds to traffic, weather, and visibility to improve safety and cut energy use.

## Description

Road and tunnel lighting can adapt in real time to vehicle flow, fog/rain, ambient luminance, and incident response. Coupled with glare-aware optics and robust controls, adaptive schemes improve visibility and reduce energy by dimming when conditions allow—while maintaining standards for safety-critical corridors.
