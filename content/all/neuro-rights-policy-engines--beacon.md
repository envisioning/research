# Neuro-Rights Policy Engines

## Summary

Machine-readable constraints on brain-data use.

## Description

Policy engines that encode neuro-rights regulation into enforceable access rules for brain-computer interfaces, mediating what forms of inference, storage, and sharing are permissible for neural and emotional states.
