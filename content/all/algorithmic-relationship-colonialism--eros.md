# Algorithmic Relationship Colonialism

## Summary

Resistance to Western relationship norms embedded in global platforms.

## Description

Critical frameworks examining how dating apps, social platforms, and AI companions export Western individualist, romantic-love-centric models globally—eroding diverse cultural practices around arranged marriage, collective decision-making, and non-nuclear family structures. Advocates for pluralistic design.
