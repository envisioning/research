# Sybil-Resistance Mechanisms

## Summary

Preventing fake identities in digital democracy.

## Description

Advanced verification systems, such as 'Proof of Humanity' or social-graph analysis, that prevent bad actors from creating thousands of fake accounts (Sybils) to skew polls, manipulate consensus, or drain public resources.
