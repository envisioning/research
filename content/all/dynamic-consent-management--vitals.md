# Dynamic Consent & Data Governance

## Summary

Fine-grained consent systems that adapt to evolving data uses and patient preferences.

## Description

Dynamic consent platforms let patients specify how their data may be used—for direct care, quality improvement, research, or AI training—and update those choices over time. Integrated governance rules ensure that downstream systems respect consent flags, enabling ethically aligned innovation while preserving trust and regulatory compliance.
