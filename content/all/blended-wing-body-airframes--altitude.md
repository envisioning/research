# Blended Wing Body (BWB) & Novel Airframes

## Summary

Step-change aerodynamic efficiency through radical aircraft geometry.

## Description

BWB designs integrate the wing and fuselage into a single lifting surface, offering ~20% better lift-to-drag ratios than tube-and-wing. Challenges include passenger acceptance (limited windows), emergency egress, structural certification, and the need for new manufacturing / airport infrastructure. China, NASA, and Airbus are all pursuing demonstrators.
