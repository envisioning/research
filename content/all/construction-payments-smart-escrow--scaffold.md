# Digital Payments, Smart Escrow & Lien Automation

## Summary

Automated pay apps, lien releases, and escrow workflows that reduce payment friction across tiers.

## Description

The financial plumbing of construction—pay applications, retainage, lien waivers, and escrow—creates delay and dispute. Emerging payment platforms integrate compliance documents, automate lien workflows, and improve transparency across subcontractor tiers, impacting working capital and project delivery risk.
