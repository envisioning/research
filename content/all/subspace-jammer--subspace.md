# Subspace Jammer

## Summary

Electronic-warfare system that degrades enemy FTL comms and sensors.

## Description

Injects broadband subspace noise and targeted harmonics to disrupt links and desynchronize relay networks. Often paired with frequency-agile comms for friendly throughput.
