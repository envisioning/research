# Federated Learning Networks

## Summary

Collaborative AI training across multiple factories without sharing raw data.

## Description

Federated learning enables AI models to train on data from thousands of machines across different organizations without ever moving the raw data from its local device. This allows manufacturers to build powerful, industry-wide predictive maintenance or quality models while strictly preserving intellectual property and data privacy.
