# Cross-Cultural Relationship Pattern Libraries

## Summary

Databases of relationship norms, scripts, and expectations across cultures.

## Description

Comprehensive libraries that map how different cultures approach courtship, marriage, family roles, conflict, and intimacy. These inform matchmaking, coaching tools, and intercultural relationship support—helping partners navigate differences in communication styles, family expectations, and life transitions.
