# Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP)

## Summary

Orbital solar arrays beaming clean energy wirelessly to Earth.

## Description

SBSP involves massive satellite arrays capturing continuous solar energy in orbit (unaffected by night or weather) and transmitting it to Earth via microwave or laser beams. Recent advances in lightweight modular structures and wireless power transmission are moving this from sci-fi to a viable baseload energy solution.
