# Climate Migration Relationship Continuity Tools

## Summary

Infrastructure to maintain bonds during displacement and relocation.

## Description

Applications that help families and communities preserve relationships through climate-induced migration. They combine logistics (reunion planning, resource sharing), cultural preservation (language, traditions), and emotional support to counter the relational trauma of forced displacement.
