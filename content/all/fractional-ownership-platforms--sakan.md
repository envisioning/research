# Fractional Ownership & Tokenized Real Estate

## Summary

Regulated digital platforms allowing investors to purchase fractional shares of properties, democratizing real estate investment.

## Description

Fractional ownership platforms utilize digital technologies and legal structures (SPVs) to allow investors to buy small shares of real estate assets, starting from as little as $150. In the GCC, particularly Dubai (regulated by DFSA) and increasingly Saudi Arabia (Fintech Saudi), these platforms are booming. They democratize access to high-yielding real estate classes previously reserved for wealthy individuals. Tokenization adds a layer of liquidity, potentially allowing secondary market trading of property shares.

The signal represents a shift from 'lumpy', high-capital real estate investment to liquid, accessible, and diversified portfolios. Key players like Stake and SmartCrowd are validating the model. Challenges include regulatory navigation across different GCC jurisdictions and building trust in digital asset ownership.
