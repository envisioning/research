# Future Generations & Interspecies Representation

## Summary

Proxy mechanisms for non-present stakeholders.

## Description

Institutional designs and software that give voice to future generations, ecosystems, and non-human species in policy decisions. Includes long-term scenario modeling, guardianship roles, and stakeholder weighting schemes that expand the 'demos' beyond current humans.
