# Reality Capture & 4D/5D BIM

## Summary

Continuous site scanning linked to BIM for schedule and cost intelligence.

## Description

Workflows that combine drones, mobile LiDAR, and 360° imaging with 4D/5D BIM models to detect deviations from plan in near real time. By comparing as-built geometry and progress against design and baseline budgets, they enable early clash detection, automated progress payments, and data-rich claims management.
