# Long-Distance Ritual Platforms

## Summary

Apps that structure recurring micro-rituals for remote relationships.

## Description

Applications designed around daily and weekly rituals—shared breakfasts, end-of-day check-ins, synchronized walks—that integrate video, audio, and ambient cues. They emphasize rhythm and continuity rather than just chat or video calls.
