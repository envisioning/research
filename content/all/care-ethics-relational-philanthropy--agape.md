# Care Ethics & Relational Philanthropy

## Summary

Growing influence of care ethics emphasizing relationships, interdependence, and emotional dimensions of giving.

## Description

Care ethics offers an alternative to utilitarian and rights-based frameworks, emphasizing relationships, interdependence, and the emotional and relational dimensions of human life. This signal captures how care ethics is influencing philanthropic practice—valuing ongoing relationships over transactional grants, recognizing the labor of care, and centering those who are marginalized by traditional approaches. Care-based philanthropy challenges the detachment and rationalism of traditional funding models.
