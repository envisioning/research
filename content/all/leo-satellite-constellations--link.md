# LEO Satellite Constellations

## Summary

Swarm-like low-Earth orbit satellites for global broadband coverage.

## Description

Large constellations of satellites in low Earth orbit (typically 300–1,200 km altitude) that provide low-latency broadband to remote and underserved regions. They rely on inter-satellite laser links, phased-array antennas, and automated ground station networks to deliver continuous coverage.
