# Cognitive Digital Twins (Neuro-Architecture)

## Summary

Simulating human cognitive and emotional responses to built environments before construction.

## Description

Integrating neuroscience data with digital twins allows designers to predict how spaces affect stress, focus, and wellbeing. By simulating the 'user experience' at a cognitive level, buildings can be optimized for mental health and productivity, not just structural efficiency.
