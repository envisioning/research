# Child Synthetic Identity Safeguards

## Summary

Protecting minors from premature digital permanence.

## Description

Legal and technical protections that limit the creation, storage, and commercial use of children's synthetic identities, providing 'right to be forgotten' mechanisms, delayed consent requirements, and restrictions on training AI models on childhood data.
