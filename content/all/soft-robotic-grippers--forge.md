# Soft Robotic Grippers

## Summary

Flexible, compliant end-effectors for handling delicate or irregular objects.

## Description

Made from elastomeric materials and actuated by fluid or air pressure, soft grippers can deform around objects of any shape. This allows industrial robots to handle fragile items like food, electronics, or biological samples that would be damaged by traditional rigid claws, expanding the scope of automated handling.
