# Indigenous Identity Protection Frameworks

## Summary

Safeguarding collective and cultural identity rights.

## Description

Governance systems aligned with indigenous worldviews that treat identity as collective rather than individual property, preventing unauthorized AI replication of cultural identities, sacred practices, or traditional roles, with enforcement rooted in tribal sovereignty.
