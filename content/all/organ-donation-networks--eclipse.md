# Body & Organ Donation Networks

## Summary

Logistics platforms for tissue, organ, and whole-body donation.

## Description

Integrated systems coordinating organ procurement organizations, medical schools, and tissue banks with real-time matching, transportation logistics, consent verification, and family communication, maximizing donation rates and reducing waste in life-saving transplants and medical education.
