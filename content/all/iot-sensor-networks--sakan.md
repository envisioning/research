# IoT Sensor Networks

## Summary

City-wide sensor deployments for environmental monitoring, traffic management, and real-time urban data collection.

## Description

IoT sensor networks form the nervous system of smart cities, collecting real-time data on air quality, traffic flow, energy consumption, water usage, and public safety. In GCC smart city initiatives, these networks enable data-driven decision making and automated urban management.

Dubai's Smart City initiative deploys thousands of sensors across the emirate, while Saudi Arabia's NEOM envisions the world's most connected city. Applications range from environmental monitoring in extreme heat conditions to predictive maintenance of infrastructure and crowd management during events like Expo and Hajj.

The signal reflects the foundational shift toward data-driven urban governance, though challenges remain around data privacy, interoperability between systems, and the need for robust infrastructure in harsh desert environments.
