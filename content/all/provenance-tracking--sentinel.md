# Supply Chain & Document Provenance Tracking

## Summary

End-to-end traceability of assets and documents to secure origin and integrity.

## Description

Provenance Tracking applications bind digital and physical assets to cryptographically verifiable histories, enabling organizations to trace origin, custody, and transformation. Applied to documents, media, and products, these systems use signed metadata, tamper-evident logs, and sometimes distributed ledgers to establish authenticity and fight counterfeiting and misinformation.
