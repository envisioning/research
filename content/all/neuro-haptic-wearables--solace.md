# Neuro-Haptic Wearables

## Summary

Devices delivering tactile stimulation to regulate nervous system response.

## Description

Wearable devices that utilize specific vibration frequencies and patterns to stimulate the vagus nerve, helping to down-regulate the parasympathetic nervous system. These devices are used for stress management, panic attack mitigation, and improving general emotional resilience in high-stress environments.
