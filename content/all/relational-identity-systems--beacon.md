# Relational Identity Systems

## Summary

Non-Western models of contextual, social identity.

## Description

Identity management systems built on relational and contextual models (prevalent in many Asian, African, and indigenous cultures) rather than atomistic individualism, where identity attributes are dynamically defined by social role, relationships, and context.
