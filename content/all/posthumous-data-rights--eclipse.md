# Posthumous Data Privacy

## Summary

Frameworks for ownership and consent of deceased data.

## Description

Emerging legal and technical standards defining who owns and can access a person's data after death. This includes 'digital wills' and terms of service that explicitly address the status of accounts post-mortem to prevent unauthorized mining or simulation.
