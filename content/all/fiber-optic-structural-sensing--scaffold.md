# Embedded Structural Sensing (Fiber + MEMS)

## Summary

In-situ strain, vibration, and temperature sensing embedded into concrete, steel, and bridges.

## Description

Distributed fiber optic sensing and MEMS accelerometers embedded during construction enable continuous monitoring of curing, load, and structural response. These sensors support earlier detection of defects, better commissioning, and long-term infrastructure health monitoring for critical assets.
