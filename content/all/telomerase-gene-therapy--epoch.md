# Telomerase Gene Therapy

## Summary

Controlled activation of telomerase to extend cellular replicative capacity without triggering cancer.

## Description

Telomeres shorten with each cell division, eventually triggering senescence. This therapy uses adeno-associated viral vectors or CRISPR activation to transiently express telomerase (TERT) in somatic tissues, extending cellular lifespan. Safety mechanisms include tumor suppressor monitoring, dose titration, and restriction to post-mitotic or slowly dividing cells.
