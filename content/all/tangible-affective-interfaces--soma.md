# Tangible Affective Interfaces

## Summary

Physical objects that sense and communicate emotion through shape, texture, and temperature.

## Description

Smart materials and shape-changing interfaces that convey affect through haptic qualities—softness, warmth, breathing patterns, or morphing forms—providing non-screen-based emotional communication for children, elders, and material-centric cultures.
