# Hardened Refuge Infrastructure

## Summary

Global networks of deep, autonomous civilizational refuges.

## Description

Next-generation underground, subglacial, and submarine refuges equipped with independent power, agriculture, and communication systems. Designed to maintain small but diverse human populations and knowledge archives through extreme global catastrophes such as supervolcanic eruptions or nuclear winters.
