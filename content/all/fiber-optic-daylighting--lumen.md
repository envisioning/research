# Active Fiber-Optic Daylighting

## Summary

Systems that capture and transport real sunlight deep into subterranean or windowless spaces.

## Description

Using sun-tracking collectors and low-loss optical fibers to pipe natural sunlight into building cores, basements, or tunnels. Unlike passive skylights, these systems actively concentrate and route photons, providing the full-spectrum benefits of natural light to areas where it was previously impossible.
