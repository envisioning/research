# In Vitro Gametogenesis Platforms

## Summary

Lab-derived human gametes for assisted conception.

## Description

Bioreactors and culture systems that generate functional egg and sperm cells from stem cells, enabling new forms of fertility preservation and conception for people who cannot produce gametes.
