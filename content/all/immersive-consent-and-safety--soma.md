# Immersive Consent and Safety Protocols

## Summary

Real-time consent, boundary, and safety systems for XR social spaces.

## Description

Mechanisms such as personal safety bubbles, shared norms overlays, just-in-time consent prompts, and rapid reporting that protect users from harassment, non-consensual intimacy, and psychological harm in immersive environments.
