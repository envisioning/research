# Neurotechnology Consent Frameworks

## Summary

Ethics and safeguards for brain-sensing devices in intimate contexts.

## Description

Standards governing the use of EEG, fMRI, and other neurotechnologies that read or influence emotional and cognitive states in relationships. These address cognitive liberty, mental privacy, coercion risks, and the boundary between therapeutic use and manipulative enhancement.
