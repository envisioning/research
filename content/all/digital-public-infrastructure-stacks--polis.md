# Digital Public Infrastructure Stacks

## Summary

Core building blocks such as ID, payments, and data exchange as shared public rails.

## Description

Interoperable layers for digital identity, secure payments, and consented data exchange that can be reused across multiple policy domains. They enable faster rollout of new services while enforcing common standards for security, inclusion, and competition.
