# Digital Twin Governance Platforms

## Summary

Multi-scale simulations from national policy to city infrastructure.

## Description

Integrated digital replicas spanning policy models (economy, demographics, social systems) and physical infrastructure (utilities, transport, buildings). They enable policymakers to test interventions at multiple scales—from zoning changes to tax reforms—before real-world deployment, using real-time sensor data and citizen feedback.
