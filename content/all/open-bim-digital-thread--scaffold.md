# Open BIM Interoperability & Digital Thread

## Summary

IFC-based exchange, APIs, and data schemas that keep design, build, and operate datasets aligned.

## Description

Construction suffers when drawings, BIM, schedules, cost codes, and asset registers diverge. Open standards (IFC, IDS, COBie) and robust APIs enable a persistent digital thread from design through operations, reducing vendor lock-in and enabling automation across the lifecycle.
