# Satellite Backhaul for Civic Resilience

## Summary

Fallback connectivity for election sites and civic services.

## Description

Satellite and high-altitude connectivity options that keep critical civic infrastructure online during disasters, targeted network disruptions, or censorship. Useful for continuity of government services and for resilient communications in electoral operations.
