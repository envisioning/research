# Woonprotest (Housing Activism)

## Summary

Growing tenant and affordability movements demanding policy action on housing costs, investor restrictions, and tenant protections.

## Description

Woonprotest and related housing activism represents growing mobilization around housing affordability, tenant rights, and opposition to investor-driven housing markets. The 2021 Woonprotest in Amsterdam drew 15,000+ participants, making housing a top political issue.

These movements demand rent regulation, investor restrictions, social housing investment, and tenant protections. They represent a different dynamic than NIMBY opposition—supporting housing development but opposing 'extractive' development models.

The signal represents politicization of housing that can either enable or constrain development depending on framing. Key challenges include channeling activism into constructive policy (not just opposition), bridging tenant and would-be-tenant interests, and maintaining momentum beyond crisis moments.
