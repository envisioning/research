# Philanthropy as Parallel Governance

## Summary

Philanthropy acting as parallel governance where states fail, raising questions about democratic legitimacy and accountability.

## Description

In contexts where states are weak, absent, or actively harmful, philanthropy increasingly steps in to provide essential services and infrastructure. This signal reflects philanthropy operating as a form of parallel governance—funding schools, healthcare, safety nets, and social services that governments should provide. While this can fill critical gaps, it raises fundamental questions about democratic legitimacy, accountability, and whether private wealth should substitute for public responsibility.
