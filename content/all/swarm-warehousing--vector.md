# Swarm Robotics Warehousing

## Summary

Decentralized fleets of robots collaborating to manage inventory.

## Description

Warehousing systems where hundreds of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) operate in a swarm, coordinating their movements without a central controller to sort, pick, and pack goods. This approach offers high scalability, redundancy, and throughput.
