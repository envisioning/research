# Wearable Force Sensors

## Summary

In-shoe sensors measuring ground reaction forces and pressure distribution.

## Description

Advanced insoles and footwear equipped with force-sensitive resistors (FSRs) and 3D-printed pressure sensors. These devices provide lab-quality gait analysis, ground reaction force measurement, and impact distribution data in the field, crucial for injury prevention and maximizing biomechanical efficiency.
