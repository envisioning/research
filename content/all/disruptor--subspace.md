# Disruptor

## Summary

Klingon and Romulan directed energy weapon causing molecular disruption on impact.

## Description

Disruptors fire concentrated energy pulses that cause violent molecular disruption in their targets. Unlike phasers which use nadion particles, disruptors employ a different energy matrix that creates a distinctive visual signature.These weapons are generally less energy-efficient than phasers but can deliver higher peak power in shorter bursts, making them effective in alpha strike scenarios.
