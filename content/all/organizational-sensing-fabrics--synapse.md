# Organizational Sensing Fabrics

## Summary

Dense sensor networks that map how space, time, and teams are actually used.

## Description

Building-scale IoT systems that track occupancy, movement, environmental conditions, and collaboration patterns across offices, warehouses, and hybrid work hubs. By turning the workplace into a continuously updated dataset, these fabrics enable evidence-based decisions about space design, shift planning, and cross-team proximity.
