# Indigenous Knowledge Integration Systems

## Summary

Co-designed platforms incorporating Traditional Ecological Knowledge into operations.

## Description

Indigenous knowledge integration systems are co-developed with Indigenous communities to respectfully capture, protect, and apply Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK) in environmental monitoring, land management, and cultural heritage protection. They support Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) processes, ensure data sovereignty, and embed Indigenous perspectives in decision-making across project life cycles.
