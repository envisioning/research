# AI Pilot / Autonomy Certification Frameworks

## Summary

Evidence standards for safety, accountability, and limitations of autonomy.

## Description

As aviation moves toward higher automation and autonomy, certification must address learned components, dataset limits, corner cases, monitoring, and fail-safe behavior. Frameworks must align responsibility, avoid anthropomorphizing automation, and specify what tasks are automated rather than claiming an 'autonomous aircraft.'
