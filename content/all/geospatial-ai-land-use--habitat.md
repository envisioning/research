# Geospatial AI for Land Use

## Summary

Machine learning models extracting patterns from satellite and parcel data for zoning intelligence.

## Description

AI systems that fuse satellite imagery, cadastral maps, and socio-economic indicators to detect informal development, underutilized parcels, and climate-vulnerable zones. They support evidence-based zoning reforms, densification strategies, and targeted resilience investments across growing metropolitan regions.
