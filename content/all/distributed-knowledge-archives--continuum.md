# Distributed Knowledge Archives

## Summary

Decentralized, redundant networks preserving human knowledge.

## Description

Peer-to-peer protocols combining IPFS, blockchain, and error-correcting codes to replicate scientific papers, cultural works, and technical documentation across thousands of nodes worldwide. Designed to survive internet fragmentation, censorship, or localized catastrophes, ensuring no single point of failure can erase humanity's accumulated wisdom.
