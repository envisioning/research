# Targeted Memory Reactivation (TMR) Headbands

## Summary

Sleep wearables that use sensory cues to modify emotional memories during REM.

## Description

Specialized EEG headbands that detect specific sleep stages and trigger low-level audio or olfactory cues. Originally for learning, this tech is now applied to 'rewrite' nightmare scripts for PTSD patients or reinforce positive emotional states during sleep.
