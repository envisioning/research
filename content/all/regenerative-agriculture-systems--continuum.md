# Regenerative Agriculture Systems

## Summary

Farming practices rebuilding soil carbon and ecosystem health.

## Description

Integrated applications of no-till cultivation, cover cropping, agroforestry, and holistic grazing management that sequester gigatons of atmospheric carbon while increasing food security. Rooted in indigenous and Global South practices, these systems reverse land degradation and make agriculture a net climate solution.
