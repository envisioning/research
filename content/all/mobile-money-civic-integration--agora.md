# Mobile Money Civic Integration

## Summary

M-Pesa-style platforms for participatory budgeting and benefits.

## Description

Integration of mobile money systems (widespread in Global South) with civic participation: cash transfers tied to participatory budgeting, direct dividend payments, micro-grants for community proposals. Reduces friction and increases inclusion.
