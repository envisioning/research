# Disruptor Pistols & Rifles

## Summary

Standard Klingon energy weapons delivering lethal disintegrating bolts.

## Description

Klingon directed-energy weapons that fire concentrated disruptor bolts rather than variable-intensity beams. Far more lethal than Starfleet phasers, with settings optimized for maximum damage rather than stun capability. Reflects Klingon warrior culture's emphasis on decisive force.
