# Agricultural & Bio-lighting

## Summary

Precision light regimes to optimize plant growth, nutrition, and yield.

## Description

The application of specific light spectra to control plant morphology, flowering time, and nutritional content. In vertical farms and greenhouses, these precision regimes optimize photosynthesis and secondary metabolite production, decoupling food production from solar availability.
