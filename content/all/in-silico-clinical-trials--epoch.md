# In-Silico Clinical Trials

## Summary

Simulating longevity interventions on virtual human populations to safely accelerate research and drug approval.

## Description

High-fidelity computational models of human physiology used to test safety and efficacy of new drugs and therapies before they reach human subjects. This approach drastically reduces the cost and time of clinical trials, allowing for rapid iteration of lifespan-extending interventions.
