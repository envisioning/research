# Netcongestie (Grid Congestion & Connection Queues)

## Summary

Electricity grid capacity constraints delaying new-build connections, heat pump rollouts, and electrification—turning housing delivery into an energy-infrastructure problem.

## Description

Netcongestie refers to limited grid capacity that creates connection queues for new housing, commercial buildings, and even neighborhood-scale electrification upgrades. In parts of the Netherlands and Belgium, developers can receive permits but still be unable to connect projects on schedule.

This turns housing delivery into a systems constraint: electrification (heat pumps, EVs, all-electric new builds) depends on network upgrades that are slower than planning cycles.

The signal represents the collision of housing urgency with energy transition realities. Key challenges include accelerated grid investment, spatial prioritization (who gets capacity first), and aligning municipal housing plans with grid operator timelines to avoid stranded permits.
