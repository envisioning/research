# Displacement-Activated Spore Hub Drive

## Summary

Revolutionary propulsion utilizing mycelial network for instantaneous travel across vast distances and even between universes.

## Description

The spore drive represents a paradigm shift in propulsion technology, accessing the mycelial network—a subspace domain of fungal origin that spans the entire multiverse. By cultivating a specialized tardigrade-derived fungus and using it to interface with this network, vessels can jump instantaneously to any location.

Unlike traditional propulsion that moves through space, the spore drive allows a ship to effectively exist in all places simultaneously for a quantum moment, then collapse into the desired destination. This technology requires a biological navigator or advanced computer interface to safely traverse the mycelial plane.
