# Remains Solidification Press

## Summary

Machinery that processes cremated remains into stone-like solids.

## Description

A post-cremation technology that refines ash and applies high pressure to form clean, smooth, and durable stones. This hardware provides a tangible, portable, and less messy alternative to traditional urns, reshaping how families interact with remains.
