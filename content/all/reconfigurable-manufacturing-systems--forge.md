# Reconfigurable Manufacturing Systems (RMS)

## Summary

Modular production platforms that rapidly adapt to new products and volumes.

## Description

Reconfigurable Manufacturing Systems use modular machines, transporters, and fixtures with plug-and-play interfaces to reconfigure entire production lines in hours instead of months. Combining open standards, digital commissioning, and AI-driven layout optimization, RMS enables mass customization and rapid pivots (e.g., automotive to ventilator production) while maintaining economies of scale.
