# Adaptive Incentive Engines

## Summary

Dynamic compensation and reward systems tuned by organizational goals.

## Description

AI-driven engines that continuously adjust bonuses, recognition, and non-monetary rewards based on shifting strategic priorities and team performance. They coordinate incentives across departments to reduce local optimization, align behavior with long-term value creation, and test alternative reward designs in controlled experiments.
