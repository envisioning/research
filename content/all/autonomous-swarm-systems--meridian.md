# Autonomous Swarm Systems

## Summary

Coordinated drone networks for ISR and strike.

## Description

Large numbers of low-cost autonomous drones operating with distributed intelligence to conduct intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, and strike missions, overwhelming traditional air defenses through sheer numbers and adaptive coordination.
