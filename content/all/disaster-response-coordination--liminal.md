# Disaster Response Coordination

## Summary

Real-time spatial mapping for emergency response and relief.

## Description

Systems combining drone mapping, satellite data, and on-ground AR to create live spatial models of disaster zones. First responders see building stability assessments, locate survivors, coordinate rescue paths, and manage resource distribution through shared spatial interfaces, critical for regions vulnerable to earthquakes, floods, and conflicts.
