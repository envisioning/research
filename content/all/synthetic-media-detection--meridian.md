# Synthetic Media Detection

## Summary

Authenticating real content in the deepfake era.

## Description

Forensic tools and cryptographic provenance systems that identify AI-generated images, video, and audio, enabling verification of authentic communications from leaders during crises and countering influence operations that leverage synthetic personas and events.
