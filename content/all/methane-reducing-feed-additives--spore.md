# Methane-Reducing Feed Additives

## Summary

Seaweed and lipid additives cutting enteric emissions.

## Description

Supplements like Asparagopsis seaweed or 3-NOP inhibit methanogenesis in ruminant guts, achieving up to 80% emission reductions while improving feed efficiency.
