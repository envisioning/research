# Indigenous Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Frameworks ensuring Indigenous communities control tourism data about their territories.

## Description

Technical and governance protocols that vest ownership and control of visitor data, environmental monitoring, and cultural knowledge with Indigenous nations, enabling self-determined tourism development and benefit-sharing arrangements.
