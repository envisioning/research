# Community-Based Learning Networks

## Summary

Platforms matching learners with peers, mentors, and local experts.

## Description

Community-centric platforms that match learners with peers, mentors, and local experts for collaborative projects, office hours, and long-term guidance. AI routing helps align goals, availability, and expertise while human relationships remain the core medium of knowledge transfer.
