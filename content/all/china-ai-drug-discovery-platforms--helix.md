# AI Drug Discovery Platforms

## Summary

AstraZeneca paid $5.2 billion for access to CSPC's AI drug discovery platform — the largest AI-pharma deal in history — signaling China's emergence in computational drug design

## Description

CSPC Pharmaceutical's AI platform uses machine learning to identify drug candidates, predict their behavior in the body, and optimize molecular structures — the 'dry lab' approach that minimizes expensive wet-lab experiments. AstraZeneca's $5.2B deal (June 2025) covers AI-discovered preclinical cancer drugs and access to the underlying platform.

Insilico Medicine, a Chinese-founded company, advanced the first AI-discovered drug to Phase II clinical trials. Deep Intelligent Pharma (DIP) and dozens of other Chinese AI-pharma startups are compressing the traditional 10-year, $2B drug development timeline.

China leads in AI drug discovery patents but trails in clinical validation. The gap between computationally identifying a promising molecule and proving it works safely in humans remains vast. The AstraZeneca deal signals that Western pharma companies believe Chinese AI platforms are closing that gap.
