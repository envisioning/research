# Accessible Tourism Assistants

## Summary

AI-powered tools ensuring inclusive travel for people with disabilities.

## Description

Comprehensive accessibility mapping, real-time navigation assistance, venue preparation alerts, and personalized accommodation recommendations tailored to mobility, sensory, cognitive, and other accessibility needs across the entire journey.
