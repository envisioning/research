# Vehicle-Integrated Photovoltaics (VIPV)

## Summary

Solar cells seamlessly embedded into vehicle body panels for on-the-go charging.

## Description

The integration of high-efficiency photovoltaic cells directly into the roof, hood, and doors of electric vehicles. VIPV systems can generate 15-40 km of additional daily range from solar energy alone, reducing reliance on grid charging and enabling 'energy positive' usage patterns in sunny climates.
