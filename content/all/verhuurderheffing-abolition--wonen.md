# Verhuurderheffing (Landlord Levy) Abolition

## Summary

The 2023 abolition of the landlord levy that drained €10+ billion from Dutch housing associations, now enabling renewed development capacity.

## Description

The verhuurderheffing (landlord levy) was a tax on social housing landlords introduced in 2013, extracting over €10 billion from woningcorporaties over a decade. Its abolition in 2023 represents a major policy reversal, freeing up approximately €1.7 billion annually for housing development and sustainability.

Corporaties have committed to building 250,000 new social homes by 2030 in exchange for abolition. This 'National Performance Agreements' (Nationale Prestatieafspraken) framework links fiscal relief to concrete delivery targets.

The signal represents a pivotal shift from extracting value from social housing to reinvesting in it. Key challenges include whether corporaties can actually scale development capacity, construction sector constraints, and ensuring the freed resources reach housing rather than organizational overhead.
