# Regulatory Sandbox Orchestration

## Summary

Platforms that manage experimentation with new business models under relaxed rules.

## Description

Digital environments where innovators and regulators co-design and monitor pilots—such as fintech, health, or mobility sandboxes. They provide tooling for applying temporary waivers, tracking risks, and capturing evidence to inform permanent regulation.
