# Multi-Domain Battle Management Clouds

## Summary

Federated clouds fusing tracks across all warfighting domains.

## Description

Federated cloud architectures that ingest and fuse tracks from air, land, sea, space, and cyber domains into a single operational picture. Low-latency publish/subscribe mechanisms allow distributed units to collaborate on a shared truth while respecting classification and coalition boundaries.
