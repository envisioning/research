# Quantum Gravity Sensors

## Summary

Ultra-precise gravimeters for subsurface monitoring and early warning.

## Description

Atom-interferometry-based sensors detecting minute gravitational variations to map underground water, magma chambers, and tectonic stress in real time. Deployed in networks, they provide weeks of advance notice for volcanic eruptions, earthquakes, and infrastructure failures, reducing catastrophic casualties.
