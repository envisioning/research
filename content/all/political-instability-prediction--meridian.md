# Political Instability Prediction

## Summary

Early warning systems for regime fragility.

## Description

Machine learning platforms that integrate economic indicators, social media sentiment, elite network analysis, and historical patterns to forecast political instability, coups, and civil conflict with lead times sufficient for diplomatic or military preparation.
