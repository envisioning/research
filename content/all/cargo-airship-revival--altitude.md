# Modern Cargo Airships

## Summary

Hybrid airships for heavy-lift to remote sites without runways.

## Description

Airships blend aerostatic lift (helium) with dynamic lift to carry 20–200+ tons to locations with no infrastructure—mines, disaster zones, offshore platforms. They are slower but far cheaper per ton-km than helicopters. Current prototypes focus on hybrid designs with vectored thrust, but commercialization depends on proving safety, weather resilience, and operational economics.
