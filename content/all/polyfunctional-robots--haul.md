# Polyfunctional Robots

## Summary

Adaptive robots capable of performing multiple tasks and switching roles dynamically.

## Description

Unlike single-purpose robots, polyfunctional robots can be reprogrammed on-the-fly to handle sorting, picking, packing, palletizing, and inspection. They use modular end-effectors and AI-driven task planning to adapt to changing operational needs, reducing CapEx and increasing warehouse flexibility.
