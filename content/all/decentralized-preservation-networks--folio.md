# Decentralized Preservation Networks

## Summary

Distributed, censorship-resistant storage for cultural heritage.

## Description

Protocols like IPFS and Filecoin that split data into encrypted fragments and store them across thousands of independent nodes worldwide. This architecture ensures collections survive institutional collapse, natural disasters, and political censorship, with cryptographic verification of authenticity.
