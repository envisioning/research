# Free-Space Optical (Laser) Communications

## Summary

High-bandwidth, jam-resistant laser links for in-flight connectivity.

## Description

Laser comms offer data rates 10–100x higher than radio frequency (RF) links, enabling 'fiber-like' speeds for passengers and massive operational data transfer (e.g., real-time sensor streams). Beyond bandwidth, optical links are inherently harder to detect and jam (LPI/LPD), offering significant security advantages for future resilient aviation networks.
