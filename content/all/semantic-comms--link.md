# Semantic Communications

## Summary

Transmitting meaning and intent rather than just raw bits.

## Description

A paradigm shift where the system transmits the 'meaning' of information (filtered by AI) rather than the exact sequence of symbols. This drastically reduces bandwidth usage and improves reliability in noisy environments, moving from Shannon's bit-level theory to semantic-level transmission.
