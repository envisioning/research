# Circular Economy Management Platforms

## Summary

Software orchestrating reverse flows, refurbishment, and material recovery across product lifecycles.

## Description

These platforms manage take-back programs, track product passports with embedded circularity data, match returned materials with remanufacturing demand, and optimize reverse logistics networks. They enable transition from linear to circular supply chains, reducing waste and resource consumption.
