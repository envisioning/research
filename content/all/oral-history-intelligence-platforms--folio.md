# Oral History Intelligence Platforms

## Summary

Automated transcription, translation, and indexing of spoken archives.

## Description

Integrated systems for capturing, processing, and making searchable vast collections of interviews, testimonies, and recorded performances. They extract speaker identities, topics, emotions, and narrative structures—transforming audio into richly queryable knowledge resources while respecting consent and cultural context.
