# Affective Manipulation Safeguards

## Summary

Detection and prevention of emotional exploitation in interactive systems.

## Description

Regulatory frameworks and technical controls that identify and limit dark patterns, parasocial exploitation, addiction mechanics, and algorithmic emotional manipulation—ensuring affective computing serves human autonomy rather than engagement maximization.
