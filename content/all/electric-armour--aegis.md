# Electric Armour

## Summary

Active protection for ships and vehicles against shaped charges.

## Description

Electric armour uses high-voltage discharge to disrupt shaped-charge jets before they penetrate armour, improving protection for warships and armoured fighting vehicles. Prototypes exist; development continues. Applications include naval and ground vehicle protection against antitank weapons. Research continues into system integration, power requirements, and effectiveness.
