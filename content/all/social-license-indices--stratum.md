# Social License to Operate (SLO) Indices

## Summary

Quantified metrics measuring community sentiment and trust.

## Description

SLO Indices use surveys, social media sentiment analysis, and grievance data to create a quantifiable score for community acceptance. This moves 'Social License' from a vague concept to a tracked KPI, allowing companies to measure the impact of CSR initiatives and predict social risk before protests occur.
