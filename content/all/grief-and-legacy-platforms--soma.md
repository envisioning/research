# Grief and Digital Legacy Platforms

## Summary

Tools for bereavement support, memorialization, and posthumous presence.

## Description

Services that help individuals process loss through guided rituals, peer support, and continued bonds with deceased loved ones via digital memorials, conversational AI trained on their communication patterns, and legacy curation interfaces.
