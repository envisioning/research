# Research Copilot Systems

## Summary

LLM-powered assistants woven into scholarly workflows.

## Description

Domain-tuned language model assistants embedded in catalogs, discovery layers, and reference desks. They trace claims back to sources, suggest archival collections to consult next, and help synthesize findings while exposing their reasoning and citation trails.
