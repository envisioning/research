# Responsible Recommendation Systems

## Summary

Governed algorithms for fair and transparent discovery.

## Description

Frameworks and tooling to audit, explain, and tune recommendation algorithms so that content discovery avoids harmful bias, supports diversity of voices, and gives creators predictable rules of engagement.
