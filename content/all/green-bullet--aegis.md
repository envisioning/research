# Green Bullet

## Summary

Environment-friendly ammunition; reduced lead and toxins.

## Description

Green bullets use lead-free or reduced-toxin projectiles and primers. Development; applications include military and civilian shooting. Benefits: reduced environmental contamination at ranges; reduced occupational exposure.
