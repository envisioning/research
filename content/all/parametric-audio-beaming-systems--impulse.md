# Parametric Audio Beaming Systems

## Summary

Highly directional sound beams targeting individuals.

## Description

Ultrasonic speaker arrays that create narrow beams of audible sound, allowing messages or soundscapes to be delivered to specific individuals or micro-locations within a shared space. These systems can subtly direct attention, modulate arousal, or customize sonic environments without others being aware.
