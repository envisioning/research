# Mobility-as-a-Service Superapps

## Summary

Unified apps bundling journey planning, ticketing, visas, and payments.

## Description

Consumer-facing platforms that integrate route planning, real-time updates, multimodal ticketing, cross-border payments, and even visa or insurance workflows into a single interface, abstracting away the complexity of global mobility.
