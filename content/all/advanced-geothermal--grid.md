# Advanced Geothermal Systems

## Summary

Enhanced geothermal systems (EGS) and closed-loop technologies accessing deep heat.

## Description

Next-generation geothermal drilling and heat extraction methods unlock vast heat resources in non-volcanic regions, providing dispatchable, carbon-free power with minimal surface footprint.
