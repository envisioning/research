# Autonomous Inventory Scanning Drones

## Summary

Flying robots that autonomously scan barcodes and RFID tags in warehouses for cycle counting.

## Description

These drones navigate warehouse aisles during off-hours, using computer vision and RFID readers to perform inventory audits without human intervention. They detect misplaced items, verify stock levels, and update WMS systems in real-time, eliminating manual cycle counting labor.
