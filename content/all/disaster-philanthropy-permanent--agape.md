# Disaster Philanthropy Becoming Permanent

## Summary

Disaster philanthropy becoming permanent, not episodic, as crises become chronic rather than exceptional.

## Description

Traditional disaster philanthropy responded to specific events—hurricanes, earthquakes, conflicts—with time-limited responses. This signal captures how disasters are becoming chronic rather than exceptional, requiring ongoing rather than episodic responses. Climate events, pandemics, conflicts, and other crises are overlapping and persistent, forcing philanthropy to operate in permanent emergency mode rather than mobilizing for discrete events.
