# Intergenerational Wealth Transfer Reshaping Donor Intent

## Summary

Intergenerational wealth transfer reshaping donor intent, as massive wealth transfers change the philanthropic landscape.

## Description

The largest intergenerational wealth transfer in history is underway, with trillions of dollars passing from older to younger generations. This signal captures how new wealth holders are reshaping philanthropic priorities, approaches, and values—often with different ideas about legacy, impact, and the role of wealth in society. The transfer is creating both opportunities and tensions as new donors bring fresh perspectives while navigating family expectations and traditional frameworks.
