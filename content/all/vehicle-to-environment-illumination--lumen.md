# Vehicle-to-environment Illumination

## Summary

Headlights and external lighting that communicate intent between autonomous systems and humans.

## Description

Autonomous vehicles using high-resolution projection lighting to signal intentions to pedestrians and other drivers. Examples include projecting crosswalks onto the road or displaying status symbols, bridging the communication gap created by the absence of eye contact with a driver.
