# Biometric Data Rights

## Summary

Frameworks for protecting athlete physiological data ownership.

## Description

Technical and legal frameworks designed to secure sensitive biometric data collected by wearables. This includes encryption standards, consent management protocols, and ownership rights to ensure athletes retain control over their biological data and prevent unauthorized commercial exploitation.
