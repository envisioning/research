# Chroniton Detector

## Summary

Specialized sensor for detecting temporal particles and identifying time travel events.

## Description

Chroniton detectors are designed to sense the presence of chroniton particles, which are emitted by temporal phenomena and time travel events. These sensors can identify temporal anomalies, detect time travelers, and analyze the age of temporal distortions.

The technology is essential for temporal investigations and for detecting unauthorized time travel. Chroniton sensors can distinguish between naturally occurring temporal phenomena and artificial time manipulation. Federation Temporal Integrity Commission vessels are equipped with the most advanced chroniton detection arrays to monitor for temporal violations.
