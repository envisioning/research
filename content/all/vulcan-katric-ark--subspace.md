# Katric Arks (Katra Containers)

## Summary

Crystal or metallic vessels storing a person's katra (consciousness/soul).

## Description

Sacred Vulcan technology for preserving an individual's katra—their living consciousness, memories, and essence—when the body can no longer sustain it. The katra can be transferred to another living host or preserved until a suitable vessel is prepared.
