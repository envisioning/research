# Myco-Architectural Memorials

## Summary

Living, grown memorial structures made from fungal mycelium.

## Description

Bio-engineered mausoleums and memorial structures grown from mycelium composites. These living architectures are carbon-negative, self-repairing, and biodegradable, eventually returning to the earth alongside the remains they house, representing a seamless integration of built environment and biology.
