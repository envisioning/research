# Split Incentives & Retrofit Financing Stack

## Summary

Misaligned incentives between landlords and tenants (and between owners and the public) that block deep renovation without smart finance and regulation.

## Description

Deep renovation is often blocked by split incentives: landlords pay for upgrades while tenants receive energy savings; owner-occupiers may lack liquidity; and public benefits (emissions reduction, health outcomes) are not monetized in private decisions.

Benelux responses include subsidized loans, third-party energy service models, rent adjustment rules linked to efficiency gains, and targeted grants for vulnerable households.

The signal represents whether renovation policy can be made investable and fair rather than punitive and politically explosive. Key challenges include designing rent/benefit-sharing mechanisms, preventing green gentrification, and ensuring financing reaches the hardest-to-renovate buildings and households.
