# Volumetric Capture Arrays

## Summary

Multi-camera depth rigs for real-time capture of full-body motion and expression.

## Description

Dense arrays of RGB-D and depth sensors that reconstruct people and spaces as dynamic 3D meshes, enabling lifelike holographic telepresence, motion analysis, and cultural performance archival in immersive environments.
