# Visible Light Communication (VLC) / Li‑Fi Luminaires

## Summary

Lighting fixtures that provide both illumination and secure, high-density data links.

## Description

VLC and Li‑Fi modulate emitted light at high frequency to transmit data without impacting perceived illumination. Embedded in luminaires, this supports high-capacity indoor networking, low-interference operation in sensitive environments, and precise indoor localization—while the physical confinement of light can reduce eavesdropping risk compared to RF.
