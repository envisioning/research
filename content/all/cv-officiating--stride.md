# Computer Vision Officiating

## Summary

Software-based electronic line-calling and rule enforcement.

## Description

High-precision optical tracking systems that utilize standard 4K camera feeds and computer vision algorithms to automate officiating. This technology provides electronic line-calling and foul detection with millimeter accuracy, reducing human error and infrastructure costs compared to traditional hardware-heavy solutions.
