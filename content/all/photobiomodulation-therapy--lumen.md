# Photobiomodulation Therapy (PBMT)

## Summary

Therapeutic use of red and near‑infrared light for healing, pain reduction, and recovery.

## Description

PBMT applies red/NIR wavelengths to stimulate cellular pathways (often via mitochondrial chromophores), supporting wound healing, inflammation reduction, and tissue recovery. Clinical translation hinges on well-controlled dose, wavelength, and treatment protocols—driving demand for medical-grade light sources and monitoring.
