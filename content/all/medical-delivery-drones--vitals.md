# Autonomous Medical Delivery Drones

## Summary

UAVs transporting blood, vaccines, medications, and diagnostics to remote or congested areas.

## Description

Medical drones bypass road infrastructure to deliver time-sensitive supplies—blood products to trauma sites, vaccines to rural clinics, medications to island communities, and diagnostic samples from hard-to-reach areas. Deployed successfully in Rwanda, Ghana, and parts of the U.S., they reduce delivery times from hours to minutes, enabling equitable access to life-saving therapies regardless of geography or infrastructure quality.
