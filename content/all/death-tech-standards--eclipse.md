# Death Tech Standards & Certification

## Summary

Certification schemes for transparency, ecology, and fairness in death tech.

## Description

Standardization initiatives and assessment tools that certify hardware, software, and services in the death-care ecosystem against criteria such as carbon footprint, data governance, explainability, and cultural inclusion.
