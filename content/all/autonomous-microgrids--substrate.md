# Autonomous Microgrids

## Summary

Self-managing local grids capable of islanding from the main network.

## Description

Advanced microgrids use AI to balance local generation and consumption in real-time. Crucially, they can 'island' themselves during main grid failures, ensuring resilience for critical infrastructure like hospitals or data centers. New standards allow these grids to seamlessly reconnect and trade energy with neighbors.
