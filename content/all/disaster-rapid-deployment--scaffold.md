# Disaster-Resilient Rapid Deployment Structures

## Summary

Prefabricated shelters and infrastructure for post-disaster and refugee response.

## Description

Rapidly deployable housing, medical facilities, and utilities (IKEA Better Shelter, ShelterBox, military field systems) combine lightweight materials, modular design, and local assembly. These systems address acute humanitarian need and increasingly inform permanent affordable housing strategies in vulnerable regions.
