# Labor Justice Monitoring

## Summary

Oversight of ethical labor practices in content moderation.

## Description

Transparency and accountability mechanisms for the often-invisible labor that powers digital libraries—content moderators, annotators, and data workers, many in the Global South. These systems audit working conditions, fair pay, psychological support, and ensure that knowledge work respects human dignity.
