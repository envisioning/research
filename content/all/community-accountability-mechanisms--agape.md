# Community-Driven Accountability Mechanisms

## Summary

New accountability mechanisms driven by affected communities, reshaping how philanthropy is held responsible for its impacts.

## Description

Traditional accountability in philanthropy flows upward to boards, donors, or regulators. This signal captures emerging mechanisms where accountability flows from affected communities—through public reporting requirements, community review processes, participatory evaluation, and direct feedback systems. These mechanisms challenge philanthropy to be responsive not to institutional stakeholders but to those experiencing its impacts, whether positive or negative.
