# Battery-Electric Mobile Mining Equipment

## Summary

Deploying battery-electric vehicles for underground and open-pit operations.

## Description

Battery-electric load–haul–dump units, trucks, and auxiliary vehicles reduce diesel emissions, heat, and noise in mines. Their deployment can shrink ventilation requirements underground, improve worker health, and serve as a stepping stone toward fully autonomous, low-carbon fleets.
