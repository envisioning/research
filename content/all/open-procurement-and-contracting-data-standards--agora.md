# Open Procurement & Contracting Data Standards

## Summary

Machine-readable transparency for contracts, vendors, and spend.

## Description

Data standards and validation tooling (with APIs, schemas, and audit trails) that make procurement processes searchable and analyzable. Enables civil society, journalists, and auditors to detect fraud patterns, collusion risks, and delivery failures.
