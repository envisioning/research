# Self-Sovereign Identity Wallets

## Summary

Applications enabling user control over digital identity and credentials.

## Description

Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) Wallets are digital applications that allow individuals to store, manage, and present their verifiable credentials (like digital driver's licenses or diplomas). These wallets give users full control over their identity data, moving away from centralized identity providers to a user-centric model where the user serves as the integration point for their own data.
