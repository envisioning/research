# Electric Maritime Ferries

## Summary

Battery-powered ferries for island and coastal mobility.

## Description

Zero-emission electric vessels with fast-charging infrastructure serving island nations, coastal cities, and archipelago tourism routes, offering quiet, sustainable alternatives to diesel ferries and connecting remote communities to global mobility networks.
