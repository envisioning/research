# Decentralized Streaming Protocols

## Summary

Peer-to-peer content delivery networks.

## Description

Blockchain-based or mesh networking protocols that distribute video streaming across user nodes, reducing infrastructure costs, increasing resilience to censorship, and enabling creators to reach audiences without centralized gatekeepers.
