# Environmental Impact Accounting

## Summary

Lifecycle assessment of spatial computing infrastructure.

## Description

Standards and tools measuring the carbon footprint, rare earth consumption, and e-waste generation of spatial devices and cloud infrastructure. Drives transparency around the environmental cost of immersive technologies and informs sustainable design, recycling programs, and energy-efficient rendering techniques.
