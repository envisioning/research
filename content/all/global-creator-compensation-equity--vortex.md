# Global Creator Compensation Equity

## Summary

Fair payment systems for creators worldwide.

## Description

Payment infrastructure, currency conversion tools, and transparent revenue models that ensure creators in low-income regions and underrepresented communities receive equitable compensation, reducing geographic and economic disparities in the creator economy.
