# Memorial Safety & Moderation Systems

## Summary

Content and behavior moderation for digital memorial spaces.

## Description

AI-augmented moderation stacks that detect harassment, deepfakes, misinformation, or commercialization in online memorials and grief communities, combining automated detection with human review to preserve psychological safety and dignity.
