# UAE National Space Fund & Industry

## Summary

The UAE Space Agency has launched a national space fund to grow domestic space industry, with plans for asteroid belt exploration and expanded satellite manufacturing.

## Description

Building on the Hope Probe's success, the UAE Space Agency is expanding its ambitions with plans for an asteroid belt exploration mission and development of domestic satellite manufacturing capabilities. The agency has established a national space fund to support commercial space startups and is working to create a regulatory framework for space activities including satellite operations, space tourism, and launch services.

The UAE's space strategy integrates multiple national objectives: STEM education (the Mars mission inspired thousands of Emirati students to pursue science degrees), national prestige, and practical applications in earth observation, communications, and navigation. The extension of the Hope Probe mission to 2028 ensures continued scientific output while follow-on missions are developed.

The Gulf's space ambitions, with Saudi Arabia also establishing its own space commission, are creating a regional space ecosystem that attracts international aerospace companies and research partnerships. This ecosystem contributes to the broader goal of building a high-tech workforce capable of driving post-oil economic growth.
