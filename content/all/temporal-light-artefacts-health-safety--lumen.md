# Flicker & Temporal Light Artefacts: Health and Safety

## Summary

Managing flicker-related risks including headaches, seizures, and safety-critical misperception.

## Description

Even when imperceptible, temporal modulation can affect comfort and performance, and may trigger migraines or photosensitive epilepsy in vulnerable groups. In industrial settings it can create stroboscopic hazards around rotating machinery. Governance includes procurement specs, measurement standards, and duty-of-care for sensitive populations.
