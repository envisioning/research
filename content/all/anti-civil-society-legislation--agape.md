# Anti-Civil Society Legislation & NGO Restrictions

## Summary

Proliferation of laws restricting NGO operations, foreign funding, and civil society space across the globe.

## Description

Governments worldwide are enacting legislation that restricts the operations of NGOs and civil society organizations—from foreign agent registration requirements to limits on foreign funding to outright bans on certain activities. This signal captures the global closing of civic space and its implications for philanthropy. Foundations must navigate an increasingly hostile legal environment in many countries, raising questions about how to support civil society when the act of receiving foreign funding becomes criminalized or stigmatized.
