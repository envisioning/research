# Digital Repatriation Frameworks

## Summary

Technologies for returning cultural materials to origin communities.

## Description

End-to-end platforms that facilitate the digital return of artifacts, documents, and recordings held by institutions to their source communities—particularly in the Global South. These systems manage rights negotiation, high-fidelity digitization, secure transfer, and ongoing stewardship agreements.
