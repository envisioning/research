# Fractional Investment Platforms

## Summary

Tokenized real estate assets allowing democratized access to property ownership.

## Description

Digital platforms that divide property ownership into tradable tokens, lowering barriers to entry for retail investors. This technology provides liquidity to an inherently illiquid asset class, allowing individuals to diversify portfolios across multiple properties and geographies with minimal capital requirements.
