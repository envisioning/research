# Immersive Therapy Environments

## Summary

XR modalities for exposure therapy, rehabilitation, and wellbeing.

## Description

Clinical and wellness-focused experiences using controlled virtual and augmented environments to treat phobias, manage pain, or support physical rehab. Therapists can tune intensity and context in real time, while biosignals and behavior are monitored for outcomes.
