# Aviation Workforce & Automation Transition Impacts

## Summary

Managing employment, retraining, and equity as automation scales.

## Description

Autonomy and automation will displace some aviation jobs (pilots, controllers, ground crew) while creating new roles (remote operators, data analysts, cybersecurity specialists). Proactive workforce planning, retraining programs, and social safety nets are essential to manage this transition equitably, especially in regions heavily dependent on aviation employment.
