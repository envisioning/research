# Mesh Democracy Nodes

## Summary

Decentralized networking for off-grid civic coordination.

## Description

Low-power, ruggedized networking devices that form peer-to-peer mesh networks independent of centralized ISPs. They enable communities to organize, vote, and communicate securely during internet blackouts or in regions with censored infrastructure.
