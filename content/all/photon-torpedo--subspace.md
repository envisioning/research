# Photon Torpedo

## Summary

Self-propelled projectile weapon utilizing matter-antimatter warheads for devastating explosive yield.

## Description

Photon torpedoes are guided munitions containing matter-antimatter warheads. Upon detonation, the mutual annihilation of matter and antimatter releases tremendous energy, making these weapons highly effective against hardened targets.Advanced guidance systems allow torpedoes to track targets, adjust trajectory mid-flight, and even maintain warp speed for extended periods. Yield can be adjusted based on tactical requirements.
