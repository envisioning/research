# Privacy-Preserving People Analytics

## Summary

Analyzing workforce trends without exposing individual identities.

## Description

Analytics platforms utilizing differential privacy and homomorphic encryption to generate insights on employee engagement, retention, and performance. This ensures organizations can make data-driven decisions while mathematically guaranteeing that no individual's data can be reverse-engineered.
