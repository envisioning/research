# Prefabricated MEP Systems

## Summary

Factory-assembled mechanical, electrical, and plumbing modules reducing on-site installation time and improving quality control.

## Description

Prefabricated MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems involve manufacturing bathroom pods, riser modules, plant rooms, and utility corridors in controlled factory environments, then installing complete assemblies on-site. This approach dramatically reduces on-site labor and installation time while improving quality consistency.

In GCC mega-projects with thousands of similar units (hotels, residential towers, worker housing), prefabricated MEP accelerates delivery timelines by 30-40%. The approach enables parallel work streams where MEP modules are manufactured while structure rises, then installed floor-by-floor. Major contractors increasingly adopt prefab MEP for hospitality and residential projects.

The signal represents the industrialization of traditionally craft-based building systems, requiring new design approaches, supply chain integration, and logistics planning. Key benefits include reduced site congestion, improved quality, and faster occupancy. Challenges include design standardization, transport logistics, and coordination between module manufacturers and main contractors.
