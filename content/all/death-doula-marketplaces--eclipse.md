# Death Doula Marketplaces

## Summary

Platforms connecting families with end-of-life doulas and guides.

## Description

Service platforms that match individuals and families with trained death doulas, ritual facilitators, and peer supporters, providing scheduling, payment, and collaborative planning tools for emotional, spiritual, and logistical support.
