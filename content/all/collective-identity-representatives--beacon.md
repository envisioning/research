# Collective Identity Representation Systems

## Summary

AI agents representing communities, not just individuals.

## Description

Frameworks for creating and governing synthetic agents that represent collective entities—such as communities, movements, or organizations—with distributed consent mechanisms, shared narrative control, and accountability structures that prevent single-point capture.
