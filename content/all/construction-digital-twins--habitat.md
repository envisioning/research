# Construction Digital Twins

## Summary

Live, simulation-ready replicas of construction projects during execution.

## Description

Digital twins that synchronize design, schedule, procurement, and on-site telemetry into a single operational model. Project teams use them to test sequencing options, optimize crane locations, anticipate safety hotspots, and coordinate subcontractors before changes hit the field, reducing rework and downtime.
