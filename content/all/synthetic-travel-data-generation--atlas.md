# Synthetic Travel Data Generation

## Summary

AI-generated datasets preserving statistical properties while protecting privacy.

## Description

Generative models (GANs, diffusion models) creating realistic synthetic booking, mobility, and demographic datasets that enable ML development, demand forecasting, and research without exposing real traveler data to third parties or breaches.
