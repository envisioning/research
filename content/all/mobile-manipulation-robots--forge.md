# Mobile Manipulation Robots

## Summary

Robotic arms mounted on autonomous mobile bases for flexible material handling and assembly.

## Description

Mobile manipulation robots combine AMRs with collaborative arms, enabling the same platform to transport, pick, and assemble parts at different stations. With SLAM navigation, advanced perception, and safe human-robot collaboration, they support reconfigurable factories and on-demand workcell creation without fixed conveyors.
