# Real-Time Ore Tracking and Grade Control

## Summary

Continuous monitoring of ore quality from extraction through processing.

## Description

Real-time ore tracking systems use RFID, GPS, on-belt analyzers, and IoT sensors to trace individual ore parcels from blast to mill. Integrated with geometallurgical models, they enable dynamic blending, quality control, and rapid response to grade variability. This maximizes recovery, minimizes metallurgical losses, and ensures product specifications are met consistently.
