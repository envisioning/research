# Social Service Robots

## Summary

Humanoid assistants for guidance, language aid, and engagement.

## Description

Friendly, autonomous robots that navigate library floors to help patrons find books, answer questions in multiple languages, and provide social engagement for children and the elderly. They augment human staff by handling routine queries and offering 24/7 accessibility support.
