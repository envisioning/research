# SVK / Sociaal Verhuurkantoor (Social Rental Agencies)

## Summary

Belgian agencies that lease private rental properties and sublet them at social rates, expanding affordable supply without new construction.

## Description

Sociaal Verhuurkantoren (Social Rental Agencies) in Belgium lease private rental properties from owners—often offering guaranteed rent and maintenance—then sublet them to low-income tenants at regulated social rates. The SVK absorbs vacancy risk and tenant management, making private landlords willing to accept below-market returns.

This model expands affordable housing supply using existing stock, avoiding construction delays and opposition. It's particularly effective in Brussels and Flanders where private rental stock is underutilized.

The signal represents a mechanism that converts private assets to social purposes through intermediation rather than regulation. Key challenges include recruiting sufficient private landlords, maintaining property quality, and ensuring the subsidy gap between market and social rents is sustainable.
