# Holographic Telepresence

## Summary

Real-time, volumetric 3D communication.

## Description

The transmission of high-fidelity 3D holograms of people and objects in real-time. Requiring the massive bandwidth and low latency of 6G, it aims to replace 2D video calls with fully immersive, 'Star Wars-style' remote interaction.
