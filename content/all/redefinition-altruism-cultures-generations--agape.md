# Redefinition of Altruism Across Cultures & Generations

## Summary

Redefinition of altruism across cultures and generations, as diverse understandings of giving reshape philanthropy.

## Description

Traditional Western models of altruism and giving are being challenged by diverse cultural and generational perspectives that understand generosity, obligation, and social responsibility differently. This signal captures how younger generations, non-Western cultures, and marginalized communities are redefining what it means to give, who has the right to give, and what forms giving should take. These redefinitions challenge universalist assumptions about philanthropy.
