# Hypersonic & Space Plane Vehicles

## Summary

Mach 5+ aircraft and reusable suborbital systems for intercontinental rapid transit.

## Description

Hypersonic airbreathing propulsion (scramjets) and rocket-based combined-cycle systems aim to enable 2-hour global travel or direct space access. Technical barriers include thermal protection, propulsion integration, sonic boom mitigation, and extremely high development/certification costs. Both defense and commercial players (China, U.S., startups) are investing.
