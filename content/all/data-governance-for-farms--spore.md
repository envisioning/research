# Data Governance for Farms

## Summary

Ownership of soil microbiome data, genomic data, and farm sensor networks.

## Description

Data governance frameworks protect farmer autonomy in an era of algorithmic agriculture. This includes clarifying ownership rights over soil microbiome data, crop genomics, and sensor networks to prevent exploitation and ensure fair value distribution.
