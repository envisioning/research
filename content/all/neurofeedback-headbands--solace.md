# Closed-Loop Neurofeedback Headbands

## Summary

Wearable EEG headbands that provide real-time feedback for self-regulation and focus training.

## Description

Lightweight EEG-based headbands that capture basic brainwave patterns and translate them into simple feedback cues (sound, color, haptics). Users learn to self-regulate focus, relaxation, and stress responses through guided training protocols deployed in educational, workplace, and clinical contexts.
