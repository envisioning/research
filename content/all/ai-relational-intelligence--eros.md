# AI Relational Intelligence Systems

## Summary

Comprehensive AI for coaching, mediation, and conflict transformation.

## Description

Integrated AI platforms that combine real-time communication monitoring, attachment modeling, coaching, and conflict intervention. They offer tailored exercises, detect escalation patterns, suggest de-escalation strategies, and provide therapeutic frameworks—acting as coach, mediator, and guide for individuals, couples, and groups.
