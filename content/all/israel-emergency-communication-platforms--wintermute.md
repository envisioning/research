# AI-Powered Emergency Communication and Dispatch Platforms

## Summary

Carbyne's $226M-funded cloud-based emergency platform processes 911/112 calls using AI for call triage, location detection, and video streaming in 100+ jurisdictions.

## Description

Carbyne has developed a cloud-native emergency communication platform that modernizes 911/112 call handling using AI-based call triage, automatic location detection (including indoor positioning), live video streaming from callers' phones, and multi-language translation. The platform processes emergency calls in over 100 jurisdictions worldwide, providing dispatchers with richer situational awareness than traditional voice-only systems. The company has raised over $226 million.

The technology addresses a critical gap in emergency services: legacy 911 infrastructure was designed for landline voice calls and cannot handle the data-rich communications modern smartphones enable. Carbyne's platform allows callers to share live video of emergencies, transmit precise locations (even inside buildings), and communicate through AI translation — capabilities that directly save lives by reducing response times and improving situational understanding.

Strategically, Carbyne demonstrates how Israeli technology designed for security applications (multi-sensor data fusion, real-time communication under stress) translates to civilian emergency services. The company's growth across U.S., European, and Latin American markets reflects the universal need to modernize emergency communication infrastructure.
