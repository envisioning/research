# Formalized Government-Philanthropy Partnerships

## Summary

New formal structures for government-philanthropy collaboration beyond traditional grants and contracts.

## Description

Governments and philanthropy are developing more formalized partnership structures—joint governance bodies, blended funding mechanisms, shared accountability frameworks—that go beyond traditional relationships where philanthropy simply funds what government cannot. This signal captures innovation in how public and private resources combine for social impact, while raising questions about accountability, democratic legitimacy, and whether such partnerships strengthen or undermine public capacity.
