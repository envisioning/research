# Digital Twin Hospitals

## Summary

Virtual replicas of hospital operations used to simulate workflows and optimize facility design.

## Description

Digital twins create a dynamic virtual model of a physical hospital, including its layout, assets, staff, and patient flows. Administrators can run simulations to test the impact of changes—such as new staffing schedules, modified ER layouts, or pandemic surge protocols—in a risk-free environment before implementing them in the real world.
