# Universal Vaccine Platforms

## Summary

Rapid-response immunization against emerging pathogens.

## Description

mRNA, self-amplifying RNA, and viral vector technologies enabling vaccine development and manufacturing within weeks of pathogen identification. These platforms drastically reduce the window between outbreak and immunization, preventing pandemics from reaching global scale and protecting civilization against natural and engineered biological threats.
