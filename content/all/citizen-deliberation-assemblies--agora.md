# Citizen Deliberation Assemblies

## Summary

Scalable online spaces for structured public debate.

## Description

Virtual environments designed to host large-scale deliberative polls and citizens' assemblies. They use AI moderation to ensure civility, summarize arguments, and help diverse groups reach rough consensus on complex policy issues.
