# National Vision Programs

## Summary

Comprehensive national transformation strategies (Vision 2030, UAE Centennial 2071) reshaping urban development priorities.

## Description

National Vision programs like Saudi Vision 2030, UAE Centennial 2071, and Qatar National Vision 2030 establish strategic priorities that cascade into urban development. These frameworks drive diversification away from hydrocarbons, prioritize tourism and entertainment, and set sustainability targets.

Vision programs allocate capital, set regulatory priorities, and create demand for specific technologies. Developers and technology providers align offerings to Vision objectives to access projects and incentives. The programs create predictability while also concentrating decision-making.

The signal reflects the top-down planning model that characterizes GCC development, enabling rapid transformation but also creating risks if priorities shift or economic conditions change.
