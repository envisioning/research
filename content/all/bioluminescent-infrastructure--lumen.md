# Bioluminescent Infrastructure

## Summary

Synthetic biology applications creating self-sustaining, zero-electricity ambient lighting.

## Description

Engineered plants, bacteria, or fungi with enhanced bioluminescence (e.g., fungal genes in tobacco plants) offer a vision for 'living lights'. While currently low-intensity, future iterations aim to replace passive street and park lighting, reducing energy dependence and light pollution through soft, organic illumination.
