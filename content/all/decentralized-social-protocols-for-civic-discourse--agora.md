# Decentralized Social Protocols for Civic Discourse

## Summary

ActivityPub, ATProtocol, and federated town squares.

## Description

Open social networking protocols that prevent platform capture of civic discourse. Allows communities to self-host or federate while maintaining interoperability, reducing censorship risk, and preserving public sphere norms.
