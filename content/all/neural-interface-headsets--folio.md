# Neural Interface Headsets

## Summary

Direct brain-computer communication for rapid knowledge access.

## Description

Non-invasive wearable devices that interpret neural signals to navigate digital archives and retrieve information at the speed of thought. These interfaces dissolve the barrier between the human mind and external knowledge repositories.
