# Spatial Mnemonics

## Summary

AR workspaces mapping information to physical locations for recall.

## Description

Productivity tools that leverage human spatial memory (the 'Memory Palace' technique). Users can place documents, data visualizations, or tasks in specific physical locations in their office or home, retrieving them simply by looking at that spot, enhancing cognitive load management.
