# Deployable Ablative Armor Generator

## Summary

Projected hull plating that materializes on demand during combat.

## Description

Future-derived emitters 'print' ablative layers over the spaceframe, drastically reducing incoming energy transfer. Temporary armor evaporates after engagement to restore normal mass and aerodynamics.
