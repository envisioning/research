# Longevity & Mortality Risk Platforms

## Summary

Actuarial tools for aging populations.

## Description

Advanced modeling platforms combining genomics, wearables, and population health data to price life insurance, annuities, pension liabilities, and longevity bonds in an era of increasing life expectancy and demographic shifts.
