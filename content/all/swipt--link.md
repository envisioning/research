# Simultaneous Wireless Information and Power Transfer (SWIPT)

## Summary

Harvesting energy from the same radio signal used for communication.

## Description

Techniques that allow receivers to split incoming RF signals into two streams: one for decoding information and one for rectifying energy to charge a battery or supercapacitor. SWIPT enables truly autonomous, battery-less IoT networks that can operate indefinitely in hard-to-reach locations.
