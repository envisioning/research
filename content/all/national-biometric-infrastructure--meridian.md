# National Biometric Infrastructure

## Summary

Identity systems for borders and security.

## Description

Large-scale biometric databases and sensor networks (facial recognition, iris, gait, DNA) integrated into border control, law enforcement, and financial systems to manage migration, counter terrorism, and verify identity at national scale.
