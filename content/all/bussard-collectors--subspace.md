# Bussard Ramscoop Collectors

## Summary

Forward-mounted collectors gathering interstellar hydrogen as fuel during warp flight.

## Description

Bussard collectors are forward-facing ramscoop assemblies that gather interstellar hydrogen atoms while a vessel travels at warp speed. The collected hydrogen is processed and used to replenish deuterium fuel reserves, extending operational range without requiring frequent refueling stops.

The distinctive glowing nacelle caps visible on most Starfleet vessels house these collectors. The technology is most effective at higher warp speeds when relative particle density increases. Modern implementations include electromagnetic field generators that create a wide collection zone ahead of the vessel, funneling hydrogen into processing systems.
