# Mobile Body-Scanning SaaS

## Summary

Smartphone-based scanning for instant fit recommendations and virtual try-on.

## Description

Mobile body-scanning SaaS platforms use computer vision and machine learning algorithms to extract body measurements and create 3D avatars from photos or video captured with standard smartphone cameras. These cloud-based services process the images to generate accurate measurements and body models that can be used for size recommendations, virtual try-on, and custom garment production without requiring specialized hardware or in-person visits.

This innovation addresses the fundamental challenge of online apparel shopping: determining correct size and visualizing fit without trying garments on physically. By making body scanning accessible through devices consumers already own, these platforms remove barriers to accurate sizing and virtual try-on experiences. Companies like 3DLOOK, Fit3D, and various startups provide these services, with major retailers integrating them into their e-commerce platforms.

The technology is becoming essential infrastructure for online fashion retail as consumers increasingly shop digitally and expect personalized experiences. As the accuracy of mobile scanning improves and privacy concerns are addressed, these platforms could become standard tools for online shopping, potentially reducing return rates by 30-50% while improving customer satisfaction. The technology also enables new business models around made-to-measure and personalized fit that were previously impractical at scale.
