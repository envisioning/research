# Workplace Digital Twins for Roles

## Summary

Digital twins of roles mapping tasks, skills, and performance.

## Description

Digital twins of jobs and roles that map typical tasks, required competencies, tools, and performance indicators. These models are used to auto-generate upskilling plans, simulate career moves, and align training content with the actual workflows and constraints of specific roles.
