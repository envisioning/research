# Wellness Real Estate

## Summary

Residential developments designed around health and wellbeing principles, integrating air quality, biophilic design, fitness, and mental health considerations.

## Description

Wellness real estate encompasses residential developments designed with health and wellbeing as primary design drivers. Features include advanced air filtration (HEPA, UV sterilization), circadian lighting, biophilic design (nature integration), fitness facilities, spa amenities, and mental health-supportive spaces. Certifications like WELL Building Standard validate wellness features.

In GCC, wellness real estate has grown significantly post-pandemic, with developments marketing air quality, outdoor spaces, and health amenities as premium features. Dubai's luxury segment increasingly incorporates wellness, while Saudi Arabia's lifestyle projects (Qiddiya, Red Sea) integrate wellness hospitality concepts into residential components.

The signal represents the convergence of real estate and healthcare, where residential properties become platforms for health optimization. Key drivers include post-pandemic health awareness, aging population health focus, and premium buyer expectations. The segment commands 10-25% price premiums and attracts health-conscious buyers and investors.
