# Autonomous Weapons Control Treaties

## Summary

International bans on lethal AI systems without human oversight.

## Description

Diplomatic frameworks prohibiting fully autonomous weapons and establishing verification protocols for military AI. Building on nuclear non-proliferation precedents, these treaties aim to prevent arms races in lethal autonomous systems that could trigger accidental wars or be used for mass atrocities without accountability.
