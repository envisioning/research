# Volumetric Capture Rigs

## Summary

Multi-sensor arrays capturing people and places as dynamic 3D assets.

## Description

Studios and portable rigs combining RGB cameras, depth sensors, and lidar to scan humans and environments as time-varying volumetric datasets. These assets enable realistic telepresence, performance capture, and replayable spatial scenes for training, storytelling, and simulation.
