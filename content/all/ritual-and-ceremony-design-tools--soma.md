# Ritual and Ceremony Design Tools

## Summary

Frameworks for creating meaningful shared experiences in digital spaces.

## Description

Participatory design systems that scaffold the creation of rituals, rites of passage, seasonal celebrations, and collective mourning practices in immersive environments—drawing on anthropological and theatrical principles to foster communitas.
