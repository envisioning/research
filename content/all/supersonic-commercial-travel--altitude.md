# Supersonic Commercial Travel

## Summary

Next-generation quiet supersonic flight for rapid global connectivity.

## Description

A resurgence of supersonic travel aims to cut flight times in half using new aerodynamic designs that minimize the sonic boom. 'Quiet' supersonic technology seeks to enable overland supersonic flight, unlocking new viable routes for rapid commercial transport.
