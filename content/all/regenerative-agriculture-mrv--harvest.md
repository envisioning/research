# Regenerative Agriculture MRV

## Summary

Monitoring, reporting, and verification for soil and biodiversity.

## Description

Hybrid satellite, in-field sensing, and modeling systems quantify soil carbon, biodiversity, and water outcomes to underwrite incentives and supply agreements for regenerative practices.
