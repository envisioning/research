# Ambient Clinical Intelligence

## Summary

AI systems that automate clinical documentation by listening to doctor-patient interactions.

## Description

Ambient Clinical Intelligence (ACI) utilizes advanced natural language processing (NLP) and generative AI to listen to patient consultations in the background. It automatically extracts relevant medical information and drafts structured clinical notes, orders, and referrals for the Electronic Health Record (EHR). This technology significantly reduces physician burnout caused by administrative tasks and allows doctors to focus entirely on the patient.
