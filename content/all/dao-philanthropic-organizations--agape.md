# DAO-Based Philanthropic Organizations

## Summary

Decentralized autonomous organizations enabling collective giving and governance through blockchain-based coordination mechanisms.

## Description

DAO-based philanthropic organizations use blockchain technology to enable collective giving and governance without traditional hierarchical structures. These organizations allow members to propose, vote on, and execute funding decisions through smart contracts and token-based governance. However, they also raise questions about participation barriers, how to ensure diverse voices in governance, whether decentralized structures enable or hinder effective action, and how DAOs handle accountability and legal compliance. The organizations represent a shift toward decentralized, community-controlled philanthropy while highlighting tensions between decentralization and effectiveness.
