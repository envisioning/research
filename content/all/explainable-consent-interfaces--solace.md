# Explainable Consent Interfaces

## Summary

Interface patterns that make data practices and AI behavior truly understandable to non-experts.

## Description

Human-centered consent and transparency interfaces that replace dense legal text with narratives, visuals, and simulations showing how data and models behave over time. These interfaces help people anticipate downstream risks to their emotional wellbeing, identity, and autonomy, and enable granular, reversible consent choices.
