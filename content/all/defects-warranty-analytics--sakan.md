# Defects, Warranty & Claims Analytics (Digital QA/QC)

## Summary

Digital snagging, QA/QC workflows, and warranty analytics that reduce rework, predict defect hotspots, and improve handover quality at scale.

## Description

Digital QA/QC platforms standardize site inspections, snagging, testing records, and handover documentation, creating a data trail from construction to defects liability and warranty periods. Analytics applied to this data can identify recurring failure modes (leaks, MEP faults, facade issues), predict where defects will cluster, and optimize commissioning and subcontractor performance.

For GCC mega-developments and high-volume housing programs, defect management is a major cost driver—rework delays handover and creates ongoing service-charge pressure. Institutional landlords and Build-to-Rent operators also care because warranty claims affect early-life asset performance and resident experience.

The signal represents a shift from paper-based handover to data-driven quality and accountability across the supply chain. Key challenges include subcontractor adoption, evidence quality (photos, test certificates), and aligning incentives so issues are fixed upstream rather than pushed into FM and warranty periods.
