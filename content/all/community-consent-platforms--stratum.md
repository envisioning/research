# Community Consent and Grievance Platforms

## Summary

Digital platforms documenting consent, benefits, and grievances across projects.

## Description

Community consent platforms combine mobile apps, secure data stores, and analytics dashboards to record engagement processes, benefit-sharing agreements, and grievances. They increase transparency between operators, local communities, and governments, and provide auditable records of social performance over the project life cycle.
