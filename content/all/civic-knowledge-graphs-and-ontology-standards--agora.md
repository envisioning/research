# Civic Knowledge Graphs & Policy Ontologies

## Summary

Structured representations of institutions, programs, and outcomes.

## Description

Knowledge graphs that link laws, agencies, budgets, programs, and measured outcomes, enabling traceability from a policy decision to implementation artifacts and effects. Provides a substrate for transparency portals, legislative analysis, and public accountability tooling.
