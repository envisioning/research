# Outcome-Based & Regenerative Finance

## Summary

Experimentation with outcome-based or regenerative finance, exploring new economic models for social impact.

## Description

Philanthropy is experimenting with outcome-based financing, social impact bonds, and regenerative finance models that align returns with positive social or environmental outcomes. This signal captures innovative approaches to structuring capital flows that reward impact, create feedback loops between funding and outcomes, and explore alternatives to extractive economic models. However, these approaches also face challenges around measurement, attribution, and whether market mechanisms can effectively address systemic problems.
