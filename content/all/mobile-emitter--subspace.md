# Mobile Holographic Emitter

## Summary

29th century technology enabling holographic entities to exist and operate outside designated holo-environments.

## Description

The mobile emitter is an advanced device from the 29th century that projects a stable holographic matrix without requiring fixed emitters or environmental controls. This breakthrough allows holographic beings to move freely through any environment, including areas without holographic systems.

The technology is far beyond current Federation capabilities, incorporating miniaturized power generation, quantum field manipulation, and autonomous computing in a small portable unit. When provided to holographic AI like the EMH, it effectively grants them physical autonomy and the ability to serve in any capacity, not just within holodecks or specifically equipped rooms.
