# LLZO-Based Solid-State Electrolyte Membranes

## Summary

Multilayer solid-state electrolyte membranes with superior fire safety and high energy density.

## Description

LLZO-based solid-state electrolyte membranes use lithium lanthanum zirconium oxide (LLZO), a ceramic material that conducts lithium ions while providing exceptional thermal and chemical stability. These multilayer membranes replace flammable liquid electrolytes in batteries, eliminating fire risks and enabling safer operation. The ceramic structure provides mechanical strength while allowing efficient ion transport, enabling high energy density battery designs.

The LLZO material is particularly valued for its high ionic conductivity, wide electrochemical stability window, and compatibility with lithium metal anodes. Multilayer designs optimize ion transport paths and mechanical properties. These membranes enable solid-state batteries with superior safety characteristics, higher energy density potential, and improved performance at extreme temperatures. The technology is advancing toward commercial deployment in electric vehicles and high-performance consumer electronics where safety and energy density are paramount.
