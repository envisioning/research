# Deflector Shields

## Summary

Energy barriers that protect spacecraft from weapons fire, radiation, and spatial hazards.

## Description

Deflector shields generate powerful force fields around a vessel, providing protection against energy weapons, projectiles, and environmental hazards. The shield system can be modulated to different frequencies to counter various threats.Shield strength is measured in terms of capacity and regeneration rate. Multiple emitters provide redundancy, and power can be rerouted to strengthen specific shield facings during combat situations.
