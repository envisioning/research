# Cryptocurrency & Blockchain in Philanthropy

## Summary

Emergence of crypto donations, smart contracts for giving, and blockchain-based transparency in philanthropic flows.

## Description

Cryptocurrency and blockchain technologies are creating new mechanisms for philanthropic giving—from direct crypto donations to smart contracts that automate conditional giving to blockchain-based systems for tracking fund flows. This signal captures both the promise and peril of crypto philanthropy: potential for disintermediation, transparency, and reaching unbanked populations, alongside volatility risks, regulatory uncertainty, and concerns about the environmental impact and illicit uses of cryptocurrency. The collapse of major crypto platforms has tempered enthusiasm but not eliminated innovation.
