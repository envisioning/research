# Adaptive Training Plan Optimizers

## Summary

AI systems that continuously adjust periodization and sessions.

## Description

Planning engines that treat training schedules as dynamic optimization problems, updating periodization, exercise selection, and intensities daily based on readiness indicators, game calendars, and sport-specific constraints.
