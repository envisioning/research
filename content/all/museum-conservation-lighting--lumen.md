# Museum & Conservation Lighting

## Summary

Spectrally controlled lighting that minimizes material degradation while preserving viewing quality.

## Description

Museums balance visibility with preservation. Spectral tuning and dose management reduce photochemical damage to pigments and textiles, while maintaining accurate color rendering for visitors. Emerging systems track cumulative exposure per exhibit and adapt scenes to meet conservation targets over time.
