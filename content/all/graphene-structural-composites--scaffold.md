# Graphene-Enhanced Structural Composites

## Summary

Ultra-strong, lightweight, and conductive materials using graphene reinforcement.

## Description

Integrating graphene into concrete and steel can double strength while reducing weight and material usage. Beyond mechanics, its conductivity enables 'smart' structural components that self-sense stress or de-ice themselves. Scaling production cost remains the primary barrier.
