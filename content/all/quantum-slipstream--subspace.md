# Quantum Slipstream Drive

## Summary

Experimental propulsion creating quantum tunnels through subspace for velocities exceeding conventional warp by orders of magnitude.

## Description

Quantum slipstream drive projects a focused quantum field that narrows the slipstream to allow a ship to ride through it like a wave. This technology can achieve speeds approximately twenty times faster than maximum warp, potentially crossing the galaxy in a fraction of normal travel time.

The technology is highly experimental and requires precise navigation. Slight variations in the quantum field can cause catastrophic navigation errors. Advanced sensors and deflector modifications are necessary to maintain stable slipstream formation.
