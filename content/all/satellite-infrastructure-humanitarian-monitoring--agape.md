# Satellite Infrastructure for Humanitarian Monitoring

## Summary

Remote sensing and satellite imagery enabling crisis response, impact verification, and monitoring of humanitarian interventions.

## Description

Satellite infrastructure provides remote sensing capabilities for humanitarian monitoring—from tracking displacement to assessing damage to verifying program implementation. These systems enable oversight and coordination in hard-to-reach areas and during crises. However, they also raise questions about who controls satellite data, whether remote monitoring replaces or complements on-the-ground presence, and how satellite surveillance affects privacy and autonomy. The infrastructure represents a shift toward remote, objective monitoring while highlighting tensions between oversight and local control.
