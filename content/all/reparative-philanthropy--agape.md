# Philanthropy as Repair, Restitution & Reconciliation

## Summary

Redefinition of philanthropy as repair, restitution, or reconciliation, challenging hero-donor narratives in favor of collective action.

## Description

A growing movement reframes philanthropy not as charity or benevolence, but as repair, restitution, or reconciliation. This signal challenges the hero-donor narrative and questions whether philanthropy should exist at all if it requires the accumulation of wealth that may have been extracted unjustly. It emphasizes collective action over individual heroism and centers the voices of those who have been harmed by the systems that created the wealth being given away.
