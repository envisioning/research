# Global Crisis Coordination Platforms

## Summary

Digital backbones for transnational crisis management and resource routing.

## Description

Interoperable software platforms that integrate logistics networks, sensor data, and institutional mandates to orchestrate rapid, cross-border responses to disasters. They match needs and capacities in real time, minimizing bottlenecks when multiple regions are simultaneously stressed.
