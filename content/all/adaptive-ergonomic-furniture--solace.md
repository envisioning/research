# Adaptive Ergonomic Furniture

## Summary

Smart desks and chairs that sense posture and micro-movements to protect long-term musculoskeletal health.

## Description

Furniture embedded with pressure, motion, and tilt sensors to continuously infer posture, movement, and load distribution. The systems gently nudge users toward healthier postures and micro-breaks through subtle adjustments and ambient signals, preventing chronic pain and fatigue in sedentary work environments.
