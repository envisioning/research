# Collaborative Industrial Robots

## Summary

Force-limited robots designed to safely share space with humans.

## Description

Highly sensitive robotic arms and mobile manipulators with built-in safety, enabling side-by-side work with operators on tasks like assembly, inspection, and packaging.
