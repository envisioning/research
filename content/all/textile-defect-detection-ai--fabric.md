# Textile Defect Detection AI

## Summary

Computer vision inspecting fabric rolls in real time on Asian lines.

## Description

Edge AI cameras trained on millions of defect samples monitor looms and dye lines to catch misweaves, stains, and color drift instantly. Deployed across Asian manufacturing hubs, these systems cut inspection labor, reduce scrap, and provide statistical feedback to upstream process engineers.
