# Carbon-Negative Construction Processes

## Summary

Net-negative embodied carbon via biogenic materials, carbon-cured concrete, and direct air capture integration.

## Description

Combining biogenic carbon storage (mass timber, hempcrete, biochar concrete), carbon mineralization during curing, and site-integrated DAC to achieve net carbon removal over a project's lifecycle. Early demonstrations in Scandinavia, Canada, and the EU are testing commercial viability and MRV (monitoring, reporting, verification) protocols.
