# Smart City Infrastructure Networks

## Summary

Integrated connectivity fabric for traffic, utilities, and public services.

## Description

Urban-scale communication platforms that connect traffic lights, cameras, environmental sensors, public transport, and citizen services. They aggregate data across domains to optimize energy use, reduce congestion, and improve public safety, often blending fiber, cellular, and IoT connectivity.
