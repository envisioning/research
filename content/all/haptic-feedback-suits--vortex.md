# Haptic Feedback Suits

## Summary

Full-body wearable suits for physical sensations in VR.

## Description

Advanced wearable suits equipped with electro-tactile or vibro-tactile sensors that simulate physical sensations (touch, heat, resistance) to provide full-body immersion in virtual environments.
