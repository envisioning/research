# Longevity Digital Twins

## Summary

Virtual physiological replicas of individuals used to simulate the long-term impact of interventions on aging.

## Description

Computational models that integrate genomics, proteomics, metabolomics, microbiome data, imaging, and lifestyle signals into a continuously updated digital avatar of a person. These twins are used to test combinations of drugs, diets, and regenerative therapies in silico over simulated decades, identifying optimal strategies for extending healthspan while minimizing risk.
