# Haptic Authenticity Standards

## Summary

Verifying the source and safety of digital touch.

## Description

Protocols that authenticate the source of haptic feedback signals to prevent 'haptic deepfakes' or unauthorized physical actuation, ensuring that digital touch sensations are consensual and originate from trusted interactions.
