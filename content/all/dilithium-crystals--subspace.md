# Dilithium Crystals

## Summary

Rare crystalline matrix essential for regulating matter-antimatter reactions.

## Description

Dilithium crystals are a rare naturally-occurring mineral with unique properties that make them essential for warp core operation. The crystals' atomic structure allows them to regulate the matter-antimatter reaction without being consumed in the process.While dilithium doesn't decay during use, the crystal matrix can become fractured or depleted over time, requiring recrystallization or replacement. The scarcity of dilithium has been a significant factor in interstellar politics and economics.
