# Transparent Solar & Building-Integrated Photovoltaics (BIPV)

## Summary

Facades and windows that generate electricity while maintaining daylight and views.

## Description

Perovskite and organic PV cells can be tuned for partial transparency, enabling power generation from glazing. Combined with spandrel, curtain-wall, and roofing BIPV, buildings become distributed energy assets. Adoption depends on cost parity, durability, and grid integration, with strong momentum in Asia, the Middle East, and Europe.
