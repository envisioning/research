# Generative Urban Design

## Summary

AI models creating optimized neighborhood layouts based on complex constraints.

## Description

Utilization of generative AI to explore thousands of urban design permutations instantly. By defining constraints such as sunlight access, walkability, and density, planners can generate and evaluate optimal zoning configurations that maximize sustainability and livability metrics, accelerating the master planning process.
