# Social Determinants Navigation Platforms

## Summary

Digital tools that link patients to community resources addressing food, housing, and transportation needs.

## Description

These platforms embed social needs screening into clinical workflows and automatically route patients to appropriate community-based organizations, benefit programs, and support services. Closed-loop referral tracking ensures that referrals are completed and outcomes are visible to care teams, integrating social care into the fabric of healthcare delivery.
