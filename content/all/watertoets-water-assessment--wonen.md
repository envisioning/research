# Watertoets (Water Assessment)

## Summary

Mandatory climate adaptation assessments for development permits, increasingly restricting building in flood-prone or water-stressed areas.

## Description

The Watertoets (water assessment) requires developers to demonstrate that projects won't increase flood risk, impair water quality, or exceed water system capacity. With climate change intensifying, these assessments are becoming more stringent, blocking development in low-lying areas.

In the Netherlands, the principle 'water en bodem sturend' (water and soil as guiding principles) is reshaping spatial planning, potentially making large areas unsuitable for housing. Belgian and Luxembourg equivalents exist with varying stringency.

The signal represents climate adaptation requirements constraining development options. Key challenges include balancing housing needs with climate reality, the cost of climate-adaptive construction, and communicating to communities why certain areas can no longer be developed.
