# Digital Battery Passports

## Summary

Traceability systems for battery lifecycle and material provenance.

## Description

Digital passports track the chemical composition, usage history, and recycling potential of grid and EV batteries, ensuring compliance with circular economy regulations and facilitating efficient second-life repurposing.
