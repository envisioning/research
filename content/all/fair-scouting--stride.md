# Algorithmic Scouting Fairness

## Summary

Auditing AI recruitment tools to prevent bias in talent identification.

## Description

Initiatives and tools to audit AI-driven recruitment and scouting algorithms for bias. This ensures that automated systems do not discriminate based on race, socioeconomic background, or geography, promoting equitable access to opportunities in professional sports pathways.
