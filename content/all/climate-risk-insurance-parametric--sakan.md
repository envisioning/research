# Climate Risk Pricing & Insurance Innovation (Parametric)

## Summary

Flood/heat risk moving into underwriting, premiums, and financing terms—plus parametric insurance products that pay out based on measured events.

## Description

Climate risk pricing translates hazards (flooding, extreme heat, coastal exposure) into insurance premiums and financing terms. Parametric insurance products pay out when predefined triggers occur (rainfall thresholds, wind speed, temperature extremes), enabling faster payouts and clearer risk transfer.

In GCC, recent extreme weather events and fast coastal development increase the salience of climate risk in asset underwriting. As lenders and insurers adopt more granular hazard models, resilience investments (drainage, envelope performance, backup systems) become financially legible—improving insurability and reducing cost of capital.

The signal represents the shift from climate resilience as 'ESG narrative' to climate resilience as priced risk and contractual requirement. Key challenges include data availability, model transparency, and aligning incentives so developers invest in resilience rather than externalizing risk to insurers and governments.
