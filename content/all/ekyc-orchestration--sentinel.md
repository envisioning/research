# eKYC Orchestration Platforms

## Summary

Platforms that combine signals and providers into unified identity proofing flows.

## Description

eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer) Orchestration Platforms integrate multiple data sources, verification providers, and risk engines into configurable onboarding workflows. They dynamically choose verification steps (document checks, device reputation, biometrics, database lookups) based on risk, regulatory requirements, and geography, optimizing both fraud prevention and user experience.
