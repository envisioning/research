# Ecological Integrity vs. Engineered Systems

## Summary

Balancing regenerative practices with engineered microbes to avoid disruption.

## Description

The tension between preserving ecological integrity and introducing engineered systems requires careful management. This topic covers the long-term monitoring of microbial products in open environments to ensure that synthetic biology enhances rather than disrupts natural soil ecosystems.
