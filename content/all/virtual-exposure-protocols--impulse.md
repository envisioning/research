# Virtual Exposure Protocols

## Summary

Simulations for desensitization therapy.

## Description

Controlled, high-fidelity virtual reality simulations used for exposure therapy in treating phobias, PTSD, and anxiety disorders. These protocols allow clinicians to gradually expose patients to trigger stimuli in a safe environment, facilitating sensory desensitization and emotional reprocessing.
