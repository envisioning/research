# Brussels Canal Zone Development

## Summary

Large-scale urban renewal along Brussels' industrial canal, balancing housing development with gentrification concerns and industrial preservation.

## Description

The Brussels Canal Zone represents one of Europe's largest urban renewal projects, converting former industrial land along the Willebroek canal into mixed-use development. The area's transformation raises tensions between housing production, affordable housing preservation, industrial job retention, and gentrification.

Projects like Tivoli GreenCity and Tour & Taxis demonstrate high-quality sustainable development, but also displacement of existing working-class communities. The zone tests whether large-scale development can generate social value or inevitably extracts it.

The signal represents the challenge of balancing development scale with social outcomes. Key challenges include ensuring affordable housing within market-rate developments, maintaining productive activities alongside residential, and genuine community benefit rather than symbolic gestures.
