# Solid-State Batteries

## Summary

Next-generation batteries using solid electrolytes for higher safety and density.

## Description

Battery technology that replaces liquid electrolytes with solid materials, offering significantly higher energy density, faster charging speeds, and reduced fire risk. This is a critical enabler for longer-range EVs and electric aviation.
