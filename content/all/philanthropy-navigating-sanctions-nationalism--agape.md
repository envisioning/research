# Philanthropy Navigating Sanctions & Nationalism

## Summary

Philanthropy navigating sanctions, nationalism, and regulation, as geopolitical tensions constrain cross-border action.

## Description

Rising nationalism, sanctions regimes, and regulatory restrictions are making cross-border philanthropy more complex and constrained. This signal captures how foundations navigate legal restrictions, political pressures, and nationalist backlashes against international giving. These constraints force difficult choices about where to operate, how to structure programs, and whether to comply with or challenge restrictive frameworks.
