# Participatory Budgeting AI

## Summary

AI-enhanced tools for allocating public funds based on citizen input.

## Description

Machine learning systems that analyze vast amounts of citizen proposals and voting data to identify clusters of interest, estimate costs, and optimize budget allocation scenarios. This technology scales participatory budgeting from local pilots to city-wide or national levels.
