# Transporter

## Summary

Matter-energy conversion system capable of dematerializing and rematerializing objects across distances.

## Description

The transporter is a revolutionary technology that converts matter into energy, transmits it as a beam, and reconverts it back to matter at the destination. This allows for near-instantaneous transportation of personnel and cargo.The transporter system requires precise pattern buffers to maintain molecular integrity during transport. Heisenberg compensators resolve quantum uncertainties, and biofilters screen out harmful pathogens during the rematerialization process.
