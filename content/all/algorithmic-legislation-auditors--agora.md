# Algorithmic Legislation Auditors

## Summary

AI analysis of proposed laws for bias and impact.

## Description

Machine learning systems that simulate the potential outcomes of proposed legislation across different demographic groups. They flag unintended consequences, contradictions with existing laws, and potential biases before a bill is voted into law.
