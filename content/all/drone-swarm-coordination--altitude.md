# Swarm Coordination Algorithms for Multi-Drone Operations

## Summary

Distributed control for large fleets of cooperative autonomous aircraft.

## Description

Swarm algorithms enable decentralized coordination of hundreds or thousands of small UAS for cargo delivery, agriculture, surveillance, or emergency response. Key research areas include conflict resolution without central authority, resilience to communication dropouts, and regulatory frameworks for dense drone operations in shared airspace.
