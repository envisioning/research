# Industrial Data Spaces

## Summary

Sovereign data-sharing infrastructures for ecosystems and partners.

## Description

Federated architectures (inspired by Gaia-X and IDS) that let firms share operational data under fine-grained usage policies, preserving confidentiality and competitive advantage.
