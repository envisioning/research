# Quadratic Voting & Funding

## Summary

Preference-intensity mechanisms that balance minority voices with majority will.

## Description

Systems where participants allocate limited 'voice credits' across options, with costs rising quadratically (1 vote = 1 credit, 2 votes = 4 credits). This prevents wealthy actors from dominating while allowing strong preferences to be heard. Proven in Taiwan's vTaiwan platform and Gitcoin Grants for funding public goods.
