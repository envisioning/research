# AI-Triage & Patient Routing

## Summary

Intelligent systems that assess symptoms remotely and direct patients to the optimal care setting.

## Description

AI-powered triage applications use probabilistic models to assess patient symptoms via chat or voice interfaces. They determine the urgency and appropriate level of care, automatically routing patients to virtual consultations, retail clinics, urgent care centers, or emergency departments. This optimizes the utilization of the entire healthcare network and prevents ER overcrowding.
