# G42 AI & Cloud Platform

## Summary

Abu Dhabi's G42 has secured $1.5B from Microsoft, operates AI infrastructure across healthcare to government, and anchors the UAE's 1GW Stargate AI compute cluster.

## Description

G42 is Abu Dhabi's sovereign AI champion, a technology holding company that operates across cloud computing, AI development, and sector-specific AI applications in healthcare, energy, and government. Microsoft invested $1.5 billion in G42 in 2024, part of a broader $15+ billion commitment to UAE AI infrastructure. G42 is a key partner in the UAE's Stargate project — a planned 1GW AI compute cluster backed by OpenAI, Oracle, Cisco, NVIDIA, and SoftBank.

G42's significance extends beyond compute. Its subsidiaries operate AI applications in genomics (through its role in Abu Dhabi's population genome program), predictive analytics for government services, and autonomous systems. The company's pivot away from Chinese technology partnerships (divesting Huawei-linked assets under US pressure) and toward American tech giants reflects the UAE's strategic alignment decisions.

G42 is the institutional embodiment of the UAE's 'AI first' strategy, providing the infrastructure layer on which government digitization, smart city initiatives, and private sector AI adoption run. Its partnership with Microsoft gives it access to Azure cloud infrastructure while maintaining data sovereignty — a model other Gulf states are watching closely.
