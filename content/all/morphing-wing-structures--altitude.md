# Morphing Wing Structures

## Summary

Adaptive wing designs that change shape in real-time for optimal aerodynamics.

## Description

Morphing wings use compliant materials and advanced actuators to seamlessly alter their geometry during flight. This adaptability optimizes aerodynamic efficiency across different flight regimes (takeoff, cruise, landing), reducing fuel consumption and noise while improving maneuverability.
