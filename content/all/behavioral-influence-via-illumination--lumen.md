# Behavioral Influence via Illumination

## Summary

Light used intentionally or unintentionally to shape mood, attention, and compliance.

## Description

The study and regulation of how lighting design affects human behavior. This includes 'nudge' lighting to prevent crime, influence shopping habits, or crowd control. Ethical concerns arise regarding manipulation, consent, and the psychological impact of hostile lighting architecture.
