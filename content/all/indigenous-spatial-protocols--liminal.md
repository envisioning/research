# Indigenous Spatial Protocols

## Summary

Frameworks respecting indigenous knowledge and sacred sites.

## Description

Co-designed standards determining how indigenous lands, cultural practices, and traditional knowledge are represented—or not represented—in spatial computing. Ensures communities control whether sacred sites appear in AR, how stories are told, and who profits from digitized cultural heritage, countering extractive mapping practices.
