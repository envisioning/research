# Universal Energy Access Initiatives

## Summary

Decentralized solutions and policy frameworks ensuring electricity for all.

## Description

Mini-grids, solar home systems, and innovative financing models extend reliable electricity to the 750 million people without access, prioritizing productive uses, health, and education in Sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and remote regions.
