# Coercion Resistance & Anti Vote-Buying Safeguards

## Summary

Designing systems so voters cannot prove how they voted.

## Description

Cryptographic and procedural safeguards that prevent voters from generating convincing receipts of their vote choice (even if they want to), reducing coercion and vote-buying. Includes revoting designs, receipt-freeness, and careful UI/procedure constraints.
