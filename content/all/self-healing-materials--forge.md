# Self-Healing Materials & Adaptive Structures

## Summary

Materials that autonomously repair damage and extend component lifespan.

## Description

Self-healing materials incorporate microcapsules of healing agents, vascular networks, or reversible polymer bonds that activate when cracks form. In manufacturing, they enable structures that repair fatigue damage, coatings that seal scratches, and electronics that reconnect severed circuits—dramatically extending service life and enabling 'build-once' infrastructure for aerospace, automotive, and civil engineering.
