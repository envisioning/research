# Modular & Prefabricated Construction

## Summary

Factory-built housing components assembled on-site, potentially reducing construction time, costs, and disruption while enabling faster delivery.

## Description

Modular and prefabricated construction involves manufacturing building components or entire modules in factories, then assembling them on-site. This approach can reduce construction time, lower costs, improve quality control, and minimize on-site disruption, potentially enabling faster housing delivery.

In Benelux, modular construction is growing as a solution to housing shortages and construction delays. Examples include Dutch modular housing factories, Belgian prefabricated building systems, and Luxembourg off-site construction initiatives.

The signal represents a construction method that can accelerate delivery and potentially build acceptance through reduced disruption. Key challenges include overcoming perceptions of lower quality, ensuring design flexibility, and coordinating factory production with site preparation.
