# Critiques of Philanthropy as Reputational Laundering

## Summary

Critiques of philanthropy as reputational laundering, questioning whether giving legitimizes unjust wealth accumulation.

## Description

A growing critique argues that philanthropy often functions as reputational laundering—using giving to legitimate wealth that may have been accumulated through unjust means, extractive practices, or harm to communities and environments. This signal captures challenges to the idea that giving absolves or redeems wealth, and questions whether philanthropy should exist if it requires the accumulation of problematic capital. The critique forces fundamental questions about the relationship between wealth and virtue.
