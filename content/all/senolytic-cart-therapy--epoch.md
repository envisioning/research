# Senolytic CAR-T Therapy

## Summary

Engineering immune cells to target and destroy senescent 'zombie' cells that accumulate with age.

## Description

Modification of a patient's T-cells to recognize surface markers specific to senescent cells. These engineered cells are reintroduced into the body to hunt down and eliminate these harmful cells, reducing inflammation and improving tissue function across multiple organ systems.
