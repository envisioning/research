# Neuro-Adaptive Environments

## Summary

Hotel rooms and cabins that adjust to traveler stress and circadian rhythms.

## Description

Bio-feedback loops using non-invasive sensors to monitor traveler sleep states and stress levels, automatically adjusting lighting temperature, soundscapes, and scent diffusion to accelerate jet lag recovery and deepen relaxation.
