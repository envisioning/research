# Content Provenance & Authenticity Signaling

## Summary

Cryptographic provenance metadata for media integrity and trust.

## Description

Signing, watermarking, and provenance standards that attach verifiable origin and edit history to images, video, audio, and documents. Supports election integrity and civic trust by making it easier to identify manipulated media and coordinate authentic communications.
