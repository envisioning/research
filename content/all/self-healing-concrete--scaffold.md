# Self-Healing Bio-Concrete

## Summary

Concrete infused with bacteria that repair cracks automatically.

## Description

A biologically enhanced construction material containing bacteria that produce limestone when exposed to air and water. This allows the concrete to automatically seal cracks, extending structure lifespans and reducing maintenance costs.
