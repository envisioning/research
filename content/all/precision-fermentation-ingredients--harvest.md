# Precision Fermentation Ingredients

## Summary

Microbial production of proteins and functional compounds.

## Description

Engineered microbes in fermentation tanks produce dairy proteins, enzymes, and flavor compounds, decoupling ingredient supply from livestock and enabling novel food products.
