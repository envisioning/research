# Informal Economy Platforms

## Summary

Digital tools empowering informal sector workers with formal-sector capabilities.

## Description

Mobile-first platforms providing identity, credit, contract enforcement, and collective bargaining tools to the world's 2 billion informal workers. These systems bridge the gap between informal labor and global value chains, enhancing productivity and social protection without imposing rigid corporate structures.
