# Posthumous Identity Governance Platforms

## Summary

Comprehensive post-death digital identity management.

## Description

Integrated legal-technical frameworks combining secure storage, consent verification oracles, and estate management tools for governing the preservation, commercial use, or deletion of a deceased person's digital persona, synthetic representations, and interactive AI avatars.
