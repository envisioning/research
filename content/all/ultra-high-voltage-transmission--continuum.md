# Ultra-High Voltage DC Transmission

## Summary

Continental-scale grids linking renewable energy zones.

## Description

±1100kV UHVDC lines capable of transmitting gigawatts of power over thousands of kilometers with minimal loss. Pioneered in China and expanding globally, these networks synchronize solar, wind, and hydro resources across time zones and continents, creating a planetary energy web resilient to regional disruptions.
