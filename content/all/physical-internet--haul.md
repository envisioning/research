# Physical Internet

## Summary

Open, modular logistics network analogous to digital Internet; research.

## Description

Physical Internet proposes an open, modular logistics network with standardized containers, protocols, and routing—analogous to the digital Internet. Research; applications: more efficient freight, reduced waste. Significant coordination and adoption challenges.
