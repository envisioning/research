# Swarm Infrastructure Repair

## Summary

Autonomous drone fleets for post-disaster reconstruction.

## Description

Coordinated swarms of terrestrial and aerial drones equipped with 3D printing and welding capabilities. These swarms can rapidly assess and repair critical infrastructure—bridges, power lines, pipelines—following natural disasters or attacks, ensuring the continuity of civilization's lifeline services without immediate human intervention.
