# YIMBY Movements in Benelux

## Summary

Emerging 'Yes In My Backyard' advocacy countering NIMBY opposition, mobilizing support for housing density and development.

## Description

YIMBY (Yes In My Backyard) movements are emerging in Benelux as a counterweight to organized development opposition. Groups like YIMBY Netherlands, pro-housing factions within political parties (D66, VVD, Groen youth), and urbanist advocacy are actively supporting densification and development.

These movements reframe housing development as a social good rather than a threat, mobilizing renters, young people, and affordability advocates. They participate in permit processes, counter NIMBY narratives, and advocate for pro-housing policy.

The signal represents organized support for development, balancing the visibility of opposition. Key challenges include building sustained organizational capacity, avoiding capture by developer interests, and bridging the intensity gap (opposition is motivated by direct impact, support by diffuse benefit).
