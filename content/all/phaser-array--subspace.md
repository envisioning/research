# Phaser Array

## Summary

Directed energy weapons using nadion particles to deliver variable-yield energy beams.

## Description

Phaser arrays are the primary directed energy weapon system used by Starfleet vessels. They fire coherent nadion particle beams that can be precisely calibrated from stun to disintegration levels.Modern phaser arrays consist of multiple emitter segments that can fire in sequence, creating a continuous beam of considerable length and power. The arrays can target multiple threats simultaneously with computer-assisted fire control.
