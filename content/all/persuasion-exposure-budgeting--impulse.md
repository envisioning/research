# Persuasion Exposure Budgeting

## Summary

Caps and meters for individual influence exposure.

## Description

Concepts and prototype infrastructures that track the cumulative intensity of targeted influence a person is subjected to across platforms, and enforce limits or cooling-off periods. Inspired by credit scores and ad-frequency caps, these systems aim to prevent over-exposure to high-pressure persuasive environments.
