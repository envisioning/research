# Tactical Intelligence Engines

## Summary

Multi-agent analytics extracting team tactics and spatial patterns.

## Description

Analytics platforms that fuse tracking, event, and video data to model player positioning, passing networks, and space creation. Coaches receive suggested tactical adjustments, matchup insights, and scenario simulations grounded in machine-learned representations of game dynamics.
