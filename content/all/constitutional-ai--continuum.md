# Constitutional AI Frameworks

## Summary

Embedding human rights and safety rules into AI models.

## Description

A method for training AI systems where a set of principles (a 'constitution') is used to guide the model's behavior and alignment. This approach ensures that powerful AI systems governing critical infrastructure or advising on policy adhere to fundamental ethical standards, preventing catastrophic misalignment or abuse of power.
