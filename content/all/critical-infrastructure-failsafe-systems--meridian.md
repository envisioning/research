# Critical Infrastructure Failsafe Systems

## Summary

Graceful degradation under attack or failure.

## Description

Control architectures and embedded firmware for power, water, and transport systems that ensure essential services degrade in a predictable, safe manner rather than collapsing catastrophically under cyberattack or cascade failures.
