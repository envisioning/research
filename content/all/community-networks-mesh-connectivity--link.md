# Community Networks & Mesh Connectivity

## Summary

Bottom-up, cooperative infrastructure built and operated by local communities.

## Description

Decentralized wireless mesh networks using WiFi, LoRa, and TV white space, deployed by cooperatives and NGOs in underserved regions of Latin America, Africa, and Asia. They provide affordable last-mile connectivity, local content hosting, and digital sovereignty where commercial operators won't invest.
