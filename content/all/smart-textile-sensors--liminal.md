# Smart Textile Sensors

## Summary

Fabric-integrated sensors for full-body spatial tracking.

## Description

Conductive threads and flexible electronics woven into everyday clothing to capture motion, posture, and physiological signals. Unlike rigid trackers, these garments move naturally with the body, enabling unobtrusive full-body capture for animation, healthcare monitoring, and sports analysis.
