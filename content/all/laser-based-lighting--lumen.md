# Laser-based Lighting

## Summary

High-intensity, long-range illumination for mobility, infrastructure, and extreme environments.

## Description

Utilizing laser diodes excited by phosphors to produce incredibly bright, coherent, and long-throw white light. This technology is critical for automotive high beams, stadium lighting, and search-and-rescue operations where efficiency and throw distance exceed the capabilities of standard LEDs.
