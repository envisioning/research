# Construction Site Air Quality & Community Health

## Summary

Monitoring and mitigating PM2.5, NOx, and VOCs affecting nearby residents.

## Description

Construction generates dust, diesel exhaust, and volatile organic compounds that disproportionately impact low-income and minority communities near urban job sites. Real-time air quality monitoring, electrification, and community notification systems are emerging requirements in California, London, Beijing, and other cities with environmental justice mandates.
