# Secure Whistleblowing & Reporting Channels

## Summary

Anonymous disclosures for accountability and anti-corruption.

## Description

Secure dropboxes and protected reporting systems (often using anonymity networks and strong cryptography) for corruption tips, misconduct reporting, and investigative collaboration. Critical for accountability in contexts where retaliation risk suppresses oversight.
