# Space Debris & Collision Avoidance for Mega-Constellations

## Summary

Systems to prevent orbital debris and satellite collisions.

## Description

Tracking networks, autonomous collision avoidance maneuvers, and deorbiting mechanisms required as LEO constellations grow from hundreds to tens of thousands of satellites. Failure to manage debris risks Kessler syndrome, making orbits unusable and threatening all space-based communication.
