# Cross-Cultural Affective Models

## Summary

Frameworks that adapt affect recognition to cultural contexts and norms.

## Description

Culturally-aware models that account for how emotions are expressed, perceived, and regulated differently across societies—recognizing that affect is not universal but shaped by language, gesture lexicons, display rules, and social structures.
