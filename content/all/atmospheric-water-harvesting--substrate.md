# Atmospheric Water Harvesting

## Summary

Extracting drinkable water from air using advanced materials and sorbents.

## Description

New sorbent materials and radiative-cooling devices can capture humidity from the air even in arid regions. When integrated into buildings or remote infrastructure, atmospheric water generators provide decentralized, off-grid water sources for communities and critical facilities.
