# Phage Therapy

## Summary

Bacteriophages for targeted bacterial infection treatment.

## Description

Phage therapy uses bacteriophages—viruses that infect bacteria—to treat bacterial infections. First trial uses; long history in Eastern Europe. Applications: antibiotic-resistant infections. Growing interest as antibiotic resistance increases. Regulatory and manufacturing challenges remain.
