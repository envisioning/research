# In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)

## Summary

Extracting water, oxygen, and materials from extraterrestrial bodies.

## Description

Technologies for processing lunar regolith, Martian soil, and asteroid material into propellants, construction materials, and life support consumables. ISRU makes permanent off-world settlements economically viable, creating backup locations for humanity and reducing existential risk from single-planet dependence.
