# Spatial Design Collaboration

## Summary

Co-located and remote co-creation of spaces in mixed reality.

## Description

Tools for architects, urban planners, and product teams to prototype and iterate on designs at true scale. Participants can walk through evolving layouts together, annotate in situ, and test sightlines, flows, and lighting before anything is built.
