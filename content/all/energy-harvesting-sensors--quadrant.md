# Energy Harvesting Sensors

## Summary

Batteryless IoT devices powered by ambient energy.

## Description

Sensors that scavenge vibration, thermal gradients, RF, or light to operate indefinitely without batteries, enabling dense, maintenance-free industrial IoT deployments.
