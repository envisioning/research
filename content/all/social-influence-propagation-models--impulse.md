# Social Influence Propagation Models

## Summary

Simulators of how moods and behaviors spread.

## Description

Graph-based and agent-based models that simulate how emotions, opinions, and behaviors diffuse through social networks both online and offline. They enable planners and platforms to identify key nodes, predict emergent cascades, and design interventions that amplify or dampen specific collective dynamics.
