# Distributed Energy Resource Management Systems (DERMS)

## Summary

Control platforms orchestrating large fleets of distributed energy resources.

## Description

DERMS optimize scheduling and dispatch of rooftop PV, batteries, EVs, and flexible loads, maintaining grid constraints while maximizing economic and environmental value.
