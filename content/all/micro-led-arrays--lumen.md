# Micro-LED Arrays

## Summary

Ultra-dense, energy-efficient emitters enabling adaptive, pixel-level control of light.

## Description

Micro-LED technology involves arrays of microscopic LEDs forming individual pixel elements. Unlike OLEDs, they are inorganic, offering higher brightness, greater energy efficiency, and immunity to burn-in. In illumination, Micro-LED arrays enable highly precise, pixelated beam control for automotive headlights, architectural lighting, and augmented reality projection.
