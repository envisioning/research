# Industrial Demand Flexibility

## Summary

Coordinating energy-intensive industrial loads to provide grid services.

## Description

Industries like aluminum smelting, cement, and data centers modulate production and operations in response to grid signals, unlocking gigawatts of flexible capacity to absorb renewables and stabilize frequency.
