# Biosecurity Design Guards

## Summary

Automated screening for high-risk DNA, proteins, and biological constructs.

## Description

Cloud-based and on-premise tools that evaluate genetic sequences and experimental designs against up-to-date threat databases and safety policies. They are integrated into synthesis providers, lab notebooks, and AI design tools to prevent the accidental or deliberate creation of catastrophic pathogens.
