# Transwarp Drive

## Summary

Advanced propulsion technology exceeding conventional warp speeds through folded space corridors.

## Description

Transwarp drive represents the next evolution beyond standard warp technology, capable of creating stable transwarp conduits through subspace. These conduits allow travel at velocities far exceeding maximum warp.Originally developed by the Borg Collective, transwarp technology creates a network of artificial wormholes that dramatically reduce travel times between distant points in the galaxy.
