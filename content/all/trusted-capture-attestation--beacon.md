# Trusted Capture Device Attestation

## Summary

Hardware-backed proofs from cameras and recorders.

## Description

Secure hardware modules in capture devices that sign media at the point of recording, producing tamper-evident attestations that downstream systems can verify when assessing authenticity claims.
