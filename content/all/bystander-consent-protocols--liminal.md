# Bystander Consent Protocols

## Summary

Standards for protecting people who are not wearing devices.

## Description

Technical and legal frameworks that govern how passersby are detected, anonymized, or removed from spatial recordings. They address questions like whether bystanders can opt out, how long incidental data is stored, and how overlays may or may not attach to individuals.
