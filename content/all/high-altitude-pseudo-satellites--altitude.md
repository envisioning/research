# High-Altitude Pseudo-Satellites (HAPS)

## Summary

Stratospheric platforms for persistent communication, surveillance, and Earth observation.

## Description

HAPS are solar-electric aircraft or airships that loiter at 60,000+ feet for weeks or months, providing regional broadband, disaster response connectivity, border monitoring, or environmental sensing. They complement satellites at lower cost and latency. Regulatory, power storage, and atmospheric dynamics remain open challenges.
