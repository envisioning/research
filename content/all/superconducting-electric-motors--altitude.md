# Superconducting Electric Motors & Generators

## Summary

Lightweight, ultra-high power density propulsion for MW-class hybrid systems.

## Description

Superconducting machines operate at cryogenic temperatures to eliminate resistive losses, enabling power densities an order of magnitude higher than conventional motors. This is critical for large hybrid-electric or turbo-electric aircraft. The R&D bottleneck is cryocooler weight, insulation, fault behavior, and integration with airframe thermal management.
