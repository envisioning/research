# Digital Identity for Displaced & Stateless Populations

## Summary

Technology solutions for digital identity and credentials for refugees, displaced persons, and stateless populations.

## Description

Millions of displaced and stateless people lack official identity documents, limiting access to services, rights, and economic opportunities. This signal captures philanthropic investment in digital identity solutions—biometric systems, blockchain-based credentials, portable qualifications—that could help displaced populations prove identity and access services. However, these technologies also raise concerns about surveillance, data security, and whether technical solutions can address fundamentally political problems of belonging and recognition.
