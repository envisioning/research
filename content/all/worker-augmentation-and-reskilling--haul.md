# Worker Augmentation and Reskilling Platforms

## Summary

Tools and frameworks that ensure human workers are safely augmented and reskilled alongside automation.

## Description

As warehouses and logistics operations automate, augmentation and reskilling platforms help workers adapt to new roles, from robot operators to data supervisors. They incorporate ergonomic design, safety analytics, and training pathways to support just transitions in highly automated supply chains.
