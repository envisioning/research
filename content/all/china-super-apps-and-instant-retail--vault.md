# Super-Apps and Instant Retail

## Summary

WeChat runs 4 million mini-programs inside a single app — no downloads, no app stores — while Temu reached 90+ countries in under two years

## Description

China's super-app model eliminates the friction of individual app downloads. WeChat Mini Programs let users book flights, pay bills, order food, and manage government services without leaving the chat interface. Meituan delivers restaurant meals in under 30 minutes across 2,800 cities.

Pinduoduo/Temu proved the model exports. Temu reached 90+ markets by 2025 using a factory-to-consumer model that undercuts Amazon on price by 30-50%. Douyin (China's TikTok) has more advanced e-commerce than its international twin — full in-app stores, affiliate commissions, and AI-powered recommendation.

The Western app ecosystem still fragments users across dozens of single-purpose apps. China solved this a decade ago. The question now is whether regulatory concerns about data flows will prevent super-app models from taking root outside China.
