# Megawatt Charging Systems (MCS)

## Summary

High-power charging standard for heavy-duty electric trucks and vessels.

## Description

A charging standard designed for heavy-duty vehicles, capable of delivering up to 3.75 megawatts of power. MCS enables electric trucks to recharge during mandatory driver breaks, making long-haul electric transport operationally viable.
