# Automated Commissioning & Interoperability Layers

## Summary

Software that auto-discovers devices and harmonizes protocols across building and city lighting.

## Description

Commissioning remains a major cost driver for connected lighting. Interoperability layers handle auto-discovery, addressing, scene migration, and policy enforcement across heterogeneous stacks (e.g., DALI, DMX, IP, BMS). Emerging approaches treat lighting as software-defined infrastructure with repeatable, auditable deployments.
