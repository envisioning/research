# Synthetic DNA Storage

## Summary

Ultra-dense, millennia-scale biological data preservation.

## Description

Utilizing synthetic DNA strands to store vast amounts of data in microscopic volumes. This technology promises density millions of times higher than magnetic tape and stability that lasts for thousands of years without energy consumption, revolutionizing long-term knowledge preservation.
