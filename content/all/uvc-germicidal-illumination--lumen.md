# UV‑C Germicidal Illumination

## Summary

Disinfection lighting for air and surfaces in healthcare, transit, and high-occupancy settings.

## Description

UV‑C (and emerging far‑UV‑C) systems can inactivate pathogens in upper-room air, HVAC ducts, and surfaces. Applications require careful engineering to manage exposure limits and materials compatibility. The field is moving toward safer deployments with sensing, interlocks, and validated dose control.
