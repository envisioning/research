# Operator Cognitive Load Management Systems

## Summary

Interfaces and assistants that prioritize alerts and simplify decisions.

## Description

Real-time cognitive support systems that simplify complex interfaces, prioritize alerts, and recommend actions to human operators in high-tempo environments. By managing cognitive load, these systems reduce errors, shorten reaction times, and improve mission outcomes under stress.
