# Quantum Route Optimization

## Summary

Quantum computing solving complex multi-modal routing problems at scale.

## Description

Quantum algorithms running on gate-model or annealing systems to solve NP-hard routing, fleet scheduling, and resource allocation problems across global transportation networks in near real-time, unlocking efficiencies impossible with classical computing.
