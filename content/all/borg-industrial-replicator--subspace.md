# Borg Industrial Replicator

## Summary

Large-scale matter reconstruction systems capable of fabricating entire ships and drones.

## Description

Advanced replication technology that constructs Borg cubes, spheres, and drones from base matter. Operates at scales far exceeding Federation industrial replicators, enabling rapid fleet expansion and drone production.
