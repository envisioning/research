# Last-Mile Delivery Droids

## Summary

Autonomous sidewalk robots for local package and food delivery.

## Description

Small, autonomous vehicles designed to navigate sidewalks and urban environments to deliver goods over the 'last mile' to consumers. These droids reduce delivery costs and congestion associated with traditional delivery vans.
