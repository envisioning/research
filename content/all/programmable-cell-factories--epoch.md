# Programmable Cell Factories

## Summary

Microfluidic hardware that grows, differentiates, or edits stem cells for rejuvenation use.

## Description

Automated, desktop-scale bioreactors designed to culture patient-specific stem cells and differentiate them into required tissue types (e.g., neurons, cardiomyocytes) or engineer them for therapies. These 'factories' decentralize the production of regenerative medicine, reducing costs and enabling personalized cell therapies on demand.
