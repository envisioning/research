# Fusion Rocket

## Summary

## Description

Fusion rockets would use fusion reactions for propulsion. Research and development. Applications: fast interplanetary travel; limited interstellar potential. Significant physics and engineering challenges; no practical fusion propulsion yet demonstrated.
