# Ballot Marking Devices (with VVPAT)

## Summary

Accessible ballot marking with voter-verifiable paper records.

## Description

Assistive ballot marking devices that produce human-readable paper ballots (or VVPAT records) as the system-of-record. Designed for accessibility and multilingual support while preserving robust auditability through paper-based tabulation checks.
