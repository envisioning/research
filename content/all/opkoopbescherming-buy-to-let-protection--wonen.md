# Opkoopbescherming (Buy-to-Let Protection)

## Summary

Municipal powers to restrict investor purchases in designated areas, requiring owner-occupation to preserve housing for residents.

## Description

Opkoopbescherming gives Dutch municipalities the power to designate areas where property purchases require a self-occupation obligation (zelfbewoningsplicht), effectively blocking buy-to-let investors. Implemented in 2022, over 100 municipalities have adopted it.

The measure responds to investor-driven price increases that priced out owner-occupiers, particularly in urban areas. It represents direct intervention in property markets to prioritize resident ownership over investment returns.

The signal represents local government reclaiming housing markets from investors. Key challenges include enforcement (proving non-occupation), potential investor displacement to unprotected areas, and balancing ownership promotion with rental market needs.
