# Real-Time Budget & Policy Simulation

## Summary

Interactive models for participatory fiscal planning.

## Description

Agent-based and system-dynamics models that let citizens and policymakers explore 'what-if' scenarios for budgets, taxes, and policy interventions. Visualizes trade-offs in real-time to support informed participatory decision-making.
