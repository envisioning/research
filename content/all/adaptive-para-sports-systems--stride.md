# Advanced Adaptive & Para-Sports Systems

## Summary

Customized prosthetics, wheelchairs, and assistive tech for elite para-athletes.

## Description

Highly tuned racing wheelchairs, prosthetics, and seating systems optimized with CFD and biomechanics data. These platforms blur the line between equipment and body, demanding new performance paradigms and classification rules in adaptive sports.
