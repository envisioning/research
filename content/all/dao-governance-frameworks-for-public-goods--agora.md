# DAO Governance Frameworks for Public Goods

## Summary

On-chain coordination for commons stewardship.

## Description

Decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) templates adapted for public goods funding, community land trusts, cooperative utilities, and municipal services. Combines programmable treasury management with participatory governance mechanisms.
