# Mixed Reality Cultural Guides

## Summary

AR/MR overlays providing real-time translation, historical context, and navigation.

## Description

Wearable or mobile-based applications that overlay digital historical reconstructions, real-time translation of signage and speech, navigation paths, cultural cues, and informational labels onto the real-world physical environment for immersive cross-cultural exploration.
