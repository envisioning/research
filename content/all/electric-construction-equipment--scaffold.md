# Electrified Construction Equipment

## Summary

Battery-electric and hybrid machines paired with on-site charging and temporary microgrids.

## Description

Electric excavators, loaders, and aerial lifts reduce local emissions and noise, enabling work in urban or regulated environments. Coupled with battery packs, temporary microgrids, and optimized charging logistics, electrification reduces fuel volatility and helps projects meet increasingly strict air-quality requirements.
