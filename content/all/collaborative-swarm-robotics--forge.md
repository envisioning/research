# Collaborative Swarm Robotics

## Summary

Decentralized groups of robots coordinating to perform complex tasks.

## Description

Inspired by insect swarms, this technology enables large numbers of simple robots to work together without a central controller. In manufacturing, swarms can reconfigure themselves to move heavy loads, assemble large structures, or perform parallel inspection tasks, offering unmatched flexibility and redundancy.
