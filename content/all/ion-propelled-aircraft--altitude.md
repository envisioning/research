# Ion-Propelled Aircraft

## Summary

Electrohydrodynamic propulsion for silent flight; research prototypes.

## Description

Ion-propelled aircraft use electrohydrodynamic (EHD) thrust—ion wind—for propulsion. Research, development, prototypes. Benefits: silent, no moving parts. Challenges include thrust density and scaling. Applications: efficient propulsion in air.
