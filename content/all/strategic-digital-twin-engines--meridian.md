# Strategic Digital Twin Engines

## Summary

Simulating systemic shocks and escalation paths.

## Description

Large-scale simulation engines that model economic, military, cyber, and information systems as an integrated whole, supporting scenario analysis and wargaming to stress-test strategic stability and crisis responses.
