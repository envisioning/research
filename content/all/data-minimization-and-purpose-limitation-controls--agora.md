# Data Minimization & Purpose Limitation Controls

## Summary

Preventing mission creep and ‘function creep’ in civic data systems.

## Description

Technical controls (data retention enforcement, purpose tags, access boundaries, privacy budgets, automated deletion) that keep data collection and reuse aligned with democratic mandates. Reduces surveillance risk and helps preserve legitimacy in digital governance.
