# Autonomous Direct Air Capture

## Summary

Scalable, self-regulating units for atmospheric CO2 removal.

## Description

Next-generation Direct Air Capture (DAC) facilities powered by renewable energy and managed by AI systems to optimize airflow and chemical sorbent efficiency. These autonomous units are designed to be deployed in modular arrays across non-arable land, actively reducing atmospheric carbon concentrations to pre-industrial levels and sequestering it in geological formations.
