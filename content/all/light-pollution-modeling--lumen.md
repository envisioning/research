# Light Pollution Modeling & Skyglow Simulation

## Summary

Modeling tools that predict glare, trespass, and skyglow to support dark-sky compliant design.

## Description

These tools simulate outdoor lighting distributions at scale, incorporating fixture photometry, mounting geometry, reflectance, and atmospheric scattering. They enable planners to quantify and mitigate skyglow, glare, and light trespass while maintaining safety—linking engineering decisions to ecological and community outcomes.
