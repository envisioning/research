# AR Site Visualization

## Summary

Overlays of BIM models onto the physical construction site.

## Description

Augmented Reality headsets that allow workers and engineers to see digital plans (BIM data) superimposed on the real world. This helps in visualizing hidden infrastructure like pipes and detecting clashes early.
