# Water Scarcity & Resource-Driven Conflicts

## Summary

Growing water scarcity driving conflict, migration, and humanitarian crises requiring philanthropic response.

## Description

Water scarcity is intensifying due to climate change, population growth, and overextraction, creating conditions for conflict, forced migration, and humanitarian emergencies. This signal captures philanthropic attention to water security, the intersection of water and conflict, and questions about how to address resource scarcity at scale. It reflects recognition that water will be a defining challenge of the coming decades, requiring both immediate humanitarian response and long-term systemic solutions.
