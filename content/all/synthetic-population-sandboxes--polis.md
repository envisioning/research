# Synthetic Population Sandboxes

## Summary

Privacy-preserving artificial data sets for policy training and testing.

## Description

The generation of statistically accurate but entirely artificial population data. This allows agencies to train AI models, test service eligibility rules, and share data with researchers without ever exposing the sensitive personal information of real citizens, resolving the privacy-utility trade-off.
