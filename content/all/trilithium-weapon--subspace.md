# Trilithium Weapon

## Summary

Banned weapon of mass destruction capable of initiating stellar collapse and supernova detonation.

## Description

Trilithium-based weapons represent one of the most terrifying technologies ever developed. When properly deployed, trilithium can halt all nuclear fusion within a star, causing rapid gravitational collapse followed by a supernova explosion that destroys everything in the system.

The use of such weapons constitutes genocide on a stellar scale and is banned by virtually all galactic powers. Even possessing trilithium weapons technology is considered a war crime. The destructive potential extends beyond the target system, as the resulting shockwave can affect nearby systems.
