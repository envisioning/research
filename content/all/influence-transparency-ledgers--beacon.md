# Influence Transparency Ledgers

## Summary

Recording when, how, and why users were nudged.

## Description

System-wide logs that track each algorithmic intervention on user behavior—such as reordered options, highlighted defaults, or urgency cues—exposing them to regulators, auditors, and end-users in an understandable format.
