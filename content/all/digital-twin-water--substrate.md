# Digital Twin Water Networks

## Summary

Real-time virtual replicas of physical water infrastructure.

## Description

Digital twins integrate hydraulic modeling with real-time IoT sensor data. They allow utilities to simulate 'what-if' scenarios, predict pipe failures before they happen, and optimize pressure to reduce leakage and energy consumption. This shifts water management from reactive repair to predictive maintenance.
