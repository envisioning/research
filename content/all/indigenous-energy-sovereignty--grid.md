# Indigenous Energy Sovereignty

## Summary

Self-determined energy systems respecting Indigenous rights and governance.

## Description

Frameworks recognizing Indigenous peoples' rights to control energy development on their lands, incorporating traditional knowledge, ensuring free prior informed consent, and enabling community-owned renewable projects.
