# Impact-Measurement Models

## Summary

Computational systems for quantifying and tracking social outcomes, enabling data-driven assessment of philanthropic interventions.

## Description

Advanced impact-measurement models use computational approaches—from statistical analysis to machine learning—to quantify and track social outcomes across philanthropic interventions. These systems enable funders to assess effectiveness, compare approaches, and make evidence-based decisions about resource allocation. However, they also raise questions about what outcomes can be measured, whether quantification captures meaningful change, and how measurement systems shape what gets funded. The models reflect a broader shift toward data-driven philanthropy while highlighting tensions between measurable and meaningful impact.
