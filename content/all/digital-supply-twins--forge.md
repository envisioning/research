# Digital Supply Chain Twins

## Summary

Real-time virtual replicas of entire supply networks for simulation.

## Description

A digital twin of the supply chain mirrors every asset, shipment, and warehouse in real-time. It allows managers to run 'what-if' scenarios—simulating disruptions like pandemics or canal blockages—to test resilience and automatically trigger contingency plans before crises occur.
