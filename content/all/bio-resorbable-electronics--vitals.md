# Bio-Resorbable Electronics

## Summary

Transient electronic sensors and implants that dissolve safely in the body after their function is complete.

## Description

Bio-resorbable electronics (or transient electronics) eliminate the need for surgical removal of temporary implants. These devices—ranging from intracranial pressure monitors to cardiac pacemakers—are fabricated from water-soluble materials like magnesium, silk, and silicon. They operate for a precise timeframe to monitor post-operative recovery or deliver localized therapy before degrading harmlessly, reducing infection risks and long-term complications.
