# Donation Routing Engines

## Summary

Intelligent systems that route donations to optimal recipients based on need, impact potential, and donor preferences.

## Description

Donation routing engines use algorithms to match donations with recipients based on factors like need, impact potential, efficiency, and donor preferences. These systems promise to optimize resource allocation and ensure donations reach where they can do the most good. However, they also raise questions about who defines 'optimal,' whether algorithmic routing reduces or reproduces bias, and how routing decisions balance efficiency with relationships and local knowledge. The engines represent a shift toward automated, data-driven giving while highlighting tensions between optimization and human judgment.
