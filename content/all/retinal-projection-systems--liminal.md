# Retinal Projection Systems

## Summary

Ultra-compact displays projecting directly onto the retina.

## Description

Miniaturized laser or micro-LED systems integrated into contact lenses or minimal eyewear that bypass traditional optics by projecting light directly into the eye. These enable all-day wearable AR with unlimited field of view, minimal power consumption, and no visible hardware.
