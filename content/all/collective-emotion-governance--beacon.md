# Collective Emotional Data Governance

## Summary

Cooperative ownership models for group affective data.

## Description

Frameworks enabling communities, unions, or demographic cohorts to collectively negotiate the terms of emotional data collection, set shared privacy thresholds, and redistribute revenue from aggregated affective insights.
