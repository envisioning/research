# Single-Pilot Operations (SPO) Frameworks

## Summary

Human-machine teaming to enable safe cargo/commercial flights with reduced crew.

## Description

SPO is the intermediate step before full autonomy, where a single pilot is supported by advanced AI co-pilots and ground operators. Key technologies include fatigue monitoring, automated incapacitation recovery, and high-bandwidth ground control links. The economic case is strong for cargo, but certification requires proving that one pilot + AI is safer than two human pilots.
