# Humanitarian Corridors & Protected Zone Management

## Summary

Tooling to designate, monitor, and enforce protected areas in conflict.

## Description

Tooling that designates, monitors, and helps enforce humanitarian corridors and protected zones using ISR, secure communications, and AI-assisted compliance monitoring. These systems link defense capabilities to international humanitarian law and civilian protection.
