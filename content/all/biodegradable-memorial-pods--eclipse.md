# Biodegradable Memorial Pods

## Summary

Burial capsules that integrate cremains or remains with tree-planting systems.

## Description

Engineered pods made from biodegradable materials that encapsulate the body or ashes alongside tree seeds or seedlings. They are designed to optimize root growth, nutrient transfer, and soil health, transforming burial grounds into restorative memorial forests.
