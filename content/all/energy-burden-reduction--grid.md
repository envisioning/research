# Energy Burden Reduction Programs

## Summary

Targeted interventions lowering energy costs for low-income households.

## Description

Weatherization, rate design reform, efficiency upgrades, and bill assistance programs reduce the disproportionate share of income that vulnerable households spend on energy, addressing energy poverty in wealthy nations.
