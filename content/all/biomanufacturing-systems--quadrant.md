# Biomanufacturing Systems

## Summary

Engineered organisms producing industrial materials and chemicals.

## Description

Synthetic biology platforms using genetically programmed microbes, cell cultures, and enzymatic reactors to manufacture proteins, polymers, and specialty chemicals sustainably.
