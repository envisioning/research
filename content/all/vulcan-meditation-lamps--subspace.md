# Vulcan Meditation Lamps

## Summary

Meditation aids using harmonic light and sound frequencies to facilitate deep mental focus.

## Description

Traditional Vulcan meditation lamps are ceremonial devices that produce carefully calibrated light frequencies and harmonic tones designed to facilitate deep meditative states. The lamps create an environment conducive to the intense mental discipline required for Vulcan meditation practices.

The technology combines visual stimuli with subsonic harmonics to help practitioners achieve the mental clarity necessary for logic exercises, emotional control, and advanced mental techniques like mind melds. While the scientific principles are sound—using sensory entrainment to influence brainwave patterns—the devices are deeply tied to Vulcan cultural practices and philosophy. They are often family heirlooms passed down through generations, with each lamp tuned to the specific mental rhythms of its owner's lineage.
