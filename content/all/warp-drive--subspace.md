# Warp Drive

## Summary

Faster-than-light propulsion system that warps space-time to achieve superluminal velocities.

## Description

The warp drive is a revolutionary propulsion technology that allows spacecraft to travel faster than light by warping the fabric of space-time itself. Rather than moving through space conventionally, the ship creates a warp bubble that contracts space in front of it and expands space behind it.

This technology enables interstellar travel and exploration, making it possible to traverse vast cosmic distances in reasonable timeframes. The warp drive is measured in factors, with each factor representing exponentially increasing speeds.
