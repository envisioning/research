# Digital Curb Management

## Summary

AI-driven platforms dynamically allocating curb space for delivery and transit.

## Description

Digital infrastructure that digitizes and dynamically manages curbside access. Using cameras and sensors, these platforms facilitate real-time booking for loading zones, ride-hailing pickups, and EV charging, replacing static signage with dynamic regulations to reduce congestion and illegal parking.
