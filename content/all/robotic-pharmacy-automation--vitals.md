# Robotic Pharmacy Automation

## Summary

Automated storage, compounding, and dispensing systems for high-throughput, error-resistant medication workflows.

## Description

Robotic pharmacy systems manage tasks such as dose preparation, sterile compounding, inventory control, and unit-dose dispensing with high precision. By automating repetitive, high-risk steps in the medication-use process, they reduce dispensing errors, support 24/7 operation, and free pharmacists to focus on clinical activities like medication reconciliation and stewardship.
