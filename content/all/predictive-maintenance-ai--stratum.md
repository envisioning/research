# Predictive Maintenance AI

## Summary

ML models predicting equipment failure before it occurs.

## Description

Artificial intelligence algorithms analyze vast amounts of vibration, temperature, and telemetry data from heavy machinery. These models can predict component failures days or weeks in advance, allowing for scheduled maintenance interventions that prevent costly unplanned downtime.
