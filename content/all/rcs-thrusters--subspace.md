# RCS Thrusters

## Summary

Reaction-control thrusters for precision attitude and low-velocity translation.

## Description

Small impulse thrusters distributed around the hull provide fine control for docking, station-keeping, and precise maneuvers where warp/impulse systems are unsuitable. Integrated with inertial dampeners for crew safety.
