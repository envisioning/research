# Public-Interest AI Governance & Red-Teaming

## Summary

Safety processes for civic AI: audits, evaluations, and oversight.

## Description

Structured model governance practices: pre-deployment evaluation, bias testing, adversarial red-teaming, incident reporting, model cards, and continuous monitoring. Critical when AI is used in high-stakes civic contexts like case management, eligibility, or deliberation summarization.
