# Just Transition Frameworks

## Summary

Policies and programs supporting workers and communities leaving fossil fuels.

## Description

Just transition mechanisms provide retraining, economic diversification, and social protection for coal miners, oil workers, and fossil-dependent regions transitioning to clean energy, ensuring equity in the energy transformation.
