# AI Escalation Management Systems

## Summary

Safeguards against inadvertent conflict escalation.

## Description

Monitoring and control layers that track AI-enabled military and cyber operations in real time, flagging destabilizing patterns and enforcing human-in-the-loop constraints to avoid accidental escalation between major powers.
