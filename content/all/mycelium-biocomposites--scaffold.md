# Mycelium & Biobased Composites

## Summary

Fungi-based and agricultural-waste materials for insulation, panels, and temporary formwork.

## Description

Mycelium grows into molds to form lightweight, biodegradable composites suitable for non-structural applications like insulation, acoustic panels, and sacrificial formwork. Combined with hempcrete and bamboo fiber, these materials reduce embodied carbon and offer circular end-of-life pathways, with strong adoption in European and Asian green building movements.
