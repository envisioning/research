# Soldier Vitals Monitoring Systems

## Summary

Wearable sensor suites tracking physiology in real time.

## Description

Sensor-laden garments and chest rigs capture heart rate, hydration, ballistic impact, and geolocation, feeding medical telemetry to medics and commanders for rapid triage and performance management.
