# Cryonics Consent & Revival Frameworks

## Summary

Legal and ethical protocols for suspension, storage, and potential revival.

## Description

Emerging legal frameworks addressing consent for cryopreservation, rights upon potential revival (identity, property, citizenship), liability for preservation failures, and ethical boundaries for revival decisions, navigating the philosophical and practical challenges of suspended animation.
