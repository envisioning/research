# Attention Manipulation Safeguards

## Summary

Limits on persuasive design in spatial interfaces.

## Description

Technical and regulatory controls preventing exploitative use of proximity, saliency, and embodied psychology in XR. Includes limits on notification frequency, restrictions on attention-grabbing animations in peripheral vision, and mandatory disclosure of persuasive techniques used in spatial experiences.
