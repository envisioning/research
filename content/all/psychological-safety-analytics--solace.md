# Psychological Safety Analytics Platforms

## Summary

Enterprise tools that track signals of psychological safety and burnout risk in teams.

## Description

Applications that analyze aggregate communication patterns, anonymous surveys, and collaboration metadata to surface early indicators of fear, exclusion, or burnout. They provide leaders and teams with actionable, privacy-preserving insights and interventions to improve trust, inclusion, and emotional safety at work.
