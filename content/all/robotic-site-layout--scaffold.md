# Robotic Site Layout & Total Stations

## Summary

Robots and robotic total stations that mark points and lines directly from BIM models.

## Description

Layout robots translate digital plans into physical marks for anchors, walls, sleeves, and MEP penetrations. By linking to BIM and controlling survey instruments, they reduce rework from measurement errors and speed up the transition from design intent to field execution.
