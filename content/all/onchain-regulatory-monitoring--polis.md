# On-Chain Regulatory Monitoring

## Summary

Analytics and policy enforcement layers for blockchain-based economic activity.

## Description

Tools that analyze public ledgers and smart contracts to detect illicit flows, enforce sanctions, and monitor systemic risks in crypto and tokenized asset markets. They help regulators translate traditional oversight objectives into on-chain contexts.
