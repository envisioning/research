# Neuromarketing Oversight Boards

## Summary

Governance of brain-based persuasion techniques.

## Description

Multi-stakeholder governance bodies and technical auditing systems that review and approve the use of neurological insights, eye-tracking, and subconscious priming in commercial and political persuasion, establishing boundaries for ethical influence.
