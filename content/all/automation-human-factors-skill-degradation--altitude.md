# Automation Human Factors & Skill Degradation

## Summary

Managing workload, mode confusion, and manual flying proficiency over time.

## Description

Highly automated operations can reduce routine workload but increase complexity during off-nominal events. Key concerns include mode awareness, over-reliance, training to maintain manual skills, and designing automation that remains predictable and recoverable under stress.
