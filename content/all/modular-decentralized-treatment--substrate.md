# Modular Decentralized Treatment Plants

## Summary

Containerized, plug-and-play water and wastewater treatment systems.

## Description

Instead of relying solely on massive centralized plants, modular treatment units can be deployed at neighborhoods, campuses, or industrial sites. They treat water closer to where it is used, improving resilience, enabling reuse, and speeding deployment in rapidly growing or disaster-affected areas.
