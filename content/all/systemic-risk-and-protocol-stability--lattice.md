# Systemic Risk & Protocol-Scale Financial Stability

## Summary

Managing contagion and instability across interconnected protocols and chains.

## Description

Systemic risk and protocol-scale stability research investigates how failures in one protocol, asset, or chain propagate through highly interconnected financial graphs. It explores tools like circuit-breakers, backstops, and shared insurance pools for DeFi, and how these interact with traditional macroprudential policy, central banks, and lender-of-last-resort functions.
