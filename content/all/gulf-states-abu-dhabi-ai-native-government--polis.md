# Abu Dhabi AI-Native Government

## Summary

Abu Dhabi's Digital Strategy 2025-2027 deploys AED 13 billion ($3.5B) to become the world's first fully AI-native government across all digital services by 2027.

## Description

The Abu Dhabi Government Digital Strategy 2025-2027 represents the most ambitious government AI integration program globally. With AED 13 billion ($3.5 billion) allocated, the strategy aims to make Abu Dhabi the first government in the world where all digital services are AI-native — meaning AI is not an add-on but the foundational layer of every citizen interaction, from business licensing to healthcare appointments.

This initiative goes beyond chatbots and automation. It encompasses AI-driven policy simulation, predictive resource allocation, automated regulatory compliance, and personalized government services. The relatively small, wealthy population of Abu Dhabi (roughly 3 million) makes it an ideal testbed — manageable scale with sufficient resources to iterate quickly.

If successful, Abu Dhabi's model becomes exportable to other governments worldwide, particularly in the developing world where administrative capacity is limited. The strategic play is to establish Abu Dhabi as the reference implementation for AI-native governance, attracting consulting revenue, technology partnerships, and diplomatic influence.
