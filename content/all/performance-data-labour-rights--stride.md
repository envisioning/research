# Performance Data & Labour Rights

## Summary

Policies preventing misuse of athlete metrics in contracts and negotiations.

## Description

Legal and contractual frameworks that limit how granular performance and health data can be used in wage setting, roster decisions, and sponsorship deals, reducing the risk of data-driven discrimination and coercion.
