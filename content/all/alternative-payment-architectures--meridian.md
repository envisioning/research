# Alternative Payment Architectures

## Summary

Non-dollar settlement systems.

## Description

Software platforms enabling cross-border payments outside the dollar-SWIFT system, including digital currency bridges, commodity-backed settlements, and regional clearing systems that reduce exposure to Western financial sanctions.
