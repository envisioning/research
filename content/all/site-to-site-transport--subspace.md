# Site-to-Site Transport

## Summary

Direct transporter moves between two locations without pad staging.

## Description

Bypassing transporter rooms, the system calculates point-to-point solutions through complex annular confinement control and enhanced pattern buffering. Used for rapid extractions and emergency insertions through moderate interference.
