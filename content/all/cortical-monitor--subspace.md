# Cortical Monitor

## Summary

Device for continuous monitoring and support of brain activity in critical patients.

## Description

Cortical monitors provide real-time tracking of neural activity patterns, essential for patients with brain injuries or neurological conditions. The device can detect subtle changes in brain function and alert medical staff to developing problems before they become critical.

Advanced cortical monitors can provide limited life support to brain tissue, maintaining neural function even when other body systems are failing. They're often used in conjunction with other medical equipment during complex neurological procedures or when treating patients with severe head trauma.
