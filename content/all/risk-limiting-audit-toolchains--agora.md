# Risk-Limiting Audit (RLA) Toolchains

## Summary

Statistical audits that provide strong evidence election outcomes are correct.

## Description

Software that designs and manages RLAs (ballot-polling or comparison audits), computes stopping conditions, and produces transparent audit logs. RLAs can dramatically increase confidence in results while minimizing recount effort when outcomes are clear.
