# More-than-Human Governance Protocols

## Summary

Legal and technical frameworks for non-human representation in decision-making.

## Description

Mechanisms that grant voting rights or legal standing to non-human entities—such as ecosystems (rivers, forests) or future generations. These protocols often use AI agents or fiduciary guardians to represent these interests in legislative or board-level decisions, ensuring long-term ecological sustainability.
