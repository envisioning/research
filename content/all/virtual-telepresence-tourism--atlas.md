# Virtual & Telepresence Tourism

## Summary

Immersive remote visits to destinations via VR, 360° video, and telepresence robots.

## Description

Experiences that let users explore destinations, events, and natural sites remotely through high-fidelity VR, volumetric capture, or remotely controlled robots, expanding access while reducing environmental and crowding impacts.
