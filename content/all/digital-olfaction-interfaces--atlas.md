# Digital Olfaction Interfaces

## Summary

Transmission of scents to enhance virtual tourism and memory recall.

## Description

Digital scent synthesizers capable of recording and reproducing complex olfactory profiles of destinations (e.g., markets, forests), deepening immersion in virtual travel experiences and triggering potent emotional memory recall.
