# Brussels 19 Municipality Fragmentation

## Summary

The governance challenge of 19 separate municipalities within Brussels-Capital Region, creating coordination failures and permit inconsistencies.

## Description

Brussels-Capital Region comprises 19 separate municipalities (gemeenten/communes), each with its own mayor, council, and planning authority. This fragmentation creates coordination failures: different permit processes, competing development strategies, and inability to address region-wide housing needs coherently.

Attempts at regional coordination (like the Regional Development Plan) struggle against municipal autonomy. Developers face different rules in adjacent streets; social housing is unevenly distributed; and NIMBYism can push development to less resistant municipalities.

The signal represents extreme governance fragmentation creating systemic barriers. Key challenges include political resistance to municipal consolidation, finding coordination mechanisms that respect local democracy, and ensuring regional housing strategy can override local opposition.
