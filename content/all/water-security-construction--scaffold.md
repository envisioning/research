# Water Security & Construction Water Use

## Summary

Managing water consumption, dust suppression, and wastewater in water-scarce regions.

## Description

Construction is water-intensive (curing, dust control, worker welfare). In arid and drought-prone regions (Middle East, Western US, sub-Saharan Africa, Australia), water use competes with agriculture and drinking water. Emerging practices include greywater recycling, dry dust suppression, and water accounting integrated into project controls and environmental permits.
