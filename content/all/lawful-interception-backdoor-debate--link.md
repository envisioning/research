# Lawful Interception vs. Encryption Backdoor Debate

## Summary

Balancing government surveillance access with end-to-end encryption.

## Description

Technical and policy tensions between law enforcement demands for wiretapping capabilities in encrypted networks (5G, satellite, mesh) and privacy/security imperatives for end-to-end encryption. Solutions range from targeted exploits to key escrow, each with profound implications for human rights and security.
