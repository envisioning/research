# Flow State Optimization

## Summary

Systems that detect and sustain optimal performance and engagement.

## Description

Adaptive interfaces that monitor biosignals and behavioral patterns to identify flow states, then dynamically adjust challenge, feedback, and distractions to maintain the balance between skill and difficulty—applied in learning, creative work, and gaming.
