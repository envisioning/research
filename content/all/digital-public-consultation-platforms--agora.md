# Digital Public Consultation Platforms

## Summary

Structured channels for policy feedback at scale.

## Description

Platforms for collecting and organizing public comment on regulations, plans, and budget proposals, with features like topic tagging, duplicate detection, transparency dashboards, and accessibility-first interfaces. Can strengthen legitimacy by making listening visible and accountable.
