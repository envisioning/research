# Koopgarant / Koopstart (Shared Ownership)

## Summary

Schemes where buyers purchase a percentage of their home while housing associations retain the rest, enabling entry to ownership at lower income levels.

## Description

Koopgarant and Koopstart are Dutch shared-ownership schemes enabling households to purchase homes at below-market prices (typically 70-80% of value) while housing associations or municipalities retain the remaining equity. Upon resale, profits and losses are shared proportionally.

These models bridge the gap between social rental and full ownership, enabling middle-income households to build equity. They represent a 'third way' between rental and ownership that addresses affordability without requiring ongoing subsidies.

The signal represents innovative tenure models that generate social value while enabling wealth-building. Key challenges include complexity for buyers, housing association capacity to manage shared-ownership portfolios, and ensuring the discount genuinely reaches target groups.
