# Blended Care Orchestration Systems

## Summary

Platforms that coordinate human clinicians, coaches, and digital therapeutics into a single care journey.

## Description

Care orchestration systems that route individuals between self-guided digital tools, peer support, and professional care based on changing risk and need. They ensure continuity and context-sharing across modalities while maintaining strict privacy, improving outcomes for mental health, chronic conditions, and workplace wellbeing programs.
