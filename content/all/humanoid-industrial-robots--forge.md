# Humanoid Industrial Robots

## Summary

Bipedal robots with human-like form factors designed for factory environments.

## Description

Humanoid industrial robots from companies like Tesla (Optimus), Figure, Agility Robotics, and Unitree are purpose-built to work in spaces designed for humans without facility reconfiguration. With whole-body manipulation, navigation, and increasingly capable AI, they can perform diverse tasks from material handling to inspection, bridging the automation gap in legacy facilities and labor-constrained regions.
