# Cybersecurity Threats to Civil Society

## Summary

Rising cybersecurity threats targeting NGOs, activists, and civil society organizations requiring philanthropic response.

## Description

Civil society organizations, human rights defenders, and activists face growing cybersecurity threats—from state-sponsored hacking to surveillance to targeted disinformation campaigns. This signal captures the need for philanthropic investment in digital security capacity, secure communications, and protective infrastructure for civil society. It reflects how technology can be weaponized against social movements and raises questions about philanthropy's role in protecting the digital safety of grantees and the broader ecosystem.
