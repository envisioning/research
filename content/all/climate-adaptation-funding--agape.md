# Climate Adaptation Funding Surpassing Mitigation

## Summary

Climate adaptation funding surpassing mitigation, with disaster philanthropy becoming permanent rather than episodic.

## Description

As climate impacts intensify, philanthropic funding is shifting from prevention and mitigation toward adaptation and resilience. Disaster philanthropy, once episodic responses to specific events, is becoming a permanent feature of the landscape. This signal reflects the reality that some climate change is now locked in, requiring different strategies and approaches. It also highlights how philanthropy operates in contexts where systems are already failing or under extreme stress.
