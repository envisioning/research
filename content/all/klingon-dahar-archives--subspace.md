# Dahar Master Archives

## Summary

Data repositories chronicling legendary warrior achievements.

## Description

Sacred databases preserving the deeds of the Empire's greatest warriors, the Dahar Masters. Encoded in traditional Klingon script and protected by ritual passwords, these archives maintain the living history of Klingon martial excellence.
