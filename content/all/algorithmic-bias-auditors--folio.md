# Algorithmic Bias Auditors

## Summary

Automated systems to detect and mitigate prejudice in AI models.

## Description

Specialized software tools that continuously scan knowledge retrieval algorithms and datasets for diverse forms of bias. They ensure that future libraries serve all communities equitably, preventing the systemic erasure or misrepresentation of marginalized voices.
