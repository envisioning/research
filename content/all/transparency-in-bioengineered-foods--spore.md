# Transparency in Bioengineered Foods

## Summary

Clarity around engineered proteins, CRISPR-edited crops, and microbial ingredients.

## Description

Transparency frameworks are essential for building consumer trust in bioengineered foods. This includes clear labeling and communication regarding engineered proteins, CRISPR-edited crops, and microbial fermentation ingredients.
