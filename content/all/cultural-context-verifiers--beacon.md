# Cultural Context Verification Layers

## Summary

Preventing harmful decontextualization of content.

## Description

AI-assisted review systems that flag when media, text, or cultural artifacts are being used outside their original context in ways that could distort meaning, perpetuate stereotypes, or violate cultural protocols, particularly across language and geographic boundaries.
