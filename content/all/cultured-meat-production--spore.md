# Cultured Meat Production

## Summary

Bioreactor platforms scaling cultivated beef and poultry.

## Description

Cultured meat production platforms isolate animal stem cells, expand them in serum-free media, and grow them on edible scaffolds or microcarriers inside perfusion bioreactors that mimic muscle development. Inline sensors monitor metabolites and cell density, while cell-line banks ensure consistent genetics and regulatory traceability.

Companies such as Upside Foods, GOOD Meat, and Mosa Meat are scaling pilot plants to tens of thousands of liters, targeting hybrid products that blend cultivated fat with plant proteins to hit price points competitive with premium meat. Governments in Singapore, Israel, and the US are funding infrastructure and fast-tracking approvals to build strategic protein reserves.

Achieving cost parity hinges on cheaper growth factors, automated harvesting, and modular GMP facilities that can be replicated globally. Partnerships with biopharma manufacturers, renewable-powered media synthesis, and consumer education campaigns will be crucial for widespread acceptance.
