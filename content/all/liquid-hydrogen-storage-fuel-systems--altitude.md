# Liquid Hydrogen Storage & Fuel Systems

## Summary

Cryogenic tanks, insulation, and fuel handling for hydrogen-powered aircraft.

## Description

Hydrogen aviation depends on lightweight cryogenic tanks, boil-off management, leak detection, inerting, and safe ground operations. The biggest engineering challenges include volume (LH2 is bulky), thermal control over long missions, and certification of fault-tolerant fuel distribution architectures.
