# Generative AI Co-Pilot

## Summary

Lowering technical barriers through natural language interfaces and automated data engineering.

## Description

Generative AI for data and analytics acts as an analysis co-pilot for non-technical users, lowering technical barriers through natural language interfaces and automated data engineering. The trend shows sharp regional polarization: Asia and Pacific consistently rates generative AI as highly important (6.9), signaling strong momentum for AI-driven transformation, while Europe demonstrates regulatory caution with lower prioritization (5.3).

This reflects how regional contexts shape adoption speed and investment focus. Generative AI enables users to interact with data using natural language, automatically generate queries, create visualizations, and perform data preparation tasks that previously required technical expertise.

While innovation around AI is accelerating, organizations must balance the potential of generative AI with the foundational requirements of data quality and governance.
