# Metal Foam

## Summary

Porous metallic structures that combine low density with high surface area for thermal and structural applications

## Description

Metal foams combine low density with high surface area; applications include lightweight structures, thermal management, and space colonies or floating cities. Research and limited commercialization; challenges include cost and reproducibility.
