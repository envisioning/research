# Individual Room Investment Platforms

## Summary

Micro-investment platforms enabling fractional ownership of individual student housing units with rental guarantees.

## Description

Investment technology platforms that tokenize and enable fractional ownership of individual student rooms or housing units. These systems allow retail investors to purchase shares in specific rooms, track rental income, and benefit from university-backed rental guarantees. By lowering investment barriers and providing liquidity to an illiquid asset class, they democratize access to student housing investment while supporting the development of purpose-built student accommodation through innovative financing models.
