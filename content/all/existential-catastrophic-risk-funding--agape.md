# Existential & Catastrophic Risk Funding

## Summary

Growing philanthropic attention to existential risks including AI safety, nuclear threats, bioweapons, and civilizational collapse.

## Description

A growing segment of philanthropy focuses on existential and catastrophic risks that could cause civilizational collapse or human extinction—including advanced AI, nuclear war, engineered pandemics, and other threats. This signal captures the rise of longtermist philanthropy, debates about whether these risks deserve priority over present suffering, and questions about how to evaluate and compare radically different types of risk. The collapse of key longtermist funders has complicated but not ended this trend.
