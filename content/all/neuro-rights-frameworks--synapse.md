# Neuro-Rights Frameworks

## Summary

Technical and legal standards protecting mental privacy in the workplace.

## Description

As brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and attention monitoring enter the workplace, these frameworks define the boundaries of 'cognitive liberty.' They include technologies that block neural data harvesting and ensure employees retain ownership of their mental states and patterns.
