# Synthetic Companions

## Summary

AI-driven agents addressing loneliness and social isolation.

## Description

Emotionally responsive virtual beings that provide consistent companionship, conversation, and emotional support through text, voice, and embodied avatars—designed for older adults, isolated populations, and individuals with social anxiety or neurodivergence.
