# Synthetic Blood Substitutes

## Summary

Artificial blood with enhanced oxygen-carrying capacity and longevity properties.

## Description

Lab-created red blood cell analogues designed to carry more oxygen than natural cells and resist oxidative stress. These substitutes could be used to treat ischemic conditions, enhance physical endurance, and support organ preservation for transplant.
