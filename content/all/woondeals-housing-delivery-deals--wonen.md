# Woondeals (Housing Delivery Deals)

## Summary

National–regional delivery agreements in the Netherlands that bundle housing targets, infrastructure, and permitting commitments into enforceable packages.

## Description

Woondeals are negotiated agreements between the Dutch national government, provinces/metropolitan regions, and municipalities that set housing delivery targets, identify priority locations, and align infrastructure and permitting timelines. They are designed to turn national ambition into regional execution capacity.

They matter because they institutionalize cross-jurisdiction coordination: transport, utilities, land policy, and permitting are linked to a shared delivery plan. When functioning, woondeals reduce the ‘every municipality for itself’ dynamic and create accountability for both housing and enabling infrastructure.

The signal represents governance shifting from policy statements to delivery contracts. Key challenges include enforcement when parties miss targets, ensuring infrastructure actually arrives on time, and maintaining democratic legitimacy when deals override local resistance.
