# VR Palliative Therapy

## Summary

Immersive experiences for pain and anxiety management.

## Description

Virtual Reality applications designed for hospice patients to fulfill 'bucket list' wishes or provide calming environments. These therapies offer non-pharmacological relief from pain and existential distress at the end of life.
