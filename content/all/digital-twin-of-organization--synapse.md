# Digital Twin of Organization (DTO)

## Summary

Simulation models for predicting the impact of organizational changes.

## Description

Virtual replicas of an organization's processes, people, and resources. DTOs allow leaders to stress-test restructuring plans, simulate the flow of information, and identify bottlenecks in a risk-free virtual environment before implementation.
