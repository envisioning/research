# Indigenous Data Sovereignty & Community Networks

## Summary

Governance frameworks ensuring indigenous communities control their connectivity and data.

## Description

Legal and technical architectures (OCAP principles, CARE principles) that give First Nations, Aboriginal, and indigenous communities ownership over network infrastructure, data routing, and content. Addresses colonial legacy in telecom and enables self-determination in digital infrastructure.
