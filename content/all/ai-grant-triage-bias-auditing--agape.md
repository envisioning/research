# AI for Grant Triage & Bias Auditing

## Summary

AI used for grant triage, pattern detection, and bias auditing, as technology shapes decision-making processes.

## Description

Artificial intelligence is being deployed throughout philanthropic processes—from initial grant triage and application review to pattern detection across portfolios and bias auditing of decision-making. This signal reflects technology's role in reshaping how philanthropy operates at a procedural level. However, it raises critical questions about algorithmic transparency, whether AI reduces or reproduces bias, and how technology-mediated processes affect relationships between funders and grantees.
