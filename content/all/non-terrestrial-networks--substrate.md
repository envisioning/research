# Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN)

## Summary

Integrated satellite, aerial, and terrestrial networks for truly global coverage.

## Description

NTNs combine low-Earth-orbit constellations, HAPS platforms, and ground networks into a unified architecture. Devices can seamlessly roam between space-based and terrestrial links, enabling direct-to-device satellite services, resilient connectivity during disasters, and connectivity for remote infrastructure.
