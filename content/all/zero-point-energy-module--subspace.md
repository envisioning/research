# Zero-Point Energy Module

## Summary

Experimental system extracting power from quantum vacuum fluctuations in space-time.

## Description

Zero-point energy modules represent cutting-edge theoretical physics translated into practical technology. These devices extract energy from quantum vacuum fluctuations—the temporary appearance of particle-antiparticle pairs that emerge from and disappear back into the quantum foam of space-time itself.

The technology remains highly experimental and requires exotic materials and precise quantum field manipulation to function. When operational, zero-point modules can generate substantial power from seemingly empty space, though current implementations are limited by efficiency constraints and field stability issues. The potential applications range from compact power sources for away teams to emergency backup systems that require no fuel or conventional energy input.
