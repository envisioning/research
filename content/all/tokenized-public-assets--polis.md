# Tokenized Public Assets

## Summary

Blockchain representation of public infrastructure ownership.

## Description

Using blockchain tokens to represent fractional ownership or revenue rights in public infrastructure projects (like toll roads or solar farms). This enables new financing models where citizens can invest directly in local development.
