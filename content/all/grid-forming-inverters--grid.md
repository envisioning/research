# Grid-Forming Inverters

## Summary

Advanced power electronics that provide synthetic inertia and voltage control.

## Description

Grid-forming inverters enable renewable and inverter-based resources to provide system strength, frequency support, and black-start capabilities, reducing reliance on synchronous generators.
