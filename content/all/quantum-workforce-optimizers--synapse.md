# Quantum Workforce Optimizers

## Summary

Quantum computing systems solving intractable scheduling and resource allocation problems.

## Description

Quantum processors tackling combinatorial optimization challenges that classical computers cannot solve efficiently—such as shift scheduling for thousands of workers across multiple time zones, constraints, and preferences, or optimal team formation from vast skill-capability spaces. Early adopters are exploring hybrid classical-quantum workflows for logistics and large-scale coordination.
