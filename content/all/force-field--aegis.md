# Force Field

## Summary

Protective barriers using plasma or electromagnetic fields to deflect projectiles and radiation

## Description

Force fields would provide protective barriers against projectiles or radiation. Hypothetical; experiments with plasma windows exist for vacuum isolation. Applications: military, law enforcement, space travel. Significant physics challenges; practical realization not demonstrated.
