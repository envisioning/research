# Biometric Template Protection Systems

## Summary

Irreversible, privacy-preserving biometric verification.

## Description

Cryptographic techniques such as cancelable biometrics and homomorphic encryption that enable identity verification from facial, voice, or fingerprint data without storing or transmitting reversible biometric templates, preventing template leakage and identity theft.
