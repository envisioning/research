# Humanitarian & Disaster Relief Aviation

## Summary

Rapid-deploy cargo drones, airships, and eVTOL for crisis response.

## Description

Natural disasters, pandemics, and humanitarian crises demand fast logistics to difficult terrain. Autonomous cargo drones, hybrid airships, and eVTOL platforms can deliver medical supplies, food, and equipment without intact runways. Organizations like UNICEF and WFP are piloting drone corridors; scaling requires international regulatory harmonization and funding models.
