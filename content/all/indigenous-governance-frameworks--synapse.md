# Indigenous Governance Frameworks

## Summary

Organizational models based on long-term stewardship and kinship.

## Description

Formalized governance structures adapted from Indigenous knowledge systems, emphasizing multi-generational thinking, stewardship of land and resources, and consensus-based decision-making. These frameworks offer alternatives to extractive corporate hierarchies, particularly for regenerative and community-focused organizations.
