# Overtourism Mitigation Systems

## Summary

Dynamic visitor caps, pricing, and dispersion mechanisms to combat crowding.

## Description

Integrated policy and technology frameworks using real-time data, variable pricing, permitting systems, and multi-stakeholder governance to distribute tourist flows, protect fragile sites, and prevent gentrification and displacement of local communities.
