# Data Valuation & Monetization

## Summary

Measuring and maximizing data's economic worth through services and data ecosystems.

## Description

Data valuation and monetization represents a cultural shift toward assessment of data as a true business asset, measuring and maximizing data's economic worth through services and data ecosystems. This trend shows the largest performance gap of all trends: best-in-class companies rate its importance at 6.50, while laggards rate it at 3.36, meaning leaders prioritize this trend nearly twice as much as laggards.

Best-in-class companies emphasize data valuation and monetization to create scalable data products that deliver tangible business impact, turning innovation into action. The trend enables organizations to quantify the value of their data assets, identify monetization opportunities, and make informed investment decisions about data initiatives.

By treating data as a strategic asset with measurable ROI, organizations can justify data investments and unlock new revenue streams.
