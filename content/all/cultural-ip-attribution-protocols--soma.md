# Cultural IP & Attribution Protocols

## Summary

Standards to track and attribute cultural source material in generative systems.

## Description

Identity, licensing, and provenance layers that recognize communities, artists, and traditions as sources for generative culture, enabling consent, attribution, and benefit-sharing for derivative works.
