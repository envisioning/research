# Direct Air Capture (DAC) Integration

## Summary

Industrial systems removing CO₂ directly from the atmosphere powered by renewables.

## Description

Utilities are integrating DAC facilities with geothermal or waste heat sources to capture atmospheric carbon at scale, creating a new service model for carbon management and negative emissions.
