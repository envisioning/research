# Woonzorgvisie (Housing-Care Vision)

## Summary

Municipal strategies integrating housing planning with healthcare needs, particularly for aging populations and deinstitutionalization.

## Description

Woonzorgvisie are municipal strategic plans that integrate housing development with healthcare and social care needs. Required in Netherlands since 2022, they ensure housing planning considers aging populations, people with disabilities, and those deinstitutionalized from care facilities.

These visions coordinate housing associations, healthcare providers, and municipalities to plan suitable housing types and locations. They represent recognition that housing and care are inseparable for growing population segments.

The signal represents institutionalized integration of housing and care planning. Key challenges include actual implementation versus paper compliance, financing integrated housing-care models, and ensuring sufficient political priority for vulnerable groups.
