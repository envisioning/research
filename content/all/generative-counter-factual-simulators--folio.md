# Generative Counter-Factual Simulators

## Summary

AI engines for exploring historical 'what-if' scenarios.

## Description

Simulation platforms that use historical data and causal modeling to generate plausible alternative historical timelines. They allow researchers and students to explore the impact of specific events or decisions, fostering critical thinking about historical causality and contingency.
