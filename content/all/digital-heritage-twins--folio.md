# Digital Heritage Twins

## Summary

High-fidelity virtual replicas of physical sites and artifacts.

## Description

Creating exact, physics-compliant virtual models of fragile historical sites or rare manuscripts. These twins allow researchers and the public to interact with, manipulate, and study cultural heritage remotely without risking damage to the originals.
