# Huurcommissie (Rent Tribunals)

## Summary

Independent bodies adjudicating rent disputes and enforcing rent regulation, providing tenant protection and market oversight.

## Description

The Huurcommissie (Rent Tribunal) is an independent Dutch body that adjudicates disputes between tenants and landlords, particularly regarding rent levels, service costs, and maintenance. It enforces the point system (WWS) that determines maximum rents for regulated housing.

The tribunal provides accessible dispute resolution without court costs, enabling tenants to challenge excessive rents. With rent regulation expanding to mid-segment (Wet Betaalbare Huur), the Huurcommissie's role grows significantly.

The signal represents institutional infrastructure for tenant protection and market regulation. Key challenges include capacity to handle increased caseload, enforcement of rulings, and ensuring landlords don't exit the market rather than comply.
