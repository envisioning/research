# Care Economy Platforms

## Summary

Digital infrastructure valuing and coordinating reproductive and care work.

## Description

Platforms that formalize, coordinate, and compensate care work (childcare, eldercare, community maintenance) within the broader economy. These systems use tokenomics or time-banking to recognize this essential labor, integrating it into organizational benefits and societal value systems.
