# Global Catastrophic Risk Treaties

## Summary

International protocols for managing existential threats.

## Description

New diplomatic and legal frameworks designed to govern the development and deployment of high-risk technologies (such as autonomous weapons, geoengineering, and advanced biotechnology). These treaties aim to establish verification mechanisms and global consensus to prevent state or non-state actors from triggering civilizational collapse.
