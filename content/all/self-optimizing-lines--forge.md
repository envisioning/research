# Self-Optimizing Production Lines

## Summary

AI-driven manufacturing lines that autonomously adjust for maximum efficiency.

## Description

Leveraging reinforcement learning and real-time sensor data, these production lines continuously monitor their own performance. They detect micro-inefficiencies and autonomously adjust machine parameters, speed, and energy usage to optimize throughput and minimize waste without human intervention.
