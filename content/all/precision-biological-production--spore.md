# Precision Biological Production

## Summary

Fermentation-driven proteins and engineered fungi for food and biomaterials.

## Description

Precision biological production uses fermentation tanks and engineered microbes (yeasts, fungi, bacteria) to produce animal-identical proteins, lipids, and specialty fats without livestock or large cropland footprints. Software-defined recipes, perfusion bioreactors, and down-stream purification deliver casein, whey, lactoferrin, collagen, or cocoa butter alternatives at commercial purity.

CPG companies, bakeries, and biomaterials manufacturers source these ingredients to reduce reliance on volatile commodity supply chains while meeting sustainability goals. Firms like Perfect Day, Eden Brew, and MycoWorks integrate precision fermentation with contract manufacturing networks, enabling rapid SKU launches without building new farms.

Future facilities will run on renewable energy, recycle media components, and integrate CO₂ capture to further lower emissions intensity. Barriers include scaling GMP-grade production, consumer labeling debates, and the cost of growth media. Regulatory harmonization and toll manufacturing marketplaces will be key to reaching price parity with conventional inputs.
