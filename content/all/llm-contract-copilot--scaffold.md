# Contract/RFI/Submittal Copilots (LLMs)

## Summary

Language models that accelerate document review, spec lookup, and change-order rationale drafting.

## Description

Construction management is document-heavy: contracts, specs, RFIs, submittals, and meeting minutes. LLM copilots can summarize, classify, and draft responses, but must be paired with strong retrieval, audit trails, and human review to avoid hallucinated interpretations with real contractual consequences.
