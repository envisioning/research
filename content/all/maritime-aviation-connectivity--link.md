# Maritime & Aviation Connectivity

## Summary

High-throughput connectivity for ships, aircraft, and offshore platforms.

## Description

Multi-orbit satellite systems (GEO, MEO, LEO) combined with air-to-ground networks and maritime VSAT to provide broadband for passengers, crew welfare, operational telemetry, and autonomous navigation. Critical for digital transformation of global shipping, aviation, and offshore energy industries.
