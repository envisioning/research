# Digital Thread Knowledge Graphs

## Summary

Unified data models linking design, production, and operations.

## Description

Semantically rich graphs that connect CAD, simulation, MES, ERP, and field data into a continuous digital thread for traceability, root-cause analysis, and regulatory reporting.
