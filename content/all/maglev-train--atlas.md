# Maglev Train

## Summary

Magnetic levitation for high-speed rail; Transrapid, Shanghai Maglev.

## Description

Maglev trains use magnetic levitation for high-speed ground transport. Research, early commercialization, diffusion; Transrapid, Shanghai Maglev, Linimo. Applications: faster trains. Commercial deployment in China; limited elsewhere due to cost.
