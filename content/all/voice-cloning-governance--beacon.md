# Voice Cloning Governance Systems

## Summary

Detection and regulation of synthetic voice use.

## Description

Multi-layered infrastructure combining real-time voice deepfake detection, speaker verification, and consent registries to prevent unauthorized voice cloning for fraud, political manipulation, or reputational harm, with special protections for high-risk public figures.
