# Spatial Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Governance of who owns and stewards spatial experience data.

## Description

Principles, standards, and infrastructure defining ownership and control over maps, anchors, and behavioral traces from spatial devices. This includes models where communities or public institutions steward core layers of reality data instead of private platforms alone.
