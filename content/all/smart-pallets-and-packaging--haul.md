# Smart Pallets and Connected Packaging

## Summary

Instrumented pallets and packaging with embedded sensors and IDs for granular asset tracking.

## Description

Smart pallets and packaging embed RFID, BLE, and low-cost sensor tags directly into load carriers and containers. They enable item-level visibility, automated inventory counting, and condition monitoring in warehouses and across multimodal transport, feeding richer data into supply chain intelligence platforms.
