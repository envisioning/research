# Controlled Environment Agriculture

## Summary

Greenhouses with AI climate control and CO₂ enrichment.

## Description

CEA operators combine glasshouse automation, dynamic shading, and CO₂ dosing to deliver year-round leafy greens and berries near urban centers with drastically lower water usage.
