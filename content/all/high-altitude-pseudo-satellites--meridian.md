# High-Altitude Pseudo-Satellites (HAPS)

## Summary

Stratospheric platforms for persistent coverage.

## Description

Long-endurance, high-altitude platforms that provide satellite-like communications and sensing at lower cost and with greater flexibility, bolstering resilience of strategic communications and intelligence networks.
