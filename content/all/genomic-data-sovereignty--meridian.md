# Genomic Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Protecting national genetic databases.

## Description

Technical and regulatory frameworks that ensure population-level genomic data remains under national control, preventing foreign exploitation for bioweapons development, targeted pharmaceuticals, or eugenic insights while enabling domestic research.
