# Geoengineering Governance Regimes

## Summary

Global oversight for solar radiation and carbon removal projects.

## Description

Multilateral treaties establishing decision-making processes, liability frameworks, and monitoring requirements for large-scale climate interventions. They address questions of consent, regional impacts, and termination shock, ensuring geoengineering cannot be deployed unilaterally by states or private actors without transparent global deliberation.
