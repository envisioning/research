# Patient Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Systems giving patients granular control over who accesses their health data.

## Description

Moving away from institutional data silos, data sovereignty frameworks (often leveraging decentralized identity or solid pods) empower patients to own their health record. They can grant temporary, granular access to specific providers or researchers and revoke it at any time, ensuring privacy and building trust in the digital health ecosystem.
