# Liquid Democracy Interfaces

## Summary

Dynamic delegation of voting power to trusted proxies.

## Description

Voting interfaces that allow citizens to vote directly on issues they understand or delegate their vote to a trusted expert or representative for specific topics (e.g., delegating 'environmental' votes to a climate scientist). Delegations can be withdrawn at any time.
