# Biometric Sensors & Liveness Detection

## Summary

Hardware sensors with anti-spoofing capabilities for fingerprint, face, and iris authentication.

## Description

Advanced biometric sensors integrate liveness detection mechanisms to prevent presentation attacks using photos, masks, or synthetic media. Combining multi-spectral imaging, 3D depth sensing, thermal detection, and pulse measurement, these sensors ensure that captured biometric data originates from a living person. Critical for high-assurance identity verification in banking, border control, and device authentication.
