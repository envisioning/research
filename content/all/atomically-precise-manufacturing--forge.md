# Atomically Precise Manufacturing

## Summary

Fabrication of products with atomic-level accuracy.

## Description

Moving beyond nanotech, APM aims to position individual atoms to build macro-scale products with zero defects. This would create materials with theoretical maximum strength-to-weight ratios and perfect conductivity, revolutionizing aerospace, electronics, and energy sectors.
