# Additive Manufacturing for Certified Flight Hardware

## Summary

3D-printed metal parts for lighter, consolidated, and repairable systems.

## Description

Additive manufacturing enables topology-optimized brackets, ducting, fuel nozzles, and heat exchangers with fewer joints and less mass. The aviation bottleneck is process qualification, nondestructive inspection, and ensuring repeatable properties across build lots and suppliers.
