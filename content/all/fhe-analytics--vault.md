# Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)

## Summary

Computation on encrypted data without decryption.

## Description

Cryptographic breakthrough allowing banks to outsource data analysis, risk modeling, and AI training to third-party clouds while keeping the underlying customer data fully encrypted and mathematically private at all times.
