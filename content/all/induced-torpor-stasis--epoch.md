# Induced Torpor Stasis

## Summary

Controlled metabolic suppression to reduce aging rates during critical periods or long-duration travel.

## Description

Pharmacological and physical induction of a hibernation-like state in humans. By drastically lowering metabolic rate and body temperature, this technology pauses cellular damage accumulation. While primarily researched for space travel and trauma care, it represents the ultimate 'pause button' for biological aging.
