# Climate Adaptation and Disaster Resilience Planning

## Summary

Frameworks integrating climate models with supply chain design to ensure long-term viability.

## Description

These systems use climate projections (sea level rise, extreme weather frequency, temperature shifts) to assess long-term risks to facilities, routes, and suppliers. They recommend network reconfiguration, infrastructure hardening, and sourcing diversification to maintain operations through climate change.
