# Global Foundations vs. Local Sovereignty

## Summary

Tensions between global foundations and local sovereignty, as international philanthropy navigates power dynamics and cultural contexts.

## Description

Large global foundations operate across borders, bringing resources and influence that can reshape local priorities and power structures. This signal captures the growing tension between international philanthropic actors and local sovereignty—questions about who sets agendas, whose knowledge counts, and whether external funding strengthens or undermines local institutions. As nationalism rises and trust in global institutions declines, these tensions are becoming more visible and contested.
