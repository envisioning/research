# Terahertz (THz) Antennas

## Summary

Ultra-high frequency antennas enabling Terabit-per-second speeds.

## Description

Antenna systems designed to operate in the 0.1–10 THz frequency range. Essential for 6G, these antennas enable ultra-high bandwidth and extreme data rates but face challenges with propagation loss, requiring novel materials and beamforming techniques.
