# Real-Time Tailings Dam Monitoring

## Summary

Sensor networks and analytics continuously assessing tailings facility stability.

## Description

Real-time tailings monitoring systems deploy piezometers, inclinometers, drones, and satellite-based InSAR, feeding data into predictive stability models. They provide early warnings of anomalous movement or pore pressure changes, enabling operators and regulators to intervene before catastrophic failures occur.
