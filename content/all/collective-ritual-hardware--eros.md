# Collective Ritual Hardware

## Summary

Synchronized devices for group ceremonies, prayer, and communal bonding.

## Description

Networked candles, bells, drums, or haptic arrays that connect participants across distances for religious ceremonies, memorial rituals, or community gatherings. They enable distributed congregations and diaspora communities to maintain embodied collective practices.
