# Athlete Data Fusion Platforms

## Summary

Unified data layers integrating tracking, wearables, labs, and medical records.

## Description

Cloud-native platforms that harmonize data from GPS units, force plates, wearables, lab tests, and EMRs into a single longitudinal profile. They provide standardized APIs and dashboards to support cross-disciplinary performance, medical, and front-office decision making.
