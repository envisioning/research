# Digital Twin Mobility

## Summary

Real-time virtual replicas of urban transportation systems.

## Description

Comprehensive digital models of a city's physical transportation infrastructure and real-time traffic flows. These twins allow planners and algorithms to simulate scenarios, optimize traffic light timing, and manage congestion proactively across multiple modes.
