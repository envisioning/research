# Vision-Language-Action Robots

## Summary

Industrial robots powered by foundation models that understand language, vision, and action jointly.

## Description

Vision-language-action models allow robots to interpret natural language instructions, reason over visual scenes, and generate low-level motion plans. In factories, they enable rapid task reprogramming ('pick all blue parts from bin A and assemble on fixture 3') and robust handling of novel parts or layouts without hand-coded logic.
