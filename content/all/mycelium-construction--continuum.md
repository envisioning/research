# Mycelium-Based Construction

## Summary

Living building materials grown from fungal networks.

## Description

Bioengineered mycelium composites that grow into bricks, insulation, and structural panels within days. Carbon-negative, compostable, and self-repairing, these materials offer a regenerative alternative to concrete and steel, reducing construction's climate footprint while creating resilient, adaptive buildings.
