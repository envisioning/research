# Mixed-Income Housing Requirements

## Summary

Regulatory requirements or incentives for developments to include affordable housing alongside market-rate units, generating social value.

## Description

Mixed-income housing requirements mandate or incentivize developments to include affordable housing units alongside market-rate housing. These requirements can take the form of inclusionary zoning, affordable housing quotas, or density bonuses for affordable units.

In Benelux, mixed-income requirements can address both housing shortages and community concerns about exclusive development. Examples include Dutch woningbouwplafond (housing construction ceiling) with affordable quotas, Belgian social housing requirements, and Luxembourg affordable housing mandates.

The signal represents a model that generates social value while enabling development. Key challenges include ensuring affordability is meaningful, maintaining quality across income levels, and balancing requirements with developer viability.
