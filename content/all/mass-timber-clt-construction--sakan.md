# Mass Timber & Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT)

## Summary

Engineered wood products enabling timber construction at scale, offering carbon sequestration and aesthetic differentiation for sustainable developments.

## Description

Mass timber and Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT) are engineered wood products enabling large-scale timber construction previously possible only with concrete and steel. These materials sequester carbon, offer aesthetic warmth, and can achieve fire and structural performance matching traditional materials.

In GCC, mass timber is emerging in tourism, hospitality, and premium residential projects seeking sustainability credentials and design differentiation. NEOM and Red Sea Project explore timber for eco-resorts, while Dubai hospitality developments incorporate CLT for boutique properties. The material offers rapid assembly (similar to modular) and lower embodied carbon.

The signal represents diversification of construction materials beyond concrete dominance, though GCC adoption faces challenges including humidity management, fire code adaptation, and supply chain development for imported timber. The technology appeals to projects prioritizing sustainability storytelling and biophilic design.
