# Human-centric Lighting (HCL)

## Summary

Environments designed to improve sleep, productivity, recovery, and mental health.

## Description

Lighting applications focused on the non-visual effects of light on human health. HCL systems dynamically adjust intensity and spectrum to support the circadian rhythm, boosting alertness during the day and promoting melatonin secretion for better sleep at night, used in offices, hospitals, and schools.
