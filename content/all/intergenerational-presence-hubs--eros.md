# Intergenerational Presence Hubs

## Summary

Connected spaces that weave elders, adults, and youth into ongoing contact.

## Description

Large-screen portals, dedicated tablets, and lightweight apps that organize recurring calls, story sessions, and caregiving updates across extended families. They blend asynchronous updates with live contact to reduce isolation in elder populations.
