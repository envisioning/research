# Participatory AI Governance Mechanisms

## Summary

Mechanisms that give affected communities a direct role in shaping AI systems and policies.

## Description

Toolkits and platforms for citizen assemblies, community juries, and stakeholder councils that review and influence how AI systems are designed and deployed in workplaces, schools, and cities. They ensure that psychological safety, dignity, and distributive justice are central criteria in AI governance decisions.
