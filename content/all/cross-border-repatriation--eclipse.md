# Cross-Border Repatriation Systems

## Summary

Coordination platforms for international death logistics.

## Description

Digital systems streamlining the complex process of repatriating remains across borders—automating consulate notifications, death certificate translations, customs clearances, and airline cargo bookings—critical for the 400,000+ annual deaths of travelers, migrants, and expatriates.
