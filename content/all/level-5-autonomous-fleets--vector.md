# Level 5 Autonomous Fleets

## Summary

Fully autonomous robotaxi networks operating without human intervention.

## Description

Large-scale deployment of Level 5 autonomous vehicle fleets in urban environments, functioning as on-demand robotaxis. These systems utilize advanced sensor fusion, AI planning, and V2X communication to navigate complex city traffic safely and efficiently, significantly reducing the cost of personal mobility.
