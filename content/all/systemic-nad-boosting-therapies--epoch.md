# Systemic NAD+ Boosting Therapies

## Summary

Interventions that restore youthful levels of NAD+, a crucial metabolic cofactor that declines with age.

## Description

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) is essential for mitochondrial function, DNA repair, and sirtuin activation. These therapies use precursors like NMN or NR, CD38 inhibitors, or direct NAD+ delivery to reverse age-related NAD+ decline, improving energy metabolism, genomic stability, and cellular stress resistance across multiple tissues.
