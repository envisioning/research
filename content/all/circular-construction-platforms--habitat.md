# Circular Construction Platforms

## Summary

Digital marketplaces and material passports enabling deconstruction and reuse.

## Description

Platforms that track building materials from manufacturing through multiple lifecycles, using digital passports to document composition, condition, and location. Integrated with deconstruction planning tools and secondary material exchanges, they transform demolition waste into valuable inventory, reducing embodied carbon and raw material demand while creating circular supply chains.
