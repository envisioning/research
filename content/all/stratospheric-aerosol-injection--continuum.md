# Stratospheric Aerosol Injection

## Summary

High-altitude platforms for solar radiation management.

## Description

Specialized aircraft or tethered balloons designed to disperse reflective particles (sulfates, calcium carbonate) into the stratosphere. This hardware forms the backbone of solar geoengineering efforts to reflect a small percentage of sunlight back into space, potentially cooling the planet to mitigate the worst effects of global warming while longer-term decarbonization takes effect.
