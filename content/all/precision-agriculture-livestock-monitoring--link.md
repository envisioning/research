# Precision Agriculture & Livestock Monitoring

## Summary

IoT-enabled farm optimization for yield, water, and animal welfare.

## Description

Networks of soil moisture sensors, drone imagery, autonomous tractors, and livestock wearables that optimize irrigation, fertilization, pest control, and animal health. Combinations of LoRa, NB-IoT, and satellite connectivity enable data-driven farming in remote areas, crucial for food security.
