# Digital Human Animation Systems

## Summary

Real-time pipelines for lifelike virtual actors.

## Description

Toolchains that combine motion capture, facial tracking, and generative animation to drive highly realistic digital humans for virtual hosts, influencers, and characters in interactive narratives and live broadcasts.
