# Spatial Privacy Zones

## Summary

Digital no-fly areas for sensing and overlays.

## Description

Policy and protocol layers that define zones where capture, analysis, or augmentation are limited or prohibited. Devices respect machine-readable boundaries—for example, around bathrooms, clinics, or private homes—to prevent unwanted recording or targeting.
