# Self-Healing Materials

## Summary

Concrete and composites that autonomously repair cracks and extend service life.

## Description

Advanced construction materials embedded with bacteria, microcapsules, or vascular networks that trigger repair mechanisms when damage occurs. Self-healing concrete releases limestone-producing bacteria when cracked, while polymer composites use microencapsulated healing agents. These materials dramatically reduce maintenance costs and extend infrastructure lifespan in aggressive environments.
