# Synthetic DNA Data Storage

## Summary

Archival storage of digital legacies in synthesized DNA strands.

## Description

High-density data storage technology that encodes vast digital archives—photos, videos, and life logs—into synthetic DNA molecules. This offers an ultra-compact, energy-efficient, and durable medium capable of preserving a person's digital essence for thousands of years without degradation.
