# Sensory Overload Protection

## Summary

Safety protocols limiting haptic and visual intensity.

## Description

Health and safety standards built into XR hardware and software. These systems monitor cumulative sensory input (flashing lights, loud sounds, intense haptics) and automatically dampen feedback to prevent motion sickness, seizures, or psychological trauma from hyper-realistic experiences.
