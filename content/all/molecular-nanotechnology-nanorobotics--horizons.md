# Molecular Nanotechnology / Nanorobotics

## Summary

Hypothetical machines that manipulate matter at molecular scale.

## Description

Molecular nanotechnology envisions machines that manipulate matter at atomic or molecular scale—molecular assemblers, nanorobots for medicine or manufacturing. Hypothetical; experiments with molecular machines exist. Applications: machines that can make anything; cheap planetary terraforming. Significant technical and feasibility debates.
