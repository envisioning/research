# Institutional Build-to-Rent (Pension Fund Capital)

## Summary

Large-scale long-term capital funding new rental supply—often essential for delivery, but politically contested as ‘investor housing’.

## Description

Institutional build-to-rent (BTR) involves pension funds and insurers financing and holding rental portfolios long-term. In the Netherlands especially, this capital has been central to delivering mid-rent and new urban supply, but it is also increasingly politicized amid affordability crises.

When regulation changes rapidly (rent caps, taxation, purchase restrictions), institutional investors can pause or exit, immediately affecting pipeline delivery.

The signal represents the delicate balance between needing scalable capital for supply and maintaining legitimacy by ensuring developments generate social value rather than extracting it. Key challenges include stable policy frameworks, clear affordability expectations, and governance structures that align investor returns with public outcomes.
