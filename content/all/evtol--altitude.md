# eVTOL

## Summary

Electric vertical takeoff and landing aircraft; low emission, low noise air taxis.

## Description

eVTOL (electric vertical takeoff and landing) aircraft use electric or hybrid-electric propulsion for urban air mobility. Research and development; Joby, Archer, Lilium, and others. Applications: air taxis, low-emission short-haul transport. Challenges include certification, infrastructure, and economics.
