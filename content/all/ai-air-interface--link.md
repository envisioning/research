# AI-Native Air Interface

## Summary

Deep learning replacing traditional signal processing blocks.

## Description

A fundamental shift where the entire physical layer of communication (encoding, modulation, channel estimation) is designed and optimized by AI models rather than human-designed mathematical algorithms. This allows the network to adapt to complex environments in ways traditional methods cannot.
