# Boundary Intelligence Systems

## Summary

Software that helps negotiate availability, attention, and digital boundaries.

## Description

Cross-platform services that manage expectations around messaging responsiveness, do-not-disturb windows, and shared calendars. They negotiate preferences across multiple parties to prevent misaligned expectations and burnout in modern, always-on relationships.
