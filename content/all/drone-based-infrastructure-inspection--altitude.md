# Drone-Based Airport & Infrastructure Inspection

## Summary

UAS inspection for runways, lighting, bridges, and aircraft exterior checks.

## Description

Drones with photogrammetry and thermal sensors can speed up inspections, reduce closures, and improve defect detection. Adoption depends on BVLOS permissions, secure operations near critical infrastructure, and integration into maintenance workflows.
