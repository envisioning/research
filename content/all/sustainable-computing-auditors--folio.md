# Sustainable Computing Auditors

## Summary

Carbon footprint tracking for digital infrastructure.

## Description

Tools that measure and report the energy consumption, carbon emissions, and e-waste generated by storage, computation, and network operations. They enable libraries to set decarbonization targets, choose greener providers, and justify investment in energy-efficient technologies.
