# Emotional Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Governance models giving users ownership over their affective data.

## Description

Technical and legal architectures that treat emotional and biometric data as a distinct, highly protected class of personal property. These systems enable users to grant granular, revocable access to their 'emotional telemetry' only to trusted services.
