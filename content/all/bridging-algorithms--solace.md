# Pro-Social 'Bridging' Algorithms

## Summary

Content ranking systems optimized to connect diverse social groups.

## Description

Recommendation engines explicitly designed to counter polarization by identifying and promoting content that 'bridges' divides between opposing social or ideological clusters. Unlike standard engagement algorithms, these optimize for 'diverse approval' and constructive disagreement.
