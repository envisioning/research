# Housing as a Service (HaaS) Platforms

## Summary

Subscription-based housing models with integrated services, amenities, and flexible contracts managed through digital platforms.

## Description

Housing as a Service (HaaS) platforms offer subscription-based housing models with integrated services, amenities, and flexible contracts managed through digital platforms. These systems combine property management, service delivery, and community features into unified platforms accessible via mobile apps.

In GCC, HaaS platforms emerge in coliving spaces, serviced apartments, and institutional rental developments. Dubai and Abu Dhabi see growing adoption in expatriate-focused developments, while Saudi Arabia explores HaaS models for young professionals and Vision 2030 housing programs. The technology enables flexible housing arrangements with integrated services, reducing friction for residents and enabling new business models for operators.

The signal represents the transformation of housing from asset ownership to service subscription, creating new models that combine flexibility, convenience, and integrated services. Key features include app-based management, flexible contracts, integrated amenities booking, and community features. Challenges include regulatory frameworks, ensuring service quality, and building sustainable business models.
