# Real-Time Cultural Translation AI

## Summary

AI systems that bridge linguistic and cultural communication gaps in global teams.

## Description

Advanced natural language models that provide not just translation but cultural contextualization—explaining idioms, power dynamics, implicit norms, and communication styles across cultures. These systems mediate meetings, documents, and async communication to reduce misunderstandings in globally distributed organizations.
