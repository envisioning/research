# Networked & Temporary Philanthropic Entities

## Summary

Networked, temporary, or pop-up philanthropic entities, with foundations acting as conveners rather than funders.

## Description

New organizational forms are emerging that challenge the traditional foundation model. Networked entities coordinate action across multiple organizations without central control. Temporary or pop-up entities form around specific issues and dissolve when their work is done. Foundations increasingly act as conveners and facilitators rather than direct funders. This signal reflects a shift toward more flexible, responsive, and movement-aligned forms of philanthropic organization.
