# Perovskite LEDs (PeLEDs)

## Summary

Next-generation solution-processable emitters with exceptional color purity and tunability.

## Description

Perovskite Light-Emitting Diodes (PeLEDs) use metal-halide perovskite materials with high photoluminescence efficiency. They promise low-cost fabrication, vivid narrowband emission, and new form factors. Key research frontiers include operational stability, encapsulation, and scalable manufacturing for general illumination.
