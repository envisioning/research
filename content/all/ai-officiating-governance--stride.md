# AI Officiating Governance

## Summary

Standards for transparency, appeal, and accountability in automated calls.

## Description

Governance frameworks ensuring computer-vision and rule-enforcement systems in sport are explainable, auditable, and subject to human review. They define thresholds for overturning calls, logging requirements, and safeguards against vendor lock-in and hidden bias.
