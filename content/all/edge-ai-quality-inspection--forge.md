# Edge AI Quality Inspection

## Summary

On-line computer vision systems running at the edge for real-time defect detection.

## Description

Edge AI quality inspection combines high-speed cameras, industrial lighting, and accelerator hardware deployed directly on production lines. Foundation models and unsupervised anomaly detection continuously learn what 'normal' looks like, flagging micro-defects, misalignments, and surface anomalies that are invisible to human inspectors while meeting strict cycle-time constraints.
