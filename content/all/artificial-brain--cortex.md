# Artificial Brain

## Summary

Computational models of brain structure and function.

## Description

Artificial brain research builds computational models of neural circuits (Blue Brain Project, Human Brain Project). Applications: treatment of neurological disease, artificial intelligence. Research-stage; full brain simulation not yet achieved.
