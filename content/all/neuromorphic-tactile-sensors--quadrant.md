# Neuromorphic Tactile Sensors

## Summary

Event-based skin enabling robots to feel texture and slippage.

## Description

Biomimetic sensors (e.g., NeuroTac) that transduce contact information into spike trains, allowing robots to manipulate delicate objects with human-like dexterity.
