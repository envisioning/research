# Land Use & Climate Justice

## Summary

Allocation of land between biomass production, carbon farming, and restoration.

## Description

Land use and climate justice address the ethical allocation of high-quality land. Balancing the needs for biomass production, carbon farming, and biodiversity restoration is critical for a just transition to a sustainable bioeconomy.
