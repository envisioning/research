# Cross-Sport Talent Identification

## Summary

Systems mapping physical and cognitive profiles to optimal sports pathways.

## Description

Analytics that compare an athlete’s anthropometrics, movement signatures, and cognitive traits against large performance databases. They flag potential fit in alternative sports or positions, enabling earlier and more equitable talent discovery.
