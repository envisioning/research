# Circular Reverse Logistics Networks

## Summary

Optimized systems for product returns, refurbishment, remanufacturing, and material recovery.

## Description

These applications integrate consumer returns, warranty repairs, end-of-life take-back, and recycling into unified reverse networks. They use AI to route returns to optimal destinations (resale, refurb, parts harvesting, recycling) and match recovered materials with manufacturing demand, closing the loop on product lifecycles.
