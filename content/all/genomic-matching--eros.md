# Genomic Compatibility Algorithms

## Summary

Matchmaking systems utilizing biological data for partner prediction.

## Description

Algorithms that incorporate genetic markers (such as MHC complex data) alongside psychological profiles to predict biological compatibility and long-term relationship stability, moving beyond purely social or interest-based matching.
