# Blockchain Death Registry

## Summary

Immutable, global ledgers for death certificates and inheritance.

## Description

Distributed ledger systems that record death certificates, wills, and inheritance claims with cryptographic verification, enabling instant global verification, preventing fraud, and streamlining cross-border estate settlement while preserving privacy through zero-knowledge proofs.
