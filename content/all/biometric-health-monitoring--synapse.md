# Biometric Health Monitoring

## Summary

Wearables and sensors for tracking workforce physiological wellbeing.

## Description

Privacy-first wearables and furniture sensors that monitor biomarkers like heart rate variability and posture to detect burnout, stress, and fatigue in real-time. These systems enable proactive organizational health interventions and adaptive work scheduling based on physiological readiness.
