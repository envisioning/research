# Argument Mining & Deliberation Summarization

## Summary

Tools that extract claims, reasons, and consensus from public input.

## Description

NLP systems that map public comments and deliberation transcripts into structured arguments, detect duplicate themes, and surface minority viewpoints. When governed carefully, they help institutions scale listening without collapsing nuance or excluding less ‘legible’ voices.
