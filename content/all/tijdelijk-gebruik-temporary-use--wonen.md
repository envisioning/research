# Tijdelijk Gebruik (Temporary Use Agreements)

## Summary

Legal frameworks enabling interim use of vacant land or buildings for housing, activating dormant assets while permanent plans develop.

## Description

Tijdelijk gebruik (temporary use) frameworks enable interim activation of vacant buildings or land for housing while permanent development plans are finalized. These arrangements, typically 5-15 years, can include temporary housing, meanwhile uses, or experimental projects.

In Benelux, temporary use is increasingly formalized through specific permit categories and agreements between municipalities and property owners. It addresses both housing shortages and the social costs of vacancy.

The signal represents a shift from binary (developed/undeveloped) to continuous use of urban assets. Key challenges include tenant security (knowing housing is temporary), maintaining property during interim periods, and ensuring temporary use doesn't become an excuse to delay permanent solutions.
