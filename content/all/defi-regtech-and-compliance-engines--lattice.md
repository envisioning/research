# RegTech & Compliance Engines for DeFi

## Summary

Policy compilers turning regulations into executable protocol rules.

## Description

RegTech and compliance engines for DeFi are systems that translate jurisdictional regulatory rules—such as KYC/AML (know your customer/anti-money laundering), sanctions lists, and travel rule requirements (regulations requiring information sharing about transactions)—into executable policies that can be enforced at the protocol or wallet layer through smart contracts or software. They enable allow-listing (only allowing approved addresses), geo-fencing (restricting access based on location), and transaction pattern monitoring (detecting suspicious activity) while attempting to preserve composability (the ability to combine different protocols) and user privacy, creating a bridge between regulatory requirements and decentralized systems.

This innovation addresses the regulatory compliance challenge in DeFi, where decentralized systems need to meet regulatory requirements without centralization. By automating compliance, these systems can enable regulatory compliance while preserving DeFi's benefits. Companies, compliance providers, and research institutions are developing these technologies.

The technology is particularly significant for enabling DeFi to meet regulatory requirements, where compliance is necessary for mainstream adoption. As regulations evolve, compliance automation becomes increasingly important. However, balancing compliance with privacy, managing jurisdictional differences, and preserving decentralization remain challenges. The technology represents an important evolution in DeFi compliance, but requires careful design to balance competing requirements. Success could enable regulatory-compliant DeFi, but the technology must navigate complex trade-offs between compliance, privacy, and decentralization.
