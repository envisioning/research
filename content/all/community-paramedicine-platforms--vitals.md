# Community Paramedicine & Mobile Integrated Health

## Summary

Programs that redeploy EMS teams to deliver scheduled, preventive, and post-discharge care in the community.

## Description

Community paramedicine platforms coordinate specially trained paramedics to perform home visits for medication checks, wound care, chronic disease assessments, and post-discharge follow-ups. Supported by telehealth links to physicians and nurses, these models reduce unnecessary 911 calls, avoid ED utilization, and extend the reach of hospital systems into patients' homes.
