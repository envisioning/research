# Community Co-Design Platforms

## Summary

Digital platforms enabling residents to co-design public spaces, policies, and services.

## Description

Civic technology platforms that combine participatory mapping, deliberation tools, and mixed-reality visualizations so communities can collaboratively shape urban plans, mobility systems, and public services. They embed inclusive facilitation practices and safeguards for marginalized voices, aligning civic infrastructure with collective wellbeing.
