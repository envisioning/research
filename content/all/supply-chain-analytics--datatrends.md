# Supply Chain Analytics

## Summary

Optimizing supply chains using data analytics for demand forecasting, inventory management, logistics optimization, and risk mitigation.

## Description

Supply chain analytics uses data to optimize procurement, production, logistics, and distribution. Companies are implementing analytics to improve supply chain efficiency, reduce costs, and manage risks. Applications include demand forecasting, inventory optimization, route planning, supplier risk assessment, and real-time supply chain visibility.

Supply chain analytics is critical for companies operating in complex global markets. Companies analyze data from suppliers, production, logistics, and demand to optimize end-to-end supply chains. The technology helps manage complexity, reduce stockouts and overstock, optimize transportation, and respond to disruptions. E-commerce growth has increased demand for sophisticated logistics analytics.

At the Sustaining Performance to Advanced Performance stage, supply chain analytics is mature and widely deployed globally, with sophisticated systems in manufacturing, retail, and logistics. The technology continues to advance with better integration, real-time capabilities, and AI-powered optimization. Challenges include data quality, system integration, and managing global supply chain complexity.
