# Superalloy

## Summary

Heat-resistant alloys engineered for turbines and extreme-temperature applications

## Description

Superalloys are nickel-, cobalt-, or iron-based alloys with exceptional high-temperature strength and creep resistance. Research and diffusion; commercial use in jet engines and turbines. Research continues into higher-temperature variants and additive manufacturing.
