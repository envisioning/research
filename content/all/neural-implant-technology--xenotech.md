# Neural Implants

## Summary

Mysterious objects reportedly removed from alleged abductees, claimed as tracking or monitoring devices.

## Description

Alleged alien abductees report waking with scars, missing time, and mysterious objects under their skin. Physicians including Dr. Roger Leir performed surgical removals of alleged implants—small metallic or crystalline objects found in soft tissue without visible entry points.

## Composition and Properties

Analysis of removed objects shows exotic compositions: meteoritic iron, isotope ratios not matching Earth, and materials that shouldn't exist in biological tissue. Objects allegedly emit radio frequencies or respond to electromagnetic fields. Some surround themselves with biofilm preventing rejection.

## Critical Assessment

Skeptical analysis suggests objects are mundane (glass shards, naturally occurring biological calcifications) with misattributed properties. Isotope anomalies remain unexplained but don't prove extraterrestrial origin. No devices show clear electronic functionality. The implants represent physical evidence requiring explanation—whether exotic technology, biological oddities, or misidentified conventional objects remains debated. They fuel both serious investigation and abduction mythology.
