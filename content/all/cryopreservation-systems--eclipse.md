# Cryopreservation Systems

## Summary

Whole-body and neuropreservation suspension infrastructure.

## Description

Specialized medical-grade equipment for cooling, perfusing, and storing bodies or brains at -196°C in liquid nitrogen, maintaining cellular integrity for potential future revival. Includes monitoring systems, automated nitrogen replenishment, and earthquake-resistant vaults, representing a transhumanist approach to death as a reversible condition.
