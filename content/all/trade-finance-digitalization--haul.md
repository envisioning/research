# Trade Finance Digitalization Platforms

## Summary

Digital platforms enabling SMEs in emerging markets to access trade credit and documentary instruments.

## Description

These platforms digitize letters of credit, bills of lading, and insurance documents, integrating with blockchain and AI to reduce fraud and processing time. They bridge the $2.5 trillion trade finance gap in Africa, Asia, and Latin America, enabling inclusive global trade participation.
