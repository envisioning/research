# Multisensory Heritage Emitters

## Summary

Digital transmission of smell and touch for embodied archives.

## Description

Peripherals that synthesize precise scents (olfactory displays) and tactile textures (ultrasonic haptics) synchronized with digital archival records. They allow users to smell the dust of an old library or feel the texture of ancient parchment, creating deeply embodied historical connection.
