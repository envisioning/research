# Dual-Use Risk in Construction & Robotics

## Summary

Preventing construction technologies from being repurposed for military or surveillance applications.

## Description

Autonomous heavy equipment, drones, and 3D printing can be adapted for military fortification, surveillance infrastructure, or weapons production. Export controls, use-case restrictions, and ethical design principles aim to reduce dual-use risk, particularly for technologies exported to conflict zones or authoritarian regimes.
