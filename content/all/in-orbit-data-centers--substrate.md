# In-Orbit Data Centers

## Summary

Space-based computing infrastructure leveraging cold vacuum environments.

## Description

Data centers in orbit can exploit passive cooling and proximity to observation satellites, reducing latency for space-native workloads such as Earth imaging analytics. Over time, they may host AI models and digital twins that coordinate planetary-scale infrastructure from space.
