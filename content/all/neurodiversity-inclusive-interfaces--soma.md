# Neurodiversity-Inclusive Interfaces

## Summary

Adaptive systems designed for autistic, ADHD, and sensory-processing differences.

## Description

Interfaces that provide sensory regulation controls, predictability cues, social scaffolding, and customizable communication modes—co-designed with neurodivergent communities to reduce cognitive load and support autonomy in digital and immersive spaces.
