# Torsion Field Theory

## Summary

Theoretical framework proposing spacetime torsion as a mechanism for consciousness-matter interaction and exotic propuls

## Description

Torsion field theory proposes that spacetime possesses intrinsic torsion—rotational stress-energy components beyond conventional curvature—that can interact with consciousness and matter in ways not described by general relativity. This speculative framework attempts to explain psychokinetic phenomena, consciousness-matter coupling, and exotic propulsion through torsion field manipulation.

## Theoretical Framework

Torsion fields are proposed as fundamental spacetime properties arising from spin-torsion coupling in Einstein-Cartan theory extensions. Unlike electromagnetic fields (which couple to electric charge), torsion fields allegedly couple to particle spin and consciousness states. Proponents suggest torsion fields propagate instantaneously, penetrate all matter, and can be generated or modulated through mental intention or electromagnetic means.

## Consciousness Coupling and Propulsion Applications

Consciousness Coupling

The theory proposes that consciousness generates or modulates torsion fields, enabling direct mind-matter interaction. This framework attempts to explain psychokinesis, telepathy, and remote viewing through torsion field-mediated information transfer. Some researchers claim torsion field detectors can measure consciousness-generated fields and that mental intention can influence torsion field patterns.

## Propulsion Applications

Torsion field propulsion theories suggest that rotating masses or electromagnetic systems can generate torsion fields capable of modifying local spacetime geometry. This could enable inertia modification, gravity control, and exotic propulsion without conventional reaction mass. Some proposals involve rapidly rotating superconductors or electromagnetic field configurations to generate macroscopic torsion effects.

## Scientific Status and Significance

Scientific Status

Mainstream physics considers torsion field theories speculative and lacking experimental validation. While Einstein-Cartan theory includes torsion mathematically, measurable torsion effects require extreme conditions not achievable in laboratory settings. Claims of consciousness-torsion coupling lack theoretical foundation in established physics. No torsion field detector has demonstrated sensitivity to consciousness or mental intention under controlled conditions.

## Significance

Torsion field theory represents attempt to ground consciousness phenomena in fundamental physics, bridging parapsychology and theoretical physics. While scientifically unvalidated, it provides framework for understanding alleged consciousness-matter interactions and exotic propulsion claims in xenotechnology discourse.
