# Nanorobotic Bloodstream Sentinels

## Summary

Microscopic autonomous robots designed to patrol the bloodstream, detecting pathogens and clearing vascular plaque.

## Description

Nanoscale devices powered by glucose or external fields that circulate in the blood. They are engineered to identify and neutralize circulating tumor cells, bacteria, or remove atherosclerotic plaque, offering a proactive, continuous maintenance system for the human cardiovascular and immune systems.
