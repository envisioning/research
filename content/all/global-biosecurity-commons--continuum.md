# Global Biosecurity Commons

## Summary

Shared governance and infrastructure for high-consequence biology.

## Description

International frameworks, data spaces, and enforcement tools that coordinate lab standards, DNA synthesis screening, and incident reporting. They reduce asymmetries in biosafety capacity and create rapid, trusted channels to contain emerging biological threats.
