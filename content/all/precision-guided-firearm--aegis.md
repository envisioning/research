# Precision-Guided Firearm

## Summary

Self-correcting bullets that adjust mid-flight to hit moving or distant targets

## Description

Precision-guided firearms use guided projectiles (e.g., EXACTO, TrackingPoint) to increase accuracy. Research and development. Applications: military marksmanship, law enforcement. Challenges include cost, complexity, and miniaturization.
