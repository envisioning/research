# Soft Robotics and Bio-Inspired Grippers

## Summary

Robotic end-effectors made from flexible materials that mimic human dexterity for delicate handling.

## Description

Moving beyond rigid metal claws, soft robotics use silicone, fluids, and air pressure to gently grasp fragile or irregular items like groceries, electronics, and textiles. These bio-inspired grippers enable automation in sectors previously reliant on manual labor due to the risk of product damage.
