# Bouwvakker Tekort (Construction Labor Shortage)

## Summary

Severe shortage of skilled construction workers limiting development capacity regardless of permits or financing availability.

## Description

The Benelux construction sector faces severe labor shortages (bouwvakker tekort), with estimates of 30,000+ unfilled positions in the Netherlands alone. This shortage limits development capacity regardless of how many permits are granted or how much financing is available.

Causes include aging workforce, competition from other sectors, and reduced vocational training enrollment. The shortage affects all construction types but particularly impacts affordable housing where margins don't support premium wages.

The signal represents a supply-side constraint that regulatory streamlining alone cannot solve. Key challenges include attracting workers to construction careers, immigration and training policies, and whether industrialized construction (prefab, modular) can reduce labor intensity.
