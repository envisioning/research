# Generative Design & Simulation CAD

## Summary

AI copilots for engineering design and validation.

## Description

Foundation models embedded in CAD/CAE tools that propose geometries, materials, and process parameters under multi-objective constraints, then auto-generate manufacturing-ready toolpaths.
