# Real Estate Data Lakes

## Summary

Unified data platforms aggregating property, transaction, and market intelligence.

## Description

Cloud-based repositories that consolidate fragmented real estate data from MLS systems, public records, IoT devices, and alternative sources into queryable data lakes. These platforms enable portfolio-wide analytics, automated underwriting, ESG reporting, and feed AI models for pricing, risk assessment, and opportunity detection across global markets.
