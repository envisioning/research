# Tunable Spectrum LEDs

## Summary

Hardware capable of dynamically shifting wavelength to influence circadian rhythms and perception.

## Description

Advanced LED systems that can dynamically adjust their spectral power distribution (SPD). Unlike simple color-mixing RGB LEDs, these multi-channel emitters can replicate natural daylight curves or specific biological action spectra, impacting human circadian rhythms, alertness, and visual perception with high fidelity.
