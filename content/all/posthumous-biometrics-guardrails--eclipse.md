# Posthumous Biometrics Guardrails

## Summary

Protocols governing the reuse of voice, face, and DNA data after death.

## Description

Technical and legal guardrail systems that control access to a deceased person's biometric data, enforcing purpose limitations, audit trails, and family or executor approvals before samples or biometric models can be created, cloned, or shared.
