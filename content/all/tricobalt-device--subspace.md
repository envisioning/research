# Tricobalt Device

## Summary

Extremely powerful explosive ordinance capable of creating massive spatial distortions and shockwaves.

## Description

Tricobalt devices are among the most powerful conventional weapons in the Federation arsenal. Unlike photon torpedoes which use matter-antimatter annihilation, tricobalt explosives create a unique detonation pattern that generates intense spatial distortions and gravimetric shockwaves.

These weapons are particularly effective against hardened installations, spatial anomalies, and heavily fortified positions. The explosive yield can damage subspace itself, making them useful for collapsing artificial wormholes or disrupting transwarp conduits. Due to their destructive potential, tricobalt devices are typically reserved for extreme tactical situations.
