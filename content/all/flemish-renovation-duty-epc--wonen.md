# Flemish Renovation Duty (EPC-Driven)

## Summary

Transaction-triggered retrofit obligations in Flanders that push upgrades but can also freeze sales or price out buyers without subsidy access.

## Description

Flanders has introduced an EPC-driven ‘renovation obligation’: buyers of energy-inefficient homes must renovate to a target energy level within a fixed period. The policy aims to accelerate the renovation wave through market transactions.

It can generate strong long-term social value (lower energy bills, emissions) but also creates near-term friction: financing gaps, contractor scarcity, and potential price corrections that hit certain neighborhoods disproportionately.

The signal represents policy using transactions as enforcement moments for climate goals, with direct implications for affordability and market liquidity. Key challenges include adequate subsidies/loans for low- and middle-income buyers, enforcement realism, and avoiding unintended exclusion from homeownership.
