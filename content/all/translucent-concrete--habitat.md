# Translucent Concrete

## Summary

Light-transmitting concrete for buildings and sculptures.

## Description

Translucent concrete embeds optical fibers in concrete to transmit light. Commercialization; applications include Europe Gate and architectural facades. Research continues into improved transmission, cost, and structural properties.
