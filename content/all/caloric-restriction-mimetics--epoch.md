# Caloric Restriction Mimetics

## Summary

Compounds that reproduce the longevity benefits of caloric restriction without dietary limitation.

## Description

Caloric restriction is the most robust lifespan-extending intervention across species, but difficult for humans. Mimetics like metformin, resveratrol, and NAD+ precursors activate similar pathways (AMPK, sirtuins, autophagy) without requiring reduced calorie intake, offering accessible pharmacological routes to healthspan extension.
