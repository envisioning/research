# Blockchain Provenance Ledgers

## Summary

Decentralized, immutable records for tracking the journey and authenticity of goods.

## Description

Blockchain provides a shared source of truth across fragmented supply chain stakeholders. It ensures the authenticity of luxury goods, verifies ethical sourcing of raw materials, and automates customs documentation through smart contracts.
