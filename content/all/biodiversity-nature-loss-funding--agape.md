# Biodiversity Loss & Nature-Based Solutions

## Summary

Rapidly growing philanthropic attention to biodiversity collapse and nature-based solutions as climate and extinction crises converge.

## Description

The sixth mass extinction and collapse of ecosystems are driving increased philanthropic attention to biodiversity conservation, ecosystem restoration, and nature-based solutions. This signal captures the convergence of climate and biodiversity funding, growing investment in landscape-scale conservation, and debates about the role of indigenous and local communities in conservation. It reflects recognition that nature loss is as urgent as climate change and that solutions must address both simultaneously.
