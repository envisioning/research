# Anti-Gerrymandering Detection & Litigation Support

## Summary

Computational evidence for electoral boundary challenges.

## Description

Algorithms that generate ensembles of neutral redistricting plans and compute statistical outliers to detect partisan or racial gerrymandering. Provides expert-witness evidence and public visualizations for legal challenges and reform campaigns.
