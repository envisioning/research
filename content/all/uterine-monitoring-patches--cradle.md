# Wearable Uterine Monitoring Patches

## Summary

Continuous EMG and hemodynamic sensing of the uterus.

## Description

Thin, abdominal patches that record uterine electromyography, blood flow, and fetal movement to detect preterm labor risks and monitor contractions outside the clinic.
