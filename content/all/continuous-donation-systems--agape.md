# Continuous Donation Systems

## Summary

Micro-donation platforms with recurring automated flows, enabling sustained giving through small, frequent contributions.

## Description

Continuous donation systems enable micro-donations that flow automatically on a recurring basis—from round-up donations to subscription giving to automated percentage-of-income contributions. These systems promise to make giving more accessible and sustainable by reducing friction and enabling small, regular contributions. However, they also raise questions about whether micro-donations replace or supplement larger gifts, how to ensure transparency in automated flows, and whether continuous giving creates dependency or enables better planning. The systems represent a shift toward frictionless, automated giving while highlighting tensions between convenience and intentionality.
