# Gastrobot

## Summary

Robots powered by microbial digestion of organic matter for autonomous energy generation

## Description

Gastrobots use microbial fuel cells or digestive systems to convert organic matter into electrical energy. Prototypes; applications include autonomous operation in resource-rich environments. Research continues into efficiency and scalability.
