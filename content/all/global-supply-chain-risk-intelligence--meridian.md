# Global Supply Chain Risk Intelligence

## Summary

Mapping critical dependencies and chokepoints.

## Description

Continuously updated analytics platforms that fuse trade, logistics, satellite, and financial data to identify single points of failure and coercion risks in global supply chains for critical technologies and resources.
