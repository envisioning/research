# Climate Adaptation Governance Systems

## Summary

Democratic coordination for managed retreat and resilience.

## Description

Decision-support and participation tools for climate adaptation: managed retreat, zoning changes, infrastructure relocation, and resource allocation. Combines predictive modeling with deliberative legitimacy to navigate deeply contested transitions.
