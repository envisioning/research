# Autonomous Surface Movement & A-SMGCS

## Summary

Advanced surface surveillance and guidance to reduce incursions and delays.

## Description

Surface operations are a major safety and efficiency bottleneck. Advanced surface movement guidance and control (A-SMGCS), coupled with automation, can reduce runway incursions, improve taxi-time predictability, and support low-visibility operations.
