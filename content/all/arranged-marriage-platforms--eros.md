# Arranged Marriage & Family Matchmaking Platforms

## Summary

Digital systems for family-mediated partner selection across cultures.

## Description

Platforms designed for contexts where families play central roles in partner selection—common across South Asia, Middle East, East Asia, and diaspora communities. These integrate family profiles, biodata, horoscope matching, financial transparency, and multi-party consent flows, serving billions globally.
