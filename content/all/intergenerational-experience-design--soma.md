# Intergenerational Experience Design

## Summary

Platforms bridging age cohorts through shared storytelling and co-creation.

## Description

Digital and hybrid environments that facilitate knowledge exchange, play, and mutual understanding between generations—addressing ageism, preserving elder wisdom, and building age-inclusive communities through affective and accessible design.
