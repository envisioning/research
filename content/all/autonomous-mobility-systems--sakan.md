# Autonomous Mobility Systems

## Summary

Self-driving vehicles, autonomous pods, and robotic delivery systems transforming urban transport.

## Description

Autonomous mobility encompasses self-driving taxis, autonomous buses, delivery robots, and air taxis. Dubai aims for 25% of trips to be autonomous by 2030, while NEOM envisions a city built around autonomous transport from inception.

Current deployments include autonomous pods at Masdar City, Cruise robotaxi pilots in Dubai, and extensive testing programs across the region. The controlled environments of new developments offer ideal testbeds for autonomous systems. Air taxi (eVTOL) development is particularly active, with Joby, Lilium, and others partnering with GCC entities.

The signal represents the potential transformation of urban form itself, with NEOM's The Line designed around the assumption of autonomous mobility. Key challenges include regulatory frameworks, public acceptance, and integration with existing transport networks.
