# Decentralized Identity & eSIM Authentication

## Summary

Self-sovereign digital identities and programmable SIM provisioning.

## Description

Blockchain-based or W3C DID credentials that let users control their network identity, authentication, and subscription management without centralized authorities. Combined with eSIM/iSIM, this enables instant multi-operator roaming, privacy-preserving authentication, and resistance to SIM-swap fraud.
