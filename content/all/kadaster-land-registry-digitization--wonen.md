# Kadaster (Land Registry) Digitization

## Summary

Modernization of land registries enabling transparent ownership data, faster transactions, and identification of development opportunities.

## Description

Kadaster (land registry) digitization involves modernizing property registration systems to enable real-time ownership data, automated transactions, and transparent land market information. The Dutch Kadaster is highly advanced, while Belgian and Luxembourg systems are modernizing.

Digitized registries can identify land ownership patterns (concentration, speculation), streamline development transactions, and enable data-driven planning. They're essential infrastructure for policies like land mobilization or anti-speculation measures.

The signal represents foundational digital infrastructure for housing policy. Key challenges include completing digitization of historical records, ensuring data accessibility while protecting privacy, and connecting registry data with planning systems.
