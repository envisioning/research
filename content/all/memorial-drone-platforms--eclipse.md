# Memorial Drone Platforms

## Summary

Specialized drones for ash scattering and aerial rituals.

## Description

Custom drone systems with payload controls and safety features for dispersing cremated remains, projecting light, or capturing aerial footage during ceremonies, enabling access to remote or symbolically meaningful locations while complying with aviation regulations.
