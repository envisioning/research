# Subsea Cable Resilience Systems

## Summary

Monitoring and hardening of undersea data arteries.

## Description

Integrated sensing, routing, and repair systems for undersea cables that detect tampering, reroute traffic autonomously, and enable rapid physical intervention to preserve global connectivity during crises or hostile actions.
