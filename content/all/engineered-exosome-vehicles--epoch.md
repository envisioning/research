# Engineered Exosome Vehicles

## Summary

Cell-free delivery of regenerative factors using modified extracellular vesicles.

## Description

Exosomes are natural nanoparticles used by cells to communicate. By harvesting exosomes from young stem cells or engineering them to carry specific cargos (mRNA, proteins, miRNA), these vehicles deliver the 'signal' of rejuvenation without the risks of cell transplantation (rejection, teratomas), offering a potent, scalable class of therapeutics.
