# Climate Migration Governance

## Summary

International frameworks for displacement and climate refugees.

## Description

Legal and institutional architectures defining the rights, status, and support mechanisms for populations displaced by sea-level rise, desertification, and extreme weather. These protocols aim to prevent statelessness, ensure dignified resettlement, and distribute responsibility equitably among nations based on historical emissions and capacity.
