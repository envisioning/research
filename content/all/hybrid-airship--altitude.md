# Hybrid Airship

## Summary

Aircraft combining buoyancy and aerodynamic lift for fuel-efficient cargo and surveillance

## Description

Hybrid airships combine buoyancy and aerodynamic lift for air travel with low fuel consumption. Prototypes; Airlander 10. Applications: cargo, surveillance, tourism. Challenges include cost and weather sensitivity.
