# Trusted Civic Alerting & Crisis Communication

## Summary

Authentic, resilient public messaging during fast-moving events.

## Description

Systems that authenticate official alerts and coordinate multi-channel messaging (SMS, apps, radio, social, web) with provenance and anti-spoofing protections. Helps reduce panic, counter disinformation, and maintain operational legitimacy during crises.
