# Reality Filter Auditing

## Summary

Transparency tools showing how a user's reality is being edited.

## Description

Systems that log and expose which overlays, filters, or occlusions are active at any moment and who controls them. They help users, regulators, and auditors understand when information is being hidden, emphasized, or customized, and provide controls to reset to a baseline view.
