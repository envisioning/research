# Strata / HOA Governance & Service-Charge Transparency

## Summary

Owner association governance, sinking funds, and service-charge transparency becoming core investment and livability signals for apartment markets.

## Description

As GCC markets expand apartment living and Build-to-Rent, the operating model of shared assets (lobbies, lifts, chilled water interfaces, amenities) becomes decisive. Strong strata/HOA governance and transparent service-charge budgeting reduce disputes, improve maintenance outcomes, and protect long-term asset value.

This signal is especially relevant in Dubai-style high-rise environments and emerging KSA apartment markets. Investors increasingly underwrite service-charge trajectories, sinking fund sufficiency, and governance rules (collection enforcement, vendor procurement, dispute resolution) as part of acquisition decisions.

The signal represents a shift from 'buy the unit' thinking to 'buy into an operating system' thinking, where governance quality is as important as build quality. Key challenges include regulatory enforcement, owner engagement, and standardizing disclosures in ways that are comparable across developments.
