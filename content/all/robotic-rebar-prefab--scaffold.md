# Robotic Rebar & Prefab Fabrication

## Summary

Automation cells for cutting, bending, tying, and welding that shift labor from site to controlled factories.

## Description

Robotic fabrication reduces variability in rebar cages, wall panels, and MEP racks and enables just-in-time delivery. The application impact is amplified when paired with design-for-manufacture (DfMA) and coordinated procurement, but requires up-front detailing discipline and stable interfaces between trades.
