# Modular 3D Printing

## Summary

On-site additive manufacturing for rapid structural fabrication.

## Description

Large-scale 3D printers capable of extruding concrete and composite materials to build structural elements or entire housing units directly on-site. This technology drastically reduces material waste, enables complex architectural geometries, and cuts construction time by up to 50% compared to traditional methods.
