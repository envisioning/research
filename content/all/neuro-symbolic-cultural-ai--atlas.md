# Neuro-Symbolic Cultural AI

## Summary

Hybrid AI combining neural networks with knowledge graphs for cultural understanding.

## Description

Systems integrating deep learning with symbolic reasoning over cultural ontologies, enabling more nuanced cross-cultural communication, etiquette coaching, and contextualized recommendations that respect local norms and avoid stereotyping.
