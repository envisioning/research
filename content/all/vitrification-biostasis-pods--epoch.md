# Vitrification Biostasis Pods

## Summary

Advanced cryopreservation systems capable of reversibly preserving human organs and potentially whole organisms.

## Description

Next-generation cryopreservation chambers using optimized cryoprotectant perfusion, controlled cooling, and magnetic field stabilization to achieve ice-free vitrification. These systems are designed for organ banking, enabling long-term storage of transplantable organs and offering a path toward future whole-body biostasis for medical time-travel to more advanced therapies.
