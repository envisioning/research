# Lunar Mass Drivers

## Summary

Electromagnetic catapults for launching materials from the Moon to orbit.

## Description

Taking advantage of the Moon's low gravity and lack of atmosphere, mass drivers use linear motors to accelerate payloads to escape velocity. This non-rocket launch infrastructure enables the bulk export of lunar oxygen, metals, and construction materials to build large-scale orbital habitats and solar power stations.
