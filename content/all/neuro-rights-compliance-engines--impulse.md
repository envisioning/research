# Neuro-Rights Compliance Engines

## Summary

Automated checks for cognitive liberty violations.

## Description

Rule-based and machine-learning systems that scan neurotechnology products and influence platforms for compliance with emerging neuro-rights and mental privacy regulations. They help organizations detect risky practices, generate compliance reports, and propose design changes before deployment.
