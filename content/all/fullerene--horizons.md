# Fullerene

## Summary

Carbon cage molecules; experiments, diffusion, and programmable matter.

## Description

Fullerenes are carbon molecules in cage structures—buckminsterfullerene (C60) is the most common. Applications include drug delivery, superconductivity, and programmable matter. Experiments and diffusion; limited commercialization. Research continues into functionalization, composite materials, and biomedical applications.
