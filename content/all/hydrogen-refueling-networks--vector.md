# Hydrogen Refueling Networks

## Summary

Infrastructure supporting fuel cell electric vehicles (FCEVs).

## Description

A network of high-pressure hydrogen refueling stations designed primarily for heavy-duty transport and long-haul logistics. These stations enable rapid refueling comparable to diesel, supporting the decarbonization of sectors hard to electrify with batteries alone.
