# Reality Authentication

## Summary

Cryptographic verification of digital overlays to prevent deepfakes.

## Description

Protocols for verifying the source and integrity of augmented reality content. Similar to SSL for the web, this ensures that the digital sign overlaid on a store or the navigation arrow on the street is legitimate and not a malicious 'reality hack' designed to mislead or defraud.
