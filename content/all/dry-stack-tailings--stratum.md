# Dry-Stack Tailings and Filtered Dams

## Summary

High-pressure filtration hardware enabling safer, partially dewatered tailings storage.

## Description

Dry-stack tailings systems use large-scale filters, thickeners, and conveyors to remove water from mine waste before placement. By producing dense, stackable tailings, these systems reduce dam failure risk, shrink the surface footprint, and enable more effective water recycling in arid regions.
