# Choice Architecture Orchestration Engines

## Summary

Systems designing and optimizing digital nudges.

## Description

Software platforms that automatically design, test, and refine interface-level nudges—such as defaults, prompts, and friction—using large-scale experimentation. These engines optimize for targeted behaviors (adherence, retention, consumption) while increasingly raising questions around autonomy and manipulation.
