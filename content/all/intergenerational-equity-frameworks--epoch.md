# Intergenerational Equity Frameworks

## Summary

Policies addressing the social, economic, and resource implications of dramatically extended lifespans.

## Description

As longevity interventions extend healthy working years, societies must reconsider retirement, inheritance, career structures, and resource distribution across generations. These frameworks propose adaptive social contracts, including flexible retirement ages, lifelong learning systems, and mechanisms to prevent gerontocratic concentration of wealth and power.
