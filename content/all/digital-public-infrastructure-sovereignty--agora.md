# Digital Public Infrastructure Sovereignty

## Summary

National and community control over civic tech stacks.

## Description

Technical and governance frameworks that reduce dependence on foreign or corporate platforms for critical democratic infrastructure. Includes open-source ecosystems, local hosting requirements, data localization, and public procurement reform.
