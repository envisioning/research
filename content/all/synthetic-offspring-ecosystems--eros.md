# Synthetic Offspring Ecosystems

## Summary

Complex digital life forms adopted and raised by partners or individuals.

## Description

Advanced AI-driven virtual beings that grow, learn, and require care, offering a form of parenthood without biological constraints. These serve as a relationship milestone for couples, a solution for infertility/demographic collapse, or a companion for solitary individuals.
