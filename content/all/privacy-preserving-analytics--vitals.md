# Privacy-Preserving Health Analytics

## Summary

Techniques like federated learning and differential privacy that enable cross-institutional insights without sharing raw data.

## Description

Privacy-preserving analytics architectures allow health systems to collaboratively train models and generate benchmarks while keeping patient-level data local. Methods such as federated learning, secure multi-party computation, and differential privacy protect individuals’ identities and reduce regulatory friction, unlocking insights from multi-hospital and multi-region datasets.
