# Polyamory & Networked Relationship Managers

## Summary

Coordination tools for people maintaining multiple romantic relationships.

## Description

Scheduling, consent-tracking, and boundary-logging tools that help polycules and non-traditional relationship constellations align on expectations. They make invisible emotional labor—like remembering agreements and checking in—more explicit and shareable.
