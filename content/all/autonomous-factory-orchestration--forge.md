# Autonomous Factory Orchestration Platforms

## Summary

AI-driven systems that coordinate machines, labor, and material flows across the plant.

## Description

Autonomous factory orchestration platforms sit above traditional MES and scheduling tools, ingesting data from PLCs, robots, warehouse systems, and ERP. Using multi-agent optimization, they continuously re-plan work orders, dynamically balance lines, and route materials to minimize changeovers and bottlenecks while respecting maintenance windows and labor constraints.
