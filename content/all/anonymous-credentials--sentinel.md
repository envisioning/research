# Anonymous & Attribute-Based Credentials

## Summary

Credentials enabling selective disclosure and unlinkable presentations.

## Description

Anonymous and Attribute-Based Credential systems extend verifiable credentials with strong privacy guarantees, allowing holders to prove specific attributes (such as being over 18 or belonging to a group) without revealing their full identity. Using techniques like zero-knowledge proofs and unlinkable tokens, they enable fine-grained access control while minimizing data exposure.
