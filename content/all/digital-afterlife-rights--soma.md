# Digital Afterlife Rights

## Summary

Legal frameworks governing posthumous data, AI resurrection, and memorials.

## Description

Emerging policies defining consent for post-mortem data use, deepfake resurrection, conversational AI trained on deceased individuals, and rights of the living versus the dead in digital legacy and memory systems.
