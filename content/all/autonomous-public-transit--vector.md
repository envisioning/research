# Autonomous Public Transit

## Summary

Self-driving buses and shuttles for mass transit systems.

## Description

Autonomous Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) systems and electric shuttles operating on fixed or flexible routes. These systems combine the efficiency of rail with the flexibility of buses, offering sustainable mass transit solutions for cities while reducing operational costs.
