# Structural Integrity Field

## Summary

Force field system reinforcing hull structure during high-stress maneuvers and combat.

## Description

The structural integrity field (SIF) uses force field technology to strengthen a ship's spaceframe beyond what physical materials alone could withstand. This allows vessels to perform extreme maneuvers and sustain combat damage that would otherwise tear the ship apart.The SIF constantly adjusts to distribute stress throughout the hull, preventing catastrophic structural failures. Power to the SIF is critical during combat and high-warp operations.
