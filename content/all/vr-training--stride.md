# Immersive VR/AR Training

## Summary

Virtual environments for tactical cognitive training and decision making.

## Description

Utilization of Virtual and Augmented Reality to simulate game scenarios for cognitive training. Athletes can practice reading defenses, recognizing patterns, and making split-second decisions in a high-fidelity virtual environment, reducing physical wear and tear while enhancing mental acuity.
