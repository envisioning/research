# High-Speed Rail Modernization

## Summary

Next-generation rail networks in emerging markets and cross-border corridors.

## Description

Deployment of 300+ km/h rail systems across Africa, South America, Southeast Asia, and transnational corridors (e.g., Belt and Road), making rail competitive with short-haul aviation and anchoring sustainable tourism development along new routes.
