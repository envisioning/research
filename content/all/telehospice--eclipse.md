# Telehospice Services

## Summary

Remote delivery of palliative and hospice care.

## Description

Leveraging telehealth infrastructure to provide monitoring, symptom management, and family support for home-based end-of-life care. This application expands access to specialists and reduces the burden of hospital visits for fragile patients.
