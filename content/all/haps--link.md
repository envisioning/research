# High Altitude Platform Systems (HAPS)

## Summary

Stratospheric stations providing wide-area connectivity.

## Description

Unmanned airships or solar-powered aircraft operating in the stratosphere (20km altitude). HAPS function like satellites but closer to Earth, offering lower latency, higher capacity, and the ability to be returned for maintenance. They bridge the gap between terrestrial towers and satellites.
