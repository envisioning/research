# Quantum-dot Color Conversion

## Summary

Narrowband spectral engineering for high-CRI, high-gamut, tunable illumination.

## Description

Quantum dots (QDs) can convert pump light (often blue) into precisely tuned narrowband emission. In lighting, QD films and remote phosphor architectures improve color rendering and enable custom spectral recipes (including horticulture and HCL) while maintaining high efficacy and consistent binning across luminaires.
