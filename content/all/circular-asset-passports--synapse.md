# Circular Asset Passports

## Summary

Digital identity systems for tracking the full lifecycle and provenance of organizational assets.

## Description

Blockchain-backed digital twins for physical assets (furniture, IT equipment, vehicles) that record repair history, material composition, and carbon footprint. These passports enable organizations to maximize asset longevity, facilitate resale or donation, and prove compliance with circular economy mandates.
