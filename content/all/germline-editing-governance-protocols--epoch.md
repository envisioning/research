# Germline Editing Governance Protocols

## Summary

Global standards governing the safe and ethical use of heritable genome editing for disease prevention and lifespan extension.

## Description

Multilateral oversight structures, consent models, and technical thresholds that must be met before interventions can alter the genomes of embryos or germ cells. These protocols balance the potential to eliminate severe age-related diseases at the source with safeguards against eugenics, intergenerational inequality, and unintended ecological impacts.
