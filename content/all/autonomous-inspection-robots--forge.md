# Autonomous Inspection Robots

## Summary

Mobile robots and drones performing continuous facility and asset monitoring.

## Description

Autonomous inspection robots—including quadrupeds (Boston Dynamics Spot), climbing robots, and indoor drones—patrol factories, power plants, and refineries 24/7. Equipped with thermal cameras, ultrasound, and LiDAR, they detect gas leaks, corrosion, structural defects, and equipment anomalies, creating digital condition records and triggering maintenance workflows without human exposure to hazardous environments.
