# Assimilation Tubules

## Summary

Extendable injectors delivering nanoprobes directly into targets' bloodstream.

## Description

Retractable injector mechanisms, typically deployed from the hand or wrist, that pierce flesh and inject assimilation nanoprobes. The delivery system ensures rapid distribution throughout the victim's circulatory system, beginning the assimilation process within seconds.
