# Zero-Trust Identity and Privileged Access Management

## Summary

CyberArk pioneered privileged access management and is now a $10B+ market cap company defining zero-trust identity security for Fortune 500 enterprises globally.

## Description

CyberArk, founded in 1999 by Unit 8200 alumni, pioneered the category of privileged access management (PAM) — securing the administrative accounts and credentials that represent the keys to enterprise IT kingdoms. The company has evolved into a comprehensive identity security platform encompassing secrets management, endpoint privilege management, and cloud entitlement management. With $10B+ market capitalization, CyberArk is one of Israel's most successful cybersecurity companies.

Identity has become the new security perimeter as traditional network boundaries dissolve in cloud and hybrid environments. CyberArk's approach — assume breach, verify everything, limit privileges — epitomizes the zero-trust security model that has become the dominant paradigm for enterprise security architecture. The company's products protect against the most damaging attack vectors: credential theft and lateral movement using compromised administrative access.

Strategically, CyberArk represents the maturation of the Israeli cybersecurity model from point products to platform companies. Its leadership in identity security — the fastest-growing segment of cybersecurity — demonstrates how first-mover advantage in a critical security domain creates durable competitive positions. The company's growth trajectory and market influence illustrate why Israel's cybersecurity ecosystem remains the most productive outside the United States.
