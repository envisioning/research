# Rare Earth Alternatives

## Summary

Materials independence from concentrated supply chains.

## Description

Advanced materials science and substitution technologies that reduce or eliminate dependence on rare earth elements concentrated in adversary nations, critical for motors, magnets, batteries, and electronics in defense and energy systems.
