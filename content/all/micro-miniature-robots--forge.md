# Micro and Miniature Robots

## Summary

Sub-millimeter to centimeter-scale robots for precision assembly and inspection.

## Description

Micro-robots fabricated using MEMS, origami techniques, and magnetic actuation operate at scales inaccessible to conventional automation. They enable in-situ inspection of microfluidic devices, assembly of micro-optical components, minimally invasive repair of precision instruments, and swarm-based parallel micro-manufacturing—opening new frontiers in electronics, medical devices, and photonics.
