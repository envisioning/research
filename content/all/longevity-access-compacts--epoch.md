# Longevity Access Compacts

## Summary

Policy frameworks that guarantee equitable access to life-extending interventions across populations.

## Description

International and national agreements that define principles, funding mechanisms, and regulatory incentives to prevent longevity technologies from being restricted to small elite groups. These compacts seek to integrate lifespan-extending therapies into public health systems, prioritize high-burden communities, and ensure that gains in healthy years are broadly distributed.
