# Splitsen van Woningen (Apartment Splitting)

## Summary

Converting single-family homes into multiple units, increasing density within existing structures but facing regulatory and neighborhood opposition.

## Description

Splitsen (splitting) involves converting single-family homes into multiple apartments, increasing housing supply without new construction. It's particularly relevant for oversized homes in aging neighborhoods where original families have moved out.

Many municipalities have restricted or banned splitting due to concerns about neighborhood character, parking, and intensification. Recent policy shifts are relaxing restrictions to increase supply, but face NIMBY opposition.

The signal represents tension between gentle densification and neighborhood preservation. Key challenges include quality standards for converted units, balancing municipal autonomy with housing needs, and ensuring splitting produces quality housing rather than substandard subdivisions.
