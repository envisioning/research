# Tractor Beam

## Summary

Projected force field for manipulating and towing objects in space.

## Description

Tractor beams project focused graviton particles to create attractive or repulsive forces on remote objects. This technology is essential for cargo operations, assisting distressed vessels, and defensive applications.The beam's strength depends on the power allocated and the mass of the target object. Multiple emitters can be coordinated to handle particularly large objects or to perform precision manipulation of smaller ones.
