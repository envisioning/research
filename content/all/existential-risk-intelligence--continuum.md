# Existential Risk Intelligence Systems

## Summary

Integrated AI forecasting catastrophic threats and policy pathways.

## Description

Comprehensive platforms combining agent-based simulation, game theory, and real-time monitoring to model existential risks across decades. They track indicators for pandemics, nuclear escalation, AI misalignment, climate tipping points, and civilizational collapse, while simulating intervention strategies through millions of scenario runs. Used by governance bodies for strategic foresight and early warning.
