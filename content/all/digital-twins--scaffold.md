# Urban Digital Twins

## Summary

Real-time virtual replicas of physical construction sites and city districts.

## Description

Dynamic digital models that mirror physical assets in real-time using IoT sensors. In construction, they simulate progress, predict structural issues, and optimize logistics before physical work begins.
