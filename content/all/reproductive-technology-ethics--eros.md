# Reproductive Technology & Relationship Ethics

## Summary

Frameworks governing fertility, surrogacy, and genetic selection.

## Description

Ethical standards for how reproductive technologies intersect with relationship formation—from genetic matchmaking to surrogacy contracts, fertility tracking apps, and designer babies. These address consent, commodification, inequality, and the redefinition of parenthood.
