# Food Waste Valorization

## Summary

Converting peels and pulp into bioplastics, feed, and ingredients.

## Description

Regional hubs upcycle surplus produce and byproducts into fiber-rich ingredients, bioplastics, or insect feed, closing loops while generating new revenue streams.
