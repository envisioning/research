# Planetary Digital Twins

## Summary

High-fidelity simulations of Earth's systems for climate prediction.

## Description

Comprehensive virtual replicas of the Earth's physical systems, including atmosphere, oceans, and biosphere. These digital twins utilize exascale computing and real-time satellite data to simulate climate scenarios with unprecedented accuracy. They allow policymakers and scientists to test the outcomes of geoengineering interventions, policy changes, and conservation efforts before implementation.
