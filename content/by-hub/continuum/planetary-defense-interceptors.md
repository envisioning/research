# Planetary Defense Interceptors

## Summary

Kinetic impactors and lasers for asteroid deflection.

## Description

Dedicated space- and ground-based interceptor systems designed to nudge or fragment hazardous near-Earth objects on multi-year timescales. Combining precision tracking telescopes with rapid-launch vehicles, they form the hard infrastructure layer of planetary defense against impact events.
