# Climate-Resilient Food Systems

## Summary

Autonomous greenhouses, vertical farms, and algae systems for secure nutrition.

## Description

Integrated food production stacks combining controlled-environment agriculture, cellular meat, and microalgae bioreactors. Designed to operate in low-light, high-variance climates or refuges, they decouple food security from fragile global supply chains and open-field agriculture.
