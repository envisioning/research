# Adaptive Coastal Megaprojects

## Summary

Dynamic sea walls, surge barriers, and sponge-city retrofits.

## Description

Large-scale infrastructure combining movable barriers, restored wetlands, and permeable urban design to buffer coastal regions from sea-level rise and extreme storms. These systems are instrumented and reconfigurable, adapting their behavior as climate projections and local conditions evolve.
