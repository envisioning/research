# Planetary Gene Banks

## Summary

Distributed vaults preserving global biodiversity and critical genotypes.

## Description

A federated network of cryogenic and in situ repositories storing seeds, DNA, tissue samples, and microbial libraries. They safeguard the genetic basis of food systems, ecosystems, and keystone species against climate-driven collapse, conflict, or runaway diseases.
