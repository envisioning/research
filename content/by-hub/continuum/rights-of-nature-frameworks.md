# Rights of Nature Legal Frameworks

## Summary

Granting ecosystems legal personhood and enforceable protections.

## Description

Constitutional and statutory systems recognizing rivers, forests, and biomes as rights-bearing entities with standing in court. Pioneered in Ecuador, New Zealand, and indigenous legal traditions, these frameworks empower communities and future generations to defend ecosystems against extraction and degradation as fiduciary responsibilities.
