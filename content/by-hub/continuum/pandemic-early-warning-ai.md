# Pandemic Early-Warning AI

## Summary

AI models detecting anomalous health signals before outbreaks escalate.

## Description

Machine learning pipelines that fuse wastewater genomics, clinical reports, mobility data, and online signals to detect emerging pathogens weeks before traditional surveillance. These systems prioritize ambiguous, low-signal anomalies and recommend targeted testing or interventions to avert global pandemics.
