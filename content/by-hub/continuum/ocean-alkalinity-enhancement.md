# Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement

## Summary

Mineral dispersal systems for marine carbon sequestration.

## Description

Ships and coastal installations that distribute finely ground alkaline minerals (olivine, limestone) into oceans to accelerate natural weathering processes. This raises ocean pH, counters acidification, and sequesters atmospheric CO2 for millennia while supporting marine ecosystems under climate stress.
