# AI Auditing Infrastructure

## Summary

Standardized pipelines for stress-testing and evaluating powerful AI systems.

## Description

Tooling and protocols that enable continuous, third-party red-teaming and capability evaluation of frontier AI models deployed in critical domains. They provide transparent metrics, safety benchmarks, and automated alerts when systems stray beyond agreed risk thresholds.
