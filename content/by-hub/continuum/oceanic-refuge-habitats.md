# Oceanic Refuge Habitats

## Summary

Semi-autonomous marine platforms for habitation and research.

## Description

Floating or semi-submersible structures that host long-term communities, laboratories, and seed banks away from conflict-prone landmasses. Equipped with renewable energy, aquaculture, and desalination, they function as distributed refuges and testbeds for resilient settlement design.
