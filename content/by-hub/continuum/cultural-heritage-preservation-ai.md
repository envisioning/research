# Cultural Heritage Preservation AI

## Summary

Automated digitization and restoration of endangered cultures.

## Description

AI systems that digitize languages, oral histories, artifacts, and traditional knowledge at risk from climate displacement, conflict, or urbanization. Using natural language processing and 3D scanning, they create immersive archives accessible to future generations and displaced communities, preventing cultural extinction alongside biological and physical losses.
