# Synthetic Ecosystem Restoration

## Summary

Engineered organisms for rapid environmental remediation.

## Description

The application of synthetic biology to create microorganisms and plants specifically designed to degrade environmental toxins, fix nitrogen in depleted soils, or accelerate reforestation. These bio-tools are deployed to reverse ecological damage and fortify the biosphere against climate change-induced collapse.
