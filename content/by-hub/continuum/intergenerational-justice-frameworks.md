# Intergenerational Justice Frameworks

## Summary

Legal standing for future generations in policy and courts.

## Description

Constitutional amendments, ombudspersons, and legal doctrines empowering advocates to represent the interests of unborn generations in legislative and judicial proceedings. These frameworks shift time horizons in infrastructure, environmental, and fiscal policy from electoral cycles to century-scale impacts.
