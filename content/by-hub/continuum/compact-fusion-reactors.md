# Compact Fusion Reactors

## Summary

Commercially viable fusion energy for baseload power.

## Description

Next-generation tokamak, stellarator, and inertial confinement fusion devices achieving net energy gain. These reactors provide abundant, carbon-free baseload electricity independent of weather, enabling long-term energy security and supporting energy-intensive climate interventions like direct air capture at civilizational scale.
