# Distributed Resilient Microgrids

## Summary

Islandable local energy networks that self-heal during grid shocks.

## Description

Community-scale microgrids combining renewables, storage, and intelligent control systems that can disconnect from national grids when necessary. They keep hospitals, data centers, and essential services powered through storms, cyberattacks, or fuel shortages.
