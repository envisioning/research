# Genetic Rescue & De-Extinction

## Summary

Restoring extinct species and genetic diversity via biotechnology.

## Description

Applications of CRISPR, cloning, and synthetic genomics to resurrect keystone species (woolly mammoth, passenger pigeon) and restore genetic diversity in endangered populations. These programs aim to repair damaged ecosystems, reverse biodiversity collapse, and demonstrate humanity's capacity to reverse ecological harm at scale.
