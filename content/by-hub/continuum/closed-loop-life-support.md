# Closed-Loop Life Support (ECLSS)

## Summary

Regenerative systems for indefinite survival in isolated environments.

## Description

Advanced Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS) that achieve near-100% recycling of water, air, and waste. Originally developed for deep space exploration, these applications are critical for building resilient underground bunkers, underwater habitats, and self-sufficient communities capable of surviving prolonged isolation or surface biosphere collapse.
