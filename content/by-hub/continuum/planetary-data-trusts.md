# Planetary Data Trusts

## Summary

Institutions stewarding shared climate, biosphere, and risk data assets.

## Description

Legal and technical architectures that hold planetary-scale datasets—satellite imagery, ecological surveys, infrastructure maps—in fiduciary structures. They guarantee equitable access, privacy protection, and long-term preservation while enabling high-stakes modeling and governance.
