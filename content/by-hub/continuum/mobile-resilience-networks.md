# Mobile Resilience Networks

## Summary

SMS and feature-phone infrastructure for crisis coordination.

## Description

Low-bandwidth digital platforms leveraging USSD, SMS, and basic mobile networks to coordinate disaster response, deliver early warnings, and enable mutual aid in regions lacking smartphone or internet penetration. Pioneered across Africa and South Asia, they democratize resilience infrastructure and ensure no community is left offline during crises.
