# Space-Based Solar Power

## Summary

Orbital solar arrays beaming energy to Earth.

## Description

Large photovoltaic arrays in geosynchronous orbits that harvest continuous solar energy and transmit it to Earth via microwave or laser beams. This hardware reduces dependence on terrestrial generation vulnerable to climate impacts, providing stable baseload power for critical infrastructure and emergency operations.
