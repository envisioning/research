# Long-Term Stewardship Funds

## Summary

Financial vehicles optimized for multi-century resilience investments.

## Description

New classes of endowments and public wealth funds whose mandates and smart contracts prioritize risk reduction, adaptation, and public goods over short-term returns. They finance infrastructure, research, and governance capacities that pay off on generational timescales.
