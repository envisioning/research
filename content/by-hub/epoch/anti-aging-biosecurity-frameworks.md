# Biosecurity & Misuse Risks Frameworks

## Summary

Governance structures to prevent the repurposing of rejuvenation tools for harmful biological modification.

## Description

As longevity technologies like viral vectors and immune modulation become more accessible, the risk of dual-use (e.g., designing pathogens or 'anti-rejuvenation' weapons) increases. These frameworks establish oversight, access controls, and monitoring systems to ensure that powerful biological engineering tools are used exclusively for health extension and not weaponization.
