# Cognitive Liberty Frameworks

## Summary

Rights to mental self-determination and protection against unauthorized neuro-surveillance.

## Description

Legal and ethical guidelines designed to protect individuals from forced cognitive enhancement or unauthorized reading of neural data. As brain-computer interfaces advance, these frameworks ensure that thoughts and mental states remain private and under the individual's control.
