# Mitochondrial Augmentation Microdevices

## Summary

Implantable systems that deliver healthy mitochondria directly into aging tissues.

## Description

Miniaturized delivery platforms that harvest, enrich, and inject functional mitochondria from autologous or donor sources into target organs. By replacing dysfunctional mitochondria in aged cells, these devices aim to restore cellular energy metabolism, reduce oxidative stress, and reverse age-related declines in muscle, brain, and cardiac tissue.
