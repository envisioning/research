# Adaptive Metabolic Orchestration Engines

## Summary

AI systems that continuously adjust diet, activity, sleep, and therapeutics to keep metabolism in a youthful state.

## Description

Reinforcement learning and control algorithms that fuse inputs from wearables, implantable sensors, lab panels, and environmental data. These engines generate dynamic, personalized protocols—modulating nutrition, exercise, circadian alignment, and pharmacological agents like GLP-1 analogs or mTOR modulators—to minimize biological age and prevent metabolic drift toward chronic disease.
