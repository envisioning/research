# Partial Cellular Reprogramming

## Summary

Controlled expression of Yamanaka factors to rejuvenate tissues without losing cellular identity.

## Description

A therapy that transiently activates genes associated with embryonic stem cells to reset the 'epigenetic age' of adult cells. The goal is to restore youthful function and regenerative capacity to organs like the heart, liver, and skin, without causing the cells to revert fully to a stem cell state (which would cause cancer).
