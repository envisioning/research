# Neurodegenerative Risk Reduction Programs

## Summary

Early detection and interventions built around AI-based brain aging models.

## Description

Comprehensive protocols integrating cognitive biomarkers, imaging, and genetic risk scores to detect neurodegeneration decades before symptoms appear. These programs deploy multimodal interventions—ranging from specific cognitive training and sleep optimization to neuroprotective pharmacotherapy—to preserve brain plasticity and prevent cognitive decline.
