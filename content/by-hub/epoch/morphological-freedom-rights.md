# Morphological Freedom Rights

## Summary

Legal recognition of an individual's right to modify their own body form and function.

## Description

As technologies like bioelectric regeneration and genetic editing enable radical changes to the human form, this ethical framework asserts that individuals have the fundamental right to maintain, modify, or enhance their physical bodies. It serves as a defense against bioconservative restrictions on longevity and enhancement technologies.
