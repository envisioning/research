# Extracellular Matrix Remodeling Devices

## Summary

Targeted ultrasound and enzymatic systems that break down aged, crosslinked ECM and stimulate fresh matrix deposition.

## Description

Age-related tissue stiffening is driven by accumulated crosslinks (like advanced glycation end-products) in the extracellular matrix. These devices use focused ultrasound, light, or localized enzyme delivery to disrupt pathological crosslinks in skin, blood vessels, and organs, restoring tissue elasticity and improving cell-matrix signaling crucial for regeneration.
