# Longevity Intervention Insurance Models

## Summary

Financial and insurance frameworks for covering preventative and rejuvenation therapies.

## Description

Traditional healthcare insurance focuses on treating disease; longevity interventions are often preventative and fall outside coverage. These models reframe insurance to incentivize healthspan extension through coverage of senolytics, cellular therapies, and biomarker monitoring, aligning payer incentives with long-term health outcomes and reduced late-life medical costs.
