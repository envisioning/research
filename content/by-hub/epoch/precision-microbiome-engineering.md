# Precision Microbiome Engineering

## Summary

Targeted modification of gut microbiota to restore youthful metabolic and immune profiles.

## Description

The gut microbiome changes with age, contributing to inflammation, immune dysfunction, and metabolic disease. This approach uses defined consortia of beneficial bacteria, phage therapy to remove pathobionts, and prebiotics/postbiotics tailored to individual microbiome profiles. Goals include enhanced longevity metabolite production (butyrate, urolithin A) and reduced systemic inflammation.
