# Reproductive Longevity Therapeutics

## Summary

Interventions specifically targeting ovarian aging to prolong fertility and delay systemic female aging.

## Description

The ovaries age faster than any other organ, driving systemic aging in women through hormonal loss. These therapies—ranging from mTOR inhibitors to specific ovarian rejuvenation factors—aim to maintain youthful ovarian function, delaying menopause and its associated risks of cardiovascular disease, osteoporosis, and cognitive decline.
