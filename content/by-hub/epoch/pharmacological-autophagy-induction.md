# Pharmacological Autophagy Induction

## Summary

Drugs and compounds that activate cellular recycling pathways to clear damaged proteins and organelles.

## Description

Autophagy declines with age, leading to accumulation of cellular debris. Interventions like rapamycin (mTOR inhibition), spermidine, and novel autophagy enhancers restore this quality control system, removing dysfunctional mitochondria, misfolded proteins, and lipofuscin aggregates to improve cellular function and extend healthspan.
