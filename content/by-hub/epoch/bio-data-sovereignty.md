# Bio-Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Legal structures ensuring individuals own the rights to their biological and genomic data.

## Description

New models of data ownership where individuals retain control over their genetic and physiological data generated by health monitoring and treatments. This prevents exploitation by pharmaceutical companies and insurers, allowing individuals to decide how their data is used and monetization.
