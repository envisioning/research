# Therapeutic Plasma Exchange

## Summary

Blood renewal protocols that remove pro-aging factors and introduce youthful circulating factors.

## Description

Inspired by heterochronic parabiosis research, these clinical procedures use plasmapheresis or albumin dilution to filter out senescence-associated proteins, chemokines, and inflammatory mediators from aged blood. Optional supplementation with young donor plasma components or engineered rejuvenating factors aims to systemically restore tissue regenerative capacity.
