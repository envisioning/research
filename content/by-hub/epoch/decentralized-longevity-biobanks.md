# Decentralized Longevity Biobanks

## Summary

Blockchain-enabled platforms for secure, global sharing of aging biomarker and intervention data.

## Description

Distributed database architectures that allow individuals to contribute longitudinal health data, biosamples, and intervention outcomes while retaining ownership and privacy via cryptographic protocols. These biobanks aggregate massive, diverse datasets to power AI discovery of novel aging biomarkers and accelerate validation of therapies across global populations.
