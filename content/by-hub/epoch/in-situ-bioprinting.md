# In-Situ Bioprinting Arms

## Summary

Robotic systems capable of 3D printing skin and tissue directly onto patients for rapid wound healing and regeneration.

## Description

Advanced robotic arms equipped with bio-ink nozzles that scan a patient's injury in real-time and deposit layers of living cells, growth factors, and biomaterial scaffolds directly into the wound bed. This technology accelerates healing, reduces scarring, and is critical for treating severe burns and complex tissue damage.
