# Closed-Loop Metabolic Therapies

## Summary

Automated systems that combine sensors, algorithms, and drug delivery to maintain youthful metabolic profiles.

## Description

Integrated therapeutic platforms linking continuous glucose, lipid, and hormone monitoring with infusion pumps and smart dosing algorithms. Building on artificial pancreas concepts, these systems orchestrate combinations of insulin, GLP-1 agonists, and other metabolic modulators in real time to prevent metabolic syndrome, preserve mitochondrial function, and support long-term healthspan.
