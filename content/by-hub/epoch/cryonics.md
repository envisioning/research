# Cryonics

## Summary

Cryopreservation of humans at death for potential future revival.

## Description

Cryonics involves cooling legally deceased individuals to cryogenic temperatures for potential future revival. Hypothetical, research, commercialization (Alcor, Cryonics Institute). Applications: life extension. Scientific consensus: revival not currently feasible; future technology may or may not enable it.
