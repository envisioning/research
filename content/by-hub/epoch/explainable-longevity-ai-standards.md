# Longevity Model Interpretability Standards

## Summary

Requirements for transparency in digital twins and aging clocks to avoid misdiagnosis or incorrect predictions.

## Description

Standards mandating that AI models used for life-critical longevity decisions (e.g., predicting death or prescribing experimental therapies) must be 'explainable' rather than black boxes. This ensures that clinicians and patients can understand the biological reasoning behind AI predictions, building trust and preventing algorithmic errors.
