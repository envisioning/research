# Implantable Multi-Omics Sensor Grids

## Summary

Networks of implanted biosensors that continuously track molecular aging markers across the body.

## Description

Miniaturized electrochemical and optical sensors embedded in subcutaneous or vascular implants, sampling metabolites, hormones, inflammatory markers, and other biomarkers in real-time. Data streams are analyzed by AI models to detect early signs of age-related decline, guide personalized interventions, and validate the impact of rejuvenation therapies on a continuous basis.
