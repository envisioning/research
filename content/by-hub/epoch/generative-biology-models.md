# Generative Biology Models

## Summary

AI systems that design novel proteins and enzymes for metabolic enhancement not found in nature.

## Description

Machine learning algorithms, similar to large language models but trained on biological data, that generate blueprints for completely new proteins. These synthetic molecules can be designed to break down metabolic waste products, enhance cellular energy production, or target specific aging mechanisms with precision impossible for natural evolution.
