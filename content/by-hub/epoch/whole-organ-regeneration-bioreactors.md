# Whole-Organ Regeneration Bioreactors

## Summary

Perfusion bioreactors that repair, rejuvenate, and recellularize donor or patient organs ex vivo.

## Description

Advanced bioreactor systems capable of decellularizing damaged organs, removing senescent cells, and reseeding scaffolds with induced pluripotent stem cell–derived progenitors. By optimizing flow, oxygenation, and biochemical cues, these platforms aim to restore organ function prior to transplantation and enable cyclic organ rejuvenation instead of single-use transplants.
