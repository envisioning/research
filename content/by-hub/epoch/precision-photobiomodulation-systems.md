# Precision Photobiomodulation Systems

## Summary

Light-delivery hardware tuned to mitochondrial rejuvenation wavelengths.

## Description

Advanced optical systems delivering specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light to target tissues (e.g., brain, skin, deep muscle). By stimulating cytochrome c oxidase in mitochondria, these devices enhance ATP production, reduce oxidative stress, and accelerate cellular repair, with applications ranging from cognitive enhancement to wound healing.
