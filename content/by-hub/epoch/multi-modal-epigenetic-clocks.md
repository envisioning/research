# Multi-Modal Epigenetic Clocks

## Summary

Next-generation biological age predictors integrating methylation, transcriptomics, proteomics, and imaging data.

## Description

Advanced machine learning models that synthesize DNA methylation patterns, RNA expression profiles, metabolite levels, and imaging biomarkers into unified estimates of biological age and mortality risk. These clocks provide high-resolution, organ-specific aging profiles and are used to validate rejuvenation therapies with unprecedented precision.
