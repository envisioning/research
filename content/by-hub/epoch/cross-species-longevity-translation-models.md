# Cross-Species Longevity Translation Models

## Summary

AI systems that translate longevity interventions from model organisms to human therapeutics.

## Description

Computational platforms trained on comparative genomics, lifespan studies, and intervention outcomes across species (yeast, worms, flies, mice, primates). These models identify conserved aging pathways and predict which compounds or genetic modifications successful in short-lived organisms will translate to human healthspan extension, accelerating preclinical research.
