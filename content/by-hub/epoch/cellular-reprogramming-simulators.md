# Cellular Reprogramming Simulators

## Summary

Software predicting OSK/OSKM induction thresholds, rejuvenation windows, and reprogramming risk profiles.

## Description

Computational models that simulate the transcriptional landscapes of cells undergoing partial reprogramming. These tools predict the optimal dosage and timing of Yamanaka factors to achieve rejuvenation (resetting the epigenome) without crossing the threshold into dedifferentiation or oncogenesis, critical for safe clinical translation.
