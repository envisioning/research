# Engineered Thymic Organoid Grafts

## Summary

Lab-grown thymus tissue implants designed to regenerate immune function lost with age.

## Description

The thymus atrophies with age, leading to immune senescence and declining T-cell diversity. These grafts are grown from pluripotent stem cells and thymic epithelial progenitors, then surgically implanted or encapsulated in biocompatible scaffolds. They restore naive T-cell production, improving vaccine responses, cancer immunosurveillance, and infection resistance in the elderly.
