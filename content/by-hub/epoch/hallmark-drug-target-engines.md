# Drug-Target Prediction Engines

## Summary

Systems that map aging hallmarks to molecular targets across biological pathways.

## Description

Knowledge graph-based AI platforms that systematically link the hallmarks of aging (e.g., genomic instability, mitochondrial dysfunction) to druggable molecular targets. These engines accelerate drug discovery by identifying non-obvious connections between aging mechanisms and potential therapeutic compounds, repurposing existing drugs or designing new ones.
