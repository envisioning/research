# Enhancement vs. Therapy Boundary Standards

## Summary

Ethical guidelines distinguishing medical treatment from human enhancement in longevity context.

## Description

As interventions move beyond treating age-related disease to enhancing human capabilities and extending maximum lifespan, regulatory and ethical boundaries blur. These standards propose criteria for determining when an intervention is therapeutic (restoring function) versus enhancement (exceeding baseline), with implications for approval, access, and social acceptance.
