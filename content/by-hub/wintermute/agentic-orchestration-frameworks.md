# Agentic Orchestration Frameworks

## Summary

Tool-using agents coordinated via high-level workflows and policies.

## Description

Agentic orchestration frameworks provide the infrastructure and tooling needed to coordinate multiple AI agents that can use tools, access APIs, and perform complex tasks, managing their interactions, memory, task delegation, and safety according to high-level workflows and policies. These systems enable composition of complex processes from modular AI services, where different agents handle different aspects of a task and coordinate through the orchestration layer.

This innovation addresses the challenge of scaling AI capabilities beyond single-agent systems, enabling complex workflows that require multiple steps, different types of expertise, or parallel processing. By providing orchestration infrastructure, these frameworks make it possible to build sophisticated AI systems that can handle complex, multi-step tasks like research, software development, or business operations. Companies and platforms are developing these capabilities, with frameworks like LangGraph, AutoGPT, and various enterprise platforms enabling agentic workflows.

The technology is becoming essential infrastructure for deploying AI in complex applications where single agents are insufficient. As AI agents become more capable and tool-using becomes standard, orchestration frameworks provide the coordination layer needed to compose agents into effective systems. However, the technology faces challenges including ensuring reliability, managing agent interactions, preventing errors from propagating, and maintaining safety and control as systems become more autonomous.
