# Integrated Data & AI Governance

## Summary

Federated frameworks ensuring transparency and accountability for automated AI agents.

## Description

Integrated data and AI governance holds steady in the top four global trends, confirming that cultural and organizational foundations are just as essential as technical measures. The approach is moving from central control to decentralized, federated frameworks that ensure transparency and accountability for automated AI agents. It addresses critical questions about how models are trained, explained, and validated to ensure fairness across the enterprise.

Europe continues to take a more conservative stance, emphasizing data governance as a priority, while best-in-class companies recognize governance as central to building trust, agility, and scalability.

This trend addresses the growing need for frameworks that can manage both traditional data assets and the new complexities introduced by AI systems.
