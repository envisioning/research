# Management-Led Data Culture

## Summary

Embedding a data mindset as a leadership imperative to drive operational agility.

## Description

Data-driven culture has firmly established itself as a top global priority, holding steady in the top four trends. Culture is no longer a 'soft topic' but a management success factor that best-in-class companies recognize as a pillar for innovation.

The performance gap is significant: best-in-class companies rate this at 8.4, while laggards rate it at 7.1, demonstrating that leaders understand the critical role of cultural transformation. Best-in-class companies continue to distinguish themselves by prioritizing data-driven culture as an indispensable prerequisite for unlocking value from new technologies.

This human-centric driver has evolved from a 'soft skill' into a critical management performance indicator that drives operational agility and competitive advantage.
