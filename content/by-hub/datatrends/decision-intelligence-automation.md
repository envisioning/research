# Operational Decision Intelligence

## Summary

The convergence of data and AI to manage decision complexity at scale.

## Description

Decision intelligence and automation moves beyond AI hype into established practice, representing the convergence of data and AI to manage decision complexity at scale. Best-in-class companies emphasize this trend to automate routine operational decisions like fraud detection, dynamic pricing, and supply chain optimization.

The performance gap is substantial: leaders rate its importance at 6.89, while laggards rate it at 4.63, demonstrating that advanced organizations are significantly ahead in operationalizing AI-driven decision-making. Asia and Pacific consistently rates decision intelligence and automation as highly important, signaling strong momentum for AI-driven transformation.

This trend enables organizations to turn innovation into action, creating scalable decision systems that deliver tangible business impact.
