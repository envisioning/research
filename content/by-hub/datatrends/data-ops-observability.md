# Data Ops & Observability

## Summary

Agile automation and real-time monitoring of data health in motion.

## Description

Data Ops and observability provides agile automation and real-time monitoring of data health in motion, minimizing the business risks of unsound decisions by ensuring pipelines are grounded in accurate information. North America assigns higher value to data ops, reflecting a push for scalable architectures and operational excellence.

This trend applies DevOps principles to data management, enabling continuous integration, delivery, and monitoring of data pipelines. Observability goes beyond traditional monitoring to provide deep insights into data lineage, quality metrics, and pipeline performance, enabling proactive issue detection and resolution.

By ensuring data pipelines are reliable, performant, and well-documented, organizations can build trust in their data assets and accelerate time-to-insight while reducing operational risks.
