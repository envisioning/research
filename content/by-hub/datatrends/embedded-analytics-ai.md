# Embedded Analytics & AI

## Summary

Integrating insights directly into business apps to close the action loop.

## Description

Embedded analytics and AI has achieved widespread adoption, with 65% of vendor customers now using embedded BI. This trend integrates insights directly into business applications to close the action loop, empowering employees to act within native application environments without switching contexts.

North America assigns higher value to embedded analytics and AI, reflecting a push for scalable architectures and seamless user experiences. The integration of analytics and AI capabilities directly into operational workflows enables real-time decision-making at the point of action, transforming how employees interact with data.

This approach moves beyond traditional dashboards to create contextual, actionable intelligence that drives immediate business outcomes.
