# Agentic AI

## Summary

Autonomous systems capable of planning and coordinating multi-step goals.

## Description

Agentic AI represents autonomous systems capable of planning and coordinating multi-step goals, creating content and taking actions that did not previously exist. A debut trend for 2026, it currently sits at the bottom of rankings, with momentum being primarily vendor-driven.

Europe assigns lower importance to newer innovations such as agentic AI, reflecting a more conservative stance, while the technology holds potential for proactive quality management and autonomous data operations. These systems learn from large amounts of example data to create new outputs that match learned patterns, representing a significant evolution from reactive AI to proactive, goal-oriented systems.

The early-stage nature of this trend suggests it may gain prominence as use cases mature and demonstrate clear business value.
