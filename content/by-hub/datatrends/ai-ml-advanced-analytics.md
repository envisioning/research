# AI / ML / Advanced Analytics

## Summary

Core enabler of automation and innovation through machine learning and advanced analytical techniques.

## Description

AI, ML, and advanced analytics continue to rank below foundational elements in global importance, yet their growing role as enablers of automation and innovation is evident across industries. Machine learning, a major branch of artificial intelligence, automates rule derivation from large data sets through supervised learning (where models train on known outcomes to predict new results) and unsupervised learning (which infers rules from data structures alone).

In implementations often equated with data mining, machine learning's techniques are a subset of advanced analytics and the data scientist's skill set. While AI/ML continues to rank below foundational elements like data quality and security, their growing role as enablers of automation and innovation is evident across industries. Services score above average in AI/ML/advanced analytics, reflecting sector-specific adoption patterns.

This trend represents the core technology foundation that enables decision intelligence, automation, and generative AI capabilities.
