# Data Preparation by Business Users

## Summary

Self-service data preparation tools enabling business users to clean, transform, and prepare data without IT dependency.

## Description

Data preparation by business users empowers non-technical users to clean, transform, and prepare data for analysis without requiring IT intervention, reducing bottlenecks and accelerating time-to-insight. This trend extends the self-service analytics movement by providing intuitive tools that enable business users to perform data quality checks, handle missing values, merge datasets, and create calculated fields using visual interfaces.

By reducing dependency on IT teams for data preparation tasks, organizations can democratize data access and enable faster decision-making. The trend addresses the common challenge where data analysts and business users spend significant time on data preparation rather than analysis.

Modern data preparation tools incorporate AI assistance to suggest transformations, detect data quality issues, and automate repetitive tasks, making data preparation more accessible to business users while maintaining data quality and governance standards.
