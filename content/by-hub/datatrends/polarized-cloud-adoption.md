# Sovereignty-Aware Cloud Analytics

## Summary

Regionalized adoption balancing scalability against cost and regulatory concerns.

## Description

Cloud for data and analytics shows a sharp regional divide that highlights how regional contexts shape adoption speed and investment focus. Cloud is the #1 priority in Asia-Pacific (7.1), reflecting a push for scalable architectures and rapid innovation. However, it sits at the bottom in Europe (5.2) due to sovereignty risks, data residency requirements, and regulatory concerns.

North America also assigns higher value to cloud for data and analytics, reflecting a push for scalable architectures. Vendors continue to highlight cloud as a priority, underlining their push toward future-oriented adoption, while users balance scalability against cost and regulatory concerns.

This polarization demonstrates that cloud adoption is not a one-size-fits-all decision but requires careful consideration of regional regulations, data sovereignty requirements, and organizational risk tolerance.
