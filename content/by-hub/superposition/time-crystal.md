# Time Crystal

## Summary

Quantum systems with persistent periodic behavior; research for stable qubits.

## Description

Time crystals are quantum systems that exhibit periodic motion in their ground state, breaking time-translation symmetry. Research experiments; potential applications in quantum computing with stable qubits. Research continues into realization, stability, and quantum information applications.
