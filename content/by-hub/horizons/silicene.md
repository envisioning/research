# Silicene

## Summary

2D silicon allotrope for nanoscale electronics.

## Description

Silicene is a 2D allotrope of silicon, analogous to graphene. Early research; synthesis on substrates has been demonstrated. Potential applications in nanoscale electronics. Challenges include stability in air and integration with silicon fabrication.
