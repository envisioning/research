# Digital Economic Twins

## Summary

Macro-economic simulation for systemic stress testing.

## Description

AI-driven agent-based models that simulate entire national economies or market ecosystems to stress-test policy changes, predict liquidity crises, and optimize monetary policy interventions in a safe virtual sandbox.
