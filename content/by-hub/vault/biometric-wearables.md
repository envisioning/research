# Biometric Payment Wearables

## Summary

Next-gen devices for secure, frictionless payments.

## Description

Advanced wearable devices (rings, watches, wristbands) and biocompatible implants utilizing vein mapping, heartbeat signatures, or biometric fusion for ultra-secure, smartphone-free authentication and payments.
