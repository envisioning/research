# Satellite Payment Infrastructure

## Summary

Global connectivity for banking in remote regions.

## Description

Low-earth-orbit satellite constellations providing ubiquitous payment connectivity to underserved, rural, maritime, and disaster-affected areas, enabling mobile money, card payments, and CBDC access anywhere on Earth.
