# Stablecoin Payment Corridors

## Summary

Dollar-backed instant cross-border payments.

## Description

Fiat-backed stablecoins (USDC, USDT, PYUSD) and blockchain rails enabling near-instant, low-cost cross-border remittances, B2B payments, and treasury operations, bypassing traditional correspondent banking networks.
