# CBDC & Digital Currency Platforms

## Summary

Infrastructure for programmable sovereign money.

## Description

Distributed ledger platforms for central bank digital currencies with programmable money features, offline transaction support, privacy controls, interoperability with commercial bank money, and cross-border settlement capabilities.
