# Financial Inclusion Infrastructure

## Summary

Technology for the unbanked and underbanked.

## Description

Digital identity systems, agent networks, alternative credit scoring (telco, utility data), and mobile money interoperability platforms designed to bring 1.4 billion unbanked people into the formal financial system.
