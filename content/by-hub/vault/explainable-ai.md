# Explainable AI for Financial Decisions

## Summary

Transparent, auditable machine learning.

## Description

Model interpretability frameworks (SHAP, LIME, counterfactuals) providing human-readable explanations for credit denials, pricing decisions, and risk assessments—enabling regulatory compliance, customer trust, and appeals.
