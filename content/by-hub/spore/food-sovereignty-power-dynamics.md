# Food Sovereignty & Power Dynamics

## Summary

Preventing concentration of control within seed and synthetic biology platforms.

## Description

Ensuring food sovereignty involves preventing the concentration of power in seed and data platforms. Strategies include guaranteeing smallholder access to high-performance tools without dependency lock-in, fostering a more equitable agricultural landscape.
