# RNA Interference Pesticides

## Summary

Gene-silencing sprays targeting specific pests.

## Description

RNAi formulations deliver double-stranded RNA that silences lethal genes in targeted pests such as Colorado potato beetle, providing species-specific protection without harming beneficial insects.
