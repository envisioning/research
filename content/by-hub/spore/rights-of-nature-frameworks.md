# Rights of Nature Legal Frameworks

## Summary

Legal recognition of ecosystems as entities with rights, impacting land use.

## Description

Emerging legal frameworks that grant personhood or specific rights to ecosystems (rivers, soil systems, forests). For agriculture, this shifts the regulatory baseline from 'resource management' to 'co-existence', potentially mandating regenerative practices not just for yield, but as a legal obligation to the land itself.
