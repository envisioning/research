# CRISPR-Mediated Viral Resistance

## Summary

Gene-editing tools granting crops 'immune systems' against specific viruses.

## Description

Software-guided design of CRISPR-Cas systems that are inserted into plant genomes to detect and cut viral DNA. This effectively gives crops a programmable, heritable immune system against devastating pathogens like Cassava Mosaic Virus or Cacao Swollen Shoot Virus, securing staple food supplies.
