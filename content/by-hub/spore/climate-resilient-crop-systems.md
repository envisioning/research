# Climate-Resilient Crop Systems

## Summary

Drought-tolerant crops and seed coatings with microbial consortia.

## Description

Climate-resilient crop systems pair genome-edited or marker-selected varieties engineered for drought, salinity, and heat tolerance with bioactive seed coatings that deliver microbial consortia, biostimulants, and slow-release nutrients. Agronomists integrate these genetics with adaptive planting densities, supplemental irrigation scheduling, and stress-forecasting models to maintain yields under volatile weather.

National food-security programs, seed companies, and humanitarian agencies deploy these systems in climate hotspots to stabilize staple production and reduce reliance on emergency imports. Partnerships like CIMMYT’s stress-tolerant maize and AgBiome’s biological coatings showcase how genetics and microbiomes work in tandem.

Scaling requires locally adapted germplasm, regulatory approval for gene-edited traits, and extension services that translate complex protocols for smallholders. As climate models downscale to farm level, decision-support tools will help growers switch varieties or coatings dynamically. Financing mechanisms that reward resilience metrics rather than pure yield will accelerate adoption.
