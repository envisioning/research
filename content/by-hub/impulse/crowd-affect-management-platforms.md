# Crowd Affect Management Platforms

## Summary

Real-time modulation of collective mood at scale.

## Description

Platforms used in venues, transit hubs, and public spaces that fuse video analytics, sound level monitoring, and social media feeds to infer crowd affect. They orchestrate lighting, soundscapes, and messaging to defuse tension, prevent panic, or increase engagement during events and emergencies.
