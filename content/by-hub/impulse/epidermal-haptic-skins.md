# Epidermal Haptic Skins

## Summary

Ultra-thin, flexible wearable interfaces.

## Description

Soft, stretchable electronic skins that adhere directly to the body like a tattoo. Embedded with micro-actuators and thermal elements, they provide localized tactile and thermal feedback, enabling realistic simulations of touch, texture, and temperature for immersive sensory modulation.
