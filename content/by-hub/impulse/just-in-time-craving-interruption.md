# Just-in-Time Craving Interruption Systems

## Summary

Wearable-triggered micro-interventions at peak craving.

## Description

Integrated systems that detect precursors to craving episodes—such as specific movement patterns, heart rate variability shifts, or geolocation cues—and trigger short, multi-sensory interventions. These may include haptic patterns, guided micro-meditations, or contextual messaging aimed at disrupting automatic consumption loops.
