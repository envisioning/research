# Digital Satiation Therapies

## Summary

VR/AR simulating consumption to manage cravings.

## Description

Therapeutic interventions utilizing immersive VR or AR to simulate the sensory experience of consuming food or substances. By providing visual and auditory cues of consumption, these therapies aim to trigger satiety signals or reduce craving intensity without actual physical intake.
