# Influence Transparency Registries

## Summary

Public catalogs of active persuasive systems.

## Description

Regulatory and industry-operated registries that document the presence, goals, and mechanisms of sensory and behavioral influence systems deployed in public and digital spaces. They aim to give citizens visibility into when and how they are being targeted, enabling informed consent and watchdog oversight.
