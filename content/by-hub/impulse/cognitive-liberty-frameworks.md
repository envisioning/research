# Cognitive Liberty Frameworks

## Summary

Standards protecting mental privacy.

## Description

Emerging legal and technical frameworks aimed at defining and protecting the right to mental self-determination. This includes preventing unauthorized monitoring of neural data, protecting against coercive neuro-interventions, and ensuring freedom of thought in an age of advanced neurotechnology.
