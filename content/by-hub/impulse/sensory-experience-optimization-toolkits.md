# Sensory Experience Optimization Toolkits

## Summary

Design tools tuning color, sound, and scent for affect.

## Description

Computational design environments that combine psychophysical models with optimization algorithms to recommend or auto-generate multisensory configurations. Designers can specify target states—calm, urgency, delight—and receive parameterized settings for visuals, audio, haptics, and scent to achieve them.
