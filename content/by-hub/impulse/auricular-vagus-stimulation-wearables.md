# Auricular Vagus Nerve Stimulation Wearables

## Summary

Ear-worn devices modulating autonomic arousal.

## Description

Wearable devices that deliver transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation through electrodes placed on the outer ear. By modulating parasympathetic activity, these systems aim to reduce stress, dampen cravings, and stabilize mood in everyday contexts without the invasiveness of implanted stimulators.
