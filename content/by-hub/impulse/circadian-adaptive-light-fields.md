# Circadian-Adaptive Light Fields

## Summary

Lighting systems steering mood and alertness.

## Description

Networked lighting infrastructures that dynamically adjust spectrum, intensity, and spatial distribution of light to align with occupants' circadian rhythms and desired affective states. Used in workplaces, vehicles, and homes to enhance alertness, calm, or purchasing propensity through subtle visual modulation.
