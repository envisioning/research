# Digital Olfactory Synthesizers

## Summary

Devices generating precise scent combinations.

## Description

Compact, micro-fluidic devices capable of storing and mixing primary scent cartridges to generate a wide range of complex odors. These synthesizers can be triggered digitally to accompany visual or auditory media, leveraging the olfactory system's direct link to the limbic system to evoke powerful emotional responses or memories.
