# Multi-Function Structures

## Summary

Materials that integrate sensing, actuation, and self-healing.

## Description

Multi-function structures integrate structural load-bearing with sensing, actuation, energy harvesting, or self-healing. Hypothetical, experiments, some prototypes, few commercial. Applications include self-health monitoring, self-healing materials, and adaptive structures. Research continues into embedded sensors, actuation, and scalable fabrication.
