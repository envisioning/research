# Virtual Fitting Room Avatars

## Summary

3D body scanning that creates personalized avatars for trying on clothes digitally

## Description

Virtual fitting rooms generate photorealistic 3D avatars from smartphone scans using computer vision and photogrammetry techniques. These systems capture body measurements and proportions through a series of photos taken from different angles, creating accurate digital representations that can try on garments in real-time with realistic fabric drape and fit visualization.

This innovation addresses the critical challenge of online apparel returns, which cost retailers billions annually and create significant environmental waste. By enabling customers to visualize how garments will actually fit and look on their specific body type before purchase, virtual fitting rooms can reduce return rates by 30-50% while improving customer confidence in online shopping. Major retailers including ASOS, Zara, and H&M are deploying these technologies, while startups like Fit3D, True Fit, and Zeekit (acquired by Walmart) are powering the underlying platforms.

The technology is becoming essential infrastructure for e-commerce as consumers increasingly shop online and demand personalized experiences. As the technology improves in accuracy and becomes more accessible, virtual fitting rooms are enabling inclusive sizing initiatives by allowing customers to see how garments fit across diverse body types, potentially transforming how fashion brands approach size ranges and fit standards.
