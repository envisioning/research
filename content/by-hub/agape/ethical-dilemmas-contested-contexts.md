# Ethical Dilemmas in Contested Contexts

## Summary

Ethical dilemmas of funding in contested or authoritarian contexts, as philanthropy faces hard choices about where to operate.

## Description

Philanthropy increasingly operates in contexts marked by conflict, authoritarianism, or contested legitimacy—places where the act of funding itself becomes ethically fraught. This signal captures dilemmas about whether to fund in these contexts, how to structure programs to avoid co-option or harm, and what responsibilities funders have when operating in contested spaces. These dilemmas reflect fundamental questions about philanthropy's role in systems of power and conflict.
