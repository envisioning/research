# Foundations as Conveners Rather Than Funders

## Summary

Foundations acting as conveners rather than funders, focusing on coordination and facilitation over direct grantmaking.

## Description

Some foundations are shifting from direct grantmaking to convening and facilitation roles—bringing together diverse stakeholders, creating spaces for dialogue and collaboration, and using influence and relationships rather than just capital to drive change. This signal reflects recognition that social change often requires coordination, alignment, and collective action that money alone cannot achieve. It also raises questions about what foundations' core competencies should be.
