# Impact Measurement Fatigue & Reporting Burden

## Summary

Growing recognition that excessive impact measurement requirements burden grantees and may not improve outcomes.

## Description

The philanthropic sector's emphasis on impact measurement has created significant reporting burdens for grantees, consuming resources that could go to programs. This signal captures growing fatigue with measurement requirements that may not improve outcomes, favor large organizations with reporting capacity, and impose funder priorities on grantees. Responses include simplified reporting, trust-based approaches, and questions about whether current measurement practices serve learning or primarily serve funder accountability needs.
