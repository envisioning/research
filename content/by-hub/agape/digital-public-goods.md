# Digital Public Goods Funded by Philanthropy

## Summary

Digital public goods funded and stewarded by philanthropy, creating infrastructure that enables broader social impact.

## Description

Philanthropy is increasingly funding and stewarding digital public goods—open-source software, data platforms, and digital infrastructure that serve public benefit. This signal reflects recognition that technology infrastructure can be a form of philanthropy itself, creating platforms and tools that enable others to create impact. However, it also raises questions about who controls these digital commons and how they are maintained over time.
