# Rise of Giving Circles & Collective Giving

## Summary

Proliferation of giving circles and collective giving models that democratize donor decision-making and build community.

## Description

Giving circles—groups of individuals who pool their charitable contributions and collectively decide how to distribute them—are proliferating across demographics and geographies. This signal captures a shift toward more democratic, relational, and community-oriented giving that challenges the individualistic donor model. Giving circles build donor engagement, financial literacy, and collective agency, while also raising questions about scale, professionalization, and the relationship between collective giving and traditional philanthropy.
