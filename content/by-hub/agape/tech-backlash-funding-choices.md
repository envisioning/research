# Tech Backlash Influencing Funding Choices

## Summary

Tech backlash influencing funding choices and narratives, as critiques of technology reshape philanthropic priorities.

## Description

Growing critiques of technology—from privacy concerns to algorithmic bias to platform power—are influencing how philanthropy funds technology work and shapes narratives about tech-enabled social change. This signal captures how technology skepticism is reshaping funding priorities, evaluation criteria, and the stories philanthropy tells about technology's role in social impact. The backlash reflects broader cultural and political shifts and raises questions about how philanthropy should navigate technology optimism and skepticism.
