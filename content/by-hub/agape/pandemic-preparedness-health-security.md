# Pandemic Preparedness & Global Health Security

## Summary

Post-COVID investment in pandemic preparedness, health security, and the infrastructure to prevent future outbreaks.

## Description

COVID-19 exposed catastrophic gaps in global health security and pandemic preparedness. This signal captures increased philanthropic investment in surveillance systems, vaccine development platforms, manufacturing capacity, and governance mechanisms to prevent or mitigate future pandemics. It also reflects debates about equity in health security, whether preparedness requires centralized or distributed approaches, and how philanthropy should relate to government responsibilities in protecting public health.
