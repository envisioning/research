# Legal Challenges to Perpetual Foundations

## Summary

Legal challenges to perpetual foundations and endowments, questioning the legitimacy of wealth accumulation in perpetuity.

## Description

A growing movement is questioning the legal and ethical foundations of perpetual foundations and endowments. Lawsuits and regulatory challenges are testing whether foundations should be allowed to exist indefinitely, arguing that perpetuity can concentrate power and resources across generations. This signal reflects broader debates about intergenerational equity, the appropriate time horizons for philanthropic action, and whether perpetual structures serve the public good or primarily preserve donor legacies.
