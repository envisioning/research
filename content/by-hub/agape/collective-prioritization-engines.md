# Collective Prioritization Engines

## Summary

Systems that aggregate community preferences and priorities for funding decisions, enabling participatory resource allocation at scale.

## Description

Collective prioritization engines aggregate preferences, priorities, and input from communities to guide funding decisions. These systems use voting mechanisms, preference ranking, or other collective intelligence approaches to surface what communities value most. However, they also raise questions about who participates, how to weight different voices, whether aggregation captures nuance, and how prioritization systems balance majority preferences with minority needs. The engines represent a shift toward participatory philanthropy while highlighting tensions between collective decision-making and expert judgment.
