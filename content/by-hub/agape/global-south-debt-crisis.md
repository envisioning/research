# Global South Debt Crises & Development Finance

## Summary

Sovereign debt crises in the Global South constraining development and requiring philanthropic adaptation.

## Description

Many countries in the Global South face severe debt burdens that constrain public spending, limit development, and create humanitarian crises. This signal captures how philanthropy operates in contexts where government capacity is hollowed out by debt service, debates about debt cancellation and restructuring, and questions about whether philanthropy should fill gaps created by extractive financial systems or advocate for structural change. The intersection of debt, development, and philanthropy raises fundamental questions about global economic justice.
