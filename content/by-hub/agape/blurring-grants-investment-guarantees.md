# Blurring Lines Between Grants, Investment & Guarantees

## Summary

Blurring lines between grants, investment, and guarantees, as philanthropy experiments with hybrid capital instruments.

## Description

Traditional boundaries between grants, investments, and guarantees are dissolving as philanthropy seeks to deploy capital more effectively. This signal captures the emergence of program-related investments, recoverable grants, impact guarantees, and other hybrid instruments that blend charitable giving with market mechanisms. These tools promise to multiply philanthropic capital, but they also raise questions about mission drift, accountability, and whether market logic should shape social impact.
