# Citizen Science & Community-Generated Evidence

## Summary

Growth of citizen science and community-generated research that challenges traditional knowledge hierarchies.

## Description

Communities are increasingly generating their own evidence through citizen science, participatory research, and community-based monitoring. This signal captures the democratization of knowledge production, where affected communities collect data, conduct research, and generate evidence that challenges expert-driven approaches. Philanthropy is funding these efforts while also grappling with how to value community-generated knowledge alongside traditional academic research and how to address power imbalances in knowledge systems.
