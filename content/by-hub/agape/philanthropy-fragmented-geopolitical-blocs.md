# Philanthropy Across Fragmented Geopolitical Blocs

## Summary

Philanthropy operating across fragmented geopolitical blocs, navigating an increasingly divided world order.

## Description

As geopolitical tensions rise and alliances shift, philanthropy operates in an increasingly fragmented world order where cross-border work faces new obstacles. This signal captures how philanthropy navigates sanctions, trade restrictions, currency controls, and political tensions that divide global systems. Foundations must decide whether to operate within blocs, navigate between them, or focus on local rather than global action.
