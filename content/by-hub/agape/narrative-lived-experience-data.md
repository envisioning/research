# Narrative & Lived-Experience Data

## Summary

Narrative and lived-experience data valued alongside metrics, as philanthropy recognizes multiple forms of evidence.

## Description

A growing movement within philanthropy is recognizing that quantitative metrics tell only part of the story. This signal captures the increasing value placed on narrative data, lived experience, qualitative insights, and storytelling as legitimate forms of evidence. These approaches center the voices of those experiencing change, recognize that impact is not always measurable, and challenge the dominance of metrics-driven decision-making.
