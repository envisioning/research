# Decline of Hero-Donor Narratives

## Summary

Decline of hero-donor narratives in favor of collective action, as individualistic giving stories lose cultural resonance.

## Description

Traditional philanthropic narratives center heroic individual donors whose vision and generosity drive change. This signal captures the declining cultural resonance of these hero-donor narratives, replaced by stories of collective action, community-driven change, and systemic transformation that no individual can achieve alone. The shift reflects changing values about leadership, agency, and the relationship between individual action and social change.
