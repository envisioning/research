# Philanthropy Cooperatives & Pooled Funds

## Summary

Emergence of donor cooperatives and pooled funding mechanisms that aggregate resources for collective impact.

## Description

New cooperative structures are emerging that pool philanthropic resources from multiple donors for collective decision-making and impact. These range from formal pooled funds with governance structures to informal donor collaboratives to cooperative models where donors share ownership and control. This signal captures how cooperative approaches can achieve scale, reduce duplication, and enable more strategic giving while also raising questions about governance, power dynamics, and whether aggregation improves or complicates philanthropic decision-making.
