# Pushback Against Financialization of Social Impact

## Summary

Pushback against financialization of social impact, as critiques question whether market logic should shape social change.

## Description

As philanthropy increasingly adopts financial instruments, metrics, and market-based approaches, a counter-movement is emerging that questions the financialization of social impact. This signal captures critiques arguing that social change cannot be reduced to metrics, that market logic distorts missions, and that financial instruments can reinforce inequality rather than address it. The pushback reflects deeper tensions about what counts as legitimate knowledge, valid evidence, and appropriate means for creating social change.
