# Philanthropy Funding Epistemic Infrastructure

## Summary

Philanthropy funding epistemic infrastructure (labs, observatories), investing in knowledge production and sense-making capacity.

## Description

Philanthropy is increasingly funding not just programs but the infrastructure of knowledge production itself—research labs, observatories, data platforms, and sense-making institutions. This signal reflects recognition that addressing complex challenges requires robust knowledge infrastructure, and that philanthropy has a role in supporting the systems that generate insight, foresight, and understanding. However, it also raises questions about what knowledge gets funded and whose perspectives are included.
