# Collapse or Consolidation of Traditional Intermediaries

## Summary

Collapse or consolidation of traditional intermediaries, as direct giving and platform models disrupt traditional structures.

## Description

Traditional philanthropic intermediaries—donor-advised funds, community foundations, fundraising platforms—face pressure from direct giving models, platform-based giving, and shifts in donor preferences. This signal captures how intermediary structures are consolidating, collapsing, or being bypassed as technology enables direct connections and donors seek more control and transparency. The changes reshape the philanthropic ecosystem and raise questions about what functions intermediaries should serve.
