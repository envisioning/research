# AI-Generated Grant Applications & Content

## Summary

Proliferation of AI-generated grant applications creating new challenges for funders and questions about authenticity.

## Description

Generative AI enables rapid production of grant applications, reports, and communications that may obscure the actual capacity and voice of applicants. This signal captures how AI-generated content is reshaping funder-grantee relationships, raising questions about authenticity, capacity assessment, and whether current evaluation processes can distinguish genuine from synthetic applications. It also surfaces equity concerns, as organizations with AI sophistication may gain advantages over those without access or skills.
