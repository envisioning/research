# Time-Bound & Spend-Down Foundations

## Summary

Time-bound or spend-down foundations replacing perpetuity, as new donors choose finite time horizons for their giving.

## Description

A growing number of foundations are choosing to spend down within a generation or specific time frame rather than exist in perpetuity. This signal reflects changing donor values, concerns about maintaining mission relevance over time, and arguments that impact is maximized by deploying all resources rather than preserving endowments. The trend challenges the traditional model of perpetual foundations and raises questions about intergenerational responsibility and the appropriate time horizons for social change.
