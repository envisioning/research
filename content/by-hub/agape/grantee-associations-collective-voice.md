# Grantee Associations & Collective Voice

## Summary

Formation of grantee associations and collective organizing to advocate for better funder practices and power balance.

## Description

Grantees are increasingly organizing collectively to advocate for changes in philanthropic practice—from reducing reporting burdens to increasing unrestricted funding to challenging problematic power dynamics. This signal captures the emergence of grantee associations, collective advocacy, and organized voice that shifts the traditional funder-grantee power imbalance. These efforts reflect broader movements toward worker voice and collective action while raising questions about how philanthropy should respond to organized grantee advocacy.
