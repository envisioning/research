# Participatory Grantmaking

## Summary

Shift from donor-led to community-led decision-making, with participatory grantmaking becoming default rather than experimental.

## Description

Participatory grantmaking represents a fundamental shift in how philanthropic resources are allocated, moving decision-making power from donors to the communities most affected by the issues being addressed. This signal reflects a growing recognition that those closest to problems often have the best insights into solutions. By 2045, this approach may become the norm rather than the exception, fundamentally changing the power dynamics of philanthropy.
