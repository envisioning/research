# Migration, Displacement & Statelessness

## Summary

Migration, displacement, and statelessness shaping priorities, as forced movement becomes a defining feature of 2045.

## Description

Climate change, conflict, economic disruption, and other forces are driving migration and displacement at unprecedented scales. This signal captures how philanthropy is responding to forced movement, statelessness, and the needs of displaced populations. It reflects recognition that migration and displacement will be defining features of the coming decades, requiring new approaches to rights, services, and belonging that transcend traditional nation-state frameworks.
