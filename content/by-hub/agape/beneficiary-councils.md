# Beneficiary Councils with Veto Power

## Summary

Rise of beneficiary councils with veto or allocation power, creating new accountability mechanisms driven by affected communities.

## Description

Beneficiary councils represent an evolution of participatory approaches, giving communities not just input but actual decision-making authority. These councils may have veto power over funding decisions or direct allocation authority, creating accountability mechanisms that flow upward from affected communities rather than downward from donors. This signal challenges traditional notions of who has the right to decide how philanthropic resources are used.
