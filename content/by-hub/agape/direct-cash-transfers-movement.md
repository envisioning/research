# Direct Cash Transfers & Give Directly Movement

## Summary

Growing movement toward unconditional direct cash transfers as the most efficient and dignified form of aid.

## Description

The direct cash transfer movement challenges traditional program-based philanthropy by arguing that simply giving money to people in need is more effective, dignified, and efficient than designing programs for them. Organizations like GiveDirectly have demonstrated strong evidence for cash transfers, prompting broader adoption and debate. This signal captures a fundamental challenge to the philanthropic sector's existence—if cash is best, what is the role of foundations, NGOs, and the entire apparatus of programmatic intervention?
