# Tension Between Evidence-Based & Values-Based Action

## Summary

Tension between evidence-based and values-based action, as philanthropy navigates what counts as legitimate justification.

## Description

Philanthropy increasingly faces tension between evidence-based approaches that rely on data and outcomes, and values-based approaches that prioritize principles, ethics, and moral commitments regardless of measurable impact. This signal captures debates about whether social change should be driven by evidence or values, whether all important outcomes are measurable, and how to navigate situations where evidence and values conflict.
