# Smart-Contract Logic for Conditional Giving

## Summary

Programmable giving with automated conditions, enabling trustless, transparent, and self-executing philanthropic transactions.

## Description

Smart contracts enable conditional giving where funds are automatically released when predefined conditions are met, without requiring intermediaries or manual oversight. This technology promises to reduce overhead, increase transparency, and enable new forms of trustless giving. However, it also raises questions about how to encode complex social outcomes in code, whether automated execution reduces flexibility needed for adaptive interventions, and how smart contracts handle edge cases or unintended consequences. The technology represents a shift toward programmable philanthropy while highlighting tensions between automation and human judgment.
