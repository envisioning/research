# Philanthropy Underwriting Public Infrastructure

## Summary

Philanthropy underwriting public infrastructure and risk, as private capital steps in where public resources fall short.

## Description

Philanthropy is increasingly providing the capital, guarantees, and risk absorption that enable public infrastructure projects—from social housing to transportation to digital infrastructure. This signal reflects philanthropy stepping into roles traditionally held by governments, using philanthropic capital to unlock public or private co-investment. While this can accelerate needed infrastructure, it raises questions about democratic control, long-term sustainability, and whether private wealth should subsidize public responsibilities.
