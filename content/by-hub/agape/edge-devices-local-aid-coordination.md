# Edge Devices for Local Aid Coordination

## Summary

Distributed computing devices enabling offline and remote coordination of aid delivery in areas with limited connectivity.

## Description

Edge computing devices enable local aid coordination in areas with limited or unreliable internet connectivity. These devices can process data, coordinate resources, and manage logistics locally without requiring constant connection to central servers. However, they also raise questions about data synchronization, how to maintain consistency across distributed systems, and whether edge devices create fragmentation or enable better local autonomy. The devices represent a shift toward resilient, distributed aid infrastructure while highlighting tensions between centralization and localization.
