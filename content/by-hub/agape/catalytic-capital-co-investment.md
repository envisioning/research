# Catalytic Capital to Unlock Co-Investment

## Summary

Use of catalytic capital to unlock public/private co-investment, using philanthropy to mobilize larger pools of capital.

## Description

Philanthropy is increasingly positioning itself as catalytic capital—using relatively small amounts of philanthropic funding to unlock larger pools of public or private investment. This signal reflects strategic use of philanthropic capital to absorb risk, provide guarantees, or demonstrate proof-of-concept that enables others to invest. While this can multiply impact, it also raises questions about whether philanthropy should focus on what others won't fund rather than enabling what others might fund anyway.
