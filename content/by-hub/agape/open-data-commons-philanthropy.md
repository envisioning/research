# Open Data Commons for Philanthropic Intelligence

## Summary

Open data commons for philanthropic intelligence, creating shared knowledge infrastructure for the field.

## Description

Philanthropy is increasingly investing in open data platforms, shared knowledge bases, and collaborative intelligence systems that serve the entire field rather than individual institutions. This signal reflects recognition that addressing complex challenges requires shared understanding, and that philanthropy benefits from open rather than proprietary intelligence. However, it also raises questions about data ownership, privacy, and who benefits from these knowledge commons.
