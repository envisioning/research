# Automated Grantmaking Platforms

## Summary

End-to-end systems automating grant allocation from application to disbursement, reducing overhead and increasing efficiency.

## Description

Automated grantmaking platforms streamline the entire grant process—from application submission to review to disbursement—using technology to reduce manual work and overhead. These systems promise to make grantmaking faster, cheaper, and more accessible while enabling funders to process more applications. However, they also raise questions about whether automation reduces or reproduces bias, how to maintain human judgment in automated processes, and whether efficiency gains come at the cost of relationships and nuance. The platforms represent a shift toward streamlined, technology-mediated grantmaking while highlighting tensions between efficiency and human connection.
