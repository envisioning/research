# Philanthropy Embedded in Movements

## Summary

Philanthropy embedded inside movements rather than institutions, as giving becomes more movement-aligned and grassroots.

## Description

Traditional philanthropy operates through institutions—foundations, NGOs, charities—that exist separately from the movements and communities they serve. This signal captures how philanthropy is becoming more embedded within movements themselves, with giving structures that are movement-aligned, community-controlled, or directly integrated into organizing efforts. This shift challenges the separation between funders and funded, and raises questions about how philanthropy should relate to social movements.
