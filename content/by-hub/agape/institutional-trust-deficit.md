# Institutional Trust Deficit Affecting Philanthropy

## Summary

Declining public trust in institutions extending to foundations and large-scale philanthropy.

## Description

Public trust in institutions of all kinds—government, media, corporations—has declined precipitously, and philanthropy is not immune. This signal captures how declining institutional trust affects foundations' ability to operate, partner, and maintain legitimacy. It raises questions about whether traditional philanthropic structures can rebuild trust, whether new forms of giving that bypass institutions will dominate, and how philanthropy should respond to a low-trust environment where its intentions and impacts are questioned.
