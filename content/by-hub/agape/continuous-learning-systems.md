# Shift from Evaluation to Continuous Learning

## Summary

Shift from evaluation to continuous learning systems, moving beyond periodic assessments to real-time adaptive intelligence.

## Description

Traditional evaluation models rely on periodic assessments and retrospective analysis. This signal captures the shift toward continuous learning systems that provide real-time feedback, adaptive intelligence, and ongoing sense-making about what's working and why. These systems emphasize learning over judgment, adaptation over accountability, and recognize that complex social change requires ongoing navigation rather than linear planning.
