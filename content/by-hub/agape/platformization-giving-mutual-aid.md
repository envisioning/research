# Platformization of Giving & Mutual Aid

## Summary

Platformization of giving and mutual aid, as technology enables new forms of resource sharing and coordination.

## Description

Technology platforms are transforming how giving happens, enabling direct peer-to-peer support, mutual aid networks, and distributed resource sharing. This signal captures how platforms can bypass traditional intermediaries, connect needs with resources more directly, and enable forms of giving that are more responsive and community-driven. However, it also raises questions about platform governance, power concentration, and whether technology-mediated giving strengthens or weakens relationships.
