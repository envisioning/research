# Mission-Aligned Endowments & 100% Deployment

## Summary

Growth of mission-aligned endowments and debates around 100% deployment, challenging the perpetuity model of foundations.

## Description

The movement toward mission-aligned investing and 100% deployment challenges the traditional foundation model of maintaining endowments in perpetuity. This signal reflects growing pressure to align all assets with mission, not just grantmaking budgets, and questions whether foundations should exist forever or spend down within a generation. The debate touches on questions of intergenerational equity, impact maximization, and the appropriate role of philanthropic capital.
