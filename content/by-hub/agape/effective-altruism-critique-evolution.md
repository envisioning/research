# Effective Altruism Critique & Evolution

## Summary

Post-FTX reckoning with effective altruism, challenging its assumptions while elements are absorbed into mainstream philanthropy.

## Description

The collapse of FTX and associated scandals have triggered a profound reckoning with effective altruism (EA) and its influence on philanthropy. This signal captures critiques of EA's assumptions about measurability, its demographic homogeneity, its relationship to power and wealth, and its longtermist turn. Yet elements of EA—rigorous impact analysis, cause prioritization, evidence-based giving—continue to influence mainstream philanthropy. The debate reflects deeper questions about how philanthropy should make decisions and whose values should guide them.
