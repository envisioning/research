# Exit to Community & Steward Ownership

## Summary

Companies and organizations transitioning to community ownership or steward-ownership models supported by philanthropy.

## Description

A growing movement supports companies and organizations transitioning from traditional ownership to community ownership or steward-ownership models—where control rests with workers, users, or communities rather than investors. Philanthropy is supporting these transitions and experimenting with similar models internally. This signal reflects broader questioning of extractive ownership and the exploration of alternative structures that align economic activity with social purpose and community benefit.
