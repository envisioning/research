# Longtermism & Intergenerational Ethics Debates

## Summary

Philosophical and practical debates about prioritizing future generations and the ethics of longtermist philanthropy.

## Description

Longtermism argues that positively influencing the long-term future should be a key moral priority, potentially justifying focus on future generations over present needs. This signal captures intense debates about whether longtermism is a legitimate ethical framework or a justification for neglecting present suffering, whether future people have moral standing comparable to present people, and how philanthropy should weigh present versus future impacts. These philosophical debates have practical implications for funding priorities.
