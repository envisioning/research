# Participatory Funding Apps

## Summary

Mobile and web applications enabling community-driven funding decisions, putting resource allocation power in the hands of beneficiaries.

## Description

Participatory funding apps enable communities to propose, discuss, and vote on funding decisions through mobile and web interfaces. These applications make participatory grantmaking accessible at scale by reducing barriers to participation and enabling remote engagement. However, they also raise questions about digital divides, how to ensure meaningful participation beyond voting, whether app-based participation replaces or complements in-person engagement, and how to handle governance and accountability in digital spaces. The apps represent a shift toward accessible, scalable participatory philanthropy while highlighting tensions between digital and in-person engagement.
