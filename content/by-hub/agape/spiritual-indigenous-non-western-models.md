# Spiritual, Indigenous & Non-Western Models

## Summary

Spiritual, indigenous, and non-Western models gaining visibility, as diverse traditions reshape philanthropic practice.

## Description

Philanthropic models rooted in spiritual traditions, indigenous practices, and non-Western cultural frameworks are gaining visibility and influence. This signal captures how diverse traditions of giving, mutual aid, stewardship, and social responsibility are reshaping philanthropy, challenging Western dominance, and offering alternative frameworks for understanding wealth, obligation, and social change. These models often emphasize collective rather than individual action, relational rather than transactional giving, and long-term stewardship rather than short-term impact.
