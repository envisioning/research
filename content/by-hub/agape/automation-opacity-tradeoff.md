# Automation Reducing Overhead, Increasing Opacity

## Summary

Automation reducing overhead but increasing opacity, as efficiency gains come with transparency costs.

## Description

Technology automation promises to reduce overhead and increase efficiency in philanthropic operations, but these gains often come with reduced transparency about how decisions are made. This signal captures the tradeoff between operational efficiency and procedural transparency—when AI systems handle grant triage, when algorithms allocate resources, or when automation streamlines processes, it can become harder to understand, challenge, or modify those processes. The efficiency-opacity tradeoff raises questions about accountability and control.
