# Funding to Combat Misinformation & Epistemic Crisis

## Summary

Growing philanthropic investment in combating misinformation, supporting journalism, and addressing the broader epistemic crisis.

## Description

As misinformation, disinformation, and declining trust in institutions threaten democratic discourse, philanthropy is increasingly funding efforts to combat these trends. This signal captures investments in fact-checking, journalism, media literacy, and research on information ecosystems. However, it also raises difficult questions about who decides what is misinformation, whether philanthropic intervention can solve structural problems, and how to support truth-seeking without appearing partisan or paternalistic.
