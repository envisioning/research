# Civic Redistribution Dashboards

## Summary

Public-facing transparency tools showing resource flows, enabling accountability and public oversight of philanthropic and public spending.

## Description

Civic redistribution dashboards provide public-facing transparency about how resources flow through philanthropic and public systems. These tools show where money comes from, where it goes, and what outcomes result, enabling accountability and public oversight. However, they also raise questions about privacy, whether transparency alone enables accountability, how to present complex data accessibly, and whether dashboards create pressure for short-term, measurable outcomes. The dashboards represent a shift toward transparent, accountable resource allocation while highlighting tensions between transparency and privacy.
