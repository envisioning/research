# Prediction Models for Social Outcomes

## Summary

AI and machine learning systems forecasting intervention effectiveness, enabling predictive allocation of philanthropic resources.

## Description

Prediction models use artificial intelligence and machine learning to forecast the likely effectiveness of social interventions before resources are committed. These systems analyze historical data, identify patterns, and predict outcomes to guide funding decisions. However, they also raise questions about whether past patterns predict future outcomes in complex social systems, whether prediction models perpetuate existing biases, and how predictive approaches handle uncertainty and context. The models represent a shift toward anticipatory philanthropy while highlighting tensions between prediction and adaptation in complex social change.
