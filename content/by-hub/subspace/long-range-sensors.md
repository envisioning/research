# Long-Range Sensors

## Summary

Advanced detection array capable of scanning multiple light-years for objects and phenomena.

## Description

Long-range sensor arrays can detect and analyze objects, energy signatures, and spatial phenomena across multiple light-years. These systems use a combination of electromagnetic, subspace, and gravimetric sensing to build comprehensive pictures of surrounding space.The sensor suite can differentiate between natural phenomena and artificial objects, identify vessel classifications, and detect cloaked ships through various analysis techniques. Resolution degrades with distance but remains useful for navigation and threat detection.
