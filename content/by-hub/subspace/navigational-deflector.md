# Navigational Deflector Array

## Summary

Multi-purpose deflector dish providing particle deflection, sensor enhancement, and beam projection.

## Description

The navigational deflector is a primary ship system that projects a deflector field ahead of the vessel to clear the path of space debris, micrometeorites, and interstellar gas. At warp speeds, even microscopic particles carry tremendous kinetic energy that would damage the ship.

Beyond its primary function, the deflector array serves as a powerful multi-purpose tool. It can be reconfigured to emit various types of energy beams, enhance long-range sensors, or create specialized field effects. Many improvised solutions to novel problems involve creative use of the deflector dish, making it one of the most versatile ship systems.
