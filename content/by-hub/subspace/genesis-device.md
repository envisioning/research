# Genesis Device

## Summary

Experimental terraforming technology capable of rapidly reorganizing matter to create life-supporting planets.

## Description

The Genesis Device uses a matrix of life-generating protomatter to reorganize all matter within its effect radius at the subatomic level, transforming lifeless worlds into lush, life-supporting planets within hours. While intended as the ultimate terraforming tool, its potential as a weapon of mass destruction is equally profound.

When deployed, Genesis creates a cascading effect that restructures planetary geology, atmosphere, and biochemistry. The process destroys any existing life forms, replacing them with Earth-based ecosystems. Ethical concerns, combined with instability issues from the protomatter matrix, led to the project's classified status and eventual abandonment.
