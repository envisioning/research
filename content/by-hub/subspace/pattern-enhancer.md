# Transport Pattern Enhancer

## Summary

Field generator improving transporter lock and signal clarity through interference.

## Description

Pattern enhancers are portable devices deployed in a triangular configuration around a transport site to strengthen the transporter signal. They're essential when attempting to beam through interference, atmospheric conditions, or at extreme range.

The three-unit array creates a subspace field that amplifies the transporter carrier wave and helps maintain pattern integrity. Pattern enhancers are standard equipment for away teams operating in challenging environments. They can mean the difference between successful transport and fatal pattern degradation in emergency beam-outs.
