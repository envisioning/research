# Fusion Reactor

## Summary

Impulse power generation through controlled nuclear fusion reactions.

## Description

Fusion reactors provide power for impulse drive and serve as backup power systems when the warp core is offline. These reactors fuse lighter atomic nuclei into heavier ones, releasing substantial energy in the process.Modern fusion technology achieves remarkable efficiency and can operate continuously for years. Multiple reactors provide redundancy, ensuring that loss of one system doesn't cripple the ship.
