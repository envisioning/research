# Cortical Stimulator

## Summary

Device for stimulating neural activity to revive unconscious patients or enhance cognitive function.

## Description

The cortical stimulator applies precisely calibrated electromagnetic pulses to neural tissue, either to revive unconscious patients or to enhance specific cognitive functions during medical procedures. It's a critical tool in emergency medicine.The device must be carefully calibrated for each patient's neural patterns to avoid adverse effects. Advanced models can target specific brain regions for therapeutic purposes, including treatment of neurological conditions.
