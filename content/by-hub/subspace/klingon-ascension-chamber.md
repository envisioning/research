# Rite of Ascension Chamber

## Summary

Ritual space with ceremonial electronics for warrior coming-of-age trials.

## Description

Specialized chamber equipped with painstik emitters and ceremonial electronics used in the Age of Ascension ritual. The controlled energy discharges test young Klingons' endurance while symbolizing their rebirth as full warriors of the Empire.
