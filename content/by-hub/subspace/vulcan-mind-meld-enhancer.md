# Mind Meld Enhancers

## Summary

Devices amplifying or stabilizing telepathic connections for long-distance or high-risk melds.

## Description

Technology used by Vulcan healers and scholars to strengthen mental connections when telepathic contact exceeds safe biological limits. Enables deep mental fusion across greater distances or with non-telepathic species.
