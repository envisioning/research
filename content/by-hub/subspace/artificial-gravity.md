# Artificial Gravity Plating

## Summary

Deck-embedded gravity generation providing Earth-standard gravity throughout vessels and stations.

## Description

Artificial gravity systems generate gravitational fields within spacecraft and stations, eliminating the need for rotation-based artificial gravity. The technology is embedded in deck plating throughout the vessel, providing consistent downward pull that simulates planetary gravity.

Gravity plating can be adjusted for different species' requirements and can be disabled in cargo bays or other areas where zero-gravity operations are beneficial. The system is remarkably reliable and power-efficient, operating continuously with minimal maintenance. Failure of artificial gravity is rare but creates significant operational challenges.
