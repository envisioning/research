# Quantum Torpedo

## Summary

Advanced torpedo utilizing zero-point energy extraction for enhanced destructive capability.

## Description

Quantum torpedoes represent an evolution of photon torpedo technology, incorporating zero-point energy extraction to achieve significantly higher yields in a smaller package. These weapons are particularly effective against heavily shielded targets.The quantum detonation creates a cascading subspace rupture that can penetrate shields more effectively than conventional antimatter warheads. Guidance systems are enhanced with quantum entanglement for unhackable command links.
