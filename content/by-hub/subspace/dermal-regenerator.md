# Dermal Regenerator

## Summary

Handheld medical device for accelerated healing of wounds, burns, and tissue damage.

## Description

The dermal regenerator is a standard medical tool that stimulates cellular growth to rapidly heal skin and tissue damage. It works by generating a bio-field that accelerates the natural healing process, closing wounds and eliminating scarring within minutes.

The device is calibrated to work with various species' biochemistries and can treat everything from minor cuts to severe burns. More powerful versions can regenerate deeper tissue layers and even assist with bone repair. Dermal regenerators are standard equipment in medical kits and away team gear.
