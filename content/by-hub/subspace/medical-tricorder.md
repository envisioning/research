# Medical Tricorder

## Summary

Portable diagnostic device capable of comprehensive biological and medical analysis.

## Description

The medical tricorder is specifically designed for health diagnostics, capable of scanning patients to identify injuries, diseases, and physiological abnormalities. It can monitor vital signs, detect toxins, and analyze biological samples at the molecular level.The device provides real-time analysis and can suggest treatment protocols based on its diagnostic findings. It interfaces with medical databases to access information on thousands of known species and medical conditions.
