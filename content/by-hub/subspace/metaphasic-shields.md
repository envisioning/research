# Metaphasic Shields

## Summary

Advanced shielding technology allowing vessels to withstand the intense radiation within stellar coronas.

## Description

Metaphasic shields represent a revolutionary advancement in shield technology, capable of protecting a vessel from the extreme temperatures and radiation found within a star's corona. The shields work by generating a metaphasic field that shifts harmful radiation into harmless frequencies.

This technology enables scientific research within stellar environments that would normally be instantly fatal to any vessel. Applications extend beyond stellar research to protecting against certain exotic weapons and phenomena. The power requirements are substantial, limiting operational duration.
