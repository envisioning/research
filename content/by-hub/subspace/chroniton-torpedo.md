# Chroniton Torpedo

## Summary

Temporal weapon utilizing chroniton particles to phase through conventional shields.

## Description

Chroniton torpedoes incorporate temporal technology, causing them to exist slightly out of phase with normal time. This temporal displacement allows them to pass through conventional deflector shields as if they weren't there, making them extremely effective against shielded targets.

The warhead delivers both kinetic and temporal damage, potentially causing localized time distortions on impact. Defending against chroniton weapons requires specialized temporal shielding. The technology raises significant concerns about temporal mechanics and causality violations.
