# Hypospray

## Summary

Needleless injection system delivering medications directly through the skin.

## Description

The hypospray uses high-pressure air jets to deliver medications transdermally without needles. This allows for rapid drug administration with minimal discomfort and no risk of needle-based complications.The device can be programmed with various medication dosages and can even mix multiple compounds for combination therapy. Emergency hyposprays come pre-loaded with common treatments for field use.
