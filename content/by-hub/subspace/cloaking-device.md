# Cloaking Device

## Summary

Technology that renders a vessel undetectable to sensors and visual observation.

## Description

The cloaking device bends light and other forms of electromagnetic radiation around a vessel, rendering it effectively invisible. Advanced models also mask subspace signatures and energy emissions.While cloaked, a vessel typically cannot fire weapons or raise shields without decloaking. The technology requires significant power and sophisticated field generators to maintain stable operation.
