# EPS Conduits

## Summary

Electro-Plasma System distributing power throughout the vessel.

## Description

The Electro-Plasma System (EPS) consists of a network of conduits that distribute power from the warp core and fusion reactors to all ship systems. The plasma travels through these conduits at high pressure and temperature, requiring robust containment.EPS conduits include safety systems to prevent cascade failures and can be isolated in sections for maintenance or damage control. Power distribution is managed by computer systems that optimize energy flow based on current demands.
