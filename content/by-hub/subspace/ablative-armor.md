# Ablative Armor

## Summary

Advanced hull plating that dissipates energy weapon impacts through controlled vaporization.

## Description

Ablative armor consists of specialized alloys designed to absorb and dissipate energy weapon impacts. When struck, the outer layer vaporizes in a controlled manner, carrying away the heat and energy of the attack.This technology provides an additional defensive layer beyond shields, particularly effective against sustained energy weapon bombardment. The armor regenerates slowly using replicator technology integrated into the hull structure.
