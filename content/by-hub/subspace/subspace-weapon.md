# Subspace Weapon

## Summary

Banned technology that tears rifts in the fabric of subspace, causing permanent spatial damage.

## Description

Subspace weapons create devastating tears in the fabric of subspace itself, effectively damaging the structure of reality. These rifts can render entire regions of space impassable to warp travel and disrupt subspace communications across vast areas.

The effects of subspace weapons are permanent and cumulative—repeated use in an area can create expanding dead zones where faster-than-light travel becomes impossible. The Second Khitomer Accords banned the development and deployment of such weapons by all signatories due to their potential to make space travel untenable.
