# Temporal Shielding

## Summary

Protects vessels from chronometric weapons and time distortions.

## Description

Creates a local temporal stability field that damps chroniton influx and isolates the ship's proper time from external perturbations. Required when operating near temporal rifts or against chroniton munitions.
