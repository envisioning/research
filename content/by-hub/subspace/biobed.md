# Biobed

## Summary

Advanced medical treatment platform with integrated diagnostic and life support systems.

## Description

The biobed is the primary treatment station in Starfleet sickbays, featuring comprehensive diagnostic sensors, automated treatment systems, and life support capabilities. It can monitor all vital signs continuously and alert medical staff to changes in patient condition.Advanced models include surgical support systems, bio-regenerative fields, and emergency stasis capabilities. The biobed interfaces with the ship's medical database to assist in diagnosis and treatment planning.
