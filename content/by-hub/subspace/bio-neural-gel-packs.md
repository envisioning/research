# Bio-Neural Gel Packs

## Summary

Organic computing substrate using cultured neural tissue for enhanced processing speed and adaptability.

## Description

Bio-neural gel packs represent a hybrid approach to computing, using genetically engineered neural tissue suspended in nutrient gel as organic processors. These biological computers can process information more fluidly than traditional isolinear systems, learning and adapting to new situations.

The organic nature of gel packs allows for more intuitive problem-solving and pattern recognition, though they require regular maintenance and are susceptible to biological contamination. When infected or damaged, gel packs can affect ship systems in unpredictable ways. Despite these challenges, bio-neural systems offer significant performance advantages in complex computational tasks.
