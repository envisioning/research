# Regenerative Shielding

## Summary

Advanced shield technology capable of self-repair and adaptation to incoming weapons fire.

## Description

Regenerative shields represent a significant advancement over conventional deflector technology, incorporating adaptive algorithms that automatically adjust to counter repeated attacks. The shields can redistribute power dynamically and repair damaged sections using surrounding shield energy.

Originally developed by the Borg Collective and later adapted by other species, regenerative shields can maintain effectiveness even under sustained bombardment. The system learns from each impact, adjusting frequency and modulation to better resist subsequent attacks of the same type. This makes regenerative shields particularly effective against weapons that rely on frequency matching.
