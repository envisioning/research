# Borg Nanoprobes

## Summary

Microscopic machines used for assimilation, medical applications, and technological integration.

## Description

Borg nanoprobes are sophisticated microscopic machines that serve multiple functions in the Borg Collective. Primarily used for assimilation, they rewrite DNA, interface cybernetic implants with organic tissue, and connect individuals to the collective consciousness.

When controlled and used therapeutically, nanoprobes demonstrate remarkable medical capabilities, able to repair tissue, fight diseases, and even revive the recently deceased. They can adapt to counter biological threats and maintain the health of their host. However, uncontrolled nanoprobes attempt to assimilate any biological matter they encounter, making them extremely dangerous.
