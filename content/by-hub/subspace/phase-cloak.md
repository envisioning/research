# Phased Cloaking Device

## Summary

Banned Federation technology combining cloaking with phase shifting to pass through solid matter.

## Description

The phased cloaking device not only renders a vessel invisible but also shifts it slightly out of phase with normal space-time, allowing it to pass through solid matter like planets, asteroids, and even other ships. This represents the ultimate stealth technology.

Development of this device violated the Treaty of Algeron between the Federation and Romulan Empire. The technology is extremely dangerous—loss of phase synchronization while within solid matter would result in catastrophic molecular disintegration upon re-phasing.
