# Bat'leth (Sword of Honor)

## Summary

Crescent-shaped melee weapon used in ritual combat and warrior tradition.

## Description

Traditional Klingon blade weapon with distinctive curved design, invented by Kahless the Unforgettable. More than a weapon, the bat'leth represents warrior honor and is often passed through family lineages. Every blade tells a story of its wielders' deeds.
