# Impulse Drive

## Summary

Sublight propulsion system using fusion reactors to achieve high relativistic speeds.

## Description

Impulse drive provides sublight propulsion for spacecraft, utilizing controlled fusion reactions to generate thrust. This system is used for maneuvering in planetary systems and combat situations where warp drive would be impractical or dangerous.

The impulse drive can accelerate a vessel to significant fractions of light speed, though it cannot exceed it. Advanced models include relativistic compensation to account for time dilation effects at high velocities.
