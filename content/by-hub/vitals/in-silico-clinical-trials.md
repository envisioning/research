# In Silico Clinical Trials

## Summary

Virtual clinical trials using digital twins to test treatments before human testing.

## Description

In silico trials use computer simulations and virtual physiological human models to replicate the effect of drugs and devices on diverse patient populations. This technology accelerates regulatory approval, reduces the need for animal and human testing, and allows researchers to test scenarios (like pediatric or rare disease responses) that are difficult or unethical to study in traditional trials.
