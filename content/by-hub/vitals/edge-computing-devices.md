# Edge-Computing Medical Devices

## Summary

Medical devices that process critical data locally to ensure low latency and continuous operation.

## Description

Edge computing in medical devices allows for data processing to occur directly on the device or a local server rather than in the cloud. This is critical for life-saving equipment like ventilators and robotic surgery systems where millisecond latency matters. It also ensures that critical monitoring continues uninterrupted even during internet outages, enhancing the resilience of hospital technology.
