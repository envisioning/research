# Integrated Behavioral Telehealth in Primary Care

## Summary

Embedding virtual mental health services into primary and chronic care pathways.

## Description

Integrated behavioral telehealth models place virtual psychologists, psychiatrists, and counselors inside primary care workflows. Screening, brief interventions, and warm handoffs occur during or immediately after primary visits, with follow-up therapy and medication management delivered remotely. This reduces access barriers and treats mental and physical health as a unified continuum.
