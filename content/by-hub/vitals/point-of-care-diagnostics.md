# Point-of-Care Diagnostics Platforms

## Summary

Portable imaging and lab devices that bring diagnostics to the bedside or home.

## Description

Handheld ultrasound, compact lab analyzers, and cartridge-based molecular testing platforms allow clinicians to perform high-quality diagnostics directly at the point of care. By shortening the time from sample collection to result, these devices accelerate clinical decision-making in emergency departments, ambulances, rural clinics, and patients’ homes, reshaping how and where diagnostic-intensive care is delivered.
