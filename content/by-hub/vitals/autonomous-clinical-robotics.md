# Autonomous Clinical Robotics

## Summary

Robotic systems for medication delivery, disinfection, and logistics within healthcare facilities.

## Description

Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) are increasingly deployed in hospitals to handle non-clinical tasks such as transporting linens, meals, and medications, as well as disinfecting patient rooms with UV-C light. These robots navigate dynamic hospital environments safely, freeing up clinical staff to focus on direct patient care and reducing the physical burden on support staff.
