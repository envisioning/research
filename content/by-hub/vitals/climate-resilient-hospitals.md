# Climate-Resilient Hospital Infrastructure

## Summary

Buildings and systems designed to withstand extreme weather and maintain operations during climate disruptions.

## Description

Climate-resilient hospitals integrate backup microgrids, solar arrays, flood-resistant design, and passive cooling systems to remain operational during hurricanes, heatwaves, floods, and wildfires. As climate change intensifies extreme weather events, these adaptations ensure continuity of care, protect vulnerable patients, and enable hospitals to serve as community resilience hubs during disasters.
