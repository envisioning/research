# Continuous Monitoring Wearables

## Summary

Networked biosensor patches and wearables for real-time patient monitoring inside and outside the hospital.

## Description

Lightweight, medical-grade wearables—such as adhesive patches, smart garments, and wrist devices—continuously capture vitals like heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, and oxygen saturation. When integrated with hospital monitoring systems, they extend telemetry outside traditional high-acuity wards, enabling earlier detection of deterioration on general floors and smooth transitions to home-based monitoring after discharge.
