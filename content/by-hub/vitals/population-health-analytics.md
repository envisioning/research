# Population Health Analytics Platforms

## Summary

Analytics layers that stratify patient populations by risk to guide proactive interventions.

## Description

Population health platforms aggregate claims, clinical, and social determinants data to identify patients at risk of hospitalization, readmission, or disease progression. They generate prioritized outreach lists for care managers, highlight geographic and demographic disparities, and support value-based care programs by aligning limited resources with the highest-impact interventions.
