# High-Acuity Virtual Wards

## Summary

Hospital-at-home models treating acutely ill patients with continuous remote monitoring.

## Description

Virtual wards expand the definition of a hospital by treating patients with acute conditions (such as pneumonia, heart failure, or post-surgical recovery) in their own homes. Using continuous remote monitoring devices and rapid-response community teams, these models deliver hospital-level care at a lower cost and with higher patient satisfaction, keeping physical beds free for the most critical cases.
