# Modular Micro-Hospitals & Mobile Clinics

## Summary

Rapidly deployable clinical environments for surge capacity and underserved regions.

## Description

Prefabricated micro-hospitals and mobile clinic units can be assembled or deployed within days to provide essential services such as emergency care, imaging, and short-stay beds. Equipped with digital connectivity and integrated diagnostics, they extend hospital capabilities into rural communities, disaster zones, and dense urban neighborhoods where traditional infrastructure is limited or overburdened.
