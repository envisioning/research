# Care Pathway Orchestration Engines

## Summary

Software that codifies and automates evidence-based care pathways across settings.

## Description

Care orchestration platforms translate clinical guidelines into dynamic workflows that span outpatient clinics, hospitals, and home care. They trigger the right tasks, notifications, and orders at each step of a patient journey—pre-op, inpatient, post-acute—while personalizing pathways based on risk and comorbidities. This reduces unwarranted variation and ensures that every patient receives consistent, guideline-concordant care.
