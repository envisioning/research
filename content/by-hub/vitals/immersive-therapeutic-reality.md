# Immersive Therapeutic Reality

## Summary

VR and AR environments used for pain management, rehabilitation, and mental health therapy.

## Description

Going beyond distraction, immersive therapeutics use Virtual Reality (VR) to rewire neural pathways for chronic pain management, PTSD treatment, and stroke rehabilitation. By placing patients in responsive, gamified environments, these tools increase neuroplasticity and patient engagement, offering a non-pharmacological alternative to opioids and traditional physical therapy.
