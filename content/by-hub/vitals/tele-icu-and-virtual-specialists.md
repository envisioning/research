# Tele-ICU & Virtual Specialist Networks

## Summary

Hub-and-spoke models providing remote intensivist and specialist support to community hospitals.

## Description

Tele-ICU platforms connect smaller hospitals and rural facilities to centralized teams of intensivists and subspecialists through high-bandwidth audio-video links and shared monitoring dashboards. Remote clinicians can co-manage critical patients, guide complex procedures, and support local teams, enabling high-quality care closer to home without unnecessary transfers.
