# 5G/6G-Connected Smart Ambulances

## Summary

Ultra-high-bandwidth mobile units enabling real-time diagnostics and remote guidance during transport.

## Description

Next-generation ambulances equipped with 5G/6G connectivity transmit high-resolution video, continuous vitals, ultrasound imaging, and ECG data to receiving hospitals in real-time. Emergency physicians and specialists can remotely guide paramedics through complex procedures, make pre-arrival decisions, and prepare surgical teams, dramatically compressing the time from symptom onset to definitive treatment—critical in stroke, trauma, and cardiac arrest.
