# Voice Biomarker Diagnostics

## Summary

AI models that analyze vocal features to detect respiratory, neurological, and cardiovascular conditions.

## Description

Voice biomarker platforms analyze micro-tremors, pitch, and cadence in human speech to detect diseases often invisible to the human ear. These non-invasive software tools can screen for conditions like COVID-19, COPD, depression, and even coronary artery disease using just a smartphone microphone. This enables scalable, remote population screening and continuous monitoring of disease progression without specialized hardware.
