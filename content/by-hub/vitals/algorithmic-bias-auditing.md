# Algorithmic Bias Auditing

## Summary

Protocols to test clinical AI models for bias against specific demographics.

## Description

As AI takes on a greater role in diagnosis and resource allocation, ensuring fairness is critical. Algorithmic bias auditing involves rigorous testing of AI models against diverse datasets to identify and mitigate performance disparities across race, gender, and socioeconomic status. This ensures that technological advancements reduce, rather than exacerbate, health inequities.
