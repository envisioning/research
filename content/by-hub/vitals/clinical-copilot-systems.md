# Clinical Decision Co-Pilot Systems

## Summary

Generative AI assistants embedded in EHRs to support real-time clinical decision-making.

## Description

Clinical co-pilots surface patient-specific insights, summarize longitudinal records, and suggest evidence-based diagnostics or treatment options at the point of care. Tightly integrated into clinical workflows, they reduce cognitive load in complex cases, flag potential drug interactions or contraindications, and help clinicians navigate rapidly evolving guidelines, particularly in high-acuity environments.
