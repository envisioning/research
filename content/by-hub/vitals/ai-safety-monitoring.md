# AI Safety & Performance Monitoring

## Summary

Frameworks for continuous surveillance of AI tools deployed in clinical workflows.

## Description

AI safety monitoring systems track real-world performance of clinical algorithms after deployment, comparing outcomes across populations and care settings. They detect model drift, emergent harms, and unexpected failure modes, triggering alerts, rollbacks, or retraining. This creates a feedback loop between frontline practice and model developers, aligning AI-enabled care with patient safety standards.
