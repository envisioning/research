# Real-Time Multilingual Intelligence

## Summary

On-the-fly translation and transcription at scale.

## Description

Neural machine translation and automatic speech recognition systems optimized for dozens of languages, including low-resource and endangered languages. They break down linguistic barriers in global collections, enabling researchers to query and understand materials regardless of original language.
