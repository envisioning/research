# Content Authenticity Protocols

## Summary

Immutable provenance tracking for digital truth.

## Description

Cryptographic standards and blockchain-based ledgers that permanently attach origin and edit history to digital media. This technology fights misinformation by allowing any user to verify the source and integrity of a document or image.
