# Personal Knowledge Graph Agents

## Summary

Agents that build and maintain individual scholarly graphs.

## Description

Background agents that watch what a person reads, writes, and bookmarks—across devices and platforms—and assemble a private, structured graph of their interests and claims. They connect this personal graph to institutional collections, surfacing serendipitous links and gaps in a researcher's understanding.
