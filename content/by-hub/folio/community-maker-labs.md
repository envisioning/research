# Community Maker Labs

## Summary

Open-access spaces for physical and digital fabrication.

## Description

Library spaces equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, data sandboxes, and coding workstations. They democratize access to production tools, allowing patrons to not just consume information but use library data to create new physical objects, software, and art.
