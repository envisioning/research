# Spatial Computing Displays

## Summary

XR and holographic surfaces for immersive knowledge spaces.

## Description

Head-mounted, handheld, and room-scale spatial displays that project 3D visualizations of archives, timelines, and knowledge graphs into physical reading rooms. They turn libraries into mixed-reality environments where visitors walk through data landscapes and narrative maps.
