# Ambient Preservation Sensing

## Summary

Continuous environmental monitoring for collection health.

## Description

Networks of low-power IoT sensors and edge AI modules embedded in shelves, stacks, and storage rooms to track temperature, humidity, light, pollutants, and vibration. These systems predict degradation risks and trigger adaptive controls to extend the life of physical collections.
