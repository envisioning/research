# GLAM Interoperability Grids

## Summary

Discovery networks linking galleries, libraries, archives, and museums.

## Description

Shared data standards and query layers that connect siloed collections across different types of cultural institutions. They allow a single search to surface a painting from a gallery, a book from a library, and a specimen from a museum, creating a unified view of cultural heritage.
