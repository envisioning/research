# Computational Humanities Notebooks

## Summary

Literate programming environments for reproducible scholarship.

## Description

Interactive notebook systems that blend prose, code, data, and visualizations. Scholars can document their analytical processes transparently, share methods alongside findings, and enable others to verify and build on their work—bringing computational reproducibility to archival research.
