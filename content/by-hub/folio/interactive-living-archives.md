# Interactive Living Archives

## Summary

AI personas simulating historical figures for conversational inquiry.

## Description

Large Language Models fine-tuned on the complete corpus of an author, scientist, or historical figure. These 'living' avatars allow users to interview the past, asking questions and receiving answers grounded in the subject's actual writings and recorded thoughts.
