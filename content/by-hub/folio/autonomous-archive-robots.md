# Autonomous Archive Robots

## Summary

Robotic systems for safe handling and digitization of collections.

## Description

Mobile and gantry-style robots equipped with advanced vision and haptic sensing for non-destructive page turning, shelf scanning, and bulk digitization. They enable continuous, precise handling of fragile materials while building high-resolution digital surrogates at scale.
