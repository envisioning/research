# Zero-Knowledge Access Protocols

## Summary

Verifying access rights without revealing user identity.

## Description

Cryptographic systems allowing researchers to prove they have the credentials (e.g., 'Ph.D. student at accredited university') to access sensitive archives without sharing their name, location, or research history. This protects intellectual privacy while enforcing necessary restrictions.
