# Photonic Neuromorphic Accelerators

## Summary

Light-based, brain-inspired computing for ultra-low-power AI.

## Description

Specialized chips that use photons instead of electrons and mimic neural architectures to process AI workloads. They deliver orders-of-magnitude improvements in speed and energy efficiency, making advanced semantic search and indexing feasible even in resource-constrained library environments.
