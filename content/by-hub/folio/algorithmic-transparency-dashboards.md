# Algorithmic Transparency Dashboards

## Summary

User-facing interfaces revealing how search results are ranked.

## Description

Interactive tools that explain why a specific book or document was recommended or ranked highly. They expose the weights of different factors (e.g., popularity, date, semantic relevance) and allow users to tweak these parameters, restoring agency and trust in the discovery process.
