# Particle Tomography Scanners

## Summary

Non-invasive imaging to read closed books and fragile scrolls.

## Description

Advanced imaging systems using X-ray micro-tomography or muon scattering to virtually 'unwrap' and read delicate, sealed, or fused historical documents (like the Herculaneum scrolls) without physically touching them, unlocking lost texts that cannot be opened.
