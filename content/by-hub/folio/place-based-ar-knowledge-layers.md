# Place-Based AR Knowledge Layers

## Summary

Augmented reality overlays tying spaces to stories.

## Description

Geo-anchored AR experiences that reveal archival photographs, oral histories, and data narratives in situ—on campuses, in neighborhoods, and around cultural sites. Libraries extend beyond their walls, turning cities into explorable, annotated texts.
