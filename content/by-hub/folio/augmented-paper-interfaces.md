# Augmented Paper Interfaces

## Summary

Interactive printed media bridging physical books and digital content.

## Description

Conductive inks, embedded micro-sensors, and projection mapping transform traditional paper books into interactive interfaces. Readers can trigger audio narration, translations, or data visualizations by touching words or images, preserving the tactile joy of reading while adding digital depth.
