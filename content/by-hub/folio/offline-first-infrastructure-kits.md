# Offline-First Infrastructure Kits

## Summary

Low-cost, resilient systems for disconnected regions.

## Description

Modular hardware bundles combining solar power, local mesh networks, edge servers, and ruggedized storage designed for communities without reliable internet or electricity. They enable Global South institutions to host digital collections and provide services independently of external infrastructure.
