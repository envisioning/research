# Water Recycling & Greywater Systems

## Summary

On-site water treatment and recycling systems reducing freshwater consumption and enabling sustainable development in water-scarce regions.

## Description

Water recycling and greywater systems treat and reuse water from showers, sinks, and laundry for irrigation, toilet flushing, and cooling tower makeup. In GCC's water-scarce environment, where desalinated water is energy-intensive and expensive, on-site water recycling is both environmentally and economically compelling.

UAE's Estidama and green building regulations increasingly require greywater systems in new developments. Masdar City, The Sustainable City Dubai, and premium developments incorporate water recycling as standard. Technologies range from simple greywater diversion for irrigation to advanced membrane treatment producing near-potable quality water.

The signal represents the shift from centralized water infrastructure to distributed treatment and reuse, reducing demand on desalination and distribution networks. Key benefits include 30-50% reduction in freshwater consumption, reduced utility costs, and sustainability certification credits. Challenges include maintenance requirements, health regulations, and occupant acceptance.
