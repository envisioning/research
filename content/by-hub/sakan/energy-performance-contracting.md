# Energy Performance Contracting (ESCOs)

## Summary

Performance-based contracts where energy service companies finance and implement efficiency improvements, paid through guaranteed savings.

## Description

Energy Performance Contracting involves Energy Service Companies (ESCOs) financing, designing, and implementing building energy efficiency improvements, with payment tied to verified energy savings. This model removes upfront capital barriers and transfers performance risk to the ESCO.

In GCC, where cooling represents up to 70% of building energy consumption, ESCO contracts offer substantial savings potential. UAE's Super ESCO (Etihad ESCO) and Saudi Arabia's National Energy Services Company (Tarshid) drive public building retrofits, while private ESCOs target commercial and residential portfolios. The model enables efficiency investments without owner capital outlay.

The signal represents the financialization of energy efficiency, creating investable products from operational savings. Key applications include HVAC optimization, LED retrofits, building envelope improvements, and renewable integration. Challenges include measurement and verification complexity, contract structuring, and building owner awareness of ESCO benefits.
