# Flood & Climate Resilience Infrastructure

## Summary

Stormwater management, permeable surfaces, and resilient design addressing increasing extreme weather events in coastal and low-lying developments.

## Description

Flood and climate resilience infrastructure encompasses stormwater drainage systems, permeable pavements, retention ponds, elevated construction, and resilient building design addressing extreme weather events. The 2024 UAE flooding highlighted vulnerabilities in rapid development areas, driving investment in resilience infrastructure.

GCC coastal cities face increasing risks from sea-level rise, extreme precipitation events, and temperature increases. Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Qatar are investing in drainage upgrades and resilient design standards. New developments increasingly incorporate climate resilience from inception rather than retrofit.

The signal represents the integration of climate adaptation into development planning, moving from reactive (emergency response) to proactive (resilient design) approaches. Key elements include sustainable urban drainage systems (SUDS), elevated critical infrastructure, and building designs accommodating temperature extremes. The trend particularly impacts coastal and low-lying developments.
