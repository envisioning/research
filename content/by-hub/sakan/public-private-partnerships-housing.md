# Public-Private Partnerships for Housing

## Summary

Partnership models combining public and private resources for housing development, offering innovative financing and risk-sharing.

## Description

Public-private partnerships for housing combine public and private resources to develop housing projects, offering innovative financing models and risk-sharing. These models enable public and private sectors to work together to scale affordable housing production.

In GCC, PPPs are relevant for scaling affordable housing production and attracting private investment. Saudi Arabia's Sakani program and UAE housing initiatives explore PPP models to combine public resources with private expertise. The technology is being adopted in housing programs that seek to combine public and private resources, especially relevant where public resources are limited but housing demand is high.

The signal represents the transformation from exclusively public housing development to partnership models that combine resources and expertise, creating new financing models that scale housing production. This is especially relevant where housing deficits require innovative financing models. Key challenges include structuring agreements, risk allocation, and ensuring affordability while attracting private capital.
