# Predictive Maintenance & AI Asset Management

## Summary

AI-driven systems predicting equipment failures before they occur, optimizing maintenance schedules and reducing operational costs.

## Description

Predictive maintenance utilizes AI and machine learning to analyze sensor data from building systems (HVAC, elevators, electrical), predicting failures before they occur and optimizing maintenance schedules. This approach reduces emergency repairs, extends equipment life, and lowers operational costs.

In GCC's extreme climate, where HVAC systems run intensively and equipment faces accelerated wear, predictive maintenance is particularly valuable. Major property managers and facility management companies deploy AI platforms that integrate with Building Management Systems to identify anomalies and schedule proactive interventions.

The signal represents the shift from reactive (fix when broken) and scheduled (maintain on calendar) to predictive (maintain when needed) asset management. Key benefits include 20-30% reduction in maintenance costs, improved tenant satisfaction through fewer disruptions, and extended asset life. Challenges include sensor deployment costs, data quality, and integration with existing building systems.
