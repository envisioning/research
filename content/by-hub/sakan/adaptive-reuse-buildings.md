# Adaptive Reuse of Buildings

## Summary

Conversion of building typologies (commercial to residential, industrial to mixed-use) to repurpose existing structures and reduce embodied carbon.

## Description

Adaptive reuse transforms existing buildings for new uses rather than demolition and reconstruction, preserving embodied carbon and urban infrastructure. This approach converts typologies (commercial to residential, industrial to mixed-use) through interventions that adapt existing structures, reducing environmental impact and construction costs.

In GCC, adaptive reuse responds to office vacancies in central business districts, demand for well-located housing, and sustainability goals. Dubai's DIFC and older districts see conversions of office buildings to residential, while industrial areas transform into mixed-use developments. The approach aligns with circular economy principles and net-zero commitments.

The signal represents a shift from demolition to transformation, requiring new competencies in structural assessment, reinforcement engineering, and regulatory approval processes. Key challenges include building code adaptation, structural capacity for new uses, and financial viability. The technology creates opportunities for specialized professionals and investors interested in urban regeneration.
