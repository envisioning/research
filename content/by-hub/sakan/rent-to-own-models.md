# Rent-to-Own (RTO) Schemes

## Summary

Contractual arrangements allowing tenants to build equity through rental payments, bridging the affordability gap for the mid-income workforce.

## Description

Rent-to-Own (RTO) schemes provide a pathway to homeownership for residents who can afford monthly payments but lack the substantial down payments (typically 20-25%) required by GCC central banks. These contracts convert a portion of rent into equity or offer an option to purchase at a fixed price after a set period.

In the GCC, where the expatriate workforce is transient but increasingly seeking stability, RTO bridges the gap between renting and buying. Developers use it to offload inventory in softening markets, while governments (like in UAE and KSA) encourage it to boost homeownership rates. The signal transforms the tenant-landlord relationship into a pathway for asset accumulation, critical for retaining talent in the region.
