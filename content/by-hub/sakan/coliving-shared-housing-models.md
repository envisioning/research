# Coliving & Shared Housing Models

## Summary

Shared accommodation models with common spaces, amenities, and community features, especially relevant for expatriate communities and young professionals.

## Description

Coliving and shared housing models offer shared accommodation with common spaces, amenities, and community features, especially relevant for expatriate communities, young professionals, and digital nomads. These models combine private living spaces with extensive shared facilities, creating community-oriented housing solutions.

In GCC, coliving gains traction in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and emerging markets, addressing expatriate communities and young professionals seeking flexibility and community. The model offers cost-effective housing with integrated services, reducing barriers to entry for newcomers. Dubai and Abu Dhabi see growing coliving developments targeting expatriates and young professionals.

The signal represents the transformation of housing from individual units to shared community models, creating new housing typologies that combine privacy with community, especially relevant where flexibility, cost-effectiveness, and community are priorities. Key features include shared kitchens, lounges, workspaces, and community events. Challenges include managing community dynamics, ensuring privacy, and building sustainable business models.
