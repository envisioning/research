# Heat-Mitigation Building Envelopes (Cool Roofs, Advanced Glazing, PCM)

## Summary

Envelope technologies that cut cooling loads through reflective surfaces, high-performance glazing, shading, and phase-change materials—especially valuable for retrofits.

## Description

Heat-mitigation envelope technologies reduce solar heat gain and improve thermal performance through cool roofs (high albedo coatings), spectrally selective glazing, external shading systems, insulated facades, and phase-change materials (PCM) that buffer temperature swings.

In GCC cities, envelope performance is one of the most practical levers for reducing cooling demand and service charges—particularly in existing building stock where HVAC upgrades alone can’t deliver deep savings. These technologies also support embodied-carbon goals by extending building life and avoiding full replacement.

The signal represents a shift from 'HVAC-first' efficiency to integrated building physics and retrofit economics. Key challenges include retrofit logistics, tenant disruption, facade access, and ensuring long-term performance under dust, UV, and high humidity conditions.
