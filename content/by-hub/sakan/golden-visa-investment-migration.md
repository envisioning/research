# Golden Visa & Investment Migration

## Summary

Residency and citizenship programs linked to property investment, driving demand from global investors seeking lifestyle, tax, and mobility benefits.

## Description

Golden Visa programs offer long-term residency or citizenship in exchange for qualifying property investments. UAE's Golden Visa (10-year residency for AED 2M+ property investment) has become a major demand driver for Dubai and Abu Dhabi real estate, attracting investors from Russia, India, Europe, and beyond seeking tax efficiency, lifestyle, and global mobility.

For housing markets, investment migration creates a distinct demand segment with different preferences: prime locations, branded residences, and properties meeting visa thresholds. Developers increasingly design products specifically for this market, offering concierge visa services and community features appealing to global investors.

The signal represents the convergence of immigration policy and real estate markets, where property investment becomes a pathway to residency. Key dynamics include competition between GCC destinations, evolving investment thresholds, and the emergence of specialized advisory services connecting investors with qualifying properties.
