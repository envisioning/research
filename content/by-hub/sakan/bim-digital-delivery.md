# BIM & Digital Delivery

## Summary

Building Information Modeling enabling integrated design, clash detection, and lifecycle management for complex projects.

## Description

Building Information Modeling (BIM) creates digital representations of buildings that integrate design, engineering, and construction data. For GCC mega-projects with multiple stakeholders and aggressive timelines, BIM is essential for coordination and error prevention.

UAE and Saudi Arabia have mandated BIM for government projects, driving adoption across the industry. Applications extend from design coordination to construction sequencing, facilities management, and digital twin creation. Major projects like Expo 2020 Dubai demonstrated BIM at unprecedented scale.

The signal reflects the digitalization of construction project delivery, with BIM becoming table stakes for major developments. The evolution toward integrated digital twins connects construction data to operational building management.
