# Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) & Filtration-as-Standard

## Summary

HVAC filtration, ventilation monitoring, and air-quality SLAs becoming baseline requirements—driven by health expectations, dust events, and premium rental markets.

## Description

IAQ technologies include continuous monitoring (CO₂, PM2.5, VOC), upgraded filtration (MERV/HEPA), UV treatment, balanced ventilation, and humidity control. Beyond 'wellness branding', IAQ is becoming an operational spec that affects leasing outcomes, tenant retention, and claims risk.

In GCC, frequent dust events and year-round AC usage make filtration and ventilation performance particularly visible to residents. Institutional landlords and branded operators increasingly treat IAQ as a measurable service level (response times, filter replacement schedules, target thresholds).

The signal represents the professionalization of indoor environmental quality as a managed utility, similar to temperature and water. Key challenges include maintenance discipline, energy trade-offs, and making IAQ transparent (dashboards, reporting) without creating liability exposure.
