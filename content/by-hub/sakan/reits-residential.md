# Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)

## Summary

Regulated investment vehicles allowing retail and institutional investors to access diversified residential real estate portfolios with liquidity and transparency.

## Description

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) are regulated investment vehicles that own, operate, or finance income-generating real estate, allowing investors to access diversified portfolios without direct property ownership. GCC markets have developed REIT frameworks, with UAE (Emirates REIT, ENBD REIT) and Saudi Arabia (multiple Tadawul-listed REITs) leading adoption.

For housing, REITs represent a structural shift in how residential assets are owned and financed. Institutional ownership through REITs brings professional management, portfolio diversification, and liquidity to traditionally illiquid residential investments. Saudi Arabia's housing program and Vision 2030 see REITs as tools for attracting private capital to affordable and mid-market housing.

The signal represents the financialization and institutionalization of residential real estate, creating new channels for capital deployment and exit. Key challenges include yield compression in competitive markets, regulatory harmonization across GCC jurisdictions, and building investor confidence in residential-focused REITs.
