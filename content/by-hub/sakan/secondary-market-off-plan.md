# Secondary Market for Off-Plan Contracts

## Summary

Trading of pre-completion property contracts, creating liquidity and speculation opportunities in off-plan real estate markets.

## Description

Secondary markets for off-plan contracts enable buyers to sell their purchase agreements before property completion, creating liquidity in traditionally illiquid pre-construction investments. In Dubai, platforms and brokerages facilitate 'contract flipping', where early buyers profit from price appreciation before handover.

For investors and developers, secondary markets impact pricing dynamics, payment plan structures, and market velocity. Active secondary markets signal strong demand and enable early investors to exit, while also creating speculation dynamics that can amplify market cycles.

The signal represents the financialization of development risk, where off-plan contracts become tradeable instruments. Key considerations include regulatory oversight to prevent manipulation, developer policies on contract transfers, and the balance between liquidity benefits and speculation risks. The market is particularly active in Dubai and increasingly relevant for Saudi mega-projects.
