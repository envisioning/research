# Circular Economy in Construction

## Summary

Design for disassembly, materials passports, and construction waste recycling creating closed-loop material flows in building lifecycle.

## Description

Circular economy principles in construction encompass design for disassembly (enabling future component reuse), materials passports (digital records of building materials for future recovery), and construction waste recycling (crushing concrete for aggregates, repurposing steel). These approaches minimize virgin material consumption and waste.

GCC construction generates massive waste volumes, with demolition and construction debris straining landfill capacity. UAE and Saudi Arabia are implementing construction waste regulations, driving demand for recycling facilities and circular design approaches. NEOM and sustainable developments incorporate circular principles in their design guidelines.

The signal represents the shift from linear (extract-build-demolish-dispose) to circular (design-build-maintain-recover-reuse) construction models. Key enablers include materials passports tracking component provenance, design standards enabling disassembly, and economic incentives for waste reduction. Challenges include upfront design costs, industry culture change, and developing secondary markets for recovered materials.
