# Construction Exoskeletons & Wearables

## Summary

Powered exoskeletons and smart wearables enhancing worker productivity, reducing injuries, and enabling work in extreme heat conditions.

## Description

Construction exoskeletons are wearable robotic devices that augment worker strength and endurance, reducing fatigue and injury risk. Smart wearables include biometric monitors, heat stress sensors, and AR-enabled helmets for real-time information. These technologies address labor productivity and safety challenges in construction.

In GCC's extreme heat environment, where construction during summer months faces significant worker safety constraints, exoskeletons and wearables enable extended work periods and reduce heat-related incidents. Major contractors like Saudi Aramco and Emaar-affiliated builders explore exoskeletons for heavy lifting and repetitive tasks.

The signal represents the augmentation of human workers rather than replacement, extending productive hours and reducing workplace injuries. Key applications include overhead work (reducing shoulder strain), heavy material handling, and real-time monitoring of worker vital signs. Challenges include cost, worker acceptance, and maintenance in harsh environments.
