# Cross-Border Emotional Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Jurisdictional frameworks for emotional data flows.

## Description

International protocols and technical infrastructure for managing the cross-border transfer of emotional and neural data, respecting varying cultural norms around mental privacy and enabling regional opt-outs from global affective AI training.
