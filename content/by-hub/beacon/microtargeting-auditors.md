# Microtargeting Transparency Auditors

## Summary

Exposing hyper-personalized manipulation.

## Description

Independent auditing systems that reverse-engineer and expose microtargeting strategies used in advertising and political campaigns, revealing how different demographic, psychographic, and behavioral segments receive systematically different persuasive messages.
