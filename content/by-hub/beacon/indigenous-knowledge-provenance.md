# Indigenous Knowledge Provenance Systems

## Summary

Protecting cultural heritage from misappropriation.

## Description

Specialized provenance frameworks rooted in indigenous data sovereignty principles that track the source, permissions, and appropriate use contexts for traditional knowledge, ceremonies, and cultural expressions, preventing decontextualization by AI systems.
