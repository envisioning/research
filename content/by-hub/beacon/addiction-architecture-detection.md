# Addiction Architecture Detection Systems

## Summary

Identifying dopamine-hacking design patterns.

## Description

Neuroscience-informed analysis tools that scan digital products for design patterns known to trigger addictive behaviors—such as infinite scroll, streak mechanics, or variable ratio reinforcement—and provide addiction risk scores and mitigation recommendations.
