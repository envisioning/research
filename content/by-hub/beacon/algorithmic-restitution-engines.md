# Algorithmic Restitution Engines

## Summary

Automated compensation for algorithmic harm.

## Description

Smart contract-based systems that automatically trigger micro-reparations or service credits to users when an algorithmic audit confirms they were subject to bias, discrimination, or service denial, moving from detection to remediation.
