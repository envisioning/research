# Affective Labor Protection Systems

## Summary

Safeguarding workers from emotional exploitation.

## Description

Monitoring and intervention systems for emotionally intensive work environments (content moderation, customer service, care work) that detect burnout signals, enforce mandatory breaks, and prevent employer surveillance of workers' genuine emotional states.
