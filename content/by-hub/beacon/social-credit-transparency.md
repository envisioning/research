# Social Credit Transparency & Appeal Systems

## Summary

Accountability for algorithmic reputation systems.

## Description

Technical and governance infrastructure for social credit and algorithmic reputation systems that ensures transparency of scoring criteria, provides meaningful appeal and correction mechanisms, and prevents discriminatory or opaque profiling practices.
