# Child Cognitive Protection Systems

## Summary

Safeguarding developing minds from manipulation.

## Description

Age-appropriate interfaces and algorithmic constraints that prevent exploitative behavioral design targeting children and adolescents, including bans on variable reward schedules, para-social manipulation, and attention-hacking patterns during critical developmental windows.
