# Interspecies Translation Governance

## Summary

Standards for AI-mediated animal communication.

## Description

Ethical frameworks and verification tools for AI systems that claim to decode or translate animal communication, preventing anthropomorphic projection and ensuring that non-human 'voices' are represented accurately and respectfully in legal or social contexts.
