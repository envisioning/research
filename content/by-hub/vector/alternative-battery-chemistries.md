# Alternative Battery Chemistries

## Summary

Sodium-ion, lithium-sulfur, and other next-gen battery technologies.

## Description

Diverse battery technologies beyond lithium-ion including sodium-ion (abundant materials, lower cost), lithium-sulfur (higher theoretical energy density), and aluminum-air batteries. These alternatives address resource constraints, cost, and specific use-case requirements across transportation sectors.
