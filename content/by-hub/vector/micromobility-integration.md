# Micromobility Integration

## Summary

E-scooters, e-bikes, and shared cycles in urban transport networks.

## Description

Integration of lightweight electric vehicles into city transportation ecosystems through dedicated infrastructure, parking zones, and digital MaaS platforms. Micromobility offers sustainable first/last-mile solutions, reduces congestion, and is particularly prevalent in Asian and European cities.
