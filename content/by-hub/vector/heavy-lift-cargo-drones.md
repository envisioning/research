# Heavy-Lift Cargo Drones

## Summary

Autonomous aerial vehicles for middle-mile and remote logistics.

## Description

Large unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) capable of carrying significant payloads (100kg+) over medium distances. These drones bypass ground traffic for time-critical deliveries, medical supply transport, and reaching remote or hard-to-access locations.
