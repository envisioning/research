# Bidirectional V2X Grids

## Summary

Vehicle-to-Everything systems allowing EVs to stabilize the power grid.

## Description

Advanced charging infrastructure that enables electric vehicles to discharge energy back to the grid (V2G), home (V2H), or building (V2B). This turns the global fleet of EVs into a massive distributed energy storage system, helping to balance renewable energy intermittency.
