# UAM Traffic Management (UTM)

## Summary

Digital airspace management for drones and air taxis.

## Description

Automated traffic management systems for low-altitude airspace, ensuring the safe coordination of drones, eVTOLs, and other aerial vehicles. UTM handles flight authorization, conflict detection, and real-time monitoring independent of traditional air traffic control.
