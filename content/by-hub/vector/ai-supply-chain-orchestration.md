# AI Supply Chain Orchestration

## Summary

Autonomous AI platforms managing end-to-end logistics operations.

## Description

Intelligent systems that use machine learning, predictive analytics, and agentic AI to autonomously optimize supply chain decisions. These platforms integrate real-time data from IoT sensors, demand forecasts, and risk signals to dynamically route shipments, manage inventory, and respond to disruptions. Gartner predicts 50% adoption by 2030.
