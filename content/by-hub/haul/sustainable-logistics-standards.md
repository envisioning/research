# Sustainable Logistics Standards

## Summary

Protocols and technologies for measuring, reporting, and reducing carbon footprints.

## Description

Emerging standards for calculating scope 3 emissions in complex supply chains. This includes digital carbon passports for products and verified tracking of green fuel usage in shipping, driving the industry towards net-zero goals.
