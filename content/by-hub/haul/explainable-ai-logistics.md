# Explainable AI (XAI) in Logistics

## Summary

Frameworks ensuring AI routing and hiring decisions are transparent and fair.

## Description

As AI takes over driver dispatch and route planning, XAI ensures these systems don't inadvertently discriminate against certain neighborhoods or penalize drivers unfairly. It provides transparency into the 'black box' of optimization algorithms.
