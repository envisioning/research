# Ambient IoT and Smart Dust Sensors

## Summary

Ultra-low-cost, battery-free micro-sensors providing pervasive end-to-end supply chain visibility.

## Description

Ambient IoT leverages energy harvesting and backscatter communication to deploy sensors at costs below $0.10 per unit. These 'smart dust' sensors enable tracking of individual items, perishables, and high-value assets across global supply chains without battery replacement, revolutionizing traceability.
