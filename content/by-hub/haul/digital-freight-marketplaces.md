# Digital Freight Marketplaces and Dynamic Pricing

## Summary

Real-time platforms matching shippers with carriers using algorithmic pricing and smart contracts.

## Description

Digital freight platforms aggregate capacity from thousands of carriers and use real-time supply-demand matching with dynamic pricing algorithms. They automate quoting, booking, tracking, and payment through APIs and smart contracts, bringing transparency and efficiency to fragmented freight markets.
