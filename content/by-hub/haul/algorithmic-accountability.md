# Algorithmic Accountability and Audit Frameworks

## Summary

Standards and tools for auditing AI systems in supply chains for bias, fairness, and compliance.

## Description

These frameworks establish methodologies for testing, documenting, and auditing algorithmic decision-making in logistics. They ensure AI systems don't discriminate in hiring, routing, or service allocation, and provide regulatory compliance pathways for emerging AI governance laws globally.
