# Tele-Driving and Remote Operation Platforms

## Summary

Remote control centers where human operators drive trucks and forklifts from a distance.

## Description

Tele-driving bridges the gap to full autonomy by allowing human operators to remotely control vehicles during complex maneuvers or edge cases. This technology unlocks 'human-in-the-loop' automation, enabling 24/7 operations and allowing drivers to work from centralized offices rather than living on the road.
