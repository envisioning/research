# Humanitarian Logistics and Disaster Response AI

## Summary

AI-driven systems optimizing relief supply delivery in crisis zones with damaged infrastructure.

## Description

These applications use satellite imagery, mobile network data, and predictive models to optimize delivery of food, medicine, and shelter materials after disasters. They route supplies through degraded infrastructure, coordinate with NGOs and military assets, and prioritize vulnerable populations.
