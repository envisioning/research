# The Physical Internet (π-Logistics)

## Summary

Global open protocols standardizing the movement of physical goods like data packets on the internet.

## Description

The Physical Internet (PI) is a paradigm shift that conceptualizes physical goods as standardized, modular packets (π-containers) moving through a shared, open network of logistics hubs. It aims to decouple freight from specific carriers, maximizing asset utilization and radically reducing the carbon footprint of global logistics.
