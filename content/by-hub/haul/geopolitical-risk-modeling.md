# Geopolitical Risk Modeling and Scenario Planning

## Summary

AI systems that monitor sanctions, conflicts, and trade policy shifts to predict supply chain impacts.

## Description

These applications aggregate news, satellite imagery, trade data, and policy signals to forecast geopolitical disruptions. They simulate impacts of tariffs, export controls, and regional conflicts on supply networks, enabling proactive sourcing adjustments and contingency planning.
