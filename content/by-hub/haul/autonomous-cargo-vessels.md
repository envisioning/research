# Autonomous Cargo Vessels

## Summary

Unmanned maritime ships capable of navigating open oceans without an onboard crew.

## Description

Leveraging sensor fusion, satellite communication, and AI captains, these vessels promise to reduce operating costs and human error in global shipping. While currently in testing and coastal operation phases, they represent the future of maritime logistics.
