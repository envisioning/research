# Last-Mile Drone Delivery

## Summary

Aerial delivery systems for rapid transport of packages in urban and remote areas.

## Description

Drone networks bypass road congestion to deliver medical supplies and small parcels directly to consumers. Advances in battery life, noise reduction, and airspace management systems are enabling scalable commercial operations.
