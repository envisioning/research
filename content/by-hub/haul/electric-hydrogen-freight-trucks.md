# Electric and Hydrogen Freight Trucks

## Summary

Zero-emission heavy-duty vehicles for regional and long-haul freight corridors.

## Description

Battery-electric and hydrogen fuel cell trucks are emerging as viable alternatives to diesel in regional and long-haul freight. Coupled with corridor-based charging and refueling infrastructure, they reduce emissions and noise while meeting tightening regulations around urban access and green logistics.
