# Smart Ports and Automated Terminals

## Summary

Fully automated container terminals with AI-orchestrated cranes, AGVs, and predictive berth allocation.

## Description

Smart ports deploy automated stacking cranes, autonomous yard trucks, and AI-driven vessel scheduling to maximize throughput and minimize dwell time. They integrate with shipping lines' systems for predictive ETA and optimize gate operations, transforming global trade gateways into digital logistics hubs.
