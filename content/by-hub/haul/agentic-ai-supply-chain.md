# Agentic AI for Autonomous Supply Chain Operations

## Summary

Self-governing AI agents that make independent decisions across planning, procurement, and fulfillment.

## Description

Agentic AI goes beyond predictive analytics to autonomous action. These agents negotiate with suppliers, reroute shipments during disruptions, and adjust inventory policies without human intervention. They coordinate across organizational boundaries, creating self-healing supply chains that adapt in real-time.
