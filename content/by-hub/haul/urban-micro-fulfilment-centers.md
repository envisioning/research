# Urban Micro-Fulfilment Centers

## Summary

Small, highly automated warehouses embedded in cities for ultra-fast delivery.

## Description

Micro-fulfilment centers combine dense storage, robotics, and last-mile integration to support same-hour and same-day delivery in urban areas. They reposition inventory closer to demand and often integrate with dark stores, ride-hailing fleets, and bike couriers for flexible last-mile execution.
