# Autonomous Truck Platooning Corridors

## Summary

Coordinated convoys of semi-autonomous trucks traveling closely together to reduce drag and fuel consumption.

## Description

Truck platooning uses V2V communication and advanced driver assistance to allow multiple trucks to travel in tight formation on designated corridors. The lead vehicle sets the pace while following trucks automate acceleration and braking, improving fuel efficiency and increasing road throughput.
