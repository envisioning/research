# Generative AI for Supply Chain Planning

## Summary

Large language models and generative AI creating supply chain scenarios, documentation, and insights.

## Description

Generative AI assists planners by synthesizing complex datasets into natural language insights, generating RFP documents, creating network design alternatives, and simulating conversational interfaces for supply chain systems. It democratizes access to advanced analytics and accelerates decision-making.
