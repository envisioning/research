# Supply Chain Digital Twins

## Summary

Virtual replicas of the entire supply chain network for simulation and optimization.

## Description

Digital twins integrate real-time data from IoT, ERPs, and external sources to model the physical supply chain. They allow logistics managers to run 'what-if' scenarios, predict bottlenecks, and optimize network design for resilience against disruptions.
