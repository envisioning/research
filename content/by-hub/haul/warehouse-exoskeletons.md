# Warehouse Exoskeletons

## Summary

Wearable powered suits that augment human strength and reduce injury in manual handling tasks.

## Description

Passive and active exoskeletons support workers lifting heavy packages, reducing musculoskeletal injuries and extending career longevity. They integrate sensors to monitor ergonomics and fatigue, feeding data into worker safety and performance analytics systems.
