# Bio-Integrated and Living Packaging

## Summary

Packaging materials grown from biological matter that react to their environment or self-decompose.

## Description

Using mycelium, algae, or bacteria, this packaging is 'grown' rather than manufactured. It can feature bioactive properties—changing color to indicate spoilage or releasing preservatives—and is fully home-compostable, offering a radical alternative to single-use plastics in the cold chain.
