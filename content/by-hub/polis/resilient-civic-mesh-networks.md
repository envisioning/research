# Resilient Civic Mesh Networks

## Summary

Decentralized connectivity for critical services during infrastructure failures.

## Description

Community-operated wireless mesh and edge computing nodes that keep essential communications and public information services online during disasters or outages. They complement centralized telecoms with bottom-up resilience, especially in underserved areas.
