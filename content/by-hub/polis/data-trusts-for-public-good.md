# Data Trusts for Public Good

## Summary

Intermediary structures for managing data rights and access.

## Description

Legal and technical frameworks where a fiduciary organization (the Trust) manages data rights on behalf of a group of people (e.g., patients or residents). The Trust negotiates how data can be used for public benefit (like medical research) while protecting individual privacy.
