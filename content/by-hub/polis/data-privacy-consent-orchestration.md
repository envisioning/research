# Data Privacy Consent Orchestration

## Summary

Centralized consent and preference management aligned with data protection laws.

## Description

Infrastructure that lets residents see, update, and revoke how their data is used across multiple agencies and third parties. It encodes regulatory requirements (such as GDPR-style rights) while providing logs that can be audited by regulators and civil society.
