# Urban Digital Twin Platforms

## Summary

High-fidelity, real-time models of city infrastructure and services.

## Description

Digital replicas of transport, utilities, buildings, and public spaces that ingest live data from sensors and operational systems. They allow planners and operators to simulate interventions—from zoning changes to flood defenses—and coordinate across agencies around a shared spatial model.
