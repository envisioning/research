# Cross-Border Regulatory Passporting

## Summary

Mutual recognition frameworks for regulatory compliance across jurisdictions.

## Description

Digital protocols enabling businesses certified in one jurisdiction to operate in others without duplicating compliance processes. Examples include EU single-market passporting and emerging digital trade agreements. They reduce friction for cross-border services while maintaining regulatory standards through trusted data sharing.
