# Deliberative Assembly Platforms

## Summary

Digital tools for structured, large-scale citizen deliberation and consensus-building.

## Description

Platforms that combine random selection (sortition), moderated discussion spaces, and decision-support tools to run digital citizens' assemblies and juries. They help governments surface considered public judgement on complex issues beyond simple polling or referenda.
