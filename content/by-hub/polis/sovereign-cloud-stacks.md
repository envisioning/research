# Sovereign Cloud Stacks

## Summary

Nationally controlled cloud infrastructure for critical data.

## Description

Open-source, locally hosted cloud computing stacks that ensure government data remains within national borders and under local legal jurisdiction. This reduces dependency on foreign tech giants and enhances national digital resilience.
