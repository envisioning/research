# AI Bias Auditing Frameworks

## Summary

Automated tools for detecting discrimination in public algorithms.

## Description

Standardized software suites and methodologies for inspecting government AI systems (e.g., for hiring, policing, or benefits) to detect and mitigate statistical biases against protected groups, ensuring algorithmic fairness and legal compliance.
