# E-Residency Programs

## Summary

Transnational digital identities enabling cross-border entrepreneurship.

## Description

Government-issued digital identities for non-citizens, allowing them to establish businesses, sign documents, and access services remotely. Estonia's pioneering program has issued over 100,000 e-residencies, enabling a global network of digital entrepreneurs and tax revenue from borderless economic activity.
