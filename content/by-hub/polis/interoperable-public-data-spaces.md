# Interoperable Public Data Spaces

## Summary

Shared data infrastructure enabling secure cross-agency and cross-border collaboration.

## Description

Federated data architectures, standards, and governance models that allow ministries, municipalities, and partner institutions to share sensitive data under strict controls. They support missions such as health, mobility, or climate across jurisdictions without centralizing all information.
