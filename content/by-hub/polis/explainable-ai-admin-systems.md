# Explainable AI for Administrative Decisions

## Summary

Transparent reasoning chains for AI-driven government determinations.

## Description

AI systems that provide human-interpretable explanations for administrative decisions (benefits eligibility, permit approvals, risk assessments). Required by laws like the EU AI Act, they generate audit trails showing which factors influenced outcomes, enabling citizens to challenge errors and regulators to verify fairness.
