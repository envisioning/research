# Decentralized Citizen Sensing

## Summary

Community-owned IoT networks for environmental monitoring.

## Description

Networks of low-cost, connected sensors (measuring air quality, noise, traffic) owned and maintained by citizens. These systems provide granular, independent data to hold authorities accountable and inform local policy decisions.
