# Civic Reputation Credentials

## Summary

Portable records of constructive civic contributions and public service.

## Description

Digital credentials that capture verified civic actions—such as volunteering, participation in assemblies, or leadership roles in community projects. These credentials can be used to weigh input in expert-driven processes, offer recognition, or gate sensitive participation without exposing full identity.
