# Autonomous Infrastructure Maintenance

## Summary

Robotic systems for self-repairing public assets.

## Description

Fleets of autonomous drones and robots equipped with computer vision and repair tools (e.g., asphalt 3D printers) that continuously monitor and fix public infrastructure like roads, bridges, and pipes. They shift maintenance from scheduled/reactive to continuous/preventative, reducing costs and disruptions.
