# Post-Quantum Government Cryptography

## Summary

Quantum-resistant encryption protecting long-term state secrets.

## Description

Migration of government communications, archives, and identity systems to cryptographic algorithms resistant to quantum computer attacks. Based on NIST's post-quantum standards, these systems protect sensitive data with decades-long secrecy requirements from 'harvest now, decrypt later' threats.
