# Zero-Knowledge Compliance Layers

## Summary

Cryptographic proofs for verifying status without revealing data.

## Description

Protocols allowing citizens and businesses to prove they meet specific criteria (e.g., 'solvent', 'resident', 'tax-compliant') without sharing the underlying financial or personal records. This minimizes data liability for regulators while preserving absolute privacy for the regulated entities.
