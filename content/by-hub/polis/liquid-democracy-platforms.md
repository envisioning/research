# Liquid Democracy Platforms

## Summary

Hybrid voting systems combining direct and representative democracy.

## Description

Digital platforms that allow citizens to vote directly on issues or delegate their vote to trusted experts or representatives for specific topic areas. These systems enable dynamic, revocable delegation, offering a middle ground between direct referendum and traditional representation.
