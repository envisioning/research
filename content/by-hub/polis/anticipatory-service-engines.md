# Anticipatory Service Engines

## Summary

Proactive delivery of public benefits based on life events.

## Description

Systems that leverage interoperable data to trigger services automatically—such as enrolling a child in school upon birth registration or issuing tax credits upon income changes—without requiring citizens to apply. This 'no-stop shop' model shifts government from reactive bureaucracy to proactive support.
