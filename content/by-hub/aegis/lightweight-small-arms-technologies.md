# Lightweight Small Arms Technologies

## Summary

Caseless, telescoped, and advanced materials for lighter weapons.

## Description

Lightweight Small Arms Technologies (LSAT) encompasses caseless ammunition, telescoped rounds, and advanced materials to reduce soldier load. Research and development. Applications: infantry weapons, logistics. Benefits: lighter ammunition, reduced burden.
