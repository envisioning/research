# Electrolaser

## Summary

Laser-ionized channels for directed electrical discharge in defense applications

## Description

Electrolasers use a laser to ionize a path, then discharge electricity along that path. Research and development. Potential applications include non-lethal weapons and directed energy. Challenges include range, reliability, and environmental effects.
