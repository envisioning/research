# Cyber Kill Chain Automation

## Summary

AI-driven orchestration of reconnaissance through response.

## Description

Security orchestration platforms automate each phase of the cyber kill chain—reconnaissance, weaponization, delivery, exploitation, installation, C2, and actions—accelerating defensive cycles and counter-offensives.
