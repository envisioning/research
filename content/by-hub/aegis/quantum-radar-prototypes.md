# Quantum Radar Prototypes

## Summary

Entanglement-enhanced sensing to detect stealth objects.

## Description

Early-stage quantum illumination radars leverage entangled photons to improve detection of low-RCS targets amidst noise, offering potential breakthroughs in anti-stealth surveillance if scaled.
