# Supply Chain Security & Sanctions Resilience

## Summary

Monitoring and rerouting critical material flows under disruption.

## Description

AI- and sensor-backed systems that map, monitor, and reroute critical material and component supply chains under conditions of disruption or sanctions. They identify single points of failure, counterfeit risks, and opportunities for diversification to maintain defense readiness.
