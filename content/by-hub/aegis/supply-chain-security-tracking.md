# Supply Chain Security Tracking

## Summary

RFID and blockchain-backed ledgers tracing defense logistics.

## Description

Defense logisticians tag shipments with RFID, satellite trackers, and blockchain attestations to monitor custody, detect tampering, and automate sanctions compliance across global suppliers.
