# Critical Infrastructure Resilience

## Summary

Autonomous monitoring of grids and pipelines with islandable microgrids.

## Description

Autonomous monitoring of grids, pipelines, and ports using edge-deployed sensors and drones. Islandable microgrids supply continuity during outages or attacks, ensuring essential services remain operational.
