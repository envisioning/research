# Electrothermal-Chemical Technology

## Summary

Enhanced propellants using electrical discharge for tank, artillery, and close-in weapons.

## Description

Electrothermal-chemical (ETC) technology uses electrical discharge to enhance propellant combustion for guns. Research and development. Applications: tank, artillery, close-in weapon systems. Potential for higher muzzle velocity and reduced propellant mass.
