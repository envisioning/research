# Algorithmic Targeting Transparency & Auditability

## Summary

Tooling to log, explain, and audit AI-assisted targeting decisions.

## Description

Frameworks and tooling that log, explain, and audit how AI systems contribute to target identification and engagement decisions. These mechanisms support post-hoc review, accountability, and coalition trust in AI-enabled targeting processes.
