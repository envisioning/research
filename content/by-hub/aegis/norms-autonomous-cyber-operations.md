# Norms for Autonomous Cyber Operations

## Summary

Guidelines for when autonomous cyber agents may act without humans.

## Description

International and coalition norms specifying when autonomous cyber agents may act without live human authorization, what constraints apply, and which targets are categorically off-limits. These guardrails constrain escalation and collateral damage in automated cyber campaigns.
