# Information Operations & Cognitive Security Platforms

## Summary

Detection of coordinated influence and design of counter-messaging.

## Description

Platforms that monitor narrative dynamics across media channels, detect coordinated influence operations, and propose counter-messaging or inoculation strategies. They treat the information environment as a primary theater, defending populations against manipulation and cognitive attacks.
