# MAHEM Molten Metal Penetrator

## Summary

Formed penetrator using molten metal; explosively pumped flux compression.

## Description

MAHEM (Magneto-Hydrodynamic Explosive Munition) uses explosively pumped flux compression to form and accelerate molten metal penetrators. Research and development. Applications: anti-armor. Alternative to self-forging explosives.
