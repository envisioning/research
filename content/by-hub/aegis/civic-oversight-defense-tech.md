# Civic Oversight & Democratic Governance of Defense Tech

## Summary

Mechanisms for democratic scrutiny of advanced defense systems.

## Description

Mechanisms for parliaments, civil society, and independent review bodies to scrutinize, question, and constrain the deployment of advanced defense systems. Ensures that rapidly evolving capabilities remain aligned with democratic values and public consent.
