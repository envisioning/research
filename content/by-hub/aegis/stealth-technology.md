# Stealth Technology

## Summary

Radar-absorbent materials and design for reduced detectability.

## Description

Stealth technology reduces radar cross-section via shaping, radar-absorbent materials, and electronic countermeasures. Research, development, commercialization. Applications: stealth aircraft, ships, vehicles. Established technology; research continues into multi-spectral stealth.
