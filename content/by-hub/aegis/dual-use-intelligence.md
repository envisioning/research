# Dual-Use Intelligence

## Summary

Managing risks of defensive tech leveraged for non-defensive purposes.

## Description

Managing risks when satellite, cyber, or sensing technologies are leveraged for non-defensive purposes. Addresses the proliferation of military-grade capabilities to non-state actors or for domestic repression.
