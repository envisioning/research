# Adversary Digital Twins

## Summary

Continuously updated behavior models of adversary forces and doctrine.

## Description

Digital twins that model adversary units, organizations, and doctrine using OSINT, SIGINT, HUMINT, and battlefield telemetry. These models support scenario testing, red-teaming, and wargaming by simulating how opponents adapt under different strategies and pressure.
