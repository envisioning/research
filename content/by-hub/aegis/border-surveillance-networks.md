# Border Surveillance Networks

## Summary

Sensor towers, aerostats, and AI analytics covering vast frontiers.

## Description

Integrated border systems combine radar towers, lidar, aerostats, and unattended ground sensors with AI-enabled analytics to detect incursions across deserts, mountains, and maritime boundaries in all weather conditions.
