# Fonds du Logement / SNHBM (Luxembourg Delivery Vehicles)

## Summary

State-linked institutions that develop and finance affordable housing, increasingly central to Luxembourg’s ability to deliver in a land-constrained market.

## Description

Luxembourg relies on state-linked delivery vehicles such as Fonds du Logement and SNHBM to develop and finance affordable and social housing. In a market with extreme land costs and concentrated ownership, these institutions can act as counterweights by assembling land, shaping projects, and stabilizing affordability.

Their effectiveness depends on land access (including pre-emption/expropriation tools), stable funding, and coordination with municipalities under Pacte Logement.

The signal represents whether Luxembourg has sufficiently empowered institutions to deliver public value at scale rather than only regulate a market it cannot control. Key challenges include land acquisition, scaling production capacity, and ensuring delivery is geographically and socially equitable.
