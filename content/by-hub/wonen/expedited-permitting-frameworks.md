# Expedited Permitting Frameworks

## Summary

Streamlined approval processes that reduce approval cycles and uncertainty while maintaining quality standards.

## Description

Expedited permitting frameworks are governance arrangements designed to accelerate approval processes for housing and energy projects while maintaining quality and compliance standards. These frameworks often involve pre-approval processes, digital submission systems, coordinated multi-jurisdictional reviews, and clear decision timelines.

In the Benelux context, where multi-layered governance creates prolonged approval cycles, expedited frameworks can significantly reduce time-to-approval. Examples include digital permitting platforms, one-stop-shop approaches, and fast-track procedures for projects meeting specific sustainability or social value criteria.

The signal represents a shift from reactive, sequential approval processes to proactive, coordinated governance that enables development while maintaining oversight. Key challenges include balancing speed with thoroughness, ensuring transparency, and maintaining democratic accountability.
