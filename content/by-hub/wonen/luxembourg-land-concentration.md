# Luxembourg Land Ownership Concentration

## Summary

Extreme concentration of developable land ownership among few families, enabling strategic withholding and price manipulation.

## Description

Luxembourg has extreme concentration of developable land ownership, with estimates suggesting a small number of families control the majority of buildable land. This concentration enables strategic land withholding—holding land off the market to increase scarcity and prices.

Unlike markets with dispersed ownership where individual decisions average out, concentrated ownership allows coordinated supply restriction. Land mobilization tools (Baulandmobilisierungsgesetz) specifically target this dynamic.

The signal represents structural market failure requiring policy intervention. Key challenges include politically powerful land-owning constituencies, strong property rights traditions, and the difficulty of forcing development without expropriation.
