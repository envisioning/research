# Grondbanken (Land Banks)

## Summary

Public or non-profit entities that acquire land for strategic long-term development, removing speculative pressure and enabling social goals.

## Description

Grondbanken (Land Banks) are entities established to acquire, hold, and release land for development according to strategic goals rather than market timing. By removing land from the speculative market, they enable developments that prioritize affordability and social value over maximizing immediate returns.

In Benelux, land banks are used by municipalities and regional governments to steer development, particularly for affordable housing and nature compensation (stikstof reduction). They allow proactive rather than reactive planning.

The signal represents a structural intervention in the land market to reclaim public control over development direction. Key challenges include high capital requirements for acquisition and political will to hold land long-term.
