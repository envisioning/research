# Anti-Speculatiebedingen (Anti-Speculation Clauses)

## Summary

Deed restrictions preventing quick resale or requiring profit-sharing, ensuring housing serves residents rather than investors.

## Description

Anti-speculatiebedingen are contractual clauses attached to property sales that restrict resale within a specified period (typically 5-10 years) or require sellers to share profits with the municipality or original developer. These clauses aim to ensure housing serves actual residents rather than investors seeking quick returns.

In the Netherlands, these clauses are increasingly used for affordable housing and new developments, particularly those with public subsidy. Belgian and Luxembourg equivalents exist but are less systematically applied.

The signal represents a legal tool to prevent extractive investment while maintaining private ownership. Key challenges include enforcement complexity, determining appropriate restriction periods, and ensuring clauses don't trap owners in unsuitable housing situations.
