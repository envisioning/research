# Industrialized Construction at Scale (Procurement + Standardization)

## Summary

The shift from ‘modular exists’ to ‘modular delivers’: standard designs, repeatable approvals, and aggregated procurement that make factories viable.

## Description

Industrialized construction becomes transformative only when procurement and standardization enable repetition: standardized typologies, repeatable approvals, framework contracts, and aggregated demand (from housing associations and municipalities) that justify factory investment.

This can reduce labor intensity, improve quality control, and shorten delivery timelines—directly addressing construction labor shortages and cost volatility.

The signal represents the operational layer needed to make faster, lower-disruption delivery politically and economically viable. Key challenges include balancing standardization with place-based design quality, coordinating buyers, and ensuring industrialization does not reduce social acceptance through perceived ‘cookie-cutter’ outcomes.
