# Flexwoningen (Flex Housing)

## Summary

Factory-built relocatable housing units with 15-30 year lifespans, enabling rapid deployment on temporary sites to address acute shortages.

## Description

Flexwoningen are prefabricated, relocatable housing units designed for temporary placement (typically 10-30 years) on sites awaiting permanent development. The Dutch government has set targets of 37,500 flexwoningen by 2024-2025 as an emergency response to housing shortages and asylum seeker accommodation needs.

These units can be deployed on temporarily available land (awaiting development, remediation, or infrastructure), then relocated when the site is needed. Quality has improved significantly, with modern flexwoningen indistinguishable from permanent housing.

The signal represents pragmatic acceleration of housing delivery by decoupling construction from permanent land allocation. Key challenges include finding suitable temporary sites, community acceptance ('not in my backyard'), ensuring quality of life for residents, and the economics of relocation.
