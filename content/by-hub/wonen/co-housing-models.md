# Co-Housing Models

## Summary

Intentional communities combining private homes with shared facilities, building acceptance through resident involvement in design and management.

## Description

Co-housing models create intentional communities where residents have private homes but share common facilities like kitchens, gardens, and community spaces. Residents are involved in design, development, and ongoing management, building strong community connections and acceptance.

In Benelux, co-housing is well-established, particularly in the Netherlands and Belgium, where it addresses both housing needs and community building. Examples include Dutch ecodorp initiatives, Belgian co-housing projects, and Luxembourg intentional communities.

The signal represents a development model that builds acceptance through resident involvement and community building. Key challenges include finding suitable sites, financing models, and managing community dynamics over time.
