# Baulandmobilisierungsgesetz (Land Mobilization)

## Summary

Tax and regulatory tools to force the development of hoarded buildable land, addressing speculative withholding of supply.

## Description

Inspired by German legislation and relevant to Luxembourg's extreme land costs, 'Land Mobilization' laws (Baulandmobilisierungsgesetz) introduce tools to penalize land hoarding. These include 'building obligations' (Bauverpflichtung) for zoned land and higher taxes on undeveloped buildable plots (Baulandsteuer).

In Luxembourg and border regions, where land ownership is highly concentrated, these measures aim to unlock supply without new zoning. They represent a regulatory intervention against the 'extractive' passive holding of land assets.

The signal represents a shift from incentivizing development to penalizing non-development of strategic resources. Key challenges include strong property rights protections and political resistance from land-owning constituencies.
