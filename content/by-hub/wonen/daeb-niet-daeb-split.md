# DAEB / Niet-DAEB Split

## Summary

EU-mandated separation of social housing activities from commercial activities, reshaping what housing associations can build and for whom.

## Description

The DAEB (Diensten van Algemeen Economisch Belang) / niet-DAEB split separates housing association activities into regulated social tasks (DAEB: social rental, specific target groups) and commercial activities (niet-DAEB: mid-rent, market sale). This EU state-aid compliant structure limits cross-subsidization.

The split fundamentally affects what corporaties can build: DAEB is restricted to households below income thresholds, while niet-DAEB requires market-rate financing and risk. This has reduced middle-income housing ('middenhuur') production by corporaties.

The signal represents how EU competition rules reshape national housing policy. Key challenges include the 'missing middle' created by the split, complexity of managing separate administrative structures, and debates about whether the split should be relaxed.
