# Middenhuur (Mid-Rent Segment)

## Summary

Policy focus on the 'missing middle' rental segment between social housing and free market, addressing middle-income housing needs.

## Description

Middenhuur refers to rental housing in the 'middle segment'—above social housing income limits but below unaffordable free-market rents (roughly €750-1,000/month in NL). This segment has shrunk dramatically as social housing tightened targeting and private rents increased.

Policies to stimulate middenhuur include rent regulation extending to mid-segment, density bonuses for mid-rent units, and housing association niet-DAEB activities. The 2024 Dutch rent regulation (Wet Betaalbare Huur) specifically targets this segment.

The signal represents recognition that housing policy must address middle incomes, not just lowest incomes. Key challenges include defining the segment (income limits vary), ensuring mid-rent doesn't crowd out social housing, and whether regulation enables or discourages mid-rent development.
