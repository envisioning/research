# PFAS Soil Contamination

## Summary

Emerging 'forever chemicals' contamination crisis blocking brownfield development and requiring costly remediation across Benelux.

## Description

PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) contamination is emerging as a major barrier to housing development, particularly on brownfield sites. These 'forever chemicals' don't break down naturally, requiring expensive remediation. Stricter testing requirements have revealed widespread contamination previously unknown.

In Flanders, the 3M Zwijndrecht scandal triggered emergency measures, while NL and Luxembourg are implementing stricter soil standards. Sites previously cleared for development now require remediation, adding costs and delays.

The signal represents an emerging environmental barrier comparable to the nitrogen crisis. Key challenges include the scale of unknown contamination, remediation costs (often making projects unviable), and balancing precautionary standards with development needs.
