# Woningpas (Building Passport)

## Summary

Digital documentation systems compiling all building information (energy, permits, renovations) to enable informed decisions and streamline transactions.

## Description

The Woningpas (Flemish) and similar building passport initiatives create comprehensive digital records for each property, including energy performance, permits, renovation history, and compliance status. This transparency enables informed buying decisions and identifies renovation needs.

In Flanders, the Woningpas is mandatory for sales, while Dutch and Luxembourg equivalents are developing. The systems can identify which buildings need energy retrofits, streamline permit applications, and reduce information asymmetries.

The signal represents digitization enabling better housing decisions and policy targeting. Key challenges include data accuracy and completeness, privacy concerns, and ensuring passports don't create barriers for older properties that lack documentation.
