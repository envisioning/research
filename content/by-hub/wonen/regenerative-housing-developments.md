# Regenerative Housing Developments

## Summary

Housing projects designed as long-term regenerative partners that contribute to local wellbeing and urban resilience.

## Description

Regenerative housing developments are designed to contribute positively to their surrounding neighborhoods and ecosystems, going beyond sustainability to actively improve local conditions. These developments integrate affordable housing, public amenities, environmental restoration, economic opportunities, and social infrastructure.

In Benelux, regenerative models can reposition housing from extractive to contributive, addressing community concerns while meeting housing needs. Examples include mixed-income developments with public spaces, energy-positive buildings that feed local grids, and projects that restore natural habitats.

The signal represents a shift from minimizing negative impacts to maximizing positive contributions, positioning housing as infrastructure for community wellbeing. Key challenges include higher upfront costs, measuring regenerative outcomes, and scaling beyond individual projects.
