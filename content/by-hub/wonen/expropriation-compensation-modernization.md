# Expropriation & Compensation Modernization

## Summary

Updates to compulsory purchase and compensation rules aimed at making land assembly feasible for housing while maintaining legitimacy and fair treatment.

## Description

Expropriation (compulsory purchase) exists across Benelux but is politically sensitive and legally complex. Modernization debates focus on whether current compensation rules (often tied to speculative market value) make land assembly prohibitively expensive, and whether procedures are too slow to meet housing urgency.

Tools like pre-emption rights, mandatory development obligations, and stronger justification standards for public interest are often part of the same policy package.

The signal represents a high-stakes governance lever: whether the state can assemble land for housing at scale without triggering backlash that undermines legitimacy. Key challenges include constitutional property protections, court scrutiny, political optics, and avoiding the perception of state-enabled extraction.
