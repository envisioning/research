# Social Impact Bonds for Housing

## Summary

Financial instruments where private investors fund housing interventions and receive returns based on measurable social outcomes.

## Description

Social Impact Bonds (SIBs) for housing are 'pay for success' contracts where private investors provide upfront capital for housing interventions, and governments repay investors (with returns) only if predefined social outcomes are achieved. Metrics might include reduced homelessness, improved health outcomes, or neighborhood stability.

In Benelux, SIBs are emerging for homelessness prevention, supportive housing, and energy retrofits. They align private capital with public goals by making returns contingent on social value.

The signal represents innovative finance that makes social value measurable and investable. Key challenges include defining appropriate metrics, ensuring outcomes aren't gamed, and the transaction costs of complex contracting structures.
