# Participatory Planning Models

## Summary

Co-creation frameworks that involve communities in design and planning processes from project inception.

## Description

Participatory planning models engage local communities in the design and planning of housing and development projects from the earliest stages, rather than seeking approval for pre-designed proposals. These models include co-design workshops, community planning charrettes, resident advisory boards, and iterative feedback processes.

In Benelux, where community opposition can delay or block projects, participatory models can build acceptance by ensuring developments reflect local needs and values. Examples include Dutch wooncooperaties (housing cooperatives), Belgian community land trusts, and Luxembourg participatory budgeting for housing.

The signal represents a shift from consultation to co-creation, positioning communities as partners rather than obstacles. Key challenges include managing expectations, ensuring representative participation, and balancing community input with technical and financial constraints.
