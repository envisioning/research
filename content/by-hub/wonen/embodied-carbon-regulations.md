# Embodied Carbon Regulations

## Summary

Regulatory requirements to measure and reduce carbon emissions from construction materials and building processes, not just operational energy.

## Description

Embodied carbon regulations require developers to measure and reduce carbon emissions from construction materials, manufacturing, transportation, and building processes, in addition to operational energy use. These regulations address the full lifecycle carbon footprint of buildings.

In Benelux, embodied carbon regulations are emerging as part of broader climate policies. Examples include Dutch material passport requirements, Belgian lifecycle assessment mandates, and Luxembourg carbon footprint regulations for construction.

The signal represents expanding sustainability requirements beyond operational energy to include construction impacts. Key challenges include measurement methodologies, availability of low-carbon materials, and balancing carbon reduction with other goals like affordability.
