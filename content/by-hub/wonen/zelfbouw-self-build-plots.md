# Zelfbouw / Collectief Particulier Opdrachtgeverschap

## Summary

Self-build plots and collective private commissioning models enabling individuals and groups to develop their own housing.

## Description

Zelfbouw (self-build) and CPO (Collectief Particulier Opdrachtgeverschap / collective private commissioning) enable individuals or groups to develop their own housing rather than buying from developers. Municipalities allocate plots; individuals or groups commission architects and contractors.

These models can produce more diverse, affordable, and community-rooted housing than standard development. Amsterdam targets 10% self-build; other municipalities have similar programs.

The signal represents democratizing development by enabling non-professional actors to build. Key challenges include capacity of individuals to manage complex projects, financing for non-standard developments, and ensuring self-build doesn't become only viable for wealthy households.
