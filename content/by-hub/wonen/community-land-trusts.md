# Community Land Trusts

## Summary

Nonprofit organizations that own land and lease it to residents, separating land ownership from housing to ensure permanent affordability and community control.

## Description

Community Land Trusts (CLTs) are nonprofit organizations that acquire and hold land in trust for community benefit, leasing it to residents who own their homes but not the land. This model ensures permanent affordability and prevents speculation while maintaining community control.

In Benelux, CLTs are emerging as a model for affordable housing that addresses both housing needs and community concerns about displacement. Examples include Belgian CLT initiatives in Brussels and Wallonia, and Dutch community land trust experiments.

The signal represents a model that can generate long-term social value while building community acceptance. Key challenges include initial land acquisition costs, legal frameworks for land trusts, and scaling beyond pilot projects.
