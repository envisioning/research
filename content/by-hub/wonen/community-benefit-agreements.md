# Community Benefit Agreements

## Summary

Formal agreements that ensure housing developments generate demonstrable social value for surrounding neighborhoods.

## Description

Community Benefit Agreements (CBAs) are formal contracts between developers and community organizations that specify social value contributions, such as affordable housing units, local employment, public amenities, environmental improvements, or community investment funds. These agreements make social value commitments legally binding and measurable.

In Benelux, CBAs can address community concerns about extractive development by ensuring tangible benefits. Examples include affordable housing quotas, local hiring requirements, public space improvements, and community investment funds tied to project approval.

The signal represents a shift from voluntary community contributions to formal, enforceable social value commitments. Key challenges include ensuring agreements are meaningful, monitoring compliance, and balancing developer viability with community benefits.
