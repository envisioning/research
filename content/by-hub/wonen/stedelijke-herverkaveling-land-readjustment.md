# Stedelijke Herverkaveling (Land Readjustment)

## Summary

Legal and planning tools that pool fragmented parcels and redistribute development rights, enabling infill without full expropriation.

## Description

Land readjustment (stedelijke herverkaveling) enables municipalities to assemble fragmented ownership in redevelopment areas by pooling parcels, replotting boundaries, and redistributing land/value so each owner receives an equitable share after development. It is a middle path between purely voluntary assembly and expropriation.

In dense Benelux cities where ownership fragmentation blocks infill and brownfield redevelopment, readjustment can unlock sites while reducing perceived unfairness and litigation risk.

The signal represents institutional capacity to overcome micro-level land friction that stalls macro-level housing targets. Key challenges include complex valuation, owner trust, legal robustness across jurisdictions, and the administrative capacity required to run readjustment processes well.
