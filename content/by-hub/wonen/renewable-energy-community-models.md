# Renewable Energy Community Models

## Summary

Legal frameworks enabling communities to collectively own and benefit from renewable energy, integrated with housing development.

## Description

Renewable energy community models are legal frameworks that enable groups of citizens, businesses, or organizations to collectively own, develop, and benefit from renewable energy projects. The EU Renewable Energy Directive requires member states to enable these models, which can be integrated with housing development.

In Benelux, energy community models can address opposition to renewable energy by ensuring local ownership and benefit. Examples include Dutch energy cooperatives, Belgian citizen energy communities, and Luxembourg renewable energy communities integrated with housing.

The signal represents a model that can build acceptance for renewable energy while generating local value. Key challenges include regulatory implementation, financing models, and technical coordination between housing and energy systems.
