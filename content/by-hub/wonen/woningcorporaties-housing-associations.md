# Woningcorporaties (Housing Associations)

## Summary

The Netherlands' unique system of non-profit housing associations owning 29% of all housing stock, the largest social housing sector in Europe.

## Description

Woningcorporaties are private non-profit organizations that own, manage, and develop social housing in the Netherlands. With approximately 2.3 million homes (29% of total stock), they represent the largest social housing sector in Europe. They are key players in any housing development discussion.

Corporaties operate under strict regulation (Woningwet), with mandates for affordability, quality, and neighborhood investment. Recent reforms split their activities into regulated social tasks (DAEB) and commercial activities (niet-DAEB), affecting their development capacity.

The signal represents a massive institutional infrastructure for social value generation in housing. Key challenges include rebuilding development capacity after years of taxation (verhuurderheffing), navigating the DAEB/niet-DAEB split, and balancing affordability mandates with sustainability investments.
