# Zorgwoningen (Care-Integrated Living)

## Summary

Hybrid housing-care models that allow elderly to stay in neighborhoods, reducing resistance to densification by serving local needs.

## Description

Zorgwoningen differ from traditional nursing homes by integrating independent living units with on-demand care infrastructure within normal residential neighborhoods. Driven by the 'aging in place' policy shift in Benelux, these developments often face less opposition than standard apartments because they serve a visible local need.

Models range from 'Knarrenhof' (senior courts) in NL to technology-enabled smart apartments. They release larger family homes back to the market while keeping seniors in their community.

The signal represents innovation in housing typology driven by demographic necessity. Key challenges include financing the care infrastructure and ensuring affordability for middle-income seniors.
