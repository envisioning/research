# Digital Permitting Platforms

## Summary

Technology platforms that streamline permit applications, coordinate multi-jurisdictional reviews, and provide transparency.

## Description

Digital permitting platforms use technology to streamline permit application processes, coordinate reviews across multiple jurisdictions, provide real-time status updates, and increase transparency. These platforms can reduce approval times, improve coordination, and build trust through visibility.

In Benelux, where multi-layered governance creates complexity, digital platforms can coordinate processes while maintaining accountability. Examples include Dutch Omgevingsloket (environmental permit portal), Belgian digital building permit systems, and Luxembourg integrated planning platforms.

The signal represents technology-enabled governance that can reduce barriers while maintaining oversight. Key challenges include ensuring accessibility, maintaining data privacy, and coordinating across different authority systems.
