# Wooncooperaties (Housing Cooperatives)

## Summary

Member-owned housing organizations where residents collectively own and manage their housing, common in Netherlands and growing in Belgium.

## Description

Wooncooperaties are housing cooperatives where residents are both owners and members, collectively making decisions about their housing. This model is well-established in the Netherlands, where cooperatives own significant portions of the housing stock, and is growing in Belgium and Luxembourg.

Cooperatives can enable community-led development, build acceptance through resident ownership, and create long-term stewardship models. They often integrate social housing, sustainability, and community amenities. Examples include Dutch housing associations, Belgian co-housing cooperatives, and Luxembourg cooperative housing initiatives.

The signal represents community ownership as both a development model and an engagement strategy. Key challenges include financing cooperative development, managing democratic decision-making, and scaling beyond individual projects.
