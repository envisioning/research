# Housing First Approaches

## Summary

Evidence-based policy providing permanent housing to homeless individuals without preconditions, demonstrating housing as social infrastructure.

## Description

Housing First is an evidence-based approach providing permanent housing to homeless individuals without requiring sobriety, employment, or program participation as preconditions. Support services are offered but not mandated. Research consistently shows better outcomes than 'treatment first' approaches.

In Benelux, Housing First is expanding through pilot programs and policy adoption. It reframes housing as a right and precondition for addressing other challenges, rather than a reward for 'housing readiness.'

The signal represents housing policy as social policy, demonstrating that housing itself is a solution to complex social problems. Key challenges include scaling beyond pilots, securing sufficient housing stock, and shifting institutional cultures from gatekeeping to housing provision.
