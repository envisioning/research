# Co-Living Models

## Summary

Housing models combining private bedrooms with shared living spaces, addressing affordability and community building while potentially reducing opposition.

## Description

Co-living models provide private bedrooms with shared kitchens, living spaces, and amenities, often targeting young professionals, students, or older adults. These models can address affordability, build community, and potentially reduce opposition through smaller individual units.

In Benelux, co-living is growing as a response to housing shortages and changing lifestyles. Examples include Dutch co-living platforms, Belgian shared housing initiatives, and Luxembourg co-living developments.

The signal represents a housing model that can address affordability and community building. Key challenges include regulatory frameworks for shared housing, managing community dynamics, and ensuring quality of shared spaces.
