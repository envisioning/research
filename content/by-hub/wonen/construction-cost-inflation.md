# Construction Cost Inflation

## Summary

Post-pandemic material and labor cost increases making previously viable housing projects uneconomical, particularly affecting affordable housing.

## Description

Construction costs in Benelux have increased 20-40% since 2020, driven by material price inflation (timber, steel, concrete), energy costs, labor shortages, and supply chain disruptions. Projects that were financially viable pre-pandemic may no longer work at current costs.

This particularly affects affordable housing and social housing, where sale prices or rents are capped but construction costs are market-driven. Housing associations report projects becoming unviable mid-development.

The signal represents economic barriers that can halt development even when regulatory barriers are cleared. Key challenges include whether cost inflation is temporary or structural, how to maintain affordability commitments when costs rise, and whether industrialized construction can reduce cost volatility.
