# Omgevingsvisies (Spatial Strategy) as Constraint

## Summary

Long-range spatial strategies (national, provincial, municipal) that increasingly decide what can be built before any project enters permitting.

## Description

Omgevingsvisies (environmental/spatial visions) are strategic plans that set long-term direction for land use, mobility, climate adaptation, nature protection, and housing growth. Under Omgevingswet logic, these visions become a ‘pre-permitting’ layer that can enable or preclude entire development approaches.

In practice, disputes about housing density, greenfield expansion, and climate constraints (water/soil, nitrogen, biodiversity) are increasingly decided at the strategy level—then later defended in courts and permit procedures.

The signal represents governance moving upstream: the fight shifts from individual projects to the strategic frameworks that define what counts as acceptable development. Key challenges include making visions actionable rather than aspirational, ensuring public participation is meaningful, and managing conflicts between climate limits and housing urgency.
