# Aardgasvrij (Gas-Free Neighborhoods)

## Summary

Mandatory transition from natural gas heating in existing and new neighborhoods, requiring coordination between housing and energy infrastructure.

## Description

Aardgasvrij wijken (gas-free neighborhoods) are designated areas where natural gas infrastructure is being removed and replaced with alternatives (district heating, heat pumps, hydrogen). New construction in NL has been gas-free since 2018; existing neighborhoods are transitioning on municipal timelines.

The transition requires coordination between housing renovation, energy infrastructure investment, and resident engagement. It can face opposition from residents concerned about costs and disruption.

The signal represents the intersection of housing policy with energy transition, where development decisions are inseparable from infrastructure choices. Key challenges include costs of retrofit and new infrastructure, ensuring affordability for lower-income households, and coordinating multiple stakeholders (grid operators, municipalities, housing associations, residents).
