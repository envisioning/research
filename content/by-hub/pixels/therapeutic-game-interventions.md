# Therapeutic Game Interventions

## Summary

Clinically validated games for mental health and neurorehabilitation.

## Description

Therapeutic game interventions are built with clinicians to target ADHD, anxiety, depression, and stroke rehab. They weave FDA-cleared mechanics—working-memory tasks, gait training mini-games, exposure therapy modules—into engaging loops, often paired with wearables or telehealth portals. Products like Akili’s EndeavorRx, DeepWell’s catalog, and XR rehab platforms deliver measurable outcomes, logging biomarker improvements directly into electronic health records.

Hospitals prescribe these games as digital therapeutics, insurers evaluate reimbursement based on longitudinal data, and pediatricians prefer them to pills for certain cases. Mental-health startups mix game design with CBT, letting patients rehearse coping strategies inside stylized worlds. For neurorehab, clinics deploy mixed-reality titles where patients swat holographic targets to rebuild motor control, while remote-monitoring dashboards alert therapists when adherence drops.

TRL 7 solutions navigate regulatory pathways (FDA, CE marks) and privacy laws (HIPAA, GDPR). Studios must run randomized controlled trials, maintain quality systems, and manage clinical content updates carefully. Partnerships with pharma, payers, and academic researchers are key to scaling distribution. As XR hardware lightens and AI personalization tailors difficulty to individual biomarkers, therapeutic games will sit alongside medications and physical therapy as mainstream tools in the healthcare arsenal.
