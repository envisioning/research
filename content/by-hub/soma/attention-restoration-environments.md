# Attention Restoration Environments

## Summary

Nature-based XR and ambient systems for mental fatigue recovery.

## Description

Immersive and ambient environments based on Attention Restoration Theory, using natural soundscapes, biophilic design, and low-stimulation visuals to reduce cognitive load, restore directed attention, and counter digital exhaustion.
