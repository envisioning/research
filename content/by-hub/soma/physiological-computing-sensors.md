# Physiological Computing Sensors

## Summary

Multi-parameter biosignal devices tracking HRV, GSR, respiration, and muscle tension.

## Description

Wearable or embedded sensor arrays that measure heart rate variability, galvanic skin response, breathing patterns, and electromyography to provide real-time indices of stress, arousal, cognitive load, and emotional valence for adaptive systems.
