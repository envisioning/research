# Neurodivergent Design Standards

## Summary

Accessibility guidelines centered on cognitive and sensory differences.

## Description

Co-created standards ensuring affective technologies don't pathologize neurological variation, provide opt-outs from social-emotional inference, and design for stimming, masking reduction, and sensory regulation rather than normalization.
