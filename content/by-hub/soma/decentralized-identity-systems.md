# Decentralized Identity Systems

## Summary

Self-sovereign protocols and orchestration layers for cross-platform identity.

## Description

Blockchain-based identity standards (SSI) combined with middleware that manages identities, avatars, consent states, and reputation across immersive platforms and social spaces without centralized control, enabling portable digital selfhood.
