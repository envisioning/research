## Summary

Agents that guide discussions, conflict resolution, and co-creation in real time.

## Description

Affective and discourse-aware facilitation systems that monitor group dynamics, suggest prompts, manage turn-taking, and surface shared ground in meetings, assemblies, and cultural workshops.
