# Olfactory and Gustatory Interfaces

## Summary

Devices that synthesize smell and taste in XR experiences.

## Description

Wearable or room-scale devices that release or electrically stimulate scent and taste sensations synchronized with visual and auditory content, expanding immersive environments into multi-sensory narratives and therapeutic experiences.
