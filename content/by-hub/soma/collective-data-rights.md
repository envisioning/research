# Collective Data Rights

## Summary

Governance models for group-level data ownership and decision-making.

## Description

Legal and technical frameworks that extend beyond individual consent to recognize communities, tribes, and collectives as rights-holders over shared cultural data, behavioral patterns, and aggregated affective profiles.
