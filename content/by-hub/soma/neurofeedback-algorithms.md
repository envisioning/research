# Neurofeedback Algorithms

## Summary

Real-time brain state monitoring and regulation training systems.

## Description

Algorithms that process EEG, fNIRS, or fMRI signals to provide individuals with immediate feedback on their neural activity, enabling self-regulation of attention, anxiety, focus states, and supporting conditions like ADHD, PTSD, and meditation practice.
