# Expressive Androids

## Summary

Humanoid robots with naturalistic facial expressions and body language.

## Description

Advanced social robots with silicone skin, micro-actuators for facial animation, and fluid body movement that communicate affect through human-like non-verbal cues, designed for caregiving, education, companionship, and cultural mediation.
