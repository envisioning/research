# Behavioral Digital Twins

## Summary

Simulation models that mirror individual or group behavior and routines.

## Description

Data-driven models that learn from mobility, communication, and interaction traces to create digital twins of people and communities, enabling scenario testing for policy, architecture, and culture-aware service design.
