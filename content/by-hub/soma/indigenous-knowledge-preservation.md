# Indigenous Knowledge Preservation

## Summary

Culturally-centered platforms for oral tradition, language, and ecological knowledge.

## Description

Community-controlled systems combining audio-visual archiving, language revitalization tools, mapping of sacred sites, and AI-assisted transcription/translation that respect Indigenous data sovereignty and protocols for knowledge transmission.
