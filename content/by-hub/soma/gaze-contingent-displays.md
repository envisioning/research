# Gaze-Contingent Displays

## Summary

Eye-tracking systems that adapt content based on attention and pupil response.

## Description

High-resolution eye-tracking integrated into AR/VR headsets and screens that measures visual attention, cognitive load, and affective response through gaze patterns and pupil dilation, enabling gaze-based interaction and attention-aware interfaces.
