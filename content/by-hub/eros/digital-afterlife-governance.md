# Digital Afterlife Governance

## Summary

Legal and ethical frameworks for posthumous data, avatars, and grief technologies.

## Description

Standards that define who controls a person's data, likeness, and AI-based avatars after death. They address inheritance, consent boundaries, and the potential harm or comfort that digital ghosts can create for surviving friends and family.
