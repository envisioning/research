# Social Neurofeedback Wearables

## Summary

Brain-sensing headbands that reflect interpersonal synchrony in real time.

## Description

Lightweight EEG-based wearables that visualize levels of shared attention, calm, or stress between people through simple signals or haptics. They are used in couples therapy, group bonding exercises, and collaborative work sessions.
