# Immersive Metaverse Dating

## Summary

Social VR environments designed specifically for romantic interaction.

## Description

Virtual reality platforms that go beyond chat, offering shared activities, full-body avatar tracking, and spatial audio to create a sense of 'being there' with a date. These spaces allow for physical-like social cues in a digital realm.
