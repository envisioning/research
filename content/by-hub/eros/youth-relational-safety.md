# Youth Relational Safety Protocols

## Summary

Safety-by-design frameworks for children's and teens' social and romantic tools.

## Description

Guidelines and enforcement mechanisms that shape how under-18 users can interact in social, gaming, and dating-adjacent environments. They combine age-aware consent flows, abuse detection, and education to protect emerging relational agency.
