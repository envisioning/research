# Zero-Knowledge Intimacy Proofs

## Summary

Privacy-preserving verification of health, consent, and compatibility.

## Description

Cryptographic protocols allowing users to prove specific facts (e.g., STI status, age, consent history, specific fetishes) to a potential partner without revealing their underlying identity or data. This enables 'trustless' safety in casual or anonymous intimacy.
