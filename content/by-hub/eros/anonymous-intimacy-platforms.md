# Anonymous & Pseudonymous Intimacy Platforms

## Summary

Spaces for vulnerable sharing without identity exposure.

## Description

Applications enabling deep emotional connection and support while protecting identity—critical for LGBTQ+ individuals in hostile regions, people in abusive situations, or those exploring identity. These balance anonymity with accountability through reputation systems and moderation.
