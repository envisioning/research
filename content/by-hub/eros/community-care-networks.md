# Community Care Network Infrastructure

## Summary

Platforms for organizing mutual aid, childcare, and collective support.

## Description

Systems that coordinate care work across neighborhoods, diaspora networks, and intentional communities. They manage childcare swaps, elder care rotations, meal trains, and emotional support networks—making invisible care labor visible and distributable.
