# Artificial Womb Bonding Pods

## Summary

Interfaces for parents to bond with fetuses in ectogenesis environments.

## Description

As artificial womb technology matures, these interfaces allow parents to communicate, sing to, and feel the movements of a fetus developing outside the body. They bridge the physical gap of ectogenesis, ensuring emotional attachment and prenatal bonding.
