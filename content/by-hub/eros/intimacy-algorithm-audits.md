# Intimacy Algorithm Audit Tooling

## Summary

Frameworks and tools to audit recommendation engines that shape relationships.

## Description

Audit practices and open tooling that examine how dating apps, feed algorithms, and recommender systems sort, rank, and filter people. They focus on fairness, representational harm, and the long-term impact of opaque matching on social cohesion.
