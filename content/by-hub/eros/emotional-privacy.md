# Emotional Data Sovereignty

## Summary

Frameworks for protecting intimate biometric and sentiment data.

## Description

Legal and technical standards ensuring that data derived from intimate interactions (heart rates, chat logs, emotional states) remains owned by the user and cannot be exploited for targeted advertising or manipulation.
