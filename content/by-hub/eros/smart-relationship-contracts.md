# Smart Relationship Contracts

## Summary

Blockchain-based agreements for multi-partner assets and custody.

## Description

Programmable legal frameworks running on blockchains that manage shared assets, visitation rights, and co-parenting funds for complex relationship structures (e.g., polycules, co-living groups). These allow for modular, transparent, and automated execution of agreements without relying on traditional marriage law.
