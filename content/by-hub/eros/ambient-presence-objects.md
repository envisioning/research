# Ambient Presence Objects

## Summary

Connected lamps, frames, and artifacts that signal subtle emotional presence.

## Description

Household objects that change color, texture, light, or motion in response to a loved one's status, mood, or proximity. Instead of explicit messages, they create a calm background layer of shared awareness in homes and workplaces.
