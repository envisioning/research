# Biosynthetic Bonding Devices

## Summary

Wearables that release oxytocin, pheromones, or other bonding biochemicals.

## Description

Transdermal or inhalation devices that augment natural bonding chemistry during intimacy, conflict resolution, or reconnection. These raise profound questions about authentic connection, consent, and dependency while offering therapeutic applications for trauma or attachment disorders.
