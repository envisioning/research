# Relationship Graph Analytics

## Summary

Systems that model and visualize an individual's social and intimacy networks.

## Description

Analytics layers that sit on top of messaging, calendars, and social platforms to map who we spend time with, how interactions feel, and where connections are thinning. They generate insights and nudges to rebalance attention across partners, friends, family, and community.
