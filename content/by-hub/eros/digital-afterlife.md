# Digital Afterlife Presences

## Summary

Interactive AI avatars based on the data of deceased individuals.

## Description

Services that compile videos, voice recordings, and text history to create interactive AI representations of loved ones. These 'griefbots' allow for continued conversation and memory preservation after death.
