# Artificial Parasocial Dependency

## Summary

Studying and mitigating the risks of one-sided AI attachments.

## Description

Research and guidelines addressing the psychological impact of users forming deep emotional bonds with AI. This includes design ethics to prevent exploitation of vulnerable users seeking connection.
