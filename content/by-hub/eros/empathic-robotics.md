# Empathic Companion Robots

## Summary

Robotic assistants designed for emotional support and social presence.

## Description

Domestic robots equipped with emotion recognition and expressive capabilities. Unlike functional assistants, these are designed to build rapport, offer comfort, and alleviate loneliness through non-verbal cues and empathetic interaction patterns.
