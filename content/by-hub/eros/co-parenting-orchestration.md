# Co-parenting Orchestration Platforms

## Summary

Digital infrastructures for coordination between separated or blended families.

## Description

Platforms that centralize schedules, expenses, health updates, and emotional check-ins for children across households. They support conflict mediation features and child-centric perspectives, reframing co-parenting as a shared service rather than a constant negotiation.
