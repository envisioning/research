# Financial Intimacy & Transparency Standards

## Summary

Protections around dowry, bride price, and relationship economics.

## Description

Frameworks ensuring transparency and consent in financial exchanges tied to relationships—from traditional dowry and bride price systems to modern prenuptial platforms and financial coordination apps. These combat coercion, fraud, and exploitation while respecting cultural practices.
