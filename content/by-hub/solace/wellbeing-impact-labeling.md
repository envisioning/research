# Wellbeing Impact Labeling Schemes

## Summary

Labeling standards that rate digital products on their psychological and social impact.

## Description

Certification and labeling schemes—similar to nutrition labels—that communicate how apps and platforms affect sleep, attention, mood, and social connection. Backed by independent audits and open methodologies, these labels allow individuals, schools, and organizations to choose tools aligned with humane technology principles.
