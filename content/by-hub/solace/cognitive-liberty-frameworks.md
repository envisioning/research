# Cognitive Liberty Frameworks

## Summary

Standards to protect mental privacy and freedom of thought.

## Description

Emerging legal and technical frameworks aimed at protecting 'neurorights'—specifically the right to mental privacy and freedom from non-consensual manipulation. These standards guide the development of neurotechnology to prevent the misuse of brain data.
