# Trauma-Informed AI Conversation Frameworks

## Summary

Design and safety frameworks for conversational AI interacting with vulnerable users.

## Description

Software toolkits and policy frameworks that guide how conversational AI models detect distress, avoid re-traumatization, and escalate to human or professional support. They encode guardrails for language, pacing, and topic boundaries, plus transparent consent flows for collecting and using sensitive mental health data.
