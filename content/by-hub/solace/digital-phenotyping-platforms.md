# Ethical Digital Phenotyping

## Summary

Tools for early detection of mental health issues via device interaction patterns.

## Description

Platforms that analyze passive data from smartphone and computer interactions to identify early markers of depression, anxiety, or cognitive decline. Designed with strict consent and privacy controls, these tools enable proactive mental health support and personalized interventions.
