# Algorithmic Wellbeing Audits

## Summary

Protocols for assessing AI's impact on human psychological health.

## Description

Standardized audit processes that evaluate algorithms not just for performance, but for their long-term effects on user wellbeing, addiction potential, and social polarization. These audits are becoming a requirement for responsible AI deployment in sensitive sectors.
