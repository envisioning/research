# Biodynamic Lighting Systems

## Summary

Intelligent lighting that mimics natural daylight to support circadian rhythms.

## Description

Advanced architectural lighting systems that dynamically adjust spectral output and intensity throughout the day to align with human biological clocks. These systems reduce eye strain, improve sleep quality, and enhance mood and cognitive function in indoor environments.
