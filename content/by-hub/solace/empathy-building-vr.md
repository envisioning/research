# Empathy Building VR

## Summary

Simulations allowing users to experience diverse perspectives.

## Description

VR training modules that allow users to 'inhabit' the experiences of others (e.g., facing discrimination, living with a disability). These applications are used in civic and corporate training to build deeper emotional understanding, reduce implicit bias, and foster psychological safety in teams.
