# Synthetic Relationship Disclosure

## Summary

Protocols ensuring users know when they are interacting with AI.

## Description

Technical standards and UI patterns that prevent 'bot deception' by mandating clear, persistent indicators whenever a user is interacting with an AI agent. This protects human dignity by ensuring that emotional energy is not unknowingly invested in synthetic relationships.
