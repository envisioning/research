# Neurofeedback Headbands for Learning

## Summary

Consumer-grade neural wearables for attention and focus training.

## Description

Consumer-grade EEG and fNIRS-lite headbands integrated into learning flows to provide real-time feedback on attention, arousal, and mental fatigue. These systems support structured attention training, focus drills, and guided practice, while raising new questions around data quality, consent, and over-interpretation of neural signals.
