# Skills Wallets & Learning Records

## Summary

Portable, verifiable records of skills across school, work, and life.

## Description

Skills wallets and comprehensive learning records that capture achievements, competencies, and experiences across formal education, workplace training, and informal learning. These records are designed to be portable, machine-readable, and verifiable, forming the substrate for more precise matching between people, opportunities, and learning paths.
