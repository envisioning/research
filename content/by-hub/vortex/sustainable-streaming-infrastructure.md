# Sustainable Streaming Infrastructure

## Summary

Green data centers and energy-efficient delivery networks.

## Description

Technologies and practices that reduce the carbon footprint of video delivery—renewable-powered CDNs, adaptive bitrate tuned for energy efficiency, and carbon accounting dashboards—addressing the environmental impact of global streaming growth.
