# Cultural Preservation Archives

## Summary

Digital repositories safeguarding endangered media traditions.

## Description

Initiatives using AI digitization, metadata tagging, and distributed storage to preserve indigenous storytelling, regional cinema, oral histories, and marginalized voices, ensuring cultural continuity and equitable representation in streaming catalogs.
