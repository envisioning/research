# Adaptive Personalization Engines

## Summary

AI systems that tailor content using biometric and behavioral signals.

## Description

Integrated platforms that combine emotion detection, viewing patterns, biometric data, and preference graphs to dynamically personalize recommendations, pacing, difficulty, and even narrative branches in real-time across all content types.
