# Neuro-Rights & Privacy

## Summary

Protection of neural data from BCI devices.

## Description

Legal and technical frameworks aimed at protecting the privacy and mental integrity of users, ensuring that brain data collected by BCIs cannot be accessed, sold, or manipulated without explicit consent.
