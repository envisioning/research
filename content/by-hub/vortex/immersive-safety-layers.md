# Immersive Safety Layers

## Summary

Safety, moderation, and reporting tools for XR spaces.

## Description

Technical and policy layers that provide blocking, muting, geofencing, session recording, and real-time moderation in virtual worlds, live events, and social co-watching spaces to reduce harassment and abuse.
