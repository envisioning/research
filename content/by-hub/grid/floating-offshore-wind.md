# Floating Offshore Wind Platforms

## Summary

Deep-water wind turbines on floating foundations for untapped ocean energy.

## Description

Floating platforms enable wind farms in waters too deep for fixed foundations, unlocking vast offshore wind resources in regions like Japan, California, and the Mediterranean with higher capacity factors.
