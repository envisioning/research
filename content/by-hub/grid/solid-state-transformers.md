# Solid-State Transformers

## Summary

Power-electronic transformers for precise, flexible voltage conversion and control.

## Description

Solid-state transformers integrate power conversion, protection, and communication, enabling bidirectional power flow, integration of DC loads, and improved resilience at distribution level.
