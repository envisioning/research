# Biodiversity-Positive Infrastructure

## Summary

AI and sensors to protect wildlife at renewable energy sites.

## Description

Smart camera systems and ultrasonic deterrents detect birds and bats at wind farms, curtailing turbines in real-time to prevent collisions, enabling 'nature-positive' renewable energy development.
