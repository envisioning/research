# Satellite Methane Monitoring

## Summary

Space-based detection of methane leaks from gas infrastructure.

## Description

Constellations of satellites equipped with spectrometers detect and quantify methane plumes from pipelines and facilities with high precision, enabling gas utilities to rapidly fix leaks and meet strict emissions targets.
