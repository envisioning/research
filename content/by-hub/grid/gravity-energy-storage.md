# Gravity-Based Energy Storage

## Summary

Mechanical storage systems using gravity and mass for long-duration discharge.

## Description

Technologies like lifting concrete blocks or lowering weights in mine shafts provide scalable, low-cost energy storage without rare materials or degradation, ideal for daily cycling.
