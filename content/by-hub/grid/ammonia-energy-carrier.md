# Ammonia as Energy Carrier

## Summary

Using ammonia for long-distance hydrogen transport and maritime fuel.

## Description

Green ammonia synthesized from renewable hydrogen and nitrogen serves as a dense, shippable energy vector and carbon-free fuel for vessels, turbines, and fuel cells, enabling global hydrogen trade.
