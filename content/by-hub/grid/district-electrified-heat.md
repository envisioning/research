# District Electrified Heat Systems

## Summary

Large-scale heat pump and thermal network solutions for buildings and industry.

## Description

Electrified district heating and cooling networks replace fossil boilers with high-temperature heat pumps, thermal storage, and waste-heat recovery integrated into the power system.
