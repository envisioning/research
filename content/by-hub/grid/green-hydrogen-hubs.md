# Green Hydrogen Hubs

## Summary

Integrated clusters for producing and utilizing hydrogen from renewables.

## Description

These hubs decarbonize hard-to-abate sectors like heavy industry and transport by providing a centralized supply of clean hydrogen produced via electrolysis.
