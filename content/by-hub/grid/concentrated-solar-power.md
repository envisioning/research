# Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) with Storage

## Summary

Solar thermal plants using mirrors to generate dispatchable electricity.

## Description

CSP systems concentrate sunlight to heat molten salt or other media, storing thermal energy for hours or days to provide reliable solar power after sunset, complementing PV in arid regions.
