# Tidal & Wave Energy Converters

## Summary

Harnessing ocean currents and waves for predictable renewable generation.

## Description

Tidal stream turbines and wave energy devices capture kinetic and potential energy from oceans, providing highly predictable, high-capacity-factor renewable electricity in coastal regions.
