# Community Energy Governance Platforms

## Summary

Digital tools enabling communities to co-design and oversee energy projects.

## Description

Governance platforms provide participatory budgeting, benefit-sharing models, and transparent impact reporting to ensure that new energy infrastructure supports local social and environmental priorities.
