# Space-Based Solar Power

## Summary

Orbital solar arrays beaming energy to Earth via microwaves or lasers.

## Description

Space-based solar power stations collect sunlight 24/7 in orbit and transmit it wirelessly to ground receivers, offering baseload renewable energy without weather or diurnal constraints—demonstration missions underway in US, China, and Europe.
