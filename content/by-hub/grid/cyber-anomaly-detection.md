# Cyber-Physical Anomaly Detection

## Summary

AI-driven monitoring of OT networks and grid behavior for cyber threats.

## Description

Anomaly detection engines correlate network traffic, device telemetry, and power system states to identify intrusions or misconfigurations before they escalate into outages.
