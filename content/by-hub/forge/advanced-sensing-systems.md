# Hyperspectral & Terahertz Sensing

## Summary

Non-contact imaging technologies revealing hidden material properties.

## Description

Hyperspectral cameras capture hundreds of wavelength bands to identify material composition, coatings, and contaminants invisible to RGB cameras. Terahertz imaging penetrates plastics and composites to detect sub-surface voids, delamination, and moisture without ionizing radiation. Together, they enable pharmaceutical quality control, food safety inspection, and non-destructive testing of advanced composites in aerospace.
