# Digital Thread & Model-Based Enterprise

## Summary

Unified digital representation linking design, manufacturing, and lifecycle data.

## Description

The Digital Thread connects CAD models, simulations, process parameters, quality data, and field performance in a single authoritative data backbone. Model-Based Enterprise (MBE) replaces paper drawings with 3D annotated models as the source of truth, enabling real-time traceability from requirements through disposal and powering AI-driven optimization across the entire product lifecycle.
