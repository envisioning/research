# Physical Internet (PI)

## Summary

An open, global logistics system based on standard interconnectivity.

## Description

The Physical Internet proposes a paradigm shift where goods are handled in standardized, modular containers within an open network, similar to data packets on the digital internet. This maximizes space utilization, reduces empty runs, and radically lowers the carbon footprint of global logistics.
