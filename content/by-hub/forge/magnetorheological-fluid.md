# Magnetorheological Fluid

## Summary

Fluids whose viscosity changes with magnetic field; seismic damping applications.

## Description

Magnetorheological (MR) fluids change viscosity in response to magnetic fields, enabling controllable damping. Limited commercialization in MR dampers for vehicles and structures; research continues into seismic damping for earthquake resistance. Applications include suspension systems, brakes, and adaptive structures.
