# Circular Manufacturing Systems

## Summary

Closed-loop production models that recover, refurbish, and remanufacture products.

## Description

Circular manufacturing systems integrate reverse logistics, automated disassembly, material sorting, and remanufacturing processes into production networks. Using digital product passports and AI-driven condition assessment, they maximize material recovery, reduce virgin resource consumption, and create economic value from end-of-life products—aligning with EU Circular Economy Action Plans and global sustainability mandates.
