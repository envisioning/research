# Programmable Mechanical Metamaterials

## Summary

Engineered microstructures that exhibit exotic mechanical properties not found in nature.

## Description

Programmable metamaterials use periodic lattice structures, auxetic geometries, and embedded actuation to decouple stiffness, strength, and density. Manufactured via advanced additive processes, they enable ultra-light crash structures, vibration and noise isolation, and components whose behavior (such as stiffness or damping) can be tuned in operation.
