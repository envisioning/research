# Distributed Manufacturing Networks

## Summary

Hyperlocal production ecosystems that bring manufacturing closer to end customers.

## Description

Distributed manufacturing networks deploy small-scale, flexible production units near demand centers, leveraging additive manufacturing, micro-factories, and shared digital designs. They reduce transportation emissions, shorten lead times, enhance supply chain resilience against geopolitical disruptions, and enable mass customization—reshaping global production geography in response to post-pandemic vulnerabilities.
