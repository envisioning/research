# Carbon Capture & Utilization (CCU) Manufacturing

## Summary

Processes that convert industrial CO2 emissions directly into raw materials.

## Description

CCU technologies capture carbon dioxide from factory smokestacks and catalytically convert it into useful polymers, concrete, fuels, or carbon nanotubes. By turning a liability (pollution) into an asset (feedstock), these systems enable carbon-negative manufacturing loops, particularly in hard-to-abate sectors like cement and chemicals.
