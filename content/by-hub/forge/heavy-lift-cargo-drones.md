# Heavy-Lift Autonomous Cargo Drones

## Summary

Large-scale VTOL drones for middle-mile logistics and hard-to-reach areas.

## Description

Filling the gap between trucks and helicopters, heavy-lift cargo drones (eVTOLs) transport industrial payloads (100kg-500kg+) autonomously. They are critical for just-in-time logistics to offshore rigs, remote mines, and across congested urban areas, bypassing ground infrastructure entirely.
