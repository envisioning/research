# Neuromorphic Control Systems

## Summary

Brain-inspired computing architectures for adaptive industrial control.

## Description

Neuromorphic chips mimic the neural structure of the human brain, allowing for highly efficient, parallel processing of sensory data. In industrial automation, these systems enable machines to adapt to changing conditions in real-time with minimal power consumption, surpassing traditional PID controllers in complex, non-linear environments.
