# Quantum Logistics Optimization

## Summary

Using quantum computing to solve intractable routing and inventory problems.

## Description

Quantum algorithms, such as annealing, can solve the Traveling Salesperson Problem and other combinatorial optimization challenges instantly. In supply chains, this enables dynamic, real-time routing of thousands of vehicles and optimal inventory placement across global networks, accounting for infinite variables.
