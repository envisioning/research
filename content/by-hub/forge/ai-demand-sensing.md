# AI Demand Sensing and Dynamic Planning

## Summary

Real-time demand forecasting using external signals and adaptive planning algorithms.

## Description

AI demand sensing fuses POS data, macroeconomic indicators, social signals, and IoT telemetry from connected products to infer short-term demand shifts. Coupled with dynamic planning engines, it continuously rebalances inventories, purchase orders, and production plans to reduce stockouts, obsolescence, and bullwhip effects.
