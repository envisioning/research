# Blockchain Provenance Networks

## Summary

Shared ledgers tracking material origins, certifications, and handling events across partners.

## Description

Blockchain-based provenance networks record every transformation, shipment, and custody change of parts on an immutable ledger shared across suppliers, OEMs, and regulators. They underpin trusted sustainability claims, enforce sourcing rules (such as conflict minerals), and enable rapid, targeted recalls when quality issues are detected.
