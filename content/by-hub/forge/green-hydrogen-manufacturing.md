# Green Hydrogen for Industrial Processes

## Summary

Using renewable-powered hydrogen to decarbonize high-temperature manufacturing.

## Description

Green hydrogen—produced via electrolysis from renewable electricity—replaces fossil fuels in energy-intensive processes like steel production (direct reduction), ammonia synthesis, and high-temperature furnaces. Pioneered in Europe (H2 Steel, HYBRIT) and Asia, it's essential for achieving net-zero manufacturing, offering carbon-free heat and chemical reduction while storing intermittent renewable energy.
