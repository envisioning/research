# Active Industrial Exoskeletons

## Summary

Powered wearable robotics to augment worker strength and reduce fatigue.

## Description

Next-generation industrial exoskeletons use sensors to predict worker intent and provide assistive force for lifting, holding, or squatting. These devices reduce the risk of musculoskeletal injuries in environments where full automation is not feasible, blending human dexterity with robotic endurance.
