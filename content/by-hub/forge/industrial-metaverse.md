# Industrial Metaverse & Spatial Computing

## Summary

Immersive AR/VR environments for design collaboration, training, and operations.

## Description

The Industrial Metaverse merges physical and digital worlds through AR headsets (HoloLens, Vision Pro) and VR platforms, enabling globally distributed teams to co-design in 3D, run virtual commissioning of production lines, and train workers on digital twins of equipment. Spatial computing overlays real-time IoT data, assembly instructions, and safety warnings directly onto physical machinery.
