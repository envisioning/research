# Autonomous Freight and Yard Logistics

## Summary

Self-driving trucks, yard tractors, and handling vehicles orchestrated for continuous material flow.

## Description

Autonomous freight and yard logistics apply AV stacks to ports, warehouses, and factory yards, where structured environments accelerate deployment. Coordinated fleets of driverless trucks, tuggers, and yard tractors reduce dwell times, eliminate bottlenecks at docks, and create 24/7 material flow between plants, cross-docks, and customers.
