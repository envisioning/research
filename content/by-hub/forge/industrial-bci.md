# Industrial Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)

## Summary

Direct neural control of machinery and robotic systems for hands-free operation.

## Description

Non-invasive Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) use EEG caps or wearable headbands to decode operator intent, allowing hands-free control of robots, drones, or exoskeletons. This technology enables 'telepathic' interaction in high-load cognitive tasks, allows workers with disabilities to operate machinery, and provides a direct channel for safety overrides based on worker attention or stress levels.
