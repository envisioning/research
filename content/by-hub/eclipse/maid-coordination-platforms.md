# Medical Assistance in Dying (MAiD) Platforms

## Summary

End-to-end coordination for legal assisted death services.

## Description

Comprehensive platforms managing the entire MAiD process—eligibility screening, psychiatric assessments, witness coordination, medication procurement, and post-death documentation—in jurisdictions where assisted dying is legal, ensuring compliance, dignity, and informed consent throughout.
