# Algorithmic Bias in Care

## Summary

Ensuring fairness in AI-driven end-of-life decision support.

## Description

Methodologies to detect and mitigate bias in mortality prediction models, which may otherwise lead to disparities in palliative care access based on race, age, or socioeconomic status.
