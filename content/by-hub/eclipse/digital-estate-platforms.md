# Digital Estate Platforms

## Summary

Collaborative platforms for managing digital assets and legacy planning.

## Description

Comprehensive platforms that aggregate passwords, social media accounts, crypto-assets, and digital files while enabling patients, clinicians, and loved ones to co-author advance directives, care preferences, and legacy projects. They execute pre-set instructions upon death, ensuring digital assets are transferred, memorialized, or deleted according to the user's wishes.
