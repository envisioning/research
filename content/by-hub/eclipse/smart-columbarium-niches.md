# Smart Columbarium Niches

## Summary

Networked memorial vaults with sensors and digital interfaces.

## Description

Modular columbarium units embedded with lighting, environmental sensors, and NFC or QR interfaces that connect visitors to digital stories, environmental data, or family updates, blending physical resting places with responsive, connected memorial experiences.
