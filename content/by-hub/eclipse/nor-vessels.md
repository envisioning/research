# Natural Organic Reduction Vessels

## Summary

Specialized chambers for converting human remains into soil.

## Description

Modular, reusable vessels designed for human composting. These systems control airflow, moisture, and temperature to accelerate natural decomposition, transforming a body into nutrient-rich soil in approximately 30 days, promoting ecological regeneration.
