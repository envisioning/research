# Generative Griefbots

## Summary

AI agents simulating deceased individuals based on digital footprints.

## Description

Large Language Models (LLMs) trained on a person's texts, emails, and voice recordings to emulate their personality and speech patterns. These bots offer interactive conversations for the bereaved, raising questions about the nature of memory and closure.
