# Alkaline Hydrolysis Units

## Summary

Systems for water-based cremation using heat, pressure, and alkali.

## Description

Also known as aquamation or water cremation, this hardware dissolves the body's tissues, leaving only the bone structure. It is significantly more energy-efficient than flame-based cremation and releases no harmful emissions, offering a sustainable alternative for body disposition.
