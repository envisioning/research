# Psychedelic Palliative Protocols

## Summary

Guided psychedelic therapies for existential distress at end-of-life.

## Description

Clinically-guided protocols using psilocybin, MDMA, or ketamine to treat severe existential anxiety, depression, and fear of death in terminal patients. These therapies facilitate profound spiritual or psychological experiences that can fundamentally alter a patient's relationship with their mortality.
