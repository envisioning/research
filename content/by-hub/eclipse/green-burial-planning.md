# Green Burial Planning Networks

## Summary

End-to-end planning tools for low-impact, ecological burials.

## Description

Applications that help families compare ecological impact, cost, and regulatory details across natural burial options—from forest cemeteries to reef memorials—while coordinating logistics with local providers and conservation organizations.
