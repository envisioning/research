# Avatar Consent Governance

## Summary

Controls and policies for posthumous AI avatars.

## Description

Governance layers and control panels that bind AI griefbots and digital replicas to explicit, revocable consent terms, enabling time limits, use restrictions, and family veto rights over how a deceased person's likeness and speech patterns are deployed.
