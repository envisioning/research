# Neuromodulation Pain Devices

## Summary

Implantable and wearable devices for terminal pain management.

## Description

Spinal cord stimulators, peripheral nerve stimulators, and transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) devices that modulate pain signals in patients with refractory terminal pain, offering non-pharmacological alternatives when opioids become ineffective or intolerable.
