# Digital Executor Authentication

## Summary

Secure protocols for verified access to deceased accounts.

## Description

Cryptographic and biometric verification systems enabling designated 'digital executors' to unlock specific parts of a digital estate without compromising the overall security or privacy of the deceased's remaining network.
