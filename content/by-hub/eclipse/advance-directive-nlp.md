# Advance Directive NLP Parser

## Summary

Natural language processing for complex end-of-life instructions.

## Description

AI systems that parse, interpret, and flag ambiguities in living wills, POLST forms, and advance directives written in natural language, extracting structured decision rules, identifying conflicts, and translating preferences into actionable clinical protocols across jurisdictions.
