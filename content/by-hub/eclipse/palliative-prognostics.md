# Palliative AI Prognostics

## Summary

Predictive algorithms for end-of-life care and grief planning.

## Description

Machine learning models analyzing electronic health records, engagement patterns, and self-reported data to identify patients at high risk of mortality and bereaved individuals at risk of complicated grief. These tools prompt timely palliative care interventions, grief support referrals, and resource allocation in healthcare systems.
