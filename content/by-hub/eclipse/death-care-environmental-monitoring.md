# Death Care Environmental Monitoring

## Summary

Real-time tracking of ecological impact of death practices.

## Description

Sensor networks, LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) tools, and reporting systems that measure and disclose the carbon footprint, water usage, soil contamination, and biodiversity impact of burial grounds, crematoria, and body disposition methods, enabling evidence-based green death care.
