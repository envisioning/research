# Open Legislation & Bill Tracking

## Summary

Public, machine-readable legislative text with change history.

## Description

Open publishing systems for bills, amendments, committee actions, and votes that include diffs, citations, and structured metadata. Supports civic tech, watchdogs, and researchers in understanding what’s changing and who shaped it.
