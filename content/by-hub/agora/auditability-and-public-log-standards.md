# Auditability & Public Log Standards

## Summary

Tamper-evident logs and transparent governance records by default.

## Description

Design patterns and standards for generating immutable, privacy-aware logs of key administrative events: policy changes, access decisions, model updates, procurement steps, and incident responses. Enables after-the-fact accountability and independent verification.
