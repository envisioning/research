# Sortition & Citizen Jury Selection Systems

## Summary

Fair random selection and stratification for mini-publics.

## Description

Tools that implement transparent, auditable random selection for citizens’ assemblies and juries, often with stratification to ensure demographic representativeness. Includes contact workflows, consent, compensation handling, and public audit trails of the selection process.
