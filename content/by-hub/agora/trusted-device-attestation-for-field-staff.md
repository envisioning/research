# Trusted Device Attestation for Field Staff

## Summary

Assuring poll-worker and inspector devices are uncompromised.

## Description

Mobile devices and tablets used by poll workers and inspectors that support hardware-backed attestation (secure boot, measured boot, OS integrity proofs). Helps ensure official checklists, incident reports, and provisional ballot workflows run on known-good endpoints.
