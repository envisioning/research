# Mixnet Anonymization Systems

## Summary

Breaking linkability between voters and ballots with verifiable shuffles.

## Description

Verifiable mixnets that permute and re-encrypt ballots (or messages) to eliminate linkability between input and output. Used in privacy-preserving voting, whistleblowing, and secure civic communications where anonymity must be provable, not just promised.
