# Digital Rights Enforcement Mechanisms

## Summary

Technical implementation of human rights in digital governance.

## Description

Systems that operationalize digital rights (privacy, free expression, due process, non-discrimination) as enforceable constraints in civic tech. Includes rights-violation reporting, remedy mechanisms, and accountability chains for rights abuses.
