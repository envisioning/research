# Cross-Border Democratic Coordination

## Summary

Tools for transnational governance and solidarity networks.

## Description

Platforms enabling cities, regions, or issue-based networks to coordinate on climate, migration, trade, and human rights without waiting for national governments. Supports municipal diplomacy, solidarity economies, and 'glocal' democratic innovation.
