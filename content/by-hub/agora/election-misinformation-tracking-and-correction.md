# Election Misinformation Tracking & Correction

## Summary

Coordinated debunking and rumor control infrastructure.

## Description

Cross-platform monitoring, rapid-response fact-checking, and coordinated correction systems for false election claims. Combines human judgment with automation to counter systematic disinformation while avoiding censorship or partisan capture.
