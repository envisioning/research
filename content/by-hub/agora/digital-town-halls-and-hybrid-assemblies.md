# Digital Town Halls & Hybrid Assemblies

## Summary

Blended in-person and remote democratic forums.

## Description

Video-conferencing and spatial-audio platforms designed for democratic legitimacy: turn-taking protocols, interpretation services, breakout groups, and equal voice distribution. Enables inclusive participation without requiring physical co-presence.
