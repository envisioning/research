# Automated Redistricting with Fairness Constraints

## Summary

Algorithmic boundary drawing to prevent gerrymandering.

## Description

Optimization algorithms that generate electoral district boundaries under fairness constraints (compactness, population equality, community preservation) while preventing partisan manipulation. Makes redistricting transparent and contestable with quantifiable metrics.
