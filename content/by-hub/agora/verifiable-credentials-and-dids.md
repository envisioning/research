# Verifiable Credentials & Decentralized Identifiers (VC/DID)

## Summary

Portable, privacy-respecting identity proofs for civic services.

## Description

Standards-based ecosystems for issuing and verifying credentials (residency, eligibility, permits) without requiring centralized identity providers to be online for every check. Supports selective disclosure and reduces data exposure across agencies and vendors.
