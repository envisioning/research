# Algorithmic Transparency & Explainability

## Summary

Making civic automation contestable and inspectable.

## Description

User-facing and auditor-facing explanations for automated decisions (benefits eligibility, risk scoring, content moderation, queue prioritization). Includes appeal pathways, disclosure of data sources, and reproducible audits to prevent ‘black box’ governance.
