# AI-Assisted Constitutional Design

## Summary

Computational support for institutional architecture.

## Description

Language models and constraint-satisfaction tools that help constitutional assemblies explore design space, check for internal contradictions, learn from comparative constitutions, and simulate institutional interactions before adoption.
