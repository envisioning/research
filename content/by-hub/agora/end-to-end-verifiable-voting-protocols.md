# End-to-End Verifiable Voting (E2E-V) Protocols

## Summary

Cryptographic voting where voters and observers can verify outcomes.

## Description

E2E verifiable election protocols that let each voter confirm their ballot was recorded as intended and included as cast, while allowing public verification that tallies match the encrypted ballots. Typically uses mixnets or homomorphic tallying and strong privacy protections.
