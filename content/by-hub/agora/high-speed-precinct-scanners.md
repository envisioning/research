# High-Speed Precinct & Central Scanners

## Summary

Optical scanning infrastructure for auditable paper elections.

## Description

Modern ballot scanners with hardened firmware, strict chain-of-custody logging, and exportable cast vote records (CVRs) used for audits. When paired with strong procedures, scanners enable fast tabulation without sacrificing recountability.
