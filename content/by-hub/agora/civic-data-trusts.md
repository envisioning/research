# Civic Data Trusts

## Summary

Community governance of public data assets.

## Description

Legal and technical frameworks where a fiduciary organization manages data collected in public spaces (e.g., smart city sensors) on behalf of the residents. This ensures data is used for public benefit rather than private profit or surveillance.
