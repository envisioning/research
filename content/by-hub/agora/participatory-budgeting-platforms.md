# Participatory Budgeting Platforms

## Summary

Direct citizen allocation of public funds.

## Description

Digital platforms that guide citizens through the process of proposing, debating, and voting on how a portion of the municipal budget should be spent. These tools visualize trade-offs and foster transparency in public spending.
