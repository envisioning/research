# Differential Privacy for Public Statistics

## Summary

Publishing useful civic data with formal privacy guarantees.

## Description

Differential privacy mechanisms for releasing census-like aggregates, mobility patterns, and service usage statistics while limiting the risk of re-identification. Helps governments be transparent without turning public datasets into surveillance infrastructure.
