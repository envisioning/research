# Civic Issue Reporting & Casework Portals

## Summary

Two-way service channels that close the loop with residents.

## Description

Digital platforms for reporting potholes, outages, safety hazards, and service requests with transparent status tracking. When integrated with back-office workflows, they improve legitimacy by making responsiveness visible and measurable.
