# Accessibility & Inclusion Assurance

## Summary

Ensuring civic systems work for everyone, not just ‘default’ users.

## Description

Testing and compliance tooling for accessible elections and digital public services (screen readers, language access, low-bandwidth modes, disability accommodations). Includes inclusive design processes and measurable equity targets tied to system performance and outcomes.
