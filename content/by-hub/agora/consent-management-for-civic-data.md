# Consent Management for Civic Data

## Summary

Granular, revocable control over personal data in public systems.

## Description

Consent infrastructure that lets citizens grant, audit, and revoke permission for specific uses of their data across government services. Includes notification of new uses, data portability, and deletion rights enforced through technical controls.
