# Counter-UAS (C-UAS) for Airport Protection

## Summary

Technologies to detect and mitigate unauthorized drone activity near airports.

## Description

As drone usage proliferates, unauthorized incursions into controlled airspace pose a severe safety threat. Counter-UAS systems use radar, RF detection, electro-optical sensors, and defeat mechanisms to protect airports—while requiring careful governance to avoid interference with legitimate communications and civil liberties.
