# Non-CO₂ Climate Accounting & Policy

## Summary

Operational and regulatory approaches for contrails, NOx, and water vapor.

## Description

Aviation climate impact is not only CO₂. Contrails and NOx effects complicate measurement and incentives. Emerging frameworks aim to quantify and manage non-CO₂ forcing in planning, reporting, and policy—potentially shifting routing, scheduling, and fuel strategies.
