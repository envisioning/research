# Atmospheric Satellite

## Summary

High-altitude long-endurance platforms for communications and sensing.

## Description

Atmospheric satellites are UAVs or balloons operating at high altitude for extended periods (weeks or months) for communications relay, Earth observation, or sensing. Experimentation; Loon discontinued; others in development. Applications: more widespread communications, connectivity in remote areas.
