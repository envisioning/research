# GNSS Resilience & Anti-Spoofing Navigation

## Summary

Detection/mitigation of jamming and spoofing plus alternative PNT.

## Description

GNSS interference is an increasing operational and security risk. Resilient navigation combines receiver autonomy (signal anomaly detection), multi-sensor fusion (INS, vision, DME/DME), and alternative PNT concepts to keep aircraft safe and compliant under hostile RF conditions.
