# AI-Assisted Flight Deck Decision Support

## Summary

Onboard/connected decision aids for checklists, energy, and abnormal handling.

## Description

Next-gen decision support can synthesize aircraft state, weather, performance, and procedures to help crews manage complex abnormal situations. The frontier is proving reliability, preventing over-trust, and ensuring predictable behavior under rare failure combinations.
