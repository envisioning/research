# Hydrogen Airport Fueling Infrastructure

## Summary

Cryogenic storage, liquefaction, dispensing, and safety systems at airports.

## Description

Hydrogen flight is as much an airport transformation as an aircraft one. Airports need safe LH2 storage, ground handling, emergency response procedures, and energy integration. Co-location with renewable energy and liquefaction is a major systems engineering and regulatory undertaking.
