# Vertiport Network Deployment & Urban Integration

## Summary

Infrastructure for scaling eVTOL operations in cities worldwide.

## Description

Vertiports are the physical nodes of UAM networks—rooftops, parking structures, purpose-built pads—with charging, passenger facilities, and ATC integration. Deployment requires urban planning coordination, zoning approvals, noise/safety studies, and business models that align real estate, operators, and municipalities. Early deployments are happening in the U.S., Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.
