# Wake Energy Retrieval (Formation Flight)

## Summary

Automated formation flying to harvest lift from leading aircraft.

## Description

Inspired by migratory birds, this operational concept uses precise autopilot coupling to position a trailing aircraft in the updraft of a leader's wake vortex. This 'surfing' effect can reduce fuel burn by 5–10% on long-haul flights. Airbus (Fello'fly) and others are testing the safety, scheduling, and passenger comfort implications of commercial formation flight.
