# Contrail Avoidance Optimization

## Summary

Route and altitude optimization to reduce non-CO₂ climate forcing.

## Description

Persistent contrails can drive substantial warming. Contrail avoidance uses high-resolution weather prediction and flight planning to reroute around ice-supersaturated regions with minimal fuel/time penalties. Key challenges are forecast uncertainty, operational constraints, and incentive alignment.
