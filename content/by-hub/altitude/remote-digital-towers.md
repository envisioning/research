# Remote / Digital Towers

## Summary

Camera-and-sensor towers enabling remote ATC services and resiliency.

## Description

Remote towers replace or augment physical tower views with high-resolution cameras, IR, radar overlays, and decision aids. They can improve service continuity, reduce costs for low-traffic airports, and enable new redundancy models—while requiring robust human factors validation and cybersecurity.
