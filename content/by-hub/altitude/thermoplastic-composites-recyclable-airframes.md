# Thermoplastic Composites & Recyclable Airframes

## Summary

Next-gen composite manufacturing enabling faster builds and circularity.

## Description

Thermoplastic composites offer weldability, improved damage tolerance, and potentially faster production cycles than thermosets. They also enable more realistic recycling pathways, which matters as fleets grow and end-of-life airframes become a larger sustainability and cost factor.
