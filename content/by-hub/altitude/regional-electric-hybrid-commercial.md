# Regional Electric & Hybrid-Electric Commercial Aviation

## Summary

Zero/low-emission 9–50 seat aircraft for short-haul routes.

## Description

Electric and hybrid-electric regional aircraft target 100–500 nm routes currently served by turboprops and regional jets. Pioneers like Heart Aerospace, Universal Hydrogen, and ZeroAvia are pursuing certification pathways. Success hinges on battery/fuel-cell performance, charging/refueling infrastructure at regional airports, and demonstrating safety and dispatch reliability.
