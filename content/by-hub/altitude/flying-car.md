# Flying Car

## Summary

Vehicles designed to drive on roads and fly, bridging personal transport and aviation

## Description

Flying cars combine road and flight capability. Early commercialization; prototypes (Terrafugia, AeroMobil). Applications: more effective transportation. Challenges include certification, cost, and infrastructure.
