# Airline Disruption Recovery AI

## Summary

Optimization for crew, aircraft, and passenger recovery during irregular ops.

## Description

IRROPS recovery is a multi-constraint optimization problem spanning maintenance, crew legality, gates, passenger connections, and curfews. AI/optimization engines can recommend recovery plans faster, reducing knock-on delays and customer impact.
