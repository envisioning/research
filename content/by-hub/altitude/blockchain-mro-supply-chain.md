# Blockchain for MRO & Supply Chain Provenance

## Summary

Immutable ledgers for parts traceability, maintenance records, and AOG logistics.

## Description

Blockchain can provide tamper-evident logs of component history, reducing counterfeit parts risk, improving regulatory compliance, and streamlining AOG (aircraft on ground) logistics by enabling instant access to verified records across OEMs, MROs, and airlines. Adoption challenges include integration with legacy systems, data privacy, and ensuring real-world operational value beyond hype.
