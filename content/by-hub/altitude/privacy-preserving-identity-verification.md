# Privacy-Preserving Identity Verification

## Summary

On-device and cryptographic methods to verify travelers without over-collecting data.

## Description

Air travel pushes identity checks across airlines, airports, and governments. Privacy-preserving approaches (secure enclaves, selective disclosure credentials, minimization) aim to reduce the blast radius of breaches and prevent function creep, while still supporting security and throughput.
