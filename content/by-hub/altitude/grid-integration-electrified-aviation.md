# Grid Integration & Energy Impacts of Electrified Aviation

## Summary

Managing electricity demand, renewable pairing, and grid stability.

## Description

Large-scale electrification of aviation will significantly increase airport electricity demand (potentially gigawatts at major hubs). Grid integration requires coordination with utilities, on-site renewable generation (solar canopies, wind), battery storage, and demand-response strategies. Poorly managed, it could strain grids or increase carbon intensity if served by fossil generation; done right, it can anchor renewable energy projects.
