# Circular Economy & Sustainable Aircraft Decommissioning

## Summary

End-of-life recycling, remanufacturing, and material recovery for airframes.

## Description

Aviation generates thousands of retired airframes annually. Traditional decommissioning harvests high-value parts, but most composite and aluminum structure goes to landfill. Circular economy initiatives aim to recycle composites (pyrolysis, solvolysis), remanufacture components, and design-for-disassembly in new aircraft. Economic viability depends on material commodity prices, reverse logistics, and certification pathways for reused parts.
