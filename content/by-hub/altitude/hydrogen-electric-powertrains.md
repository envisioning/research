# Hydrogen-Electric Powertrains

## Summary

Zero-emission propulsion systems utilizing hydrogen fuel cells for aviation.

## Description

Hydrogen-electric propulsion systems replace traditional jet fuel engines with electric motors powered by hydrogen fuel cells. These systems offer a pathway to zero-emission commercial aviation, significantly reducing the industry's carbon footprint. Challenges include onboard hydrogen storage, infrastructure development, and thermal management.
