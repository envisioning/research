# Electric Taxiing Systems

## Summary

Taxi without main engines to cut fuel, noise, and emissions on the ground.

## Description

Electric taxiing uses wheel motors or tow-vehicle automation to move aircraft on the ground without running main engines. It targets a high-friction operational domain (turnarounds) and can reduce fuel burn, brake wear, and local emissions around airports.
