# Multi-Orbit Satcom & Air-to-Ground Connectivity

## Summary

LEO/MEO/GEO connectivity and A2G links for resilient flight operations.

## Description

Modern aviation increasingly depends on data for operations, maintenance, and safety services. Multi-orbit satcom, blended with air-to-ground, enables higher bandwidth, lower latency, and route resilience, but requires careful cybersecurity, spectrum management, and avionics integration.
