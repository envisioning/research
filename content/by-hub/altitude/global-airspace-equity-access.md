# Global Airspace Equity & Developing World Access

## Summary

Ensuring inclusive aviation growth in underserved regions and the Global South.

## Description

Advanced aviation technologies (UAM, hydrogen, autonomy) risk widening the gap between wealthy regions and developing nations lacking infrastructure, capital, and regulatory capacity. Equitable frameworks involve technology transfer, capacity building, financing mechanisms (e.g., climate funds), and designing for low-infrastructure environments (STOL, off-grid energy).
