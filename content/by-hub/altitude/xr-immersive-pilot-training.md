# XR Immersive Flight Training & Synthetic Environments

## Summary

VR/AR/MR systems reducing sim time and enabling mission rehearsal anywhere.

## Description

Extended reality training moves beyond fixed-base simulators, offering portable, high-fidelity cockpit replication, spatial awareness training, emergency egress drills, and collaborative multi-crew scenarios. Modern XR combines photo-real rendering, haptic feedback, and eye-tracking for competency assessment. The challenge is regulatory acceptance for credit toward type ratings and recency requirements.
