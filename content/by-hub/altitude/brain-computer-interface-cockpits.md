# Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) Cockpits

## Summary

Direct neural control and cognitive monitoring for next-gen pilots.

## Description

Non-invasive BCIs measure neural activity to assess pilot workload, fatigue, and attention in real-time, adapting cockpit displays or automation levels accordingly. Future iterations aim for direct neural control of aircraft systems, reducing reaction times and physical workload, but raising profound ethical questions about privacy and neuro-security.
