# Autonomous Cargo Drones

## Summary

Heavy-lift unmanned aerial vehicles for middle-mile logistics.

## Description

Large-scale autonomous drones are transforming logistics by enabling rapid, point-to-point delivery of heavy cargo to remote or hard-to-reach locations. These systems bypass ground traffic and traditional infrastructure limitations, offering a flexible solution for supply chains and emergency response.
