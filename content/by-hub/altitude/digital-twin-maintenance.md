# Digital Twin Maintenance

## Summary

Virtual replicas of aircraft for predictive health monitoring and simulation.

## Description

Digital twins create high-fidelity virtual models of physical aircraft components and systems. By feeding real-time sensor data into these models, operators can predict component failures before they occur, optimize maintenance schedules, and simulate the impact of operational changes on airframe longevity.
