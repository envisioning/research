# Aviation Cybersecurity Certification & Assurance

## Summary

Security-by-design for aircraft systems, connectivity, and supply chains.

## Description

Connected aircraft increase attack surface (satcom, Wi‑Fi, EFB, maintenance links). Certification-aligned security engineering focuses on threat modeling, partitioning, secure update mechanisms, incident response, and continuous monitoring across the aircraft lifecycle.
