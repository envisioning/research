# Photonic Integrated Circuits (PICs)

## Summary

Light-based chips for sensing, communication, and computation embedded into lighting systems.

## Description

PICs integrate multiple photonic functions (lasers, detectors, modulators) onto a single chip. Embedded in lighting infrastructure, they enable ultra-fast Li-Fi communication, precise environmental sensing (LIDAR), and edge computing capabilities directly within the luminaire.
