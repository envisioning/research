# Light Trespass & Environmental Justice

## Summary

Equity impacts of glare, over-lighting, and nuisance exposure concentrated in specific communities.

## Description

Over-lighting and poorly aimed fixtures create sleep disruption, stress, and reduced quality of life—often disproportionately affecting lower-income neighborhoods near highways, industrial sites, and commercial corridors. Justice-oriented lighting practice includes community input, measurable limits on glare/trespass, and accountability for maintenance and complaints.
