# Health Externalities of Spectral Exposure

## Summary

Long-term effects of artificial light on sleep, cognition, and development.

## Description

Investigation into the chronic health impacts of artificial light at night (ALAN). This covers risks like circadian disruption, retinal damage from high-energy blue light, and potential links to metabolic disorders. It drives the need for stricter photobiological safety standards.
