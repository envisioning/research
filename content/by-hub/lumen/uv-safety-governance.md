# UV Safety Governance (UV‑C / Far‑UV‑C)

## Summary

Exposure limits, interlocks, and validation needed to deploy germicidal lighting responsibly.

## Description

UV disinfection can harm eyes and skin if misapplied. Responsible deployment requires validated dose models, occupancy sensing, shielding, automatic shutoffs, maintenance procedures, and transparent labeling. As far‑UV‑C evolves, governance must keep pace with evidence and avoid overstated safety claims.
