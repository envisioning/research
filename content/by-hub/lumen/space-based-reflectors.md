# Orbital Illumination Reflectors

## Summary

Satellites equipped with steerable mirrors to reflect sunlight onto specific terrestrial regions at night.

## Description

A macro-scale geoengineering concept: using orbital mirrors to extend twilight or provide illumination for disaster relief and polar regions. While offering vast energy savings for city lighting, it raises significant concerns regarding light pollution, astronomy interference, and geopolitical control of the 'night'.
