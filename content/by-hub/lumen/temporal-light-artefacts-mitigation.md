# Temporal Light Artefacts (TLA) Analysis & Mitigation

## Summary

Tools to detect and reduce flicker, stroboscopic effects, and temporal instability in LEDs.

## Description

PWM dimming, driver design, and power quality can introduce flicker and stroboscopic effects that impact comfort, safety, and sensitive populations. TLA software evaluates metrics (e.g., PstLM, SVM) and guides driver/control strategies to maintain stability across dimming ranges and operating conditions.
