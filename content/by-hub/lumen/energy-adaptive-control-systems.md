# Energy-adaptive Control Systems

## Summary

AI-driven optimization that adjusts illumination based on demand, occupancy, and grid conditions.

## Description

Intelligent control systems that leverage machine learning to optimize lighting energy consumption. They analyze real-time data from occupancy sensors, daylight availability, and energy grid pricing to deliver the required light levels with minimal waste, supporting net-zero building goals.
