# Digital Twins of Light Environments

## Summary

Simulated spaces to test safety, efficiency, and human response before deployment.

## Description

High-fidelity virtual replicas of physical lighting environments. Designers and engineers use these twins to simulate complex lighting scenarios, validate compliance with safety standards, and test user interactions in VR/AR before any physical hardware is installed.
