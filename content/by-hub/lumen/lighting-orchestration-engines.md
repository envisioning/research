# Lighting Orchestration Engines

## Summary

Real-time systems coordinating illumination across buildings, streets, vehicles, and events.

## Description

Centralized software platforms that manage and synchronize vast networks of lighting endpoints. These engines handle complex protocols (DALI, DMX, IoT) to create cohesive lighting experiences for smart cities, large-scale entertainment, or campus-wide energy management.
