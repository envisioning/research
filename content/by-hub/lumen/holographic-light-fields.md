# Holographic & Light Field Illumination

## Summary

Fixture-free, steerable light that can manifest 3D volumes or targeted spots in mid-air.

## Description

Using phase-modulating arrays (like SLMs) to shape light fields, this technology can project light that appears to originate from nowhere, focus on specific moving objects without mechanical parts, or create volumetric guides. It blurs the line between illumination and 3D display, offering unprecedented spatial control.
