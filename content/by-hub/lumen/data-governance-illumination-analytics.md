# Data Governance for Sensorized Lighting

## Summary

Policies for consent, retention, and secondary use of occupancy and behavioral data from luminaires.

## Description

Even without cameras, sensorized lighting can infer occupancy, movement patterns, and productivity proxies. Ethical deployment requires purpose limitation, clear notice, opt-out where feasible, retention limits, and safeguards against function creep—especially in workplaces, schools, and public spaces.
