# Luminescent Solar Concentrators (LSC)

## Summary

Light-harvesting windows that generate power while illuminating interiors with tinted light.

## Description

LSCs use luminescent materials (quantum dots, dyes) embedded in transparent guides to capture sunlight, re-emit it at specific wavelengths, and guide it to PV cells at the edges. This turns building facades into power generators while functioning as active daylighting elements that can tune indoor spectral quality.
