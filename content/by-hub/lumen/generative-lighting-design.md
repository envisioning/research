# Generative Lighting Design

## Summary

AI systems that autonomously generate dynamic, aesthetically complex lighting scenes.

## Description

Beyond static orchestration, generative algorithms create fluid, non-repetitive lighting states based on environmental inputs (music, weather, crowd mood) or artistic parameters. This transforms lighting from a utility into a responsive, living art form in entertainment, hospitality, and public spaces.
