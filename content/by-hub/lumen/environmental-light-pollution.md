# Environmental Light Pollution

## Summary

Ecological disruption and loss of dark skies from pervasive illumination.

## Description

The negative impact of excessive or misdirected artificial light on ecosystems. It disrupts migration patterns of birds, breeding of nocturnal insects, and human connection to the cosmos. Solutions involve Dark Sky compliant fixtures and regulations to reclaim the night environment.
