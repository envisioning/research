# Reusable Launch Vehicle

## Summary

Rockets designed to land and fly again, cutting launch costs for satellite and cargo missions

## Description

Reusable launch vehicles recover and reuse first stages (and sometimes upper stages) to reduce cost. Commercial deployment; SpaceX Falcon 9, Starship. Applications: circumvention of expendable launch costs for commercial and government missions.
