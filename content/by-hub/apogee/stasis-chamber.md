# Stasis Chamber

## Summary

Induced torpor for interplanetary and interstellar space travel.

## Description

Stasis chambers would induce torpor or suspended animation for crew during long-duration missions. Research and development; experiments. Applications: interplanetary and interstellar travel; medical. Overlaps with induced torpor and hibernation research.
