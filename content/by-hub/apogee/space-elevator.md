# Space Elevator

## Summary

Non-rocket spacelaunch via tether to geostationary orbit; research.

## Description

Space elevators would use a tether extending to geostationary orbit for non-rocket spacelaunch. Research; significant materials and engineering challenges. Applications: orbital ring, sky hook, space fountain. No material yet strong enough; long-term vision.
