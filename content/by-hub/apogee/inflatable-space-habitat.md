# Inflatable Space Habitat

## Summary

Expandable habitats for orbital and deep-space use; Bigelow Aerospace.

## Description

Inflatable space habitats expand after launch to provide larger interior volumes than rigid structures. Development; prototypes built and tested (BEAM on ISS). Applications: space habitats, stations, deep-space missions. Bigelow Aerospace and others developing systems.
