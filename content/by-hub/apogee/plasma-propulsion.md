# Plasma Propulsion

## Summary

Electric propulsion systems that accelerate ionized gas to maneuver satellites and spacecraft

## Description

Plasma propulsion uses electric fields to accelerate plasma for spacecraft propulsion. Research and development; Hall thrusters and ion engines in use. Applications: spacecraft propulsion; orbit raising, station-keeping. Commercial and scientific deployment.
