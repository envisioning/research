# Field Propulsion

## Summary

Electrodynamic tether, electrodeless plasma thruster; reduction of propellant requirements.

## Description

Field propulsion encompasses electrodynamic tethers, electrodeless plasma thrusters, and related concepts that reduce propellant mass. Varies by subtype; some in production, some theoretical. Applications: orbital maneuvering, long-duration interplanetary missions. Benefits: reduced propellant; extended mission range.
