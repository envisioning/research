# Artificial Gravity

## Summary

Rotating spacecraft or habitats to simulate gravity through centripetal force

## Description

Artificial gravity uses centripetal acceleration (rotation) to simulate gravity for spacecraft and habitats. Research and development; spin gravity concepts for long-duration missions. Applications: space travel, orbital habitats. Challenges include radius (for tolerable gradient), engineering, and physiological adaptation.
