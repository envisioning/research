# Quantum-Enhanced Grid Optimization

## Summary

Using quantum and quantum-inspired algorithms to manage ultra-complex power systems.

## Description

As grids grow more complex, with millions of controllable assets, optimal power flow and planning problems become computationally intractable. Quantum and quantum-inspired solvers explore huge solution spaces more efficiently, enabling better congestion management, siting of new assets, and robust contingency planning.
