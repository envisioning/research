# Subsea Cable Systems & Underwater IoT

## Summary

Next-generation transoceanic fiber cables with integrated sensing and robotics.

## Description

Modern subsea cables carry 99% of intercontinental data traffic. New systems integrate distributed acoustic sensing (monitoring earthquakes and ship traffic), AI-based fault prediction, and autonomous underwater vehicles for inspection and repair, transforming passive conduits into active infrastructure monitoring networks.
