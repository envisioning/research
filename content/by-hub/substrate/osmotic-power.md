# Osmotic Power Generation

## Summary

Harvesting energy from the salinity difference between fresh and salt water.

## Description

Also known as Blue Energy, this technology generates electricity where rivers meet the sea. Using advanced semi-permeable membranes, Pressure Retarded Osmosis (PRO) or Reverse Electrodialysis (RED) captures the chemical potential energy of mixing water, turning water treatment plants and estuaries into power stations.
