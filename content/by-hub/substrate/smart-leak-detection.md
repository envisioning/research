# Smart Leak Detection Networks

## Summary

Continuous sensing of distribution networks to detect leaks and bursts.

## Description

Acoustic sensors, fiber-optic cables, and pressure transients are combined with AI analytics to pinpoint leaks in large water distribution systems. Utilities can prioritize repairs, reduce non-revenue water losses, and avoid catastrophic main breaks in aging infrastructure.
