# Self-Healing Grid Automation

## Summary

Autonomous protection and restoration systems that reconfigure the grid in real time.

## Description

Self-healing grids use distributed sensors, high-speed protection devices, and AI-driven control to detect faults, isolate damaged sections, and reroute power in seconds. This dramatically reduces outage duration and is critical as climate-driven extreme events increase stress on distribution and transmission networks.
