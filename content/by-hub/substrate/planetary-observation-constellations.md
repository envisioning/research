# Planetary Observation & Climate Constellations

## Summary

Dense networks of Earth-observing satellites for infrastructure-aware climate intelligence.

## Description

New constellations provide high-resolution, high-cadence observations of energy assets, water systems, forests, and urban heat islands. Coupled with AI models, they deliver real-time risk assessments and planning insights for infrastructure adaptation and decarbonization.
