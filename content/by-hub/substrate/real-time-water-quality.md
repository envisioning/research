# Real-Time Water Quality Biosensing

## Summary

In-situ biosensors monitoring contaminants and pathogens at high frequency.

## Description

Networks of optical, electrochemical, and biological sensors are deployed throughout treatment plants and distribution systems. They provide continuous data on microbial and chemical contaminants, enabling early detection of pollution events and adaptive control of treatment processes.
