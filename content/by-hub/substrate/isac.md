# Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)

## Summary

Networks that double as high-fidelity environmental sensors.

## Description

ISAC merges radar sensing with communication signals. Instead of just carrying data, radio waves from cell towers and devices are used to map the physical environment, detecting vehicle movement, rainfall, or even breathing rates, turning the entire network into a pervasive sensing infrastructure.
