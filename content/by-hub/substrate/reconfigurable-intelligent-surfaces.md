# Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)

## Summary

Programmable radio surfaces that steer and shape wireless signals.

## Description

RIS panels coated with meta-material elements can dynamically reflect, refract, or absorb radio waves to improve coverage and reduce interference. Mounted on buildings or indoor walls, they turn the physical environment into an active part of the network, boosting capacity without adding more base stations.
