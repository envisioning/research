# In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)

## Summary

Extracting and processing local materials on the Moon and Mars.

## Description

ISRU focuses on harvesting local resources—such as water ice for fuel or regolith for construction materials—rather than transporting everything from Earth. This is the foundational technology for sustainable lunar bases and deep space exploration, turning celestial bodies into logistical supply nodes.
