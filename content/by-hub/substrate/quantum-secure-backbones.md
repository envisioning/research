# Quantum-Secure Communication Backbones

## Summary

Backhaul networks hardened with quantum key distribution.

## Description

Quantum key distribution (QKD) uses quantum states of photons to establish encryption keys that reveal any eavesdropping attempt. Embedding QKD into fiber and satellite backbones creates ultra-secure links for critical infrastructure traffic, such as grid control signals and financial transactions.
