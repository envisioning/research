# Transactive Energy Platforms

## Summary

Price-based coordination of millions of devices at the grid edge.

## Description

Transactive energy systems expose dynamic price signals down to buildings, appliances, EVs, and batteries. Devices automatically respond—charging, discharging, or shifting loads—creating a market-based mechanism for balancing the grid in real time while respecting local constraints and user comfort.
