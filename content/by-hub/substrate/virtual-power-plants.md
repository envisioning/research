# Virtual Power Plants (VPPs)

## Summary

Cloud-based aggregation of distributed energy resources.

## Description

VPPs network thousands of decentralized energy resources—such as residential solar panels, batteries, and EVs—into a single controllable entity. By using AI to forecast demand and orchestrate dispatch, VPPs provide grid stability and peak-shaving services without the need for new physical peaker plants.
