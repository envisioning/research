# District-Scale Circular Water Reuse

## Summary

Closed-loop systems that reclaim and recycle water within urban districts.

## Description

Advanced treatment, storage, and dual-pipe networks enable wastewater and stormwater to be reclaimed for non-potable or even potable uses. Combined with smart controls, these systems reduce freshwater withdrawals, lower energy use, and create more climate-resilient cities.
