# Genetic Doping Surveillance

## Summary

Methods to detect and prevent gene manipulation in sports.

## Description

Emerging screening technologies and ethical guidelines aimed at detecting 'gene doping'—the non-therapeutic use of cells, genes, or genetic elements to enhance performance. This field focuses on preserving the integrity of sport against biologically engineered advantages.
