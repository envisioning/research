# Smart Fabrics & E-Textiles

## Summary

Conductive fibers embedded in athletic wear for physiological monitoring.

## Description

Next-generation athletic apparel integrating conductive fibers and micro-sensors directly into the fabric. These smart textiles monitor physiological parameters like heart rate, muscle activation (EMG), and respiratory rate in real-time without bulky external devices, enhancing athlete comfort and data compliance.
