# Return-to-Play Orchestration Systems

## Summary

Integrated workflows guiding rehab and clearance decisions after injury.

## Description

Platforms that encode sport-specific return-to-play protocols, linking medical imaging, strength benchmarks, neuromuscular tests, and on-field criteria. They coordinate clinicians, coaches, and athletes around objective gates for progressing and clearing injured players.
