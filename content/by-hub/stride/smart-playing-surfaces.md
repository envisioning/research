# Smart Playing Surfaces

## Summary

Fields, courts, and tracks with embedded force and position sensors.

## Description

Surfaces instrumented with pressure, force, and position sensors that map foot-strike patterns, loading rates, and traction in real time. These surfaces turn stadiums and training facilities into giant force plates, enabling precise load management, surface optimization, and injury-prevention research.
