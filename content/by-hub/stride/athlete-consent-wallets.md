# Athlete Consent & Data Wallets

## Summary

Self-sovereign tools for managing permissions across teams and vendors.

## Description

Digital identity and consent systems where athletes can grant, track, and revoke access to their performance, health, and biometric data. These wallets help prevent uncontrolled data sharing between clubs, leagues, sponsors, and third-party analytics providers.
