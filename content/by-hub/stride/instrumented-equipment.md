# Instrumented Balls, Bats & Rackets

## Summary

Embedded IMUs measuring spin, impact, and swing kinematics in equipment.

## Description

Sport-specific equipment such as balls, rackets, bats, and clubs with embedded inertial sensors and strain gauges. These tools capture detailed metrics like spin rate, sweet-spot contact, swing path, and impact forces, providing precise feedback for technique refinement in real training and competition settings.
