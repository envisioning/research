# Neural Performance Sensors

## Summary

Wearables tracking brain signals to assess cognitive state and reaction time.

## Description

Emerging wrist-worn or headband devices that detect electrical signals from the brain and nervous system. These sensors quantify cognitive fatigue, reaction time latency, and mental readiness, allowing coaches to manage psychological load alongside physical exertion.
