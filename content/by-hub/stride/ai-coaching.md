# Real-time AI Coaching

## Summary

Automated feedback systems for immediate technique correction.

## Description

Applications that process sensor or video data to provide instant auditory or visual feedback to athletes. This acts as an always-available coach, correcting form, pacing, or technique in real-time during solo practice sessions, democratizing access to high-level instruction.
