# Mobile Digital Identity (mDL)

## Summary

ISO 18013-5 compliant mobile driver's licenses and national ID credentials.

## Description

Mobile Digital Identity (mDL) systems store government-issued credentials like driver's licenses and national IDs on smartphones, following ISO/IEC 18013-5 standards. They support biometric binding, offline verification via QR/NFC, selective disclosure, and cryptographic tamper-evidence. Deployed across US states, EU nations, and Asia-Pacific countries, mDLs are becoming the primary digital identity form factor for citizens globally.
