# Verifiable Data Registries

## Summary

Blockchain-based trust registries governing credential issuers and schemas.

## Description

Verifiable Data Registries (VDRs) are cryptographically authenticated, tamper-evident databases that maintain authoritative lists of credential issuers, schemas, revocation status, and governance frameworks. Built on distributed ledger technology, they enable verifiers to independently validate credential provenance and issuer authority without centralized trust anchors, forming the backbone of decentralized trust ecosystems.
