# Verifiable Credentials

## Summary

Digitally signed attestations that can be selectively disclosed and verified.

## Description

Verifiable Credentials are cryptographically signed statements about a subject (such as identity attributes, qualifications, or licenses) that can be presented and independently verified without contacting the issuer. Built on open standards, they enable privacy-preserving, interoperable identity verification across organizations and jurisdictions.
