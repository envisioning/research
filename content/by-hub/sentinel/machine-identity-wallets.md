# Machine Identity Wallets

## Summary

Identity and credential management for autonomous AI agents and IoT.

## Description

Machine Identity Wallets allow autonomous AI agents and IoT devices to hold verifiable credentials, sign transactions, and prove their authority and software integrity. Facilitating 'Know Your Agent' (KYA), these wallets enable trusted machine-to-machine commerce and collaboration, ensuring that an AI agent is authorized by a specific organization or individual to act on their behalf.
