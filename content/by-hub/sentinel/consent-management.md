# Consent Management Platforms

## Summary

Systems ensuring compliant, auditable user consent for data collection and sharing.

## Description

Consent Management Platforms (CMPs) capture, store, and enforce user preferences for data processing, cookie usage, and third-party sharing in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations. They provide audit trails, granular consent controls, and revocation mechanisms, enabling organizations to demonstrate regulatory compliance while respecting user privacy choices in identity and verification workflows.
