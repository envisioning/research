# Digital Twin Identity Frameworks

## Summary

Secure identity binding for digital replicas of physical assets.

## Description

Digital Twin Identity Frameworks establish a cryptographically secure link between a physical object (e.g., an aircraft engine, a building) and its digital twin. This ensures that data flowing to the twin is authentic and that commands sent from the twin are authorized, preventing spoofing attacks on critical industrial infrastructure and enabling trusted predictive maintenance.
