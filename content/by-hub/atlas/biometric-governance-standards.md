# Biometric Governance Standards

## Summary

Frameworks that set guardrails for biometric use in borders and hospitality.

## Description

Technical and policy standards defining consent, data retention, interoperability, and redress mechanisms for biometric systems deployed across airports, hotels, and cruise terminals.
