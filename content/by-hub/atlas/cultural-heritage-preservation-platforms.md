# Cultural Heritage Preservation Platforms

## Summary

Digital tools documenting and safeguarding intangible cultural heritage.

## Description

Collaborative platforms combining photogrammetry, oral history archives, and community knowledge bases to document endangered traditions, languages, and practices, creating economic incentives for preservation through tourism while maintaining community control.
