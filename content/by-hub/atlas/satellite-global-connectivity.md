# Satellite Global Connectivity

## Summary

Low Earth orbit constellations enabling internet access in remote destinations.

## Description

Mega-constellations like Starlink providing high-speed internet to previously unconnected wilderness areas, remote lodges, and developing-world tourism sites, enabling digital nomadism, emergency services, and inclusive access to online booking and information.
