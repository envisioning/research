# Bio-luminescent Night Tourism

## Summary

Engineered glowing plants and pathways replacing electric lighting.

## Description

Botanical gardens and nature trails illuminated by genetically modified bioluminescent plants and algae, creating magical, electricity-free night tourism experiences that reduce light pollution and disrupt wildlife less than artificial LEDs.
