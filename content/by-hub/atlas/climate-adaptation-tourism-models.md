# Climate Adaptation Tourism Models

## Summary

Predictive systems modeling climate impact on destinations and seasonality.

## Description

Integrated assessment models combining climate science, economic data, and tourist behavior to forecast how warming, sea-level rise, and extreme weather will reshape destination viability, optimal travel seasons, and infrastructure needs over decades.
