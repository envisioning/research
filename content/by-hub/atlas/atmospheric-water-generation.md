# Atmospheric Water Generation

## Summary

Systems extracting potable water from air for remote and island tourism.

## Description

Scalable hydro-panel arrays and condensation technologies that generate clean drinking water from atmospheric humidity, reducing reliance on plastic bottles and strained local aquifers in water-scarce tourist destinations.
