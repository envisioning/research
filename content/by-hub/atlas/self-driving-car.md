# Self-Driving Car

## Summary

Autonomous vehicles; research, development, early commercialization.

## Description

Self-driving cars use sensors and AI for autonomous operation. Research, development, early commercialization; Waymo, Tesla FSD. Applications: reducing driver fatigue; lower-cost personal transportation. Challenges include safety, regulation, and edge cases.
