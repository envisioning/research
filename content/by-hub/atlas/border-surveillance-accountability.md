# Border Surveillance Accountability

## Summary

Auditing and redress systems for automated border control technologies.

## Description

Independent oversight bodies, algorithmic impact assessments, and legal frameworks ensuring travelers can contest automated visa denials, biometric misidentifications, and discriminatory profiling at borders, with mandatory transparency and appeals processes.
