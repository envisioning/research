# Hyperloop Corridors

## Summary

Vacuum-assisted, ultra-high-speed transport systems connecting major hubs.

## Description

Experimental transport infrastructure using low-pressure tubes and magnetic levitation to move pods between cities at near-supersonic speeds, radically shrinking intercity travel times and reshaping long-haul tourism flows.
