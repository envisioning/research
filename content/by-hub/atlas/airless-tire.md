# Airless Tire

## Summary

Non-pneumatic tires using structural supports instead of pressurized air

## Description

Airless tires (non-pneumatic tires) use structural supports instead of pressurized air. Research, development, early prototypes; Tweel and others. Benefits: no flat tires, reduced maintenance. Challenges include ride quality and cost. Applications: consumer vehicles, military, construction.
