# Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI)

## Summary

User-controlled digital identity wallets for secure travel.

## Description

A decentralized identity framework where travelers own and control their credentials (passports, visas) in a digital wallet, sharing only necessary proofs without exposing raw data.
