# Gig Economy Tourism Protections

## Summary

Labour standards and social safety nets for platform-based tourism workers.

## Description

Regulatory frameworks and platform design patterns ensuring tour guides, drivers, and hospitality workers on gig platforms receive minimum wages, benefits, portable ratings, and collective bargaining rights to counter precarity in the tourism workforce.
