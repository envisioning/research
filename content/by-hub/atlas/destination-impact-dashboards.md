# Destination Impact Dashboards

## Summary

Public dashboards visualizing tourism’s social, economic, and ecological footprints.

## Description

Open, data-driven dashboards that aggregate indicators on crowding, carbon, biodiversity, and community well-being to support democratic decision-making about visitor caps, zoning, and investment priorities.
