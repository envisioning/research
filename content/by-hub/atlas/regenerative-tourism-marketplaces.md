# Regenerative Tourism Marketplaces

## Summary

Platforms steering demand toward community-owned, low-impact experiences.

## Description

Curated booking ecosystems that prioritize community-owned accommodations and experiences, channeling a share of revenues into local restoration, conservation, and cultural preservation projects.
