# Soft Exoskeletons for Adventure

## Summary

Wearable robotics enabling accessible hiking and active travel for seniors.

## Description

Lightweight, textile-based powered suits that provide assistive force to legs and back, allowing elderly travelers or those with mobility limitations to navigate rugged terrain, hiking trails, and archaeological sites comfortably.
