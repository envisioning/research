# Supersonic Commercial Aviation

## Summary

Next-generation supersonic jets cutting intercontinental flight times.

## Description

Overture-class aircraft leveraging advanced aerodynamics, sustainable aviation fuel, and carbon-neutral operations to restore supersonic travel with reduced sonic boom and environmental impact, shrinking transpacific and transatlantic journeys to under 4 hours.
