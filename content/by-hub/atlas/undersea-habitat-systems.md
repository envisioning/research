# Undersea Habitat Systems

## Summary

Pressurized underwater lodges allowing extended stays without diving gear.

## Description

Permanent, surface-pressure underwater habitats and hotels that allow non-divers to stay submerged for days, observing marine life through panoramic viewports while supporting coral restoration and oceanographic research.
