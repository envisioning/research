# Climate Justice Aviation Frameworks

## Summary

Equity-based carbon budgets and compensation for high-emission travel.

## Description

Policy and technical mechanisms that allocate aviation carbon budgets according to equity principles (historical emissions, development needs), levy progressive taxes on frequent flyers, and channel revenues to frontline climate adaptation in vulnerable tourism-dependent regions.
