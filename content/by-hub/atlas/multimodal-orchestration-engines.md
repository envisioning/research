# Multimodal Orchestration Engines

## Summary

Software that stitches flights, rail, micromobility, and lodging into a single journey graph.

## Description

Routing and optimization engines that maintain a live, multimodal graph of mobility options—air, rail, road, and micro-mobility—continuously recomputing the optimal journey as disruptions, prices, and traveler preferences change.
