# Tourism Labour Rights Traceability

## Summary

Digital tracing of labour conditions across tourism supply chains.

## Description

Blockchain and IoT-enabled traceability tools that record working conditions, certifications, and grievance mechanisms across hotels, tour operators, and transport providers to enforce ethical labour standards.
