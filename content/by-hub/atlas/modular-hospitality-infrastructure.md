# Modular Hospitality Infrastructure

## Summary

Prefabricated, relocatable hotel units for rapid deployment.

## Description

Factory-built, shipping-container-scale hospitality modules with embedded smart systems that can be assembled, moved, and reconfigured to meet seasonal demand in remote locations, events, or disaster recovery scenarios.
