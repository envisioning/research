# Wellness & Mobility Integration

## Summary

Designing travel systems that support mental health and well-being.

## Description

Human-centered design principles and evidence-based interventions embedded in mobility infrastructure to reduce stress, loneliness, and burnout among travelers, including biophilic design, social connection features, and mindfulness-integrated experiences.
