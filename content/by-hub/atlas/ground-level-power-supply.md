# Ground-Level Power Supply

## Summary

Electric roads charging vehicles while driving; Trafikverkets Elvägar.

## Description

Ground-level power supply charges electric vehicles while driving via in-road rails or inductive systems. Standardization and commercialization; Sweden, France. Benefits: smaller batteries, reduced charging stops. Applications: reduction of battery size and weight for EVs.
