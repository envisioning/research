# Hovertrain / Ground Effect Train

## Summary

High-speed rail using ground effect; Aérotrain.

## Description

Hovertrains use ground effect or air cushion for high-speed rail. Research and development; Aérotrain, Duke Hospital PRT. Applications: faster trains. Limited deployment; hyperloop and maglev have gained more attention.
