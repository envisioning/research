# Ultra-Fast EV Charging Infrastructure

## Summary

Battery-integrated ultra-fast EV chargers delivering 350 kW charging, providing backup power, and operating as distributed energy assets.

## Description

Ultra-fast EV charging infrastructure delivers charging rates up to 350 kW, enabling electric vehicles to add hundreds of miles of range in minutes rather than hours. These systems integrate battery storage to buffer power demands, reducing strain on electrical grids and enabling deployment in locations with limited grid capacity. The integrated batteries also provide backup power capabilities and allow the chargers to operate as distributed energy assets that can feed power back to the grid during peak demand.

Battery-integrated designs enable rapid deployment without requiring extensive grid upgrades, making installation possible in weeks rather than years. This is particularly valuable for fleet operations, utilities, and gas stations transitioning to EV charging. The systems can charge multiple vehicles simultaneously, manage power distribution intelligently, and participate in demand response programs. Ultra-fast charging addresses range anxiety and makes electric vehicles practical for long-distance travel, supporting broader EV adoption.
