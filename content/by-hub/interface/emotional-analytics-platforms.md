# Emotional Analytics Platforms

## Summary

Europe's leader in emotional and predictive analytics, ethical AI certified, uncovering weak signals and behaviors to empower smarter, human-centered decisions. Includes camera-based in-cabin analysis software for vehicles analyzing human characteristics and actions in real-time, enabling driver monitoring, occupant safety, and user experience capabilities.

## Description

Emotional analytics platforms use AI to analyze human emotions, behaviors, and weak signals from various data sources including facial expressions, voice patterns, body language, and behavioral data. These platforms employ ethical AI practices and certifications to ensure responsible use of emotional data, uncovering subtle patterns that inform human-centered decision-making. The technology can detect emotional states, stress levels, engagement, and other psychological indicators that may not be immediately apparent, providing insights for improving user experiences, safety, and well-being.

In automotive applications, camera-based in-cabin analysis software monitors driver and occupant characteristics in real-time, enabling driver monitoring systems that detect drowsiness, distraction, or impairment. The technology supports occupant safety by detecting distress or medical emergencies, and enhances user experience by adapting vehicle settings based on emotional state. The comprehensive software stack integrates active safety (preventing accidents), passive safety (protecting occupants), user experience optimization, and automated mobility features. This technology is particularly valuable for improving road safety, personalizing in-vehicle experiences, and ensuring driver and passenger well-being.
