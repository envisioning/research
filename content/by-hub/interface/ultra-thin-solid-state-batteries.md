# Ultra-Thin Solid-State Batteries

## Summary

Ultra-thin (0.

## Description

Ultra-thin solid-state batteries achieve remarkable thinness (0.1 mm) while delivering rapid charge and discharge capabilities, enabling full charge cycles in approximately one minute. These batteries can directly power wireless transmission systems without requiring additional capacitors, thanks to their low internal resistance and high power density. The solid-state design eliminates flammable liquid electrolytes, making them inherently safer than traditional batteries.

The non-flammable construction allows operation across extreme temperature ranges from -40 to 300°F, making them suitable for harsh environments and applications where thermal stability is critical. The ultra-thin form factor enables integration into space-constrained devices like smart cards, flexible electronics, medical implants, and IoT sensors. These batteries are particularly valuable for applications requiring rapid power delivery, safety-critical systems, and devices operating in extreme conditions where traditional batteries would fail.
