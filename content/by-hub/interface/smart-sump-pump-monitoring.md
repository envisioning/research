# Smart Sump Pump Monitoring

## Summary

Proactive smart home sump pump monitoring with daily automated tests, 24/7 monitoring, and instant alerts.

## Description

Smart sump pump monitoring systems provide continuous oversight of basement sump pumps, which are critical for preventing flooding in homes with basements. These systems use sensors to monitor pump operation, water levels, power status, and pump health, automatically testing functionality and alerting homeowners to potential issues before failures occur.

The technology typically includes water level sensors, vibration sensors to detect pump operation, and connectivity to send alerts via smartphone apps. Daily automated tests verify the pump activates correctly, while 24/7 monitoring tracks water levels and pump cycles. Advanced systems can detect early warning signs like unusual vibration patterns, extended run times, or power issues. Instant alerts notify homeowners of problems like pump failures, power outages, high water levels, or maintenance needs, enabling proactive response to prevent costly water damage.
