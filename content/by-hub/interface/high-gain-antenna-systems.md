# High-Gain Antenna Systems

## Summary

Up to 1,024 large array antenna systems and circuits.

## Description

High-gain antenna systems use large arrays of antenna elements—up to 1,024 or more—to create highly directional radio beams with significantly increased signal strength and range compared to single antennas. These phased array systems electronically steer beams without moving parts, enabling rapid reorientation and tracking of moving targets. By combining signals from many antenna elements, these systems achieve high gain (signal amplification) and narrow beamwidth for precise targeting.

Applications include satellite communications, radar systems, 5G/6G base stations, and point-to-point wireless links. The large array size enables beamforming algorithms to create multiple simultaneous beams, support multiple users, and adapt to changing conditions. High-gain antennas are essential for long-range communications, overcoming signal attenuation over distance, and enabling reliable connections in challenging environments. The technology enables next-generation wireless systems that require both high data rates and extended range.
