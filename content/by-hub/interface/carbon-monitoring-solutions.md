# Carbon Monitoring Solutions

## Summary

AI-powered platforms for real-time carbon emission monitoring using non-intrusive sensors and AI analytics to measure ship and port emissions, empowering global ports to achieve greener operations and ESG transformation.

## Description

Carbon monitoring solutions use AI-powered sensor networks and analytics to measure and track carbon emissions in real-time without requiring intrusive installation on vessels or equipment. These systems employ optical sensors, gas analyzers, and computer vision to detect and quantify emissions from ships, vehicles, and industrial operations.

The technology enables ports, cities, and industrial facilities to accurately measure their carbon footprint, identify emission sources, and track progress toward reduction goals. AI analytics process sensor data to distinguish between different emission sources, calculate total emissions, and provide actionable insights for reducing environmental impact. Applications include port operations, shipping fleets, industrial facilities, and urban air quality monitoring. By providing accurate, real-time emission data, these systems empower organizations to make data-driven decisions for ESG transformation and regulatory compliance.
