# Core Body Temperature Sensing

## Summary

Wearable sensors for continuous, non-invasive core temperature tracking.

## Description

Core body temperature sensing wearables provide continuous, non-invasive monitoring of the body's internal temperature, which is more accurate and clinically relevant than skin temperature measurements. These sensors use advanced algorithms and multiple measurement points to estimate core temperature from surface measurements, accounting for environmental factors, activity levels, and individual variations. The technology enables real-time tracking of core temperature changes, which is critical for detecting fever, monitoring health conditions, optimizing athletic performance, and preventing heat-related illnesses.

Continuous core temperature monitoring is valuable for early detection of infections and fevers, monitoring recovery from illness, tracking menstrual cycles, optimizing sleep quality, and managing heat stress during exercise or in hot environments. The non-invasive approach makes it practical for everyday wear, unlike traditional methods that require invasive probes. The sensors can detect subtle temperature changes that may indicate health issues before symptoms become apparent. Applications include health monitoring for vulnerable populations, athletic performance optimization, workplace safety in hot environments, and general wellness tracking. The technology provides actionable insights for maintaining optimal health and preventing temperature-related health complications.
