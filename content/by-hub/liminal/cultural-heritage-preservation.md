# Cultural Heritage Preservation

## Summary

Spatial archives of endangered sites, rituals, and artifacts.

## Description

Volumetric capture and AR experiences preserving cultural sites threatened by climate change, conflict, or development. Enables communities to maintain connections to ancestral spaces, researchers to study inaccessible locations, and future generations to experience heritage that may physically vanish.
