# Climate Visualization Layers

## Summary

Overlaying environmental data and climate futures onto landscapes.

## Description

AR applications that visualize invisible environmental phenomena like air quality, rising sea levels, historical temperatures, or carbon emissions directly in the spaces they affect. Used for education, policy advocacy, and urban planning to make climate change tangible and actionable in local contexts.
