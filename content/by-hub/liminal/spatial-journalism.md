# Spatial Journalism

## Summary

Immersive reporting placing audiences inside documented events.

## Description

Volumetric video and spatial audio used to create documentary experiences that transport viewers to the scene of news events. Enables more empathetic understanding of distant conflicts, climate impacts, or social issues by providing embodied perspective, particularly valuable for underreported stories from the Global South.
