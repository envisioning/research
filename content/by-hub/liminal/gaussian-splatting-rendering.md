# Gaussian Splatting Rendering

## Summary

Real-time photorealistic 3D rendering from sparse point clouds.

## Description

Neural rendering technique representing scenes as millions of 3D Gaussian primitives that can be rasterized at interactive frame rates. Offers faster training and rendering than NeRFs while maintaining photorealism, enabling real-time capture and streaming of complex environments for telepresence, gaming, and simulation.
