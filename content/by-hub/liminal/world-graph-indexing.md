# World Graph Indexing

## Summary

Graph-based indexing of spatial anchors, objects, and relationships.

## Description

Software stacks that represent the physical world as a living graph of anchors, surfaces, and entities. They allow applications to attach data, behaviors, and permissions to locations or objects, enabling persistent, shared, and queryable spatial content across devices.
