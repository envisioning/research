# Cognitive Liberty Rights

## Summary

Legal frameworks protecting neural data and thought privacy.

## Description

Emerging jurisprudence and technical standards aimed at protecting 'mental privacy'. As BCIs become common, these rights ensure that neural data (thoughts, emotions, attention) cannot be monitored, manipulated, or commodified without explicit, granular consent.
