# Embodied AI Agents

## Summary

Autonomous virtual beings navigating and acting in spatial contexts.

## Description

AI systems with spatial reasoning, navigation, and manipulation capabilities that can exist as persistent characters or assistants in mixed reality. They understand 3D space, respond to gestures and natural language, and perform tasks like guiding users, managing environments, or providing companionship.
