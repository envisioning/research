# Telepresence Tourism

## Summary

Remote exploration via sensory-rich robotic avatars.

## Description

Services allowing users to inhabit a robot in a distant location. Through VR headsets and haptic suits, users can see, hear, and feel the remote environment (e.g., walking on a beach or visiting a museum) without physical travel, reducing carbon footprints.
