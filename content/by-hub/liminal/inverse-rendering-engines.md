# Inverse Rendering Engines

## Summary

Material and lighting estimation from real-world observations.

## Description

Algorithms that reverse traditional rendering by analyzing photographs or video to extract underlying material properties, lighting conditions, and geometry. Enables automatic creation of physically accurate digital twins and seamless blending of virtual objects into real environments with correct reflections and shadows.
