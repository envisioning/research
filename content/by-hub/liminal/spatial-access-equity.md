# Spatial Access Equity

## Summary

Infrastructure bridging the digital divide in spatial computing.

## Description

Programs and technologies addressing disparities in access to spatial computing based on income, geography, or infrastructure. Includes offline-first spatial applications, device-lending libraries, community spatial computing centers, and ensuring non-smartphone pathways to participate in augmented public spaces.
