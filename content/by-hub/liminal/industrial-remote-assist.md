# Industrial Remote Assist

## Summary

AR-guided maintenance and repair with over-the-shoulder experts.

## Description

Applications that stream a field worker's view to remote specialists, who can place anchored annotations, arrows, and ghosted hands into the worker's scene. This shortens downtime for complex machinery while reducing the need to fly experts on-site.
