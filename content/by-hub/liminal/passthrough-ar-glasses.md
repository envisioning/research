# Passthrough AR Glasses

## Summary

Lightweight mixed-reality eyewear blending physical and digital views.

## Description

Head-worn devices that combine high-resolution cameras with transparent or passthrough displays to overlay digital content onto the real world. They support precise head and hand tracking for always-on spatial interfaces in everyday environments, from streets to workspaces.
