# Digital Twin Surgery

## Summary

Surgical augmentation overlaying patient anatomy in real-time.

## Description

Medical application where high-resolution scans are mapped onto a patient's body during surgery. Surgeons see 'through' tissue via AR glasses, visualizing blood vessels, tumors, and nerves in exact alignment, reducing invasiveness and improving outcomes.
