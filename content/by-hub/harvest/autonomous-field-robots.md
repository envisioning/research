# Autonomous Field Robots

## Summary

Multi-task robots for harvesting and crop care.

## Description

GPS- and vision-guided robotic platforms perform weeding, spot-spraying, and selective harvesting, reducing labor bottlenecks and chemical use in specialty and row crops.
