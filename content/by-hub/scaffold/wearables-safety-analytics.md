# Wearables for Safety & Ergonomics

## Summary

Connected wearables that detect falls, fatigue, heat stress, and proximity to hazards in real time.

## Description

Wearables and tags can alert workers to equipment proximity, track heat stress, and provide incident reconstruction. The value is highest when alerts are reliable and privacy is governed, with clear policies on data retention, purpose limitation, and non-punitive use.
