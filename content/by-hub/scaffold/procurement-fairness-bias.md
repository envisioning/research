# Procurement Fairness & Algorithmic Bias

## Summary

Ensuring AI-driven bidding, scheduling, and workforce tools don’t encode unfair or unsafe incentives.

## Description

As algorithms influence subcontractor selection, bid leveling, and staffing, bias and perverse incentives can emerge (eg, optimizing cost at the expense of safety or excluding small/local firms). Responsible deployment requires explainability, auditability, and measurable fairness/safety constraints aligned to policy goals.
