# Deep Retrofit & Building Electrification

## Summary

Industrialized retrofit packages (heat pumps, envelopes, controls) delivered at scale to decarbonize existing buildings.

## Description

Most of the 2050 building stock already exists. Emerging retrofit programs combine prefabricated envelope panels, heat pumps, advanced controls, and financing mechanisms to reduce disruption and cost. This is a core built-environment system challenge linking technology, labor, and capital structures.
