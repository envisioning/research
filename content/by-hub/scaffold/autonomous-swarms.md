# Autonomous Construction Swarms

## Summary

Coordinated groups of small robots performing collaborative construction tasks.

## Description

Swarms of small ground and aerial robots that communicate to perform complex tasks like bricklaying, site surveying, or rebar tying. Swarm intelligence allows for decentralized operation and resilience to individual unit failure.
