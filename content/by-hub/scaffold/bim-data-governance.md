# BIM/Digital Twin Data Governance (IP + Access)

## Summary

Defining who owns models, who can modify them, and how changes are audited across the lifecycle.

## Description

Models increasingly encode means-and-methods, pricing, and operational vulnerabilities. Governance covers IP rights, role-based access, immutable audit logs for revisions, and secure sharing with subcontractors and owners—reducing disputes and limiting the blast radius of data leaks.
