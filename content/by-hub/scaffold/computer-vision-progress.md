# Computer Vision Progress & Quality Tracking

## Summary

Vision models that quantify installed work, detect defects, and verify safety compliance from images.

## Description

Computer vision on site imagery (phones, 360 cameras, drones) can estimate percent complete, flag missing components, and detect quality issues like misaligned installs. When tied to schedule and pay applications, CV reduces disputes by providing objective, timestamped evidence of work-in-place.
