# Programmable Matter & 4D Printing

## Summary

Materials that change shape or properties in response to external stimuli after fabrication.

## Description

Going beyond 3D printing, '4D' printing creates objects that self-assemble or adapt over time (e.g., pipes that expand with flow, facades that open with heat). This reduces actuation complexity and enables adaptive infrastructure that responds to environmental changes without electronics.
