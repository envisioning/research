# Aerogel & Vacuum Insulation Panels

## Summary

Ultra-high-performance insulation for space-constrained retrofits and extreme climates.

## Description

Aerogels (silica-based or organic) and vacuum-insulated panels deliver R-values several times higher than conventional insulation per inch. This enables deep retrofits without sacrificing interior space and supports envelope performance in Arctic, desert, and dense urban contexts where thickness is constrained.
