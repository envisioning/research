# Safety Standards for Autonomous Machines

## Summary

Certification, training, and site rules that make autonomy deployable without increasing risk.

## Description

Deploying autonomous equipment requires more than technology: hazard analysis, fail-safe behaviors, human-machine interaction design, operator training, and clear accountability in incident scenarios. Standards and certification pathways are emerging to make autonomy auditable for insurers, regulators, and unions.
