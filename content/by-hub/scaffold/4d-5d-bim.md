# 4D/5D BIM Scheduling & Costing

## Summary

Linking BIM to time (4D) and cost (5D) for scenario planning and real-time project controls.

## Description

4D/5D workflows connect model elements to schedules, production rates, and cost codes, enabling simulation of sequencing and cash flow. When integrated with field capture and procurement systems, these models provide early warning on delays, cost overruns, and trade coordination issues.
