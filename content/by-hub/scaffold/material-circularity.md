# Circular Economy Frameworks

## Summary

Systems ensuring construction materials are reusable and recyclable.

## Description

Design philosophies and regulatory frameworks that treat buildings as material banks. This ensures that at the end of a building's life, its components can be disassembled and reused rather than sent to a landfill.
