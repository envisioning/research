# Autonomous Heavy Equipment

## Summary

Self-driving excavators, dozers, and haul trucks guided by GNSS, LiDAR, and geofencing.

## Description

Autonomy stacks for earthmoving and haulage that combine high-precision positioning (RTK GNSS), perception (LiDAR/radar/cameras), and site geofencing. These systems target repeatable tasks like grading, trenching, and material transport while improving safety by reducing human exposure to heavy machinery zones.
