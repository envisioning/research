# Ransomware Resilience for Contractors

## Summary

Backups, segmentation, and incident response tuned to construction’s project-based IT and vendor ecosystem.

## Description

Construction firms are frequent ransomware targets due to distributed job sites, many third-party systems, and time-critical deliverables. Resilience includes offline backups, least-privilege access, MFA, vendor risk management, and playbooks for restoring schedules, models, and financial systems without paying ransoms.
