# Climate Displacement & Construction Labor Migration

## Summary

Addressing the movement of construction workers due to climate, conflict, and economic shifts.

## Description

Climate change and geopolitical instability drive labor migration, creating both opportunity and exploitation risk in construction. Ethical frameworks must ensure safe working conditions, fair wages, credential recognition, and pathways to residency—especially in Gulf states, Southeast Asia, and North America receiving large migrant flows.
