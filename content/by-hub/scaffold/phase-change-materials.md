# Phase-Change Materials (PCM) for Thermal Mass

## Summary

Encapsulated waxes or salts that absorb and release heat, reducing HVAC loads.

## Description

PCMs integrated into walls, ceilings, or floors stabilize indoor temperatures by storing latent heat during the day and releasing it at night. This passive technology cuts peak cooling demand, especially in hot climates, and complements lightweight construction lacking traditional thermal mass.
