# Space-Based Solar Power for Remote Sites

## Summary

Beaming energy from orbital solar arrays to remote construction or infrastructure sites.

## Description

Wireless power transmission (microwave/laser) from space could supply baseload power to remote mega-projects (mines, dams, arctic bases) without fuel supply chains. While futuristic, recent ESA and China National Space Administration tests are moving this from sci-fi to viable long-term infrastructure strategy.
