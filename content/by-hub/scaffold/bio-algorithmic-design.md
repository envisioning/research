# Bio-Algorithmic Design (Physarum Computing)

## Summary

Using biological growth patterns (e.g., slime mold) to solve complex routing and structural problems.

## Description

Biological algorithms inspired by slime mold (Physarum polycephalum) can solve transport network and structural load problems more efficiently than traditional brute-force computing. This 'wetware' approach offers novel optimization strategies for urban planning and resilient infrastructure networks.
