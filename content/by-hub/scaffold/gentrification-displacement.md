# Gentrification & Displacement from Development

## Summary

Mitigating the socio-economic impacts of new construction on existing communities.

## Description

Transit-oriented development, mixed-use projects, and urban regeneration can trigger rent increases and displacement. Tools include inclusionary zoning, community land trusts, anti-displacement impact assessments, and participatory planning. Technology supports analysis (predictive displacement models) but policy and finance structures determine outcomes.
