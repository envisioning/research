# Urban Mining & Material Passports

## Summary

Treating cities as material banks with formalized deconstruction and reuse workflows.

## Description

Urban mining views existing buildings as repositories of valuable materials. Coupled with 'Material Passports' (digital records of component composition and disassembly instructions), this enables high-value recovery of steel, copper, and facade elements, replacing extraction with circular harvesting.
