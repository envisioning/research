# UAV LiDAR & Reality Capture

## Summary

Drones and mobile scanners producing dense point clouds for surveying and progress verification.

## Description

UAV photogrammetry and LiDAR, combined with mobile mapping rigs, can rapidly generate accurate point clouds and orthomosaics for topography, stockpiles, and as-built verification. Reality capture compresses the survey-to-model cycle and enables frequent, low-cost measurement at scale.
