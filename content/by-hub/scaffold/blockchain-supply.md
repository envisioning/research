# Blockchain Material Tracking

## Summary

Decentralized ledgers for immutable supply chain transparency.

## Description

Using blockchain to track the provenance of construction materials from source to site. This ensures compliance with sustainability standards, verifies material quality, and automates contractor payments via smart contracts.
