# Intergenerational Justice Frameworks

## Summary

Legal and ethical tools to evaluate if construction indebts future generations.

## Description

New legal frameworks (like Wales' 'Future Generations Act') require infrastructure decisions to account for long-term impacts on climate, debt, and maintenance. This shifts cost-benefit analysis from 30-year ROI to multi-century stewardship, challenging 'build-fast' models in favor of durability and adaptability.
