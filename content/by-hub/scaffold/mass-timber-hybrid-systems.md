# Mass Timber & Hybrid Structural Systems

## Summary

CLT/glulam structures combined with steel/concrete cores for faster, lower-carbon buildings.

## Description

Mass timber (CLT, glulam, LVL) enables rapid assembly, lighter foundations, and potential embodied-carbon reductions, especially when paired with hybrid cores and robust fire/acoustic detailing. Scaling requires specialized fabrication, rigorous moisture management, and evolving building codes.
