# Supply Chain Integrity & Human Rights Compliance

## Summary

Verification that materials meet labor, sanctions, and sustainability requirements across global supply chains.

## Description

Construction materials and components often traverse complex supply chains with risks of forced labor, sanctions exposure, and environmental harm. Emerging compliance stacks combine provenance data, audits, and procurement policies to meet public procurement rules and ESG expectations while avoiding greenwashing.
