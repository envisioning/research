# Mobile Microgrids & Battery Storage for Construction Sites

## Summary

Containerized solar+storage systems replacing diesel generators on remote or urban sites.

## Description

Battery-backed solar microgrids provide stable, low-emission power for tools, site offices, and EV charging. They reduce noise, eliminate diesel logistics, and enable work in emissions-restricted zones. Scaling depends on battery costs, site power profiles, and utility interconnection policies, with strong uptake in California, Nordic countries, and the Gulf.
