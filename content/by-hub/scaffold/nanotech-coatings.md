# Nanotechnology Coatings (Self-Cleaning, Anti-Microbial)

## Summary

Photocatalytic and hydrophobic coatings that reduce maintenance and improve hygiene.

## Description

Titanium-dioxide and graphene-based coatings break down organic pollutants under UV light (self-cleaning) and can inhibit microbial growth (anti-microbial). Applications include facades, tunnels, hospitals, and public infrastructure in polluted or high-traffic environments, with notable adoption in Japan, China, and Europe.
