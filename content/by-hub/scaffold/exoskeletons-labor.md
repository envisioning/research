# Industrial Exoskeletons

## Summary

Powered wearable suits to augment construction worker strength and endurance.

## Description

Robotic exoskeletons designed to reduce physical strain on construction workers, allowing for safer lifting of heavy loads and reducing fatigue-related injuries. These devices enhance human capability rather than replacing it.
