# Worker Data Privacy & Surveillance Governance

## Summary

Policies and technical controls for cameras, wearables, and location tracking on job sites.

## Description

As sites adopt cameras, wearables, and tracking, the boundary between safety and surveillance becomes critical. Governance includes purpose limitation, informed consent where applicable, retention limits, access controls, and clear rules preventing misuse for retaliation or off-site monitoring.
