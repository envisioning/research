# Autonomous Site Logistics

## Summary

Material tracking, geofenced delivery routing, and robotic movers that reduce idle time and congestion.

## Description

Construction sites lose significant time to waiting and searching for materials. Combining RFID/UWB tracking, logistics planning, and autonomous movers (AGVs, smart carts) can reduce congestion and improve trade productivity, especially on dense urban projects with constrained staging.
