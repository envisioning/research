# In-Orbit Manufacturing

## Summary

Zero-gravity production of superior materials.

## Description

Automated factories on space stations producing materials impossible to make on Earth (e.g., ZBLAN fibers, perfect protein crystals, large integrated structures) leveraging microgravity and vacuum conditions.
