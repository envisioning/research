# 4D Printing & Programmable Matter

## Summary

Smart materials that self-assemble or reshape over time.

## Description

Components printed with responsive materials that change shape or property (stiffness, conductivity) when triggered by heat, light, or moisture, enabling self-assembling structures and adaptive parts.
