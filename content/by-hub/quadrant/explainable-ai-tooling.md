# Explainable AI Tooling

## Summary

Toolchains to inspect, justify, and govern AI decisions.

## Description

Model-agnostic and domain-specific explainability layers that surface rationales, uncertainty, and counterfactuals for AI used in safety-critical industrial decisions.
