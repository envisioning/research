# Industrial Brain-Computer Interfaces

## Summary

Direct neural control for high-stakes teleoperation.

## Description

Non-invasive EEG and EMG interfaces allowing operators to control robotic avatars or machinery via thought, reducing latency and freeing hands for complex tasks in hazardous environments.
