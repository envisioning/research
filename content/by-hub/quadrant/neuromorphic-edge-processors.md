# Neuromorphic Edge Processors

## Summary

Brain-inspired chips for ultra-low power, real-time AI at the edge.

## Description

Processors mimicking neural architectures (e.g., Intel Loihi 2) to enable event-driven processing and learning directly on industrial devices with minimal energy consumption.
