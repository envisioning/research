# Worker Safety Monitoring Systems

## Summary

Real-time sensing to prevent injuries and unsafe conditions.

## Description

Computer-vision, wearables, and environmental sensors fused to detect near-misses, PPE compliance, and hazardous states, feeding into safety management dashboards and interventions.
