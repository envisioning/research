# Mobility Simulation & Optimization

## Summary

City-scale models optimizing multimodal mobility, curb space, and logistics flows.

## Description

Simulation platforms that combine traffic data, micromobility usage, freight patterns, and land use to test new street designs and policies. Planners use them to evaluate congestion pricing, bus priority, curb management, and active mobility infrastructure before implementation, balancing throughput, safety, and emissions.
