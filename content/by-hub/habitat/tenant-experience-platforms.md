# Tenant Experience Platforms

## Summary

AI-driven mobile hubs integrating access, services, and community for occupants.

## Description

Digital platforms that unify building access control, room booking, comfort requests, concierge services, and community engagement into a single interface. By layering behavioral analytics and personalization on top of building systems, they transform buildings into service-rich environments and provide landlords with new revenue and loyalty models.
