# Low-Carbon Construction Materials

## Summary

Novel concretes, bio-based composites, and modular systems cutting embodied carbon.

## Description

Material innovations such as carbon-sequestering concrete, engineered timber systems, and bio-based insulation deployed at scale in structures. These solutions target the embodied carbon of buildings and infrastructure while maintaining performance, often requiring new design standards, testing protocols, and digital material passports.
