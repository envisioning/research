# Biophilic IoT Sensors

## Summary

Environmental monitoring networks optimizing spaces for human health and wellbeing.

## Description

Dense sensor networks that monitor indoor air quality, circadian lighting levels, and acoustic comfort. These systems integrate with building controls to dynamically adjust environmental parameters, mimicking natural conditions to enhance occupant cognitive function, reduce stress, and improve overall physical health.
