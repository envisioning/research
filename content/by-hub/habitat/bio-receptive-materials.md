# Bio-Receptive Materials

## Summary

Architectural surfaces designed to host living organisms for air quality and biodiversity.

## Description

Materials engineered with specific porosity, roughness, and chemical composition to encourage the growth of mosses, lichens, and algae. Unlike green walls that require pots and irrigation, these materials (often concrete or ceramics) passively support life, absorbing pollutants, reducing urban heat islands, and reintegrating nature into the building fabric.
