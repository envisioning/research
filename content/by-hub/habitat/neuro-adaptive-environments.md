# Neuro-Adaptive Environments

## Summary

Spaces that adjust lighting, sound, and layout based on real-time human biometric feedback.

## Description

The convergence of neuro-architecture and IoT, where buildings use wearables or privacy-preserving sensors to detect stress or fatigue in occupants. The environment then automatically adjusts lighting color temperature, sound masking, and spatial configurations to restore cognitive performance and emotional wellbeing.
