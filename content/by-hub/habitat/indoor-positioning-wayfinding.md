# Indoor Positioning & Wayfinding

## Summary

Sub-meter indoor location services for navigation, asset tracking, and analytics.

## Description

Deployment of Bluetooth beacons, ultra-wideband, and Wi‑Fi sensing to locate people and assets inside complex buildings. These systems support real-time wayfinding, emergency egress guidance, equipment utilization tracking, and precise space analytics for both workplace and public infrastructure.
