# Participatory Planning Platforms

## Summary

Digital engagement tools bringing residents into spatial planning decisions at scale.

## Description

Web and mobile platforms that combine visualization, surveys, and deliberation tools to capture community input on plans and projects. Integrated with urban digital twins and AR views, they allow residents to explore trade-offs, propose alternatives, and collaborate with planners, improving legitimacy and social outcomes.
