# Blockchain Smart Contracts

## Summary

Self-executing contracts for transparent and automated real estate transactions.

## Description

Decentralized ledger technology enabling trustless, automated property transfers. Smart contracts automatically enforce agreement terms, release funds, and transfer title deeds once conditions are met, significantly reducing transaction times, legal costs, and the need for intermediaries while eliminating fraud risks.
