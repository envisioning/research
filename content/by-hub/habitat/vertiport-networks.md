# Vertiport Networks

## Summary

Infrastructure nodes designed for electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft.

## Description

The dedicated physical infrastructure required for Urban Air Mobility (UAM), transforming rooftops and parking garages into charging and passenger hubs. These networks integrate high-voltage power management, passenger security screening, and automated flight planning to enable the next generation of 3D urban transportation.
