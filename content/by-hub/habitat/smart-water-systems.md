# Smart Water Systems

## Summary

Intelligent water monitoring and management for conservation and resilience.

## Description

IoT-enabled systems that track water consumption, detect leaks, manage greywater recycling, and optimize irrigation in real time. By integrating flow sensors, quality monitors, and predictive algorithms, these platforms reduce water waste by up to 30%, prevent damage from undetected leaks, and support water-secure building operations in drought-prone regions.
