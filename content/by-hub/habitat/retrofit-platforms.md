# Smart Retrofit Platforms

## Summary

Preconfigured hardware–software kits to modernize legacy building stock at scale.

## Description

Standardized packages of sensors, controls, and cloud services designed for rapid deployment in existing buildings. By offering repeatable retrofit playbooks, financing integrations, and performance guarantees, these platforms unlock energy savings and digital capabilities in aging portfolios that cannot be rebuilt from scratch.
