# Social Impact Measurement Tools

## Summary

Analytics platforms quantifying societal value, community wellbeing, and housing equity outcomes.

## Description

Comprehensive measurement and reporting systems that track the social and societal value created by housing developments beyond financial returns. These tools measure community wellbeing metrics, housing equity impact, resident satisfaction, social connection indicators, and ESG outcomes specific to student and affordable housing. By providing quantifiable evidence of social impact, they support impact investing, inform policy decisions, demonstrate value to stakeholders, and guide development strategies that prioritize both financial sustainability and positive social outcomes.
