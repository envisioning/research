# Grid-Interactive Efficient Buildings

## Summary

Buildings that flex electricity demand and storage in response to grid signals.

## Description

Control platforms that coordinate HVAC, batteries, EV chargers, and flexible loads to participate in demand response and ancillary services markets. By forecasting occupancy and prices, they shift consumption, store excess renewable energy, and sell flexibility back to the grid, monetizing efficiency and resilience.
