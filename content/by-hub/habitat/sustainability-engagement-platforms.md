# Sustainability Engagement Platforms

## Summary

Gamified platforms encouraging sustainable living behaviors in residential communities.

## Description

Behavioral engagement systems that motivate residents to adopt sustainable practices through gamification, challenges, and social comparison. These platforms track individual and community-wide carbon footprints, energy consumption, waste reduction, and water usage, providing real-time feedback and rewards. By making sustainability visible, social, and rewarding, they transform environmental responsibility from obligation to community activity, significantly reducing building resource consumption while fostering resident engagement and environmental awareness.
