# Space-as-a-Service Orchestration

## Summary

Dynamic allocation and pricing of space across hybrid and flexible portfolios.

## Description

Orchestration engines that treat offices, retail, and logistics assets as on-demand resources rather than fixed leases. They integrate utilization sensors, booking data, and market signals to reconfigure layouts, adjust pricing, and route demand between locations, enabling new operating models for owners and operators.
