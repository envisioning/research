# Cognitive BMS

## Summary

AI-driven building management systems for predictive climate and energy control.

## Description

Next-generation Building Management Systems that move beyond reactive control to predictive optimization. Using AI to analyze occupancy patterns, weather forecasts, and energy prices, these systems proactively adjust HVAC, lighting, and shading to maximize comfort while minimizing energy consumption and carbon footprint.
