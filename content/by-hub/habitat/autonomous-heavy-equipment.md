# Autonomous Heavy Equipment

## Summary

Self-driving excavators, trucks, and loaders orchestrated from central control rooms.

## Description

Construction fleets equipped with sensors, computer vision, and autonomy stacks to perform excavation, hauling, and grading with minimal human intervention. Integrated with project management systems, they can execute optimized work plans, operate in hazardous environments, and feed telemetry back into productivity and safety analytics.
