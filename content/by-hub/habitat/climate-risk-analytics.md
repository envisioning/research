# Climate Risk Analytics

## Summary

Asset-level climate risk scoring for resilient real estate portfolios.

## Description

Analytical platforms that model physical and transition risks for each property, combining climate scenarios, flood and heat maps, and asset data. Investors and asset managers use these tools to price climate exposure into deals, prioritize adaptation capex, and align portfolios with emerging disclosure and taxonomy requirements.
