# Connected Worker Ecosystems

## Summary

IoT wearables and exoskeletons enhancing worker safety and productivity.

## Description

Integration of smart sensors, AR visors, and powered exoskeletons into the construction workforce. These technologies monitor vital signs for fatigue, detect fall risks, provide real-time digital blueprint overlays, and augment physical strength, creating a safer and more efficient job site environment.
