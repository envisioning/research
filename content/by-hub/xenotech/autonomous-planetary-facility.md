# Planetary Facility

## Summary

Alleged self-perpetuating extraterrestrial defense AI managing Earth protection through autonomous craft production.

## Description

The Autonomous Planetary Facility concept describes a self-sustaining non-human technological system allegedly operating on or near Earth for millennia. According to testimony from anonymous sources (2024-25), this system functions as an audit and defense network—monitoring planetary quantum signatures and producing defensive craft autonomously without ongoing extraterrestrial supervision.

## Operational Concept

The facility allegedly operates as a 'craft-printing' network, manufacturing surveillance and defense drones in response to detected threats or interference from other non-human entities. Recent claims suggest the system is experiencing resource depletion after extended operation, leading to production of incomplete or malfunctioning craft (identified as 'jellyfish' UAPs with damaged cloaking systems).

## Critical Assessment

The concept draws on ancient astronaut themes, AI defense networks, and von Neumann probe theory—self-replicating machines sent by advanced civilizations. However, no physical evidence, location data, or verifiable documentation supports these claims. The narrative exemplifies modern UAP mythology incorporating artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, and collapse-cycle themes. It represents pure testimony-based xenotechnology addressing the question 'why haven't they revealed themselves?' with 'automated systems operating without oversight.'
