# Plantibody

## Summary

Antibodies produced in plants for therapeutics and diagnostics.

## Description

Plantibodies are antibodies expressed in transgenic plants for therapeutic or diagnostic use. Clinical trials underway; scalable, lower-cost production than mammalian systems. Applications include vaccines and therapeutics.
