# Magnetic Nanoparticles

## Summary

Nanoscale magnetic materials for cancer treatment, wastewater, and sensing.

## Description

Magnetic nanoparticles are used in drug delivery, hyperthermia cancer treatment, wastewater treatment, and sensing. Development and limited deployment; research continues into biocompatibility, targeting, and scalable production.
