# In-Situ Recovery Injection Systems

## Summary

Subsurface extraction via solution injection, eliminating open pits and waste rock.

## Description

In-situ recovery (ISR) or in-situ leaching (ISL) systems inject leaching solutions through drilled wells into ore bodies, dissolve target metals underground, and pump pregnant solutions to surface for processing. Widely used for uranium and expanding to copper, rare earths, and lithium, ISR dramatically reduces surface disturbance, water use, and tailings compared to conventional mining.
