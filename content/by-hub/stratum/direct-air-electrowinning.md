# Direct Air Electrowinning

## Summary

Converting atmospheric CO2 directly into industrial chemicals.

## Description

An emerging application where atmospheric CO2 is captured and electrochemically converted into ethylene, syngas, or other feedstock chemicals. This supports the decarbonization of the petrochemical industry by replacing fossil-fuel-based precursors.
