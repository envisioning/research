# Bioleaching and Biomining

## Summary

Harnessing microorganisms to extract metals from low-grade ores and waste.

## Description

Bioleaching uses naturally occurring bacteria, archaea, and fungi to oxidize sulfide minerals, liberating copper, gold, nickel, cobalt, and rare earth elements. Applied in heap leaching, stirred-tank bioreactors, and in-situ operations, biomining offers lower energy consumption, reduced chemical use, and the ability to economically process ores previously considered waste.
