# AI-Driven Exploration Targeting

## Summary

Machine learning models identifying new deposits from multi-scale geoscience data.

## Description

AI-driven exploration platforms fuse geological maps, geophysics, geochemistry, and drilling data to identify subtle patterns associated with mineralization. They generate ranked targeting maps, reduce drilling in unprospective areas, and help discover blind or concealed deposits, especially for critical minerals.
