# Space Resource Utilization (ISRU)

## Summary

Extracting water, oxygen, and metals from lunar and asteroid regolith.

## Description

In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) technologies aim to harvest resources in space to support off-earth economies. Near-term focus includes extracting water ice from the lunar south pole for propellant, while longer-term goals involve mining asteroids for platinum group metals and nickel-iron for orbital manufacturing.
