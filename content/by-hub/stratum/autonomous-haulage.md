# Autonomous Haulage Systems

## Summary

Self-driving trucks and loaders navigating complex mine environments.

## Description

Autonomous Haulage Systems (AHS) utilize LiDAR, GPS, and advanced sensors to enable heavy mining trucks to operate without human drivers. These systems improve safety by removing personnel from hazardous areas, optimize fuel efficiency through precise driving, and increase operational continuity by running 24/7.
