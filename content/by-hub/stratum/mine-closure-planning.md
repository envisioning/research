# Mine Closure and Rehabilitation Planning Systems

## Summary

Life-of-mine software integrating closure, remediation, and financial provisioning.

## Description

Mine closure planning platforms model progressive rehabilitation scenarios, track closure cost estimates, and ensure compliance with bonding and financial assurance requirements. They integrate geotechnical, hydrological, and ecological data to design stable landforms, water management systems, and post-mining land uses, de-risking long-term liabilities.
