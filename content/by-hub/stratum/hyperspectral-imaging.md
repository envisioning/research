# Hyperspectral Imaging Drones

## Summary

Aerial platforms identifying mineral compositions and structural integrity.

## Description

Drones equipped with hyperspectral sensors can identify specific mineral signatures and map geological features from the air. This technology accelerates exploration, improves ore grade estimation, and assists in environmental monitoring by detecting leaks or vegetation stress.
