# eDNA Biodiversity Monitoring

## Summary

Environmental DNA sampling to track ecosystem impacts around industrial sites.

## Description

eDNA technologies analyze genetic material in water, soil, and air samples to detect the presence of species without direct observation. Applied around mines, pipelines, and processing plants, they provide sensitive, cost-effective monitoring of biodiversity impacts and help verify compliance with conservation commitments.
