# Fiber-Optic Ground and Slope Monitoring

## Summary

Distributed fiber-optic sensing embedded in slopes, pits, and tunnels.

## Description

Fiber-optic sensing cables installed in pit walls, embankments, and underground drives provide continuous strain, vibration, and temperature readings along their full length. Combined with geotechnical models, they enable early detection of slope instability and ground movement, supporting proactive evacuation and remediation.
