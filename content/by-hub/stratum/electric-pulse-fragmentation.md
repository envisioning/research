# Electric Pulse Fragmentation Systems

## Summary

High-voltage pulses disintegrating rock along grain boundaries.

## Description

Electric Pulse Fragmentation (EPF) uses short, high-voltage bursts to blast rock from the inside out. This non-mechanical comminution method breaks ore along mineral boundaries, improving liberation and reducing energy consumption in crushing and grinding circuits by up to 30%.
