# Supply Chain Blockchain

## Summary

Immutable ledgers for ethical sourcing and compliance.

## Description

Blockchain applications track raw materials from extraction to end-product, ensuring transparency. This verifies ethical sourcing practices (e.g., conflict-free minerals) and provides an immutable record of environmental compliance and carbon footprint data.
