# Closed-Loop Water Recycling Systems

## Summary

High-recovery water circuits minimizing freshwater intake and discharge.

## Description

Closed-loop water systems combine advanced thickening, filtration, membrane separation, and real-time water quality monitoring to recycle process water within mines and processing plants. They reduce dependency on freshwater sources, lower regulatory risk, and enable operations in water-stressed regions.
