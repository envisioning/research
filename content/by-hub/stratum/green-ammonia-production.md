# Green Ammonia Production

## Summary

Synthesizing ammonia from renewable hydrogen for fertilizer and energy.

## Description

Green ammonia is produced by combining green hydrogen (from water electrolysis powered by renewables) with nitrogen from air via the Haber-Bosch process. It decarbonizes fertilizer production (responsible for ~2% of global CO2), serves as a carbon-free marine fuel and energy carrier, and supports the renewable energy transition by storing intermittent power.
