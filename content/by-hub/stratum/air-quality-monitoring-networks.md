# Air Quality Monitoring Networks

## Summary

Distributed sensor arrays tracking particulate matter and emissions around industrial sites.

## Description

Air quality monitoring networks deploy low-cost PM2.5, PM10, NOx, and SOx sensors in fenceline arrays around mines, smelters, refineries, and chemical plants. Real-time data feeds into community portals and regulatory dashboards, enabling rapid response to dust events, verification of emission controls, and transparency with neighboring populations.
