# Direct Lithium Extraction (DLE)

## Summary

Selective extraction of lithium from brines and geothermal fluids.

## Description

Direct Lithium Extraction technologies use ion-exchange membranes, adsorbents, or electrochemical cells to selectively extract lithium from brines in hours or days, versus 12–18 months for evaporation ponds. DLE dramatically increases recovery rates, reduces water and land use, and unlocks lithium resources in geothermal fields and oilfield brines globally.
