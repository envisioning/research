# Geothermal Co-Production with Oil and Gas

## Summary

Leveraging existing wells to co-produce geothermal heat, power, and minerals.

## Description

Geothermal co-production projects tap into hot fluids and brines from oil and gas wells to generate electricity, process heat, or recover dissolved minerals such as lithium. This repurposes legacy infrastructure, lowers the net carbon intensity of hydrocarbons, and accelerates the transition of basin economies.
