# Geometallurgical Modeling Platforms

## Summary

Software linking orebody variability to processing performance and product quality.

## Description

Geometallurgical platforms integrate block models, mineralogy, and plant response data to predict how different ore types will behave in grinding, flotation, and smelting circuits. This enables dynamic blending strategies, life-of-mine optimization, and more accurate forecasts of recovery and product specifications.
