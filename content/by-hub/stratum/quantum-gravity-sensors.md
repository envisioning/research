# Quantum Gravity Gradiometers

## Summary

Ultra-sensitive sensors detecting subsurface density contrasts for exploration.

## Description

Quantum gravity sensors use cold-atom interferometry to detect minute changes in gravitational fields. They allow exploration teams to map subsurface geology, voids, and ore bodies with unprecedented resolution and depth, reducing the need for invasive drilling and accelerating discovery of concealed deposits.
