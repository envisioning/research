# On-Belt Ore Sorting Systems

## Summary

High-throughput sensors separating ore and waste in real time on conveyors.

## Description

On-belt ore sorting systems combine X-ray transmission, magnetic resonance, and hyperspectral sensors with automated rejection mechanisms to separate waste rock from ore before milling. This reduces energy and water use per tonne, increases head grades, and can extend the economic life of marginal deposits.
