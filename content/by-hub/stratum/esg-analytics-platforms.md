# ESG Performance Analytics Platforms

## Summary

Integrated systems measuring, reporting, and optimizing environmental, social, and governance outcomes.

## Description

ESG analytics platforms aggregate data from operations, supply chains, and communities to track metrics like carbon intensity, water use, biodiversity impact, safety performance, and social license. They automate regulatory reporting (TCFD, GRI, SASB), support investor relations, and provide decision intelligence for improving sustainability performance and accessing green finance.
