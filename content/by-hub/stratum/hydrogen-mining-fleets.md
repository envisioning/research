# Hydrogen-Powered Mining Fleets

## Summary

Decarbonizing heavy machinery with green hydrogen fuel cells.

## Description

Replacing diesel engines in massive haul trucks and excavators with hydrogen fuel cell electric powertrains. This application eliminates direct emissions from mining fleets, which are traditionally one of the largest sources of greenhouse gases in the sector.
