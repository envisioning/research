# Trusted Data-Trust Infrastructures

## Summary

Governed sharing of sensitive strategic data.

## Description

Technical and institutional frameworks—combining secure enclaves, identity, and governance tooling—that allow allies and regulated actors to pool sensitive datasets for systemic-risk analysis under clear rules and revocable access.
