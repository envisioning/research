# Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)

## Summary

Sovereign programmable money infrastructure.

## Description

State-issued digital currencies that provide governments with direct monetary policy tools, real-time economic visibility, sanctions enforcement mechanisms, and alternatives to dollar-denominated international settlement, reshaping global financial power dynamics.
