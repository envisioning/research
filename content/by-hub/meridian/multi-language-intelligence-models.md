# Multi-Language Intelligence Models

## Summary

Foundation models for global OSINT and translation.

## Description

Large language models trained on hundreds of languages and cultural contexts, enabling intelligence agencies to process open-source information, social media, and communications from any region without reliance on foreign translation services.
