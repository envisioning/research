# Geoengineering Governance Frameworks

## Summary

International oversight of climate intervention.

## Description

Technical monitoring and legal architectures to govern solar radiation management, ocean fertilization, and other planetary-scale interventions, preventing unilateral climate modification that could trigger conflicts over transboundary impacts and differential regional effects.
