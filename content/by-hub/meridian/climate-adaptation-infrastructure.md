# Climate Adaptation Infrastructure

## Summary

Resilient systems for shifting baselines.

## Description

Engineering solutions for rising seas, changing precipitation, and extreme weather—including adaptive coastal barriers, drought-resistant agriculture networks, and climate-hardened energy grids—that preserve state capacity and prevent climate-driven migration and conflict.
