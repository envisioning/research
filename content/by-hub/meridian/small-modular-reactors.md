# Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)

## Summary

Distributed nuclear for energy sovereignty.

## Description

Factory-built, transportable nuclear reactors that provide reliable baseload power for remote military bases, critical infrastructure, and regions seeking energy independence from fossil fuel imports or grid vulnerabilities.
