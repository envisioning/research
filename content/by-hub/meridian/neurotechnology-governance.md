# Neurotechnology Governance

## Summary

Ethics and controls for brain-computer interfaces.

## Description

Standards and oversight mechanisms for technologies that read or modulate neural activity, addressing cognitive enhancement equity, mental privacy, military applications, and preventing weaponization of neural interfaces in authoritarian surveillance.
