# Water Security Intelligence

## Summary

Monitoring transboundary water resources.

## Description

Integrated satellite, sensor, and modeling systems that track water levels, quality, and usage across river basins and aquifers, providing early warning of resource conflicts and enabling equitable management of shared water resources in water-stressed regions.
