# Synthetic Biology for Strategic Materials

## Summary

Bioengineered production of critical compounds.

## Description

Engineered microorganisms and biological systems that produce rare pharmaceuticals, materials, fuels, and chemical precursors, reducing vulnerability to supply disruptions and enabling production independence for medicines, semiconductors, and defense materiel.
