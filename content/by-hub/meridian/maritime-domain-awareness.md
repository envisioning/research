# Maritime Domain Awareness

## Summary

Persistent monitoring of strategic waters.

## Description

Integrated systems combining satellite reconnaissance, undersea sensors, AIS tracking, and AI analytics to provide comprehensive visibility over exclusive economic zones, shipping lanes, and contested waters, detecting illegal fishing, smuggling, and military movements.
