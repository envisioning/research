# Industrial Metaverse

## Summary

Immersive virtual environments for industrial control.

## Description

The convergence of IT and OT (Operational Technology) where factory floors, supply chains, and industrial systems are managed via immersive VR/AR interfaces. It allows remote experts to collaborate on machinery repair or layout optimization in a physically accurate virtual space.
