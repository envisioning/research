# Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)

## Summary

Metamaterials that actively reflect and steer radio signals.

## Description

Programmable surfaces composed of electromagnetic materials that can manipulate radio waves in real-time. RIS improves coverage, energy efficiency, and signal quality in 5G and 6G networks by turning the environment into a controllable part of the communication channel.
