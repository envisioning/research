# Direct-to-Device Satellite Connectivity

## Summary

Satellites that connect directly to standard phones and IoT devices.

## Description

Non-terrestrial network services where LEO satellites act as orbiting base stations, reusing cellular waveforms to connect unmodified handsets and narrowband IoT devices. This extends coverage to oceans, deserts, and disaster zones without special terminals.
