# Joint Communication & Sensing (JCAS)

## Summary

Waveforms and protocols that combine connectivity with environmental sensing.

## Description

Signal processing and AI techniques that reuse communication signals for radar-like sensing, enabling applications such as object detection, localization, and environmental mapping. JCAS turns 6G networks into distributed sensor arrays, supporting smart mobility and infrastructure monitoring.
