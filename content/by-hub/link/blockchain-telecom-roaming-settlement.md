# Blockchain for Roaming & Wholesale Settlement

## Summary

Distributed ledgers to automate inter-operator billing and trust.

## Description

Smart contracts and tokenized systems that replace manual roaming agreements and wholesale settlements with automated, transparent, and near-instant transactions. They reduce fraud, enable micro-roaming agreements between community networks, and open new models for spectrum sharing and eSIM provisioning.
