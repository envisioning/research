# Internet of Senses

## Summary

Transmitting touch, smell, and taste over networks.

## Description

Extending digital communication beyond sight and sound to include haptic (touch), olfactory (smell), and gustatory (taste) experiences. This requires ultra-low latency (sub-millisecond) to synchronize sensory inputs for remote surgery, immersive VR, and virtual tourism.
