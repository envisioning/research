# Network Neutrality & Traffic Shaping Transparency

## Summary

Technical and regulatory systems to prevent discriminatory traffic treatment.

## Description

Monitoring tools, certification schemes, and automated auditing that detect when ISPs throttle, block, or prioritize content based on commercial relationships. Ensures equal treatment of data across the internet and prevents gatekeeping by dominant platforms or carriers.
