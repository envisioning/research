# V2X & Autonomous Mobility Networks

## Summary

Vehicle-to-everything connectivity for safety, traffic efficiency, and autonomy.

## Description

Dedicated communication systems that link vehicles, roadside units, pedestrians, and cloud services using cellular V2X and DSRC. They support cooperative maneuvers, collision avoidance, and real-time traffic optimization, forming a critical layer for autonomous and connected transport.
