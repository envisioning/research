# Industrial Private 5G Networks

## Summary

Dedicated cellular networks for factories, ports, and logistics hubs.

## Description

On-premise 5G deployments owned or controlled by enterprises, tailored for robotics, AGVs, AR-assisted workers, and real-time monitoring. They provide deterministic latency, high reliability, and strong security for Industry 4.0 environments, often integrating with OT systems and edge computing.
