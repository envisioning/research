# Electromagnetic Exposure & Health Monitoring

## Summary

Real-time monitoring and guidelines for RF radiation exposure.

## Description

Sensor networks and wearable devices that measure cumulative electromagnetic field (EMF) exposure from 5G/6G, WiFi, and IoT. Combined with epidemiological research and adaptive power control, these systems address public health concerns and inform precautionary deployment policies.
