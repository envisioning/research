# Emergency & Public Safety Networks (PS-LTE)

## Summary

Dedicated, hardened networks for first responders and disaster scenarios.

## Description

Mission-critical LTE/5G systems with priority access, push-to-talk, direct device-to-device communication, and rapid deployable units. They ensure first responders maintain connectivity during natural disasters, terrorist attacks, and infrastructure failures when commercial networks are overwhelmed or damaged.
