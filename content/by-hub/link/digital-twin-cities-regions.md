# Digital Twin Cities & Regions

## Summary

Virtual replicas of entire urban areas for planning and simulation.

## Description

Real-time 3D models of cities fed by IoT sensors, cameras, GIS, and network telemetry. Planners use them to simulate traffic flows, optimize emergency response, model climate impacts, and test policy changes before deployment. Requires massive bandwidth and edge computing for continuous synchronization.
