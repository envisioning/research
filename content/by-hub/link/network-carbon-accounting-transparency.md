# Network Carbon Accounting & Transparency

## Summary

Standardized measurement and reporting of telecom carbon footprints.

## Description

Methodologies and APIs that measure end-to-end carbon emissions from device manufacturing, network operations, and data center energy use. Enables consumers and regulators to compare operators, incentivize renewable energy adoption, and hold the industry accountable for climate commitments.
