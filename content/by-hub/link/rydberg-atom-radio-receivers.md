# Rydberg Atom Radio Receivers

## Summary

Quantum sensors using excited atoms for ultra-sensitive radio detection.

## Description

Receivers that use alkali atoms excited to high-energy Rydberg states to detect electric fields over a massive frequency range (kHz to THz) with sensitivity far beyond conventional electronics. They enable self-calibrating, jam-resistant, and physically small antennas for military and deep-space communications.
