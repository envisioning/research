# Massive IoT Connectivity Platforms

## Summary

Management and connectivity for billions of low-power devices.

## Description

Platforms that combine NB-IoT, LTE-M, and LPWAN protocols with device management, security, and analytics. They enable large-scale deployment of sensors for agriculture, logistics, smart buildings, and environmental monitoring, with tight power and cost constraints.
