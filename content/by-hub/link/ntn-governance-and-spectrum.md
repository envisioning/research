# Non-Terrestrial Network Governance & Spectrum

## Summary

Policy and coordination frameworks for satellite mega-constellations.

## Description

Standards, treaties, and coordination mechanisms that govern spectrum use, orbital slots, and interference management across thousands of satellites. These frameworks aim to prevent orbital congestion and radio interference while enabling global broadband coverage from LEO and other non-terrestrial networks.
