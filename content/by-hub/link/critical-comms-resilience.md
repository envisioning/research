# Critical Communications Resilience

## Summary

Architectures to keep networks running under extreme failures or attacks.

## Description

Redundancy patterns, mesh topologies, and autonomous fallback modes that ensure connectivity for emergency services, power grids, and financial systems. This includes jam-resistant waveforms, multi-path routing across terrestrial and satellite links, and rapid restoration capabilities after large-scale outages.
