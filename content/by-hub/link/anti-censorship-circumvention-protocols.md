# Anti-Censorship & Circumvention Protocols

## Summary

Technologies to maintain open communication under authoritarian regimes.

## Description

Steganographic techniques, domain fronting, decentralized VPNs, and mesh networks designed to evade deep packet inspection and state-level internet shutdowns. Critical for journalists, activists, and civil society in regions with repressive information controls.
