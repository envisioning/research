# Neuromorphic Edge Processors

## Summary

Brain-inspired chips for ultra-efficient AI inference at the network edge.

## Description

Event-driven, spiking neural network processors that consume orders of magnitude less power than traditional GPUs for AI workloads. Deployed at cell sites and IoT gateways, they enable real-time pattern recognition, anomaly detection, and predictive maintenance without cloud connectivity, critical for remote and energy-constrained deployments.
