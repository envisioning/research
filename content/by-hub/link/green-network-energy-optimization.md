# Green Network Energy Optimization

## Summary

AI and automation to minimize energy consumption across telecom infrastructure.

## Description

Software systems that dynamically turn off unused radios, optimize cooling, shift workloads to renewable-powered sites, and coordinate with smart grids. As networks grow, energy represents a major operational cost and carbon footprint; these systems make sustainability economically viable.
