# Molecular Communication & Internet of Bio-Nano Things

## Summary

Encoding information in molecules for transmission within biological environments.

## Description

A paradigm inspired by biology where information is transmitted using chemical signals (molecules) rather than electromagnetic waves. This enables communication at the nanoscale within the human body (e.g., targeted drug delivery coordination) or in fluids where RF fails, forming the Internet of Bio-Nano Things (IoBNT).
