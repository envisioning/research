# Synthetic Corporate Entities

## Summary

Fully autonomous legal entities managed by AI for specific business functions.

## Description

Algorithmic shell companies or special purpose vehicles that can hold assets, sign contracts, and execute financial transactions without human signatories. These entities allow organizations to rapidly spin up disposable subsidiaries for high-risk experiments, IP holding, or automated trading strategies, though they challenge current legal frameworks.
