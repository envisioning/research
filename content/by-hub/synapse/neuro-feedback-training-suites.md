# Neuro-Feedback Training Suites

## Summary

Immersive environments for accelerating skill acquisition via brain-state modulation.

## Description

Training facilities equipped with EEG headsets and biofeedback interfaces that help employees achieve flow states or accelerate learning. By visualizing their own neural activity, workers can learn to regulate attention and stress responses, significantly shortening the learning curve for complex technical tasks.
