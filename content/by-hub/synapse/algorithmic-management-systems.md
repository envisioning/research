# Algorithmic Management Systems

## Summary

Platforms for automated task allocation and performance evaluation.

## Description

Applications that use algorithms to assign work, monitor progress, and nudge behavior in real-time. While currently prevalent in gig work, these systems are entering knowledge work to optimize flow, though they raise significant questions about autonomy and surveillance.
