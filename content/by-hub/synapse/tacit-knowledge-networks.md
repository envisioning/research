# Tacit Knowledge Networks

## Summary

Tools that surface and connect unwritten institutional knowledge.

## Description

Knowledge management systems that use NLP to mine informal communications (chat, email, transcripts) to identify who knows what. They automatically route questions to the right experts and surface relevant context without requiring manual documentation.
