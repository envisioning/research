# Algorithmic Right-to-Explanation Portals

## Summary

Interfaces that expose how workplace algorithms affect individual workers.

## Description

Employee-facing portals that reveal which algorithms made key decisions—such as shift allocation, promotion shortlists, or performance scores—and provide human-readable explanations and challenge mechanisms. They operationalize emerging AI regulations and rebuild trust in data-driven management.
