# DAO Governance Stacks

## Summary

Blockchain-based protocols for decentralized decision-making and ownership.

## Description

Software suites that codify organizational rules into smart contracts, enabling flat hierarchies where voting power, funding allocation, and equity distribution are transparent and programmatic. These stacks facilitate fluid, permissionless contributions and automated compliance.
