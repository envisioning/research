# Algorithmic Impact Assessors

## Summary

Tools for evaluating the societal and ethical risks of deployed AI systems.

## Description

Comprehensive assessment frameworks and software that audit AI models for potential negative impacts on stakeholders, including bias, privacy violations, and labor displacement. These tools generate risk scores and mitigation strategies, helping organizations navigate the ethical complexities of AI adoption.
