# Agentic Workflow Orchestrators

## Summary

Autonomous AI agents that manage and execute complex business processes.

## Description

Multi-agent systems capable of planning, delegating, and executing cross-functional workflows with minimal human oversight. These orchestrators can negotiate resources between departments, automate routine procurement, and dynamically reconfigure supply chains based on real-time data.
