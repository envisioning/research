# Federated Learning Consortiums

## Summary

Collaborative machine learning across organizations without sharing raw data.

## Description

Privacy-preserving ML frameworks that enable multiple organizations to train shared models on their local data without exposing proprietary information. Organizations collaboratively improve talent prediction, supply chain forecasting, or market intelligence while maintaining competitive confidentiality and regulatory compliance.
