# Asynchronous Voice Rooms

## Summary

Persistent audio spaces for non-linear voice collaboration.

## Description

Digital environments where teams can leave voice notes, updates, and feedback in a spatial or threaded context, allowing for rich vocal expression and nuance without the need for synchronous meetings. These rooms preserve the 'hallway chat' dynamic across time zones.
