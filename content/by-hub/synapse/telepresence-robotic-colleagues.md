# Telepresence Robotic Colleagues

## Summary

Mobile robots that embody remote workers in physical workplaces.

## Description

Networked telepresence robots equipped with cameras, microphones, and displays that allow remote employees to navigate offices, attend ad hoc discussions, and interact with physical artifacts. They aim to close the participation gap between in-person and remote staff in hybrid organizations.
