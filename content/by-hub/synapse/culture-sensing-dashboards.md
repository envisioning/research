# Culture Sensing Dashboards

## Summary

Real-time views into sentiment, norms, and emerging cultural fractures.

## Description

Applications that combine pulse surveys, anonymized communication analysis, and behavioral signals to track psychological safety, trust, and alignment across teams. They help leaders detect early signs of burnout, fragmentation, or value drift and experiment with targeted interventions.
