# Collaborative Cognitive Robots (Cobots)

## Summary

Adaptive robots working alongside humans in knowledge work settings.

## Description

Advanced cobots equipped with natural language processing, computer vision, and manipulation capabilities that assist in research labs, design studios, and manufacturing planning floors. Unlike industrial robots, these systems learn from observation, adapt to unstructured environments, and collaborate on creative problem-solving tasks.
