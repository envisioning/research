# Hybrid Coordination Optimizers

## Summary

Scheduling and coordination engines designed for mixed remote and on-site work.

## Description

Tools that simultaneously optimize meeting times, office attendance, time-zone fairness, and uninterrupted focus time. By modeling individuals' preferences, constraints, and collaboration graphs, these systems propose team-level rhythms and co-location patterns that minimize coordination overhead.
