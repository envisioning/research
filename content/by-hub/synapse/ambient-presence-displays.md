# Ambient Presence Displays

## Summary

Peripheral devices providing non-intrusive awareness of team status.

## Description

Dedicated hardware devices that abstractly convey the availability, mood, or activity levels of remote colleagues through light, color, or haptics. These systems aim to restore the 'sense of presence' found in physical offices without the privacy invasion of constant video feeds.
