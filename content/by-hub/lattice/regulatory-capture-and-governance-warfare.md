# Regulatory Capture & Governance Warfare

## Summary

Power struggles over rules in token-governed and regulated systems.

## Description

Regulatory capture and governance warfare focuses on how whale governance, VC cartels, and lobbying can shape both protocol rules and formal regulation. It studies attacks on governance processes, vote-buying, and cross-border lobbying strategies that can lock in favorable rules for incumbents at the expense of smaller participants and public interest.
