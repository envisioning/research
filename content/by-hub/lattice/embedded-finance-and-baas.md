# Embedded Finance & Composable BaaS

## Summary

Non-financial platforms embedding accounts, credit, and payments.

## Description

Embedded finance and composable Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) are systems that allow non-financial platforms including commerce sites, SaaS applications, and logistics platforms to embed financial services including accounts, credit, insurance, and payments directly into their user experience, so users can access financial services without leaving the platform they're using. Programmable ledgers (blockchains with smart contracts) and APIs (application programming interfaces) turn every software product into a potential distribution channel for financial services, blurring the line between banks and software platforms by enabling any company to offer financial services, creating a new model where financial services are integrated into everyday applications rather than being separate services.

This innovation addresses the fragmentation of financial services, where users must use separate platforms for different financial needs. By embedding finance, these systems can improve user experience. Fintech companies, banks, and technology platforms are developing these capabilities.

The technology is particularly significant for improving financial services accessibility, where embedded finance can make services more convenient. As these systems expand, they could transform how financial services are delivered. However, ensuring compliance, managing complexity, and maintaining security remain challenges. The technology represents an important evolution in financial services delivery, but requires continued development to address regulatory and technical challenges. Success could make financial services more accessible, but the technology must ensure compliance and security. Embedded finance is a rapidly growing area of fintech innovation.
